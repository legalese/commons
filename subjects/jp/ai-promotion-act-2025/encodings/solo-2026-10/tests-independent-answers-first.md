Seat: a business operator (活用事業者, "utilization business operator", Article 7).

## (a) Duty on a business operator to REPORT an AI incident or rights infringement

**Answer: none. No provision imposes on a business operator (or on anyone outside government) a duty to report an AI incident, an accident, a malfunction, or an infringement of rights, to anyone.**

## (b) Penalties, naming/publication, investigation, orders, inspection; duty to comply

- **Penalty (罰則, fine, imprisonment, administrative fine 過料):** none. There is no penalty chapter and no penalty provision anywhere in Articles 1-28 or the Supplementary Provisions.
- **Naming / publication of an operator (公表 of a person or company):** none. The only 公表 in the Act is Article 18(4): the Prime Minister publishes the AI Basic Plan. The secondary claim that companies concerned in rights violations "will be named publicly" is not in the text. Article 16's "情報の提供その他の必要な措置" (provision of information and other necessary measures) is open-textured, but names no naming power and attaches nothing to a named operator.
- **Investigation power over a private actor (報告徴収, 立入検査, 質問):** none. Article 16's 調査及び研究 is the State's own survey/research ("research and studies" in the English); it confers no power to compel anything from an operator.
- **Order (命令, 勧告 with consequences):** none. Article 16 provides 指導 (guidance) and 助言 (advice); no duty to follow them and no consequence of not following them is stated.
- **Inspection:** none.
- **Duty to comply:** the only mandatory verb addressed to a business operator is Article 7's 協力しなければならない (must cooperate) with national-government measures under Article 4 and local-government measures under Article 5. No sanction, no enforcement mechanism, no content of "cooperation" is stated. No duty to comply is attached to Article 16 guidance or advice, or to an Article 25 request.
- Forward-looking provisions that create nothing now: Article 10 (the State is to take legislative and financial measures), Supplementary Article 2 (review and required measures), Article 28 (Cabinet Order on matters concerning the Headquarters only).

The contrast that matters: operators MUST cooperate (Art 7, 協力しなければならない); research institutes (Art 6(1)) and citizens (Art 8) only ENDEAVOUR to cooperate (協力するよう努めるものとする).
Within Article 7 itself, the first limb is endeavour and the second is must.

## (d) Article 16

Who: the national government (国). Does what: (i) collects information on domestic and international trends in AI R&D and utilisation; (ii) analyses cases (事案) in which citizens' rights or interests were infringed as a result of (伴って) AI R&D or utilisation for wrongful purposes or by inappropriate methods, and considers countermeasures based on that analysis; (iii) other surveys and research contributing to promotion; then (iv) based on the results, takes guidance (指導), advice (助言), provision of information (情報の提供) and other necessary measures toward research institutes, utilisation business operators and others.
For an operator: no duty to provide information, no duty to report a case, no duty to follow guidance or advice, no consequence stated. The operator is a possible RECIPIENT of guidance, advice and information. How the State collects information is not stated.

## (e) Article 25

The Headquarters (commenced with Chapter IV, not on promulgation):
- 25(1): when it finds it necessary for its affairs, may REQUEST (求める) submission of materials, statement of opinions, explanations and other necessary cooperation from the heads of relevant administrative organs, local governments, incorporated administrative agencies, local IAAs, and the representatives of special corporations (as defined by reference to MIC Establishment Act Art 4(1)(viii)).
- 25(2): when it finds it PARTICULARLY necessary (特に必要がある), may ASK (依頼) necessary cooperation of persons other than those in 25(1).
A private business operator that is not a special corporation falls within 25(2) only. No duty to comply is attached in Article 25, and no consequence of declining is stated anywhere.
Ambiguity (open): whether Article 7's "must cooperate with measures implemented by the national government under Article 4" reaches an Article 25(2) request. Reading A: the Headquarters is part of the national government implementing Article 4 measures, so an operator "must cooperate" (still sanctionless). Reading B: Article 25(2) is a specific provision drafted as a mere request (依頼), so no duty attaches. My answer to "does Article 25 attach a duty to comply?" is NO; to "can I be penalised for declining?" is NO under either reading.

## (f) Commencement

Supplementary Article 1: the Act takes effect on the day of promulgation (公布の日), which is 2025-06-04 (XML attributes and e-Gov promulgation_date). Proviso: Chapter III (Art 18), Chapter IV (Arts 19-28) and Supplementary Articles 3 and 4 take effect on a day specified by Cabinet Order within a period not exceeding three months counted from (起算して) the day of promulgation.

## (g) "Am I a business operator under this Act?"

The operator definition is in Article 7, not Article 2 or 3. Article 7: 活用事業者 = 人工知能関連技術を活用した製品又はサービスの開発又は提供をしようとする者 (a person who intends to develop or provide products or services that utilise AI-related technology) その他の人工知能関連技術を事業活動において活用しようとする者 (or any other person who intends to utilise AI-related technology in business activities).
Article 2 defines 人工知能関連技術 (AI-related technology): (A) technology necessary to realise, by artificial means, functions that substitute for human intellectual abilities of cognition, inference and judgement; and (B) technology relating to information-processing systems that realise the function of processing input information using (A) and outputting the result.
Article 3 defines nothing (Article 4(1) makes "基本理念" mean the principles of Article 3).
So the question turns on: (1) does the person's product/service, or its business activity, utilise AI-related technology as Article 2 defines it; (2) is it in business activities (事業活動), as opposed to a citizen's private use (Article 8 instead).
Tests: developer or provider of an AI product → yes. A business using AI in its operations (merely as a user) → yes. A private individual using AI outside business → no (a citizen, Art 8). A business that does not utilise AI at all → no.

Ambiguities in (g):
1. しようとする者 ("intends to"): read to include those already developing/providing/using (Reading A, mine) vs only prospective actors (Reading B).
2. 事業活動 ("business activities"): whether a non-profit's activities count.
3. Territorial scope: the Act says nothing about foreign operators.
4. Overlap with 研究開発機関 (Art 6: universities, R&D corporations, "other institutions conducting AI R&D"): whether a private company doing AI R&D is also a research institute. If it is both, the stronger Art 7 "must cooperate" applies to it as an operator.
5. The English "other artificial intelligence-related technology" in Art 7 is a translator's artefact; the Japanese has no "other" before the second 人工知能関連技術.

## Other ambiguities noticed before reading the encoding

- Article 7's cooperation duty is sanctionless; whether it is "enforceable" is a question the Act does not answer (no remedy, no penalty, no order).
- Article 3(4)'s mandatory passive (講じられなければならない) has no named addressee; I read it as addressed to government, not to operators.
- Article 8's 努める scope (both limbs vs cooperation only).
- The English numbers two Supplementary Provisions "Article 4"; the Japanese extract cannot check it.
- Three-month window end: 2025-09-03 vs 2025-09-04 (does not matter for the e-Gov date of 2025-09-01).
