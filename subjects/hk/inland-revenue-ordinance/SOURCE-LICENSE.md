# Source license

The text of **Inland Revenue Ordinance** is published on the [Hong Kong e-Legislation (HKeL), Department of Justice](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/).

- **Terms**: Crown/HKSAR Government copyright, not an open licence. You may download, print, disseminate, distribute and copy legislation, but copies must be accurate and the Government can withdraw permission at any time. Bulk ('Mass Replication') and reuse in a product ('Product Reproduction', which covers databases and AI- or computer-generated content) need a separate revocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free, non-sub-licensable licence. That licence requires a prescribed copyright/licence/version-date statement, forbids misleading or derogatory use, and carries indemnities. The policy grants no express right to adapt or make derivative works.

- **From the terms**: "Subject to paragraphs 2 to 5 below, you may download, print, disseminate, distribute and make copies of Legislation published in HKeL."

- **Source**: https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap112

## Consequence for this subject

**Do not deposit statute text here, or quote it in an encoding, without written permission.** The terms allow accurate copying and distribution but grant no right to adapt, and reuse in a product is licensed separately. This directory holds metadata only until permission is obtained.

## Language

English is not the only authoritative language. Under the Official Languages Ordinance (Cap. 5), English and Chinese are both official languages. The register's s.3 text says they 'possess equal status'. The English and Chinese texts of an Ordinance are equally authentic (s.10B, cited from knowledge, not checked in the register). The register also gives legal status only to 'Verified Copy' PDFs and the printed Gazette; other formats are for information only. Encodings should note that the Chinese text may be needed to resolve ambiguity.
