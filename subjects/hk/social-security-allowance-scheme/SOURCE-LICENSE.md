## Consequence for this subject

**Metadata only until a licence decision is recorded.** Reproduction is allowed for non-commercial use only, and adaptation for commercial use is prohibited without SWD's written authorisation. This repository treats non-commercial-only terms as not clearing deposit (compare `subjects/ca/nl/`). This is an **admin-policy source, not legislation**: the scheme has no Ordinance, and SWD can change its rules and rates without legislation.

## Language

SWD publishes the scheme pages in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese. No language version is legally authoritative, because these are administrative rules, not law. The English pages were read.
