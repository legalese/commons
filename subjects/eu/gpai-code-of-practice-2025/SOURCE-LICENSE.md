### Why the status is not "determined"

Both notices license "content owned by the EU" and say that content not owned by the EU may need the rightholders' permission.
The text was drafted by the independent Chairs and Vice-Chairs the title page names, and nothing read for this deposit says whether the copyright in it is the EU's.
So no grant is recorded as applying.
The sibling deposit `subjects/comparative/ai-content-labelling/SOURCE-LICENSE.md` treated another chapter-style Code, drafted the same way, as CC BY 4.0 on the same kind of evidence; this deposit does not follow it, because the ownership question was not answered there either.
A reader who establishes EU ownership can change the status to CC BY 4.0 with no other change: the attribution the notice asks for is already given below and in the repository `NOTICE`.
