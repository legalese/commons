## How the files are made

- Layer 1 and its tests: templates in `../../../work/src/*.l4` (macros `@@Q line|text@@`, `@@Q line|start|end@@`, `@@REF cite|lines|row ids@@`), expanded by `work/build.py` into this folder, which copies every quotation out of the source file and fails if a quoted slice does not occur on the cited line.
  Edit the templates, never the expanded files.
- Seats and their cases: generated by `work/seatgen.py` from the specs in `work/seats/{individual,provider,sender,mirrors}.py`; edit the specs, never the generated files.
- Coverage: `work/coverage.py` (rows from `coverage-rows.py`, row-to-function links from the `row:` tokens in the `@ref` lines, forks from `work/forks.py`, relations from `work/jural.py`).
- `work/notes.py` writes `NOTES.md`; `work/finalize.py` writes `encoding.json` and `SOURCE-LICENSE.md`.
- `work/final.sh` runs the whole pass in the foreground: build, seat generation, srcrefs, kinds, diagnostics per file, traces on both builds, `check.sh`, coverage, then the documents.
