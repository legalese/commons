# ANSWERS (written from the text alone, before any .l4 file was opened)

Vocabulary: YES = the text says yes (JUST TRUE). NO = the text itself says no (JUST FALSE). NC = not covered (NOTHING).
"Conv:" names the sentence of the family convention or the ruling I rely on.
"CB:" marks one of the six case-by-case questions (an encoding answering differently is AMBIGUOUS, not ENCODER).
The Directive says nothing about money amounts, so there is no amount scenario; the only numbers are dates and the three years of Art 18.
