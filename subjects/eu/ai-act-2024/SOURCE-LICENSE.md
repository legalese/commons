## Official Journal texts: UNDETERMINED

`registers/source-bundle/eu-ai-act-2024-1689.txt` (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) and `registers/source-bundle/eu-omnibus-2026-1744.txt` (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) are plain-text extractions of Official Journal texts fetched from the Publications Office cellar on 2026-10-01.
No reuse grant that covers Official Journal legal texts has been read, so none is recorded here.

What was tried on **2026-10-24**, with the User-Agent `legalese-commons-source-fetcher/1.0 (+https://github.com/legalese/commons)`:

- `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/robots.txt` answered HTTP 202 with an empty body, the bot challenge `subjects/comparative/ai-content-labelling/SOURCE-LICENSE.md` recorded on 2026-10-01.
  EUR-Lex's legal notice, which states the terms for Official Journal texts, was therefore not requested.
- `https://op.europa.eu/en/web/about-us/legal-notices/publications-office-of-the-european-union-copyright` answered **HTTP 403** (an "Azure WAF" page).
  Treated as a no and not worked around.
- `https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en` answered 200.
  Its copyright notice, verbatim:

  > © European Union, 1995-2026 The Commission's reuse policy is implemented by the Commission Decision of 12 December 2011 on the reuse of Commission documents . Unless otherwise indicated (e.g. in individual copyright notices), content owned by the EU on this website is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence . This means that reuse is allowed, provided appropriate credit is given and changes are indicated.

  That notice licenses "content owned by the EU on this website", meaning commission.europa.eu.
  The Official Journal is published by the Publications Office, not on that website, so the notice is not relied on for these two texts.

This deposit follows the precedent of `subjects/comparative/ai-content-labelling/`, which deposits the same two texts under the same UNDETERMINED status.
