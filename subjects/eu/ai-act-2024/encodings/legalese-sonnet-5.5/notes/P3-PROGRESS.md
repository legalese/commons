## How the files are built (outside the deposit)

- Sources are templates `DEPOSIT/../../work/src/<name>.l4.in` (that is `dst-aia-P3/work/src/`), expanded by `work/gen.py` into `DEPOSIT/<name>.l4`.
  `@@QREF cite :: c N-M [:: b N-M]` prints the quote lines (from `srcrefs.py quote`/`quoteid`) and the matching `@ref` with exact locators; `@@REF` prints only the `@ref`.
  `@@DUTY ... @@END` expands one point of a duty into a decision function (gate, NOTHING/FALSE branches) and a regulative rule; `@@FIXTURES` and `@@DEF $x = name` give tests short names.
  The deposited `.l4` files are plain L4; the macros only save typing.
- `work/diag.py FILE` runs one file (assertion counts, errors, warnings); `work/tracecheck.py FILE` runs each `#TRACE` on both builds and compares with the `-- EXPECT` comment above it.

## Architecture decisions (taken while writing; assumed, not ruled)

- Every decision function of Articles 16 to 27 takes `the date asked`, `the AI system` (the core record) and `the circumstances` (`Section 3 circumstances`: Section B flag, Art 2(13)(a) and (b) facts), then its own facts.
  It passes through `P3-section-3.l4`'s `Section 3 gate` (Art 113 + Art 111(2) via the keel; Art 2(2) cut-off S14; Art 2(13) limit S15):
  shut -> NOTHING, cut off by Art 2(2) -> JUST FALSE, may be limited under Art 2(13) -> NOTHING, open -> the provision's own answer.
- The party's role is assumed by the function (the seat asks the role once, in its own question).
- Name direction: a function named "has met / keeps ..." answers NOTHING when the duty's own scope condition ("when under their control", "upon a reasoned request") fails;
  a function named as a bar ("is barred") answers JUST FALSE when the bar's own "where" condition fails (fork F-when-upon).
- A bare "shall" not done: JUST FALSE (fork F-bare-shall, same as the keel's F-4-1).

## Conventions taken (for NOTES.md forks)

- F-when-upon: a "has met" function answers NOTHING when the duty's own trigger ("upon", "where", "while", "to the extent", "where relevant") is absent; an effect or bar function ("is barred", "is no longer a provider", "applies mutatis mutandis") answers JUST FALSE when a "Where"/"When"/"If" fails (periods ruling rule (b); delegated-definitions ruling item (c)) and JUST FALSE after an "until" (item (d)); the two Article 26(10) third-subparagraph bars answer NOTHING outside their own limit (fork F-bars, line 87).
- F-immediately: "immediately" = WITHIN 0 on a clock of whole days (act on the day of the trigger). F-undue-delay: "without undue delay" has no number, no deadline encoded.
- F-prior-to: "before placing / making available" = inclusive deadline at the placement date in the `AI system` record (importer, authorised representative) or the date the distributor or deployer states.
- F-18-1: the 10-year period runs from the placement (`add years`); after its last day the keeps functions answer JUST FALSE (periods ruling rule (a)); before the placement NOTHING (the text gives no start).
- F-25-1-closed: "in any of the following circumstances" is not closed with "only": none holding gives NOTHING.
- F-24-as-applicable: "the provider or the importer, as applicable": the importer where there is one, else the provider.
- F-23-3-places: "where applicable" in 23(3) is one input for all the places.
- F-26-5-deeming: the financial-institution deeming reaches the duty to monitor, not the duty to inform providers.
- F-26-10-ex-ante: the 48-hour rule counts hours from the start of use; an ex ante request is at hour 0.
- F-annual: Art 26(10) annual report is due within 365 days of the date asked.
- Art 2(13) names Articles 9 to 15 and 17 to 25 only (consolidated line 58): Articles 16, 26 and 27 are never limited by it.
- Art 25(4) carve-out ("other than general-purpose AI models") has the same words in the base text (line 1014) and the consolidated text (line 487); only the list of supplied items differs ("AI model" added).
