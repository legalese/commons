### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (P9)

- `JUST FALSE` appears only where the text says no in its own words: a "means"; an "if", "only if", "unless" or "until" bar; a list closed by "only"; a flat negative ("shall not", "does not"); a "where", "when", "upon" or "after" condition on a duty or a power that is not met (the ruling of 2026-11-05 (b) and (c)); and a bare "shall" that is not done once the duty has arisen (fork F-bare-must).
  Every such branch carries `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "<the words>"` beside it (68 branches; the table under "Marginal readings for a lawyer" lists them all).
- `NOTHING` is "not covered": the text is silent; a list is not closed; "includes"; an undefined term; a point that lies outside the sources; a grant or a prohibition limited by its own wording where the case falls outside the limit; a scope limit with no "only" (ruling of 2026-11-05 (c)); and, always, a date on which the group of the provision does not apply yet, or does not apply to the AI system because Article 111(2) saves it.
  Every such branch carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)`.
  Counts in the layer-1 modules: 202 "limited by its own wording", 18 "list not closed", 13 "silent", 2 "points outside the text", 2 "silent or list not closed", 1 "includes".
- A seat question returns `JUST TRUE`, `JUST FALSE` or `NOTHING` as its layer-1 function does; it adds no branch of its own.
  The ceilings answer `JUST <euros>` or `NOTHING`.
- A regulative rule owes nothing (`FULFILLED`) before its group applies; a rule whose deadline is missed reaches `BREACH` only where the text gives a time limit or a consequence.
- The questions that ask the group's applicability answer `JUST TRUE` or `JUST FALSE` (never `NOTHING`), from the keel's function.

## What the text turns out to say (numbered findings)

1. **The fines are three tiers, and the ceilings are "whichever is higher" for an undertaking and a flat amount for anyone else.**
   Article 99(3): EUR 35 000 000 or 7 % of total worldwide annual turnover for the preceding financial year; 99(4): EUR 15 000 000 or 3 %; 99(5): EUR 7 500 000 or 1 %.
   An SME gets "whichever is lower" (99(6)); an SMC does too, but only for the infringements of paragraphs 4 and 5 (99(6a), as amended, not for Article 5): fork F-99-6a.
   An offender that is not an undertaking has the flat amount only.
2. **Article 100 has no percentage and no SME rule.**
   The European Data Protection Supervisor's fines on a Union institution, body, office or agency are up to EUR 1 500 000 for Article 5 and EUR 750 000 for any other requirement (100(2), (3)); `Art 100 the ceiling in euros of the administrative fine on the body` takes only the infringement and so has no date gate and no seat question (a question with no input would carry nothing to ask).
3. **Article 101(1) fines providers of general-purpose AI models up to 3 % of turnover or EUR 15 000 000, whichever is higher, and its rendering is garbled in one place.**
   "The Commission shall also into account commitments" lacks a verb in the consolidated file (fork F-101-1-typo); the sentence is read as "take into account".
4. **Article 99(2) sets a notification deadline that Article 113 fixes to a date: 2 August 2025.**
   "at the latest by the date of entry into application" is, for Chapter XII, the keel's `Art 113(b) ... applies from`, 2 August 2025; the time is counted from a day the caller states (fork F-99-2).
5. **Chapter X has one deadline: Article 96(1)(g), added by the Omnibus, says the guidelines on Article 8(2), Article 9(10) and Article 17(3) "shall be published by 1 August 2027".**
   Every other duty of Articles 95 and 96 has no time limit and no stated consequence.
6. **Article 112 is a calendar of the Commission's reports.**
   112(1): the need for amendment of Annex III and Article 5, once a year from the entry into force until the end of the delegation period of Article 97 (fork F-112-1); 112(2): by 2 August 2028 and every four years; 112(3): by 2 August 2029 and every four years, made public, with a proposal where appropriate; 112(5): by 2 August 2028 on the AI Office; 112(6): by 2 August 2028 and every four years on standardisation; 112(7): by 2 August 2028 and every three years on voluntary codes; 112(13): by 2 August 2031 on enforcement (fork F-112-n).
   Each has a `#TRACE` that reaches `BREACH` on the day after the deadline.
7. **Articles 102 to 109 are amendments of other instruments and Article 110 adds the Regulation to a Directive's list.**
   Each of Articles 102 to 109 adds a sentence to another Union instrument (Regulation (EC) No 300/2008, (EU) No 167/2013, (EU) No 168/2013, Directive 2014/90/EU, Directive (EU) 2016/797, Regulation (EU) 2018/858, (EU) 2018/1139 in six items, (EU) 2019/2144) so that the requirements of Chapter III Section 2 "shall be taken into account" when the Commission adopts acts on AI systems that are safety components; I treat them as machinery (reason 2), one coverage row per article and one per numbered item of Article 108.
   Article 110 adds the Regulation as point (68) of Annex I to Directive (EU) 2020/1828 (representative actions for the collective interests of consumers); I encoded it for what it means to a person (the Regulation is on the list for a representative action) and left the Directive's own conditions as inputs.
8. **Article 88(1) gives the Commission "exclusive powers" and 88(2) lets a market surveillance authority request the Commission to act.**
   A body other than the Commission answers `JUST FALSE` to "does it have the power to supervise and enforce Chapter V" (fork F-88-1).
9. **Articles 85, 87 and 94 have no paragraph numbers.**

## What the stable binary does not accept

Where the skills describe a construct the stable binary rejects, the binary governs.
Sorted by how the failure shows up, since a reader's attention should go to the ones that do not announce themselves.

**Silent (the run exits 0 and an answer is wrong or unseen):**

- **`NOT` binds looser than `OR` and `AND`.**
  A bare `NOT x OR y` is `NOT (x OR y)`, and a `NOT` before an `EQUALS` is read the same way; every `NOT` in P9 is parenthesised.
  The failure is an answer, not an error.
- **A trace outcome can differ between builds without any assertion showing it.**
  `check.sh` compares assertion counts only.
  Every `#TRACE` of P9 has an `-- EXPECT` line and `work/tracecheck.py` compared the outcome on both builds (125 traces, no difference).
- **A duty with no time limit and no consequence is `PENDING` when nobody acts and `FULFILLED` when the act is done late.**
  It is never `BREACH`, which reads like success unless the trace output is read; these rules are listed under "Rules with no breach path".

**Loud (an error or a warning, which `check.sh` counts):**

- No `REFUSE`, `#ASSERT REFUSED` or `BECAUSE` (except `LEST BREACH BY ... BECAUSE`): a case outside the encoding is `NOTHING` and is asserted.
- An `#ASSERT` is on one line; a continuation line is a parse error.
- Dates are written `Date day month year`; no `YMD` is defined (a second `YMD` collides with daydate's on a build that has one).
- `WAIT UNTIL` is spelled `WAITUNTIL`; there is no `TYPICALLY` on a field, no set, and no `GIVEN` at section level.
- A field of a record in an expression needs its own brackets: `(`the AI system`'s `route`)`, `JUST (x's field)`.
- Arithmetic is spelled `PLUS`, `MINUS`, `TIMES`, `DIVIDED BY` and a chain that crosses a line is bracketed.
- A constructor of the wrong act type, or a name defined in two imported modules, is a type error.

**For the source references (silent):** `srcrefs.py check --strict` checks the quotes that exist, not that every line of the range is quoted.
Line 1488 (Article 99(5)) was unquoted while the check printed 0 problems; the coverage script, which looks for a quoted line in every row, found it, and the quote was added.
