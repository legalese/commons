### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (P1)

A function of P1 answers `MAYBE BOOLEAN` unless it returns a definition (`BOOLEAN`) or a date.
- **`JUST TRUE`**: the text says yes to the question the name asks.
- **`JUST FALSE`** (or `FALSE` from a definition) is the text's own no, and only in these cases: a carve-out the text states (`shall not apply`, `except where`, `does not cover`, `unless`, `shall not constitute`, `without prejudice`); a list closed with `only` (`only prohibited where`, `shall be authorised only if`, `grant ... only where`); a flat `shall not qualify` or `shall not be considered`; a `where` condition that fails (Article 6(1), 6(6), 6(7), Article 7); a class the text hands to a list (Annex I, II, III); a failed `if` on a duty (Article 5(3) rejection).
- **`NOTHING`** is "not covered", with the reason in a `-- NOTHING-kind` comment: the provision does not apply on the date asked (a gate); an element of a prohibited practice is missing (limited by its own wording); a grant is limited by its own wording (`within the limits`, `in accordance with Union law`); the term is the text of another instrument (undefined term); the text is silent. A list of answers that the caller combined is not closed (list not closed).
- **Plain `BOOLEAN` functions** (Annex II, the 33 Annex III point functions, `Art 6(1) as enacted ...`, `Art 6(1) as amended ...`, `Art 6(1a) and (1b) ...`, `Art 6(1c) ...`, `Art 6(5) the provision applies ...`): `FALSE` there is the list's, the definition's or the paragraph's own no, or (for the 6(5) function) "does not apply on that date".
- **Silent failures** (wrong answer, exit code 0), each listed in `What the stable binary does not accept`: a `#TRACE` outcome is invisible to `check.sh`; `Date` rolls an impossible date forward; a negative `WITHIN` breaches at once.

## Findings: what the text turns out to say

1. **Article 6(5) is due before it applies.** The Commission must provide guidelines "no later than 2 February 2026" (line 236), and Article 113 puts Article 6(5) in the provision group that applies from 2 August 2026, the General group (lines 1657 and 1664: point (c) excepts Article 6(5)). The rule is armed from the Regulation's entry into force (fork F-6-5-gate).
2. **Article 5(4) points at information that paragraph 6 does not list as such.** Paragraph 4 says the notification "shall, as a minimum, contain the information specified under paragraph 6" (line 208); paragraph 6 (line 210) speaks of annual reports to the Commission and of a template that includes "information on the number of the decisions taken by competent judicial authorities" and "their result". It names no list of information for a notification. The rule `Art 5(4) the duty to include in the notification the information specified under paragraph 6` carries the words and cannot say what the information is; it has no breach path.
3. **There is a four-month window in which the Omnibus text is the law and points (ba) and (bb) do not yet apply.** The Omnibus is in force from 27 July 2026 and the Article 5 additions apply from 2 December 2026 (line 1660). The tests assert `NOTHING` for 27 July to 1 December 2026 and `JUST TRUE` from 2 December 2026.
4. **The text of Chapter III as enacted never applied.** Chapter III Sections 1 to 3 applied as enacted from 2 August 2026 (Annex III) and 2 August 2027 (Annex I) (base lines 2199 and 2206); the Omnibus took effect on 27 July 2026, before the first of those dates. So `Art 6(1) the AI system is high-risk` answers `NOTHING` on every date the old text was in force, and the as-enacted function is tested directly.
5. **Article 6(6) to (8) apply with Chapter III, not with the General group.** Point (c) excepts only Article 6(5) (line 1664), so the Commission's power to add or modify the conditions of paragraph 3, and Article 7's power over Annex III, apply from 2 December 2027 on the Annex III route.
6. **The derogation of Article 6(3) is written in two subparagraphs that read most naturally together.** The first subparagraph states the derogation where there is no significant risk; the second says it "shall apply where any of the following conditions is fulfilled"; profiling defeats it (line 234). Fork F-6-3-cumulative.
7. **Annex I is a different list on the two sides of 27 July 2026.** Item 1 (machinery directive) is in Section A as enacted and gone as amended; item 21 (machinery regulation) is in Section B as amended. The machinery products move from the Section A route to the Section B treatment of Article 2(2).
8. **Objective (iii) of Article 5(1)(h) needs two facts about the offence.** It must be an offence "referred to in Annex II and punishable in the Member State concerned by a custodial sentence or a detention order for a maximum period of at least four years" (line 192). Both are asked; four years is inside the exception (tested: an Annex II offence at 3 years leaves the use prohibited, at 4 and at 5 years it does not, and a 4-year offence outside Annex II leaves it prohibited).
9. **Article 5(2) makes the registration a condition of authorisation and then lets the use start without it in urgency.** The function answers the base "only if" and the proviso together (fork F-5-2-urgency).
10. **A Chapter III provision that is about no one AI system needs a placement date the keel will not save.** NEEDS-CORE.md.
11. **The person and manufacturer seats do not call the keel's scope module** (`aia-scope.l4`), so their role questions under Article 2(1)(g) and 2(1)(e) are not written (NEEDS-CORE.md).

## What the stable binary does not accept

**Loud** (an error teaches); the ones P1 met:
- An empty list written `(LIST OF MAYBE BOOLEAN)` in an `#ASSERT` is a parse error; write `EMPTY`.
- A record element of a `LIST` without its own parentheses is `check/wrong-argument-count`; write each record in parentheses.
- A `sed -i 's/.../' file` on macOS (BSD sed) is read as a write command and silently does the wrong thing; this is the tool, not the binary, and P1 edited with Python.
- From the brief and PLAN.md section 5, followed and not re-tested: `REFUSE`, sets, `TYPICALLY`, a section `GIVEN`, an opaque `DECLARE`, `YMD`, `AFTER ... WITHIN`, an input named in a `MUST` action, `MAY ... LEST`.

**Silent** (a wrong answer with exit code 0), the ones worth the words:
- A `#TRACE` outcome is invisible to `check.sh`; P1 ran every trace on both builds and compared by hand.
- A `WITHIN` that is negative is a breach at once, on both builds (tested: Article 6(4) with the consideration on the day of the placing). A reader who expects an error will not get one.
- `Date` rolls an impossible date forward (`Date 31 6 2026` is 1 July 2026; PLAN.md section 5); every date in P1 is a real one.
- A regulative rule whose gate is false returns `FULFILLED`, not `NOTHING`, so a rule asked on a date before its provision applies reads as "nothing owed" (the rules in split site (iii)).

What worked identically on both builds and is used: `BRANCH ... IF ... THEN ... OTHERWISE`, `CONSIDER ... OTHERWISE`, `MUST ... WITHIN 0`, a negative `WITHIN`, `MUST` with no `WITHIN`, `SHANT` with no `WITHIN`, `Date ... MINUS 1`, `AT LEAST` and `AT MOST`, `any (GIVEN r YIELD ...)` over a list of `MAYBE BOOLEAN`.
