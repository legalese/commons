## Fork register (P8)

Every ambiguity where the text can be read two or more ways with different outcomes, the other reading, the answer taken, and the text that licenses it.
The sites of split question (ii) (a "may" gated by "where", "when", "upon" and the like) are all one fork: the answer taken is `JUST FALSE` (the periods ruling's rule (b): in giving the power only on the condition, the text says in its own words that there is none otherwise); the other reading is `NOTHING` (the grant ruling: the text is silent about the case where the condition fails).
The sites decided by the grant ruling of 2026-11-12 (a "may" gated by "if", "provided that", "as appropriate", "with the agreement of") answer `NOTHING`; the other reading is `JUST FALSE`.

## Jural relations (P8)

Each regulative rule of layer 1, as a Hohfeldian relation.
A `MUST` is a duty of the party named and the claim of whoever the act is done to or for (the act's own words name them); a `MAY` is a privilege or power of the party, with a no-right (or a liability) in the other; a `SHANT` is a duty not to act, with the correlative claim.
The party `the party` is an input: the rule is asked of whoever is in the position (Art 78).

Summary of the relations in Chapter IX.
(1) The provider owes the market surveillance authorities (or, within the AI Office's competence, the AI Office) a report of a serious incident within the period of Art 73(2) to (4) or Art 75(1a); they hold the claim, and the authority holds the power to take measures within seven days (Art 73(8)).
(2) The provider owes the authority access to documentation and data (Art 74(12)) and, on a reasoned request and when other means are exhausted, to source code (Art 74(13)).
(3) The AI Office holds the powers of a market surveillance authority over the operator (Art 75a), the power to accept commitments (Art 75b) and to decide non-compliance, fines and periodic penalty payments (Art 75c); the operator is liable to them, holds the privilege to propose commitments and the claim to rights of defence and access to the file (Art 75d).
(4) Operators owe the market surveillance authority cooperation and corrective action (Art 79(2), (4)); the authority owes the Commission and the Member States notification (Art 79(3), (5) to (7)).
(5) Everyone involved in the application of the Regulation owes confidentiality (Art 78(1)); the authorities owe data minimisation, security and deletion (Art 78(2)).

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (P8)

A decision function answers `JUST TRUE`, `JUST FALSE` or `NOTHING` (a `MAYBE`).
`JUST FALSE` is a "no" the text gives in its own words; each such branch carries `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words)` and the quoted words (139 such branches).
`NOTHING` is "the text does not answer": each such branch carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<kind>)` with one of: silent, list not closed, includes, undefined term, points outside the text, limited by its own wording.
Counts of `NOTHING` branches by kind: limited by its own wording 305, list not closed 5, includes 2, silent 2.
`NOTHING` is also the answer for a date before the provision group applies, and for a system saved by Art 111(2); it means "not covered", never "no".
A flat negative ("shall not") is a bar: the rule is a `SHANT` that reads `FULFILLED` when the bar does not apply, and a decision function (where there is one) answers `JUST TRUE` where the bar applies and `NOTHING` elsewhere, never `JUST FALSE`, except where the bar has an end the text names: an "until" bar answers `JUST TRUE` before the event and `JUST FALSE` after it (ruling (d); Art 73(6) second subparagraph, F-73-6-until), and a limitation period answers `JUST FALSE` inside it and `JUST TRUE` after it (ruling (a); Art 75c(8), F-75c-8-inside).
A regulative rule (`DEONTIC`) answers `FULFILLED`, `BREACH` or a residual obligation: `FULFILLED` also where nothing is owed, and a `MAY` that lapses reads `FULFILLED` on both builds.

## What the text turns out to say (numbered findings)

1. Art 75a(2) second paragraph lets a market surveillance authority send a request to the AI Office where it "has reason to suspect non-compliance"; Art 75(2a) lets it ask for an assessment where it "has well-founded and sufficient reasons to suspect" an infringement. These look like the same request with two thresholds; both are kept as written (F-75a-2b-where, F-75-2a-where).
2. Art 75(1a) derogates from Art 73 for a provider within the AI Office's competence and adds that Art 73(2) to (9) "shall apply mutatis mutandis"; the encoding carries the periods of Art 73(2) to (4) across (the same period function serves both reports) and does not restate (5) to (9).
3. The text sets three dates earlier than the group that contains them applies: Art 73(7) (guidance by 2 August 2025), Art 77(2) (list by 2 November 2024) and Art 72(3) as enacted (2 February 2026), against 2 August 2026. A duty with a date that has already passed when the group begins reads `NOTHING` before the group applies and then a deadline already past (F-dated-before-application).
4. Art 75d(2) second sentence uses "entitled"; it is the one function of this part named for an entitlement, and it is encoded and listed, not decided.
5. Art 75c(5) caps a periodic penalty payment at "5 % of the average daily income or worldwide annual turnover in the preceding financial year per day": the base is not one figure (F-75c-5-base).
6. Art 75c(8) gives a five-year limitation period that runs from the day of the infringement, or from the day it ceases for a continuing or repeated one; the text does not say whether the first day counts (F-75c-8-last-day).
7. Art 74(7) second subparagraph says "should report", where its neighbours say "shall" (F-74-7-should).
8. Art 75b gives the AI Office the power to make commitments binding; the reopening power of the same Article is gated by "where" and lists three points, and its last paragraph says the AI Office "shall" reject commitments that cannot ensure compliance, a duty with no deadline (listed under Rules with no breach path).
9. Art 78(3) second subparagraph addresses "law enforcement, immigration or asylum authorities" that are providers of high-risk AI systems; the provider seat asks it only as that kind of provider (the section note says so).
10. The Omnibus replaces Art 77(1): as enacted it covered documentation "created or maintained under this Regulation" in relation to the use of high-risk AI systems referred to in Annex III and required the authority to inform the market surveillance authority of each request; as amended it reaches "any information or documentation created or maintained from the relevant market surveillance authority pursuant to this Regulation", in machine-readable format by electronic means, with no Annex III limiter and no duty to inform. Art 77(2), with its date of 2 November 2024, is untouched.
