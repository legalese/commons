# Formulas lost in conversion: `eu-ai-act-2024-1689.txt`

The text file is a mechanical html-stripping of the Official Journal XHTML, and it flattens superscripts.
This sidecar transcribes each one that a scoped provision needs, citing the authorised rendering.

## Article 51(2), line 1309

- **The text file reads:** `... measured in floating point operations is greater than 1025.`
- **The XHTML reads:** `greater than 10<span class="oj-super">25</span>` (found by `grep -o 'greater than 10<[^>]*>25<[^>]*>'` on `subjects/comparative/ai-content-labelling/source/eu-ai-act-2024-1689-oj.xhtml`, sha256 `8f0b6563…721a`, on 2026-10-24).
- **So the threshold is** ten to the power twenty-five: 10,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 floating point operations.

The encoding writes it as the exact literal `10_000_000_000_000_000_000_000_000`.
It does not write `EXPONENT 10 25`: on the main build 1.6.99 that evaluates to `10000000000000000905969664`, a floating-point result that is wrong by about 9 × 10^8, and a model trained with exactly 10^25 operations would then fall on the wrong side of "greater than".
