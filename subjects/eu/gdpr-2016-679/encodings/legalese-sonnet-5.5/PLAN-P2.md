## 3 The layer-1 modules (read these names; they are the names to take as inputs)

Every function below answers what the last column says.
`MAYBE BOOLEAN`: `JUST TRUE` and `JUST FALSE` are answers the Regulation gives in its own words and `NOTHING` is "not covered"; a caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`.
`MAYBE Duty outcome` (core): `the duty does not arise` (a gate failed), `the duty arises and is met`, `the duty arises and is not met`, and `NOTHING` for "not covered".
A DEONTIC cannot be compared in `#ASSERT`; it is traced (`#TRACE`).
Four functions answer a NUMBER of months: `Art 12(3) the months within which the information on action taken is to be provided` and `Art 14(3) the months within which the information is to be provided` (one or three, and the earliest limit), and the two functions with no input that answer 1, `Art 12(3) the months within which the data subject is to be informed of the extension` and `Art 12(4) the months within which the data subject is to be informed of the reasons for not taking action`.
The per-item functions (`Art 13(1) the controller owes the data subject the item of information`, and the siblings for Art 13(2), 14(1), 14(2)) answer `MAYBE BOOLEAN`: JUST TRUE the item is owed, JUST FALSE the text says it is not, NOTHING P1's answer is missing.
A MAYBE BOOLEAN used as an input of a function (a seam, or the answer of a point) is written as an argument with brackets: `(JUST TRUE)`, `NOTHING`.
A date is `Date day month year`; a month is counted with `add months`, which keeps the day of the month and clamps at the end of a short month (31 January plus one month is 28 February).

### 3.1 What the regulative rules count

A regulative rule counts its `WITHIN` in MONTHS where the text says "one month" (`Art 12(3)`, `Art 12(4)`, `Art 14(3)`): the clock starts at receipt of the request or at obtaining the data.
Where the text says "at the time when", `WITHIN 0` is the instant itself (`Art 13(1)`, `Art 13(2)`); where it says "prior to" or "before", the limit is an instant given as an input (`Art 13(3)`, `Art 14(4)`, `Art 18(3)`).
The deadline is inclusive: an act at the limit is timely, and `(WAITUNTIL n)` one instant after the limit makes a breach.
Where the text says "without undue delay" and gives no number (`Art 16`, `Art 17(1)`), no `WITHIN` is written and the rule cannot be breached (NOTES, Rules with no breach path).

## 5 Traps on the stable binary that I met

Loud means the binary stops with an error; silent means it answers wrongly with no error.

- **Silent.** A one-line `NOT a OR b` reads as `NOT (a OR b)`; every `NOT` in P2's files is bracketed (PLAN.md section 5, re-measured by K2 on 1.6.112).
- **Loud.** Several inputs separated by commas on one `GIVEN` line (`GIVEN a IS A LIST OF BOOLEAN, b IS A LIST OF BOOLEAN`) is a parse error (`unexpected IS`); P2 writes one `GIVEN` and continues on the next lines, indented.
- **Loud.** A MAYBE BOOLEAN argument written `JUST TRUE FALSE` gives "expects 2 arguments, but you are applying it to 3 arguments here"; write `(JUST TRUE) FALSE`.
- **Silent.** A `#TRACE` result is not an assertion and `check.sh` does not count it; P2 reads all 110 traces with `notes/trace-view.py`, and four mutations (a changed `WITHIN`, a flipped gate of the new `MUST` rules of Art 18(1) and Art 20(2)) were caught by a changed trace result and by no assertion (`notes/mutate.py`).
- **Silent, environment.** `l4 run` prints `Warning | [Import Resolution] Multiple differing copies of module prelude (and daydate)` on this machine; `check.sh` does not count it.
- **Silent.** `Art 12(3)` counts a month with `add months`: 31 January plus one month is 28 February, so a reply on 1 March is late, and a reply on 28 February is timely.
  The other reading (30 days) is a marginal reading, listed in NOTES.
- **Silent.** `Art 23(1)` is not an input of any right of Articles 12 to 22: a law that restricts a right does not change that right's answer; a caller who has such a law asks `Art 23(1) ...` and treats the right as not covered (NOTES, Findings).

## 6 The seat layer

Seats of P2: a data subject `[subject]` (95 questions, required) and a controller `[controller]` (95 questions), in the form of PLAN.md section 6.
A processor has no P2 question; there is NEVER a seat for a supervisory authority or a lawmaker (Article 23 is asked from the person's seat: may a law restrict this right; does the law contain what paragraph 2 requires).
The role facts: the data subject's seat fixes `the person is the data subject` TRUE and asks `the body is the controller`; the controller's seat fixes `the body is the controller` TRUE and asks `the person is the data subject`.
Seat helper types: `P2 subject answer`, `P2 controller answer` (yes, no, not sure) with `P2 subject answer as a yes or no` and `P2 controller answer as a yes or no`.
P1's three-valued answer `Art 11(2) Articles 15 to 20 are disapplied` is NOT asked as a yes, no or not sure anywhere in the seats (finding F-12 in NOTES): every question whose function takes it (15 questions per seat: the Art 12(2) bar; the 13 questions of Art 15(1) three, 15(2), 15(3) the copy, 16 two, 17(1), 18(1), 19 first sentence, 20(1) two and 20(2); and the Art 19 second sentence) asks two plain BOOLEANs, `the request concerns a case referred to in Article 11(2): the controller is not in a position to identify the data subject` and `the data subject has provided information enabling the controller to identify him or her`, in the place of the old input, and builds the MAYBE with the seat helper `P2 subject Art 11(2) answer from the facts` (`P2 controller ...`): not an Article 11(2) case NOTHING, an Article 11(2) case with identifying information provided JUST FALSE, an Article 11(2) case otherwise JUST TRUE.
A later part's seat that asks a three-valued answer of P1 for its own function must take care that "no" and "not sure" cannot be read as the middle meaning.
The per-item questions (Art 13(1), 13(2), 14(1), 14(2)) take a choice naming the item (`the item of information the data subject asks about in Article 13(1)`, and `the item of further information the data subject asks about in Article 13(2)`, `... in Article 14(1)`, `... in Article 14(2)`; the Art 15(1) question keeps `the item of information the data subject asks about`, so that two questions that share a name share a type) and the conditional facts, and answer whether the item is owed; the two months questions (Art 12(3) notice, Art 12(4) reasons) have no input.
A list of items is asked as one yes or no for each item (`the information provided includes the identity and the contact details of the controller`); "the data subject already has the information" is one yes or no for the whole list.
Shared inputs that P2 asks, with the same name and type wherever P2 asks them: see the `GIVEN` lines of `P2-seat-subject.l4`; the ones other parts' seats are likely to ask are the two Article 11(2) facts above (BOOLEAN each), `the processing is based on point (a) of Article 6(1) or point (a) of Article 9(2)` (BOOLEAN) and `the processing is in accordance with Article 89(1)` (BOOLEAN).
