## Copyright

**None in the text.** The Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 8293), read on 2026-10-19 from
https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/4371 :

- s.175: "no protection shall extend ... to ... any official text of a legislative, administrative or legal nature, as well as any official translation thereof".
- s.176.1: "No copyright shall subsist in any work of the Government of the Philippines." The same subsection requires prior approval of the creating agency "for exploitation of such work for profit", but then provides: "No prior approval or conditions shall be required for the use for any purpose of statutes, rules and regulations ...".

So the Acts deposited here may be reproduced for any purpose, commercial included. That rests on the statute, not on a licence from the host.
