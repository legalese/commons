## Conventions of this file

- One row per scenario: `id | provision (src:N) | facts | expected | status`.
- Every discretion or opinion the Law leaves to someone ("satisfied", "reasonable degree", "proportionate", "analogous", "reasonable excuse", "promptly", "wholly or mainly") is stated as an ASSUMED FACT in the row, so it can be supplied as an input without my deciding anything new later.
- "certain" means the text gives one answer. Ambiguous rows are in the last section with both readings; they become `ind-b-tests-ambiguous.l4`.
- Facts decided by other enactments (whether a body is a "trust", a "company", "bankrupt", "spent") are inputs.
- **Counting convention (my reading, taken):** a period of N years "for"/"after" an event on day D excludes the day of the event, so its last day is the anniversary D + N years (convention X). The other reading (day of event counted; last day is the day before the anniversary) is convention Y. Month-end: D + N years from 29 February lands on 28 February (clamp) in my reading; rolling to 1 March is the other reading. Both are written for each period.
