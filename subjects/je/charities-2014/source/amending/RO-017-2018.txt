Charities (Jersey) Law 2014 (Appointed Day) Act 2018                        Article 1




    CHARITIES (JERSEY) LAW 2014 (APPOINTED
                 DAY) ACT 2018
      Made                                                    6th March 2018
      Coming into force                                       6th March 2018

THE STATES, in pursuance of Article 44 of the Charities (Jersey) Law 20141,
have made the following Act –



1     Commencement of provisions relating to registration, governors of
      charities, taxation and related matters
      The following provisions of the Charities (Jersey) Law 20142 come into force on
      1st May 2018 –
      (a)   Part 4;
      (b)   Part 5;
      (c)   Part 7;
      (d)   Part 8 (other than Article 32);
      (e)   Article 37.


2     Commencement of remaining provisions, relating to taxation and to use of
      expressions “charity” and “charitable” and related expressions
      The remaining provisions of the Charities (Jersey) Law 20143, not brought into
      force by Article 44(2) of that Law or Article 1 of this Act, come into force on
      1st January 2019.


3     Citation
      This Act may be cited as the Charities (Jersey) Law 2014 (Appointed Day)
      Act 2018.

                                                                       L.-M. HART
                                                        Deputy Greffier of the States
