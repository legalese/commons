      STATES OF JERSEY (TRANSFER OF FINANCIAL
    SERVICES FUNCTIONS – EXTERNAL RELATIONS TO
             CHIEF MINISTER) ORDER 2023
              Made                                                                          5th April 2023
              Coming into force                                                            12th April 2023

THE CHIEF MINISTER makes this Order under Article 29A of the States of Jersey
Law 2005 –



1       Minister for External Relations and Financial Services renamed as Minister for
        External Relations
        (1)      The Minister for External Relations and Financial Services is renamed as the
                 Minister for External Relations.
        (2)      Accordingly, in Article 1 of the States of Jersey (Ministerial Offices) Order 2022 for
                 “Minister for External Relations and Financial Services” there is substituted
                 “Minister for External Relations”.
        (3)      Schedule 1 makes further amendments that are consequential on, or incidental or
                 supplementary to, that renaming.


2       Amendments to transfer financial services functions to Chief Minister
        Schedule 2 makes amendments to transfer financial services functions to the Chief
        Minister.


3       Citation and commencement
        This Order may be cited as the States of Jersey (Transfer of Financial Services Functions
        – External Relations to Chief Minister) Order 2023 and comes into force 7 days after it is
        made.

                          DEPUTY K.L. MOORE OF ST. MARY, ST. OUEN AND ST. PETER
                                                                                                     Chief Minister

2       States of Jersey Law 2005 and States of Jersey (Minister for External Relations
        and Financial Services) (Jersey) Regulations 2013 amended
        In relation to the renaming of the Minister for External Relations and Financial Services as
        the Minister for External Relations –
                (a)     in Article 18(3)(b) of the States of Jersey Law 2005 for “conducting external
                        relations in accordance” there is substituted “conducting external relations,
                        concurrently with the Minister for External Relations, in accordance”;
                (b)     the States of Jersey (Minister for External Relations and Financial Services)
                        (Jersey) Regulations 2013 are repealed; and
                (c)     Article 3 of the States of Jersey (Ministerial Offices) Order 2022 is deleted.

1       Transfer of functions from Minister for External Relations to Chief Minister
