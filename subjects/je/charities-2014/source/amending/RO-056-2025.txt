Charities (Entities) Law (Jersey) Amendment Order 2025                                         Article 1




    CHARITIES (ENTITIES) LAW (JERSEY) AMENDMENT
                     ORDER 2025

           Made                                                              9 October 2025
           Coming into force                                                16 October 2025

THE MINISTER FOR EXTERNAL RELATIONS makes this Order under
Article 2(2) of the Charities (Jersey) Law 2014 –



1      Charities (Jersey) Law 2014 amended
       In Article 2 of the Charities (Jersey) Law 2014, after paragraph (1)(b) there is inserted –
                      (ba) a body corporate that –
                             (i)     is an NPO, as defined by the Non-Profit Organizations (Jersey)
                                     Law 2008;
                             (ii)    is established under the law of Jersey; and
                             (iii)   does not fall within any of sub-paragraphs (c) to (h);


2      Citation and commencement
       This Order may be cited as the Charities (Entities) Law (Jersey) Amendment Order 2025
       and comes into force 7 days after it is made.


                           DEPUTY I.J. GORST OF ST. MARY, ST. OUEN AND ST. PETER
                                                                       Minister for External Relations
