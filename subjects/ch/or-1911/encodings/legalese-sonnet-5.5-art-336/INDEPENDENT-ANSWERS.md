# ANSWERS: Art. 336, 336a, 336b OR (written from the German text alone, before any .l4 file was opened)

Notation.
JT = JUST TRUE, JF = JUST FALSE, N = NOTHING ("not covered / the text is silent").
"Overall" = is the termination abusive at all; "ground" = does that single ground of Art. 336 make it abusive.
Overall answer when no ground holds: N (my reading, see scenario 20; the other reading is JF).
Money in CHF; "monthly wage" is the employee's monthly Lohn (the text does not define Lohn, so it is an input).
Counting the 180 days of Art. 336b(2): the day of the end of employment is day 0, the day after is day 1, so day 180 is inside the period and day 181 is outside (the text says "nach Beendigung", which I read as starting the day after).
Counting the notice period: "längstens bis zum Ende der Kündigungsfrist" includes the last day of the notice period.
"Guide:" gives the sentence or ruling I rely on where the expected value rests on the convention.
"Case-by-case" marks one of the six questions the convention leaves to the encoder (an encoding that differs is AMBIGUOUS, not ENCODER).

## A. Art. 336(1): grounds open to either party

1. 336(1)(a). An employer dismisses an employee because of the employee's religion, a characteristic that belongs to the person; it is not connected with the employment relationship and does not impair cooperation. Abusive? Overall: JT.
2. 336(1)(a). Same, but the characteristic is connected with the employment relationship ("in einem Zusammenhang mit dem Arbeitsverhältnis"). Ground (a) applies? Ground: JF; overall: N. Case-by-case: "es sei denn" once met (my lean is JF for the ground).
3. 336(1)(a). Same as 1, but the characteristic materially impairs cooperation in the business ("beeinträchtige wesentlich die Zusammenarbeit im Betrieb"). Ground (a): JF; overall N. Case-by-case: "es sei denn" once met.
4. 336(1)(b). An employer dismisses an employee because the employee exercises a constitutional right (freedom of assembly at a lawful demonstration); the exercise breaks no duty from the employment relationship and does not impair cooperation. Overall: JT.
5. 336(1)(b). Same, but the exercise of the right violates a duty from the employment relationship. Ground (b): JF; overall N. Case-by-case: "es sei denn" once met.
6. 336(1)(b). Same as 4, but the exercise materially impairs cooperation in the business. Ground (b): JF; overall N. Case-by-case: "es sei denn" once met.
7. 336(1)(b). The EMPLOYEE resigns because the employer exercises a constitutional right (no duty breached, no impairment). Abusive? JT. The chapeau says "eine Partei ... die andere Partei", so the employee's termination is covered.
8. 336(1)(c). An employer dismisses an employee exclusively to prevent a long-service entitlement from arising ("ausschliesslich um die Entstehung von Ansprüchen ... zu vereiteln"). Overall: JT.
9. 336(1)(c). Same, but the employer also had a genuine economic reason, so the dismissal is not exclusively to thwart the claim. Ground (c): JF; overall N. Guide: "ausschliesslich" is the text's own "only"; overall N because the list is not closed.
10. 336(1)(c). The EMPLOYEE resigns exclusively to prevent the employer's claim to a repayment from arising. Abusive? JT (either party).
11. 336(1)(d). An employer dismisses an employee because the employee in good faith claimed unpaid overtime. Overall: JT.
12. 336(1)(d). The employee claimed the overtime knowing it was not owed (not "nach Treu und Glauben"). Ground (d): JF; overall N. Guide: condition introduced with "wenn ... weil"; overall N because the list is not closed.
13. 336(1)(d). The EMPLOYEE resigns because the employer in good faith asserted a claim against the employee under the employment relationship. Abusive? JT.
14. 336(1)(e). An employer dismisses an employee because the employee performs compulsory Swiss military service. Overall: JT.
15. 336(1)(e). Because the employee performs Swiss civil service (Zivildienst). Overall: JT.
16. 336(1)(e). Because the employee performs military service in a foreign army. Abusive under (e)? N. Guide: scope limit "schweizerischen" with no "only" (periods ruling (c)).
17. 336(1)(e). Because the employee performs Swiss military service that is voluntary, not compulsory. Abusive under (e)? N. Guide: scope limit "obligatorischen" with no "only".
18. 336(1)(e). Because the employee fulfils a statutory duty not voluntarily assumed (a summons to serve as a lay judge). Overall: JT.
19. 336(1)(e). Because the employee fulfils a statutory duty the employee took on voluntarily (a municipal council seat stood for). Abusive under (e)? N. Guide: scope limit "nicht freiwillig" with no "only".
20. 336(1) chapeau. An employer dismisses an employee for poor performance; no listed ground applies. Abusive? N. Guide: list not closed with "only". The other reading is JF because the chapeau is introduced with "wenn" (an "if"). AMBIGUOUS between the two.

## B. Art. 336(2): grounds for the employer's termination only

21. 336(2)(a). An employer dismisses an employee because the employee belongs to an employee association (union). Overall: JT.
22. 336(2)(a). Because the employee does not belong to one. Overall: JT ("angehört oder nicht angehört").
23. 336(2)(a). Because the employee lawfully exercises union activity (distributes lawful leaflets). Overall: JT.
24. 336(2)(a). Because the employee exercises union activity unlawfully. Ground (a): JF; overall N. Guide: condition "rechtmässig" inside the "wenn" ground; overall N, list not closed.
25. 336(2) chapeau. The EMPLOYEE, an elected works-council member, resigns. Is the resignation abusive under 336(2)(b)? N. Guide: scope limit "durch den Arbeitgeber" with no "only" (periods ruling (c)).
26. 336(2)(b). An employee is an elected employee representative on the in-house works council; the employer dismisses while the mandate runs and cannot prove a justified reason. Overall: JT.
27. 336(2)(b). Same, but the employee is an elected representative on the board of an institution affiliated with the enterprise (staff pension foundation). Overall: JT ("in einer dem Unternehmen angeschlossenen Einrichtung").
28. 336(2)(b). Elected representative, but the employer proves a justified reason (theft). Ground (b): JF; overall N. Guide: "und der Arbeitgeber nicht beweisen kann" is a condition inside an "if" ground; overall N, list not closed.
29. 336(2)(b). The employee is a representative appointed by management, not elected. Ground (b)? N. Guide: scope limit "gewählter" with no "only".
30. 336(2)(b). The dismissal is pronounced after the employee's mandate has expired at the end of its term (not by a transfer). Ground (b): JF; overall N. Guide: "während" is a "while" condition, listed in the convention as a plain variant of "if".
31. 336(2)(c). A mass dismissal; a works council exists; neither it nor the employees were consulted. Overall: JT.
32. 336(2)(c). A mass dismissal; a works council exists and was consulted. Ground (c): JF; overall N. Guide: "ohne dass" condition unmet.
33. 336(2)(c). A mass dismissal; no representation exists; the employees were consulted. Ground (c): JF; overall N.
34. 336(2)(c). A mass dismissal; no representation exists; nobody was consulted. Overall: JT.
35. 336(2)(c). A mass dismissal; a works council exists; only the employees were consulted directly, not the council. Overall: JT (employees count only "falls es keine solche gibt").
36. 336(2)(c). A single dismissal (not a mass dismissal), nobody consulted. Ground (c): JF; overall N. Guide: "im Rahmen einer Massenentlassung" is a condition inside the ground.
37. 336(2)(c). The employer dismisses 5 employees of 500. Is this a mass dismissal under the text? N. The text points outside its sources (Art. 335d is not deposited).
38. 336(2)(c) with 335f(2) (the provision (2)(c) points to). A mass dismissal; the works council was informed but never given any possibility to submit proposals. Abusive under (2)(c)? JT (Art. 335f(2): "zumindest die Möglichkeit, Vorschläge zu unterbreiten", so mere information is not consultation).

## C. Art. 336(3): the representative whose mandate ended by a transfer

39. 336(3). An elected representative's mandate ended on 2026-03-31 because the business was transferred (Art. 333); it would have lasted to 2026-12-31. The transferee dismisses on 2026-06-15 and cannot prove a justified reason. Overall: JT.
40. 336(3). Same, dismissal on 2026-12-31 (the last day the mandate would have lasted). Overall: JT.
41. 336(3). Same, dismissal on 2027-01-01 (the day after). Protection under 336(3): JF; overall N. Guide: "so lange ... als" is an "as long as" condition.
42. 336(3). The mandate ended because the representative resigned from it (no transfer); the employer dismisses before the date the mandate would have ended. Protection under 336(3)? N. Guide: scope "dessen Mandat infolge Übergangs ... endet" with no "only".
43. 336(3). Transfer-ended mandate, dismissal inside the protected time, the employer proves a justified reason. Ground (b) as extended by (3): JF; overall N.

## D. Art. 336a(1): the duty to pay

44. 336a(1). An employer abusively dismisses an employee (336(2)(a)). Duty to pay compensation: JT; debtor the employer, creditor the employee.
45. 336a(1). An employee abusively resigns (336(1)(c)). Duty to pay compensation: JT; debtor the employee, creditor the employer.
46. 336a(1). A dismissal for poor performance (overall N). Duty to pay compensation under 336a(1)? N (follows from scenario 20). Case-by-case: a duty whose "if" is unmet.

## E. Art. 336a(2): the cap and the reservation

47. 336a(2). Monthly wage 5,000. The most the compensation may be: 30,000.
48. 336a(2). Monthly wage 5,000, judge awards 30,000. Permitted? JT ("nicht übersteigen": exactly the cap is within it).
49. 336a(2). Monthly wage 5,000, judge awards 30,001. Permitted? JF. Guide: flat negative "darf ... nicht übersteigen".
50. 336a(2). Does the text fix a minimum compensation? N (silent).
51. 336a(2) second sentence. Is a damages claim on another legal basis limited by the six-month cap? JF ("Schadenersatzansprüche aus einem anderen Rechtstitel sind vorbehalten").
52. 336a(2). The EMPLOYEE abusively resigned; the employer is the creditor; the employee's monthly wage is 4,000. The most the compensation may be: 24,000 (the cap is "der Lohn des Arbeitnehmers").
53. 336a(2). May the parties fix the amount by agreement, without the judge? N (the text only says the judge fixes it).

## F. Art. 336a(3): the two-month cap

54. 336a(3). Dismissal abusive under 336(2)(c) only; monthly wage 5,000. The most: 10,000.
55. 336a(3). Same, judge awards 10,000. Permitted? JT.
56. 336a(3). Same, judge awards 10,001. Permitted? JF.
57. 336a(2) and (3). Dismissal abusive under 336(2)(a), not (c); monthly wage 5,000. The most: 30,000 (six months, not two).
58. 336a(3). Dismissal abusive under both 336(2)(c) and 336(1)(a); monthly wage 5,000. The most: 10,000 (literal reading: "Ist die Kündigung nach ... Buchstabe c missbräuchlich, so darf ... nicht mehr als ... zwei Monate"). AMBIGUOUS: the other reading is 30,000, because another ground is also met.
59. 336a(3). Dismissal abusive under 336(2)(c) only; monthly wage 4,500. The most: 9,000.

## G. Art. 336b(1): the objection

60. 336b(1). Notice period ends 2026-06-30; the written objection reaches the dismissing party on 2026-06-30. Valid? JT (last day inside "längstens bis zum Ende").
61. 336b(1). Same, objection on 2026-06-29. Valid? JT.
62. 336b(1). Same, objection on 2026-07-01 (the day after). Valid? JF. Guide: period the text fixes in its own words (periods ruling (a)).
63. 336b(1). The objection is made orally on 2026-06-15. Valid? JF. Guide: flat "muss ... schriftlich"; case-by-case for a bare "must".
64. 336b(1). The written objection is sent to a cantonal labour office, not to the dismissing party. Valid? JF ("beim Kündigenden").
65. 336b(1). The objection is sent by e-mail. Does it satisfy "schriftlich"? N (the text does not say what counts as writing; the form rules are outside the sources).
66. 336b(1). The written objection is made on the day the notice was given, 2026-04-15, long before the end. Valid? JT (only an upper limit: "längstens").
67. 336b(1). The dismissed party never made any objection. May the party claim compensation under 336/336a? JF. Guide: "muss" as a precondition to claim; case-by-case for a bare "must".
68. 336b(1). Is a damages claim on another legal basis (not Art. 336/336a) barred for lack of an objection? N (336b(1) speaks only of claims "gestützt auf Artikel 336 und 336a").

## H. Art. 336b(2): the claim and its forfeiture

69. 336b(2). A valid objection was made and the parties do not agree to continue the employment. May the dismissed party assert the compensation claim? JT.
70. 336b(2). A valid objection was made but the parties agree to continue the employment. May the dismissed party assert the claim? JF. Guide: "Ist ... und einigen sich ... nicht, so kann" is an "if" condition.
71. 336b(2). The objection was late (invalid) and the parties do not agree to continue. May the dismissed party assert the claim? JF.
72. 336b(2). The party who GAVE the notice tries to assert a compensation claim against the dismissed party under 336b(2). Allowed? N. Guide: a grant limited in its own wording ("die Partei, der gekündigt worden ist"), no "only".
73. 336b(2). Employment ended 2026-06-30; the action became pending on 2026-12-26 (day 179). Is the claim forfeited? JF.
74. 336b(2). Same, action pending on 2026-12-27 (day 180). Forfeited? JF.
75. 336b(2). Same, action pending on 2026-12-28 (day 181). Forfeited? JT. Guide: period ruling (a).
76. 336b(2). Employment ended 2026-06-30; no action was ever filed; asked on 2027-02-01. Forfeited? JT.
77. 336b(2). Employment ended 2026-06-30; no action filed yet; asked on 2026-09-01 (day 63). Forfeited? JF (the 180 days have not run out).
78. 336b(2). Settlement talks run from day 30 to day 60. Does that extend the 180 days? N (the text says nothing about suspension).

## J. Seat: the employee (facts in the employee's own voice)

81. "My employer dismissed me the week after I joined the union." Was the dismissal abusive? JT. 336(2)(a).
82. "My employer dismissed me because I refused to join the union." Abusive? JT. 336(2)(a) ("oder nicht angehört").
83. "I am an elected member of the staff committee. My employer dismissed me and has not proved any reason." Abusive? JT. 336(2)(b).
84. "I am an elected member of the staff committee. My employer dismissed me and proved that I had been stealing." Abusive under the staff-committee ground? JF (ground); overall N. 336(2)(b).
85. "The employer dismissed 40 of us; we have a works council; neither we nor the council were consulted." Abusive? JT. 336(2)(c).
86. "My monthly wage is 6,000. I was dismissed for being a union member. What is the most I can be awarded?" 36,000. 336a(2).
87. "Same wage, but my dismissal is abusive only because of the unconsulted mass dismissal." 12,000. 336a(3).
88. "My notice period ends on 2026-08-31 and I wrote to my employer on 2026-08-31 objecting to the dismissal." Is my objection valid? JT. 336b(1).
89. "Same, but I wrote on 2026-09-01." Valid? JF. 336b(1).
90. "I told my boss out loud that I object." Valid? JF. 336b(1) "schriftlich".
91. "I objected in writing in time, and then my employer and I agreed that I would stay." May I still claim compensation? JF. 336b(2).
92. "My employment ended on 2026-08-31 and I filed my claim in court on 2027-02-27 (day 180)." Is my claim forfeited? JF. 336b(2).
93. "Same, but I filed on 2027-02-28 (day 181)." Forfeited? JT. 336b(2).
94. "I resigned exclusively to dodge a repayment my employer's contract would have required." Was my resignation abusive? JT, and I owe the employer compensation: JT. 336(1)(c), 336a(1).
95. "I was dismissed because I was doing my compulsory Swiss civil service." Abusive? JT. 336(1)(e).
96. "I was dismissed because I claimed my unpaid overtime, which I believed was owed." Abusive? JT. 336(1)(d).
97. "I was dismissed because my work was poor." Abusive? N. 336(1) chapeau.

## K. Seat: the employer (facts in the employer's own voice)

98. "I dismissed an employee because of her religion; it has nothing to do with the job." Is my dismissal abusive? JT. 336(1)(a). Do I owe her compensation? JT. 336a(1).
99. "I dismissed an employee because of a health condition that stops him doing the job." Abusive under the personality-characteristic ground? JF (ground); overall N. 336(1)(a), case-by-case "es sei denn".
100. "I dismissed an employee for exercising her freedom of assembly during working hours, in breach of her duty to be at her post." Abusive under the constitutional-right ground? JF (ground); overall N. 336(1)(b), case-by-case.
101. "I am carrying out a mass dismissal; there is a works council and I consulted it." Abusive under 336(2)(c)? JF (ground); overall N.
102. "Mass dismissal; there is a works council; I consulted only the staff directly." Abusive? JT. 336(2)(c).
103. "Mass dismissal; there is no works council; I consulted all the employees." Abusive under 336(2)(c)? JF (ground); overall N.
104. "I dismissed an elected staff representative and I can prove a justified reason." Abusive under 336(2)(b)? JF (ground); overall N.
105. "I dismissed an elected staff representative and I cannot prove a justified reason." Abusive? JT. 336(2)(b).
106. "The employee's monthly wage is 5,000. What is the most I might have to pay for an ordinary abusive dismissal?" 30,000. 336a(2). For an abusive dismissal only through the unconsulted mass dismissal: 10,000. 336a(3).
107. "The notice period ended on 2026-06-30 and the objection arrived on 2026-07-01." Is the objection valid? JF. 336b(1).
108. "My employee's employment ended on 2026-06-30 and he sued on 2026-12-28 (day 181)." Is his claim forfeited? JT. 336b(2).
109. "Same, but he sued on 2026-12-27 (day 180)." Forfeited? JF. 336b(2).
110. "My employee resigned exclusively to stop my claim from arising; I objected in writing on the last day of notice and we did not agree to continue. His monthly wage is 4,000." May I assert the claim? JT (336b(2)); the most: 24,000 (336a(2)).
111. "I dismissed an employee for poor performance. Do I owe compensation?" N. 336a(1) with 336(1) chapeau.
112. "The employee gave me a written objection in time, and we agreed to continue the employment. Can he still claim compensation?" JF. 336b(2).
113. "My employee, who is a union member, resigned. Is his resignation abusive under the union ground?" N. 336(2) covers only the employer's termination.
