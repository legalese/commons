Answer vocabulary: `YES` (the text says yes: JUST TRUE), `NO` (the text itself says no: JUST FALSE), `NOT COVERED` (the text is silent: NOTHING), or a number.
A question put as "does the bar / exclusion / duty apply" is answered YES when it applies; a question put as "is the appeal admissible / is the person entitled" is answered YES when the text grants it.

Policy I apply to the family convention, so every call below can be checked against one rule:

- P1. A condition introduced by `wenn`, `sofern`, `soweit`, `nur ... wenn`, `ausser`, `ausser wenn`, `sofern nicht` ("if", "only if", "provided that", "unless", "except"): condition unmet gives NO.
- P2. A restrictive relative clause or a bare scope limit with no "if/only" word (for example "Entscheide, die im Rahmen des Widerspruchsverfahrens getroffen worden sind", "Subventionen, auf die kein Anspruch besteht", "Gegen Entscheide nach Artikel 72 Absatz 2"): outside the scope the answer is NOT COVERED (ruling (c)).
- P3. A grant ("ist zulässig gegen ...", "beurteilt ...") over a list not closed with "only", or an open list ("insbesondere"): a case not on the list is NOT COVERED.
- P4. A flat negative ("ist unzulässig", "ist nicht anwendbar", "erstreckt sich nicht auf"): the case it names gives NO (for admissibility) or YES (for "does the bar apply").
- P5. An "unless/except" once met, a bare duty not done, "(other than X)": case by case (the six questions); I give my reading and expect AMBIGUOUS if the encoding differs.
- P6. A standing clause of the form "ist berechtigt, wer: a ... und b ...": I read it as the text's own "if": a person failing a limb gets NO.

Time limits: this chapter contains NO period, deadline or time limit (the filing period is Art. 100, outside the text). So there is nothing to count; scenarios 119 and 120 test that silence. The only numbers are the money thresholds (Art. 74: 15 000 / 30 000; Art. 75 Abs. 2 lit. c: 100 000; Art. 85: 30 000 / 15 000), each tested on both sides.

## Seat scenarios: a person affected by a decision (a party to civil or criminal proceedings), in their own words

S1. "I am a tenant. The cantonal court found against me on a rent claim of CHF 14 000. I took part in the case. Can I take it to the Federal Supreme Court?" Art. 74 Abs. 1 lit. a (15 000), Art. 76. Answer: NO (below the limit, no Abs. 2 ground).
S2. "Same, but the claim was CHF 15 000." YES.
S3. "Same CHF 14 000 rent claim, but my case raises a legal question of fundamental importance." YES (Art. 74 Abs. 2 lit. a).
S4. "I was sued by a supplier for CHF 25 000 for goods delivered; I lost in the cantonal court of appeal. I took part." Art. 74 Abs. 1 lit. b. Answer: NO (30 000 for other cases).
S5. "I was a party to an arbitration in Zurich between two Swiss companies over CHF 8 000; I want to challenge the award." Art. 77 Abs. 1 lit. b. Answer: YES if the Art. 389 to 395 ZPO conditions are met (regardless of value).
S6. "I did not attend the hearing before the cantonal court although I was invited; now I want to appeal a CHF 80 000 loss." Art. 76 Abs. 1 lit. a. Answer: NO.
S7. "The court never told me about the proceeding, so I could not take part; I am directly hit by the order; I want it reversed." Art. 76 Abs. 1. Answer: YES.
S8. "I am the accused in a criminal case; I took part; I was convicted by the cantonal appeal court." Art. 80 Abs. 1, Art. 81 Abs. 1 lit. b Ziff. 1. Answer: YES.
S9. "I am the victim and private claimant; the court acquitted the accused and so also rejected my civil claim; I took part." Art. 81 Abs. 1 lit. b Ziff. 5. Answer: YES.
S10. "I am the private claimant; the appeal court only changed the length of the prison sentence, my damages were untouched." Art. 81 Abs. 1 lit. b Ziff. 5. Answer: NO via Ziff. 5.
S11. "I filed a criminal complaint (Strafantrag); the court held that I filed it too late." Art. 81 Abs. 1 lit. b Ziff. 6. Answer: YES.
S12. "I am the accused and I am in pre-trial detention; the Beschwerdekammer of the Federal Criminal Court upheld my detention order (a coercive measure)." Art. 79, Art. 80 Abs. 1. Answer: YES (admissible).
S13. "The Beschwerdekammer refused to remove the prosecutor from my case (not a coercive measure)." Art. 79. Answer: NO.
S14. "I am a defendant; my civil damages claim was decided in a separate civil case, not with the criminal one." Art. 78 Abs. 2 lit. a. Answer: NO under the criminal appeal.
S15. "A court refused to let me out under a measure being executed (Vollzug von Massnahmen); I want to appeal." Art. 78 Abs. 2 lit. b. Answer: YES (criminal appeal covers it).
S16. "I am a private person; the other side's trademark opposition decision went against me." Art. 73. Answer: NO.
S17. "I am a foundation-supervision party: my family foundation's supervisor decided against me." Art. 72 Abs. 2 lit. b Ziff. 4. Answer: YES (civil appeal).
S18. "I run an occupational pension fund; the supervisory authority decided against me." Art. 72 Abs. 2 lit. b Ziff. 4. Answer: NO under the civil appeal (other reading: NOT COVERED).
