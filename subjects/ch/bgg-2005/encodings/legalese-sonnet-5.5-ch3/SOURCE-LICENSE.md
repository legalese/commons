Every `-- src:` quotation was copied by script from the deposited text file (`ch-bgg-sr173110-ch3-art-72-89-20260401.txt`, sha256 `918277c2175f13e88e2c98b4bbe8ecb4b9c44598538bb54f2ab8b9f13ad3db13`), and `srcrefs.py` and `quotecheck.py` check them against it.

The deposit does not say whether the consolidated version or only the publication of each amending act in the Official Compilation (AS) is authoritative.
