# Source licence: Swiss Federal Supreme Court Act (BGG), chapter 4 (Art. 90 to 112)

This row quotes the German text of chapter 4 of the Federal Supreme Court Act (Bundesgerichtsgesetz, BGG, SR 173.110) in its `-- src:` comment lines and in the quotations of `NOTES.md`.
The terms that quoted text carries are recorded once for the whole subject, in the subject-level licence note written when the text was deposited (`inputs/STUB-SOURCE-LICENSE.md` of this run; in canon, the `SOURCE-LICENSE.md` of the subject).
This file points at that note and does not repeat it.

What this row adds is a statement of what the quoted text is:

- It is a plain-text rendering, made by a converter script, of the consolidated German version of chapter 4 that Fedlex shows as applicable from 2026-04-01 (`ch-bgg-sr173110-ch4-art-90-112-20260401.txt`; its sha256 is in `NOTES.md` and in `inputs/SOURCES.md`).
- It is not the official text, and nothing in this row is verified against the official text.
- The deposit does not say whether the consolidated version or only the publication of each amending act in the Official Compilation of Federal Legislation is authoritative.
- The licence of this encoding is Apache-2.0 (`encoding.json`); that is not the licence of the quoted text.
