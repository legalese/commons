# ANSWERS (written from the German text alone, before opening any .l4 file)

Answer vocabulary: YES / NO = the text says so (`JUST TRUE` / `JUST FALSE`); NOTHING = the text is silent or the case is outside a grant or scope (`NOTHING`); a number or a date where the question asks for one.
"Guide:" names the convention sentence or ruling I rely on.
Dates are 2026 unless stated. A period counts from the day after the trigger: "30 days after notification on D" ends on D+30 (the day D+30 is in time, D+31 is late), the day before is D+29.
"Days the period does not run" are an input (the task says so), so I add them to the period.

## A. Art 90 to 91: final and partial decisions

1. Art 90. A decision concludes the proceedings (a final decision). May it be appealed under Art 90? YES. ("Die Beschwerde ist zulässig gegen Entscheide, die das Verfahren abschliessen.")
2. Art 90. An interim decision that does not conclude the proceedings, asked only of Art 90. NOTHING. Guide: grant not closed with "only"; Art 90 is silent about other decisions.
3. Art 91(a). A decision treats only part of the requests, and those requests can be judged independently of the others. Appeal admissible? YES.
4. Art 91(a). A decision treats only part of the requests, and they cannot be judged independently of the others. Appeal admissible under 91(a)? NO. Guide: "wenn" condition that fails, periods ruling (b).
5. Art 91(b). A decision concludes the proceedings for only some of the co-litigants (Streitgenossen). Appeal admissible? YES.
6. Art 91(b)/90. A decision concludes the proceedings for all co-litigants. Appeal admissible (Art 90)? YES.

W2. The cantonal court told me its decision on 2026-05-04 without reasons. I asked for the full decision on 2026-05-20 and received it on 2026-05-27. My appeal deadline is 2026-06-26. Filing on 2026-06-26: YES; 2026-06-27: NO. (My request on 2026-05-20 was in time: YES.)
W3. The court said it has no jurisdiction in my case, in a separate decision. Can I appeal that now? YES. If I let it pass, can I raise it after the final decision? NO.
W4. The court ordered me to hand over my confidential customer list to the other side before trial; this cannot be undone. Can I appeal now? YES.
W5. The court ordered an expert opinion; it does not hurt me irreparably and a win would not end the case. Can I appeal that order now? NO. Can I challenge it later with my appeal against the final decision if it affects the content? YES.
W6. The cantonal court granted a divorce. If I appeal, is the divorce put on hold (suspensive effect)? YES.
W7. The cantonal court ordered me to pay CHF 50,000. If I appeal, do I need not pay meanwhile? NO (no suspensive effect).
W8. In my inheritance case (about money) the court applied the wrong foreign law. Can I complain? NO. In my child-custody case (not about money) the court applied the foreign law wrongly. Can I complain? YES.
W9. I want to call a new witness whom I could have called in the cantonal proceedings. Allowed? NO. I want to rely on a letter that only the cantonal court's reasoning made relevant. Allowed? YES.
W10. I want to add a claim for damages that I never raised before. Allowed? NO.
W11. The accident insurer refused my daily allowance (money). I say the court's facts are incomplete, though not clearly wrong. Can I complain? YES. My claim is for medical treatment (in kind) and the court's facts are merely wrong. NO.
W12. The court issued an injunction (vorsorgliche Massnahme). I complain it misapplied the Civil Code. Allowed? NO. I complain it violated my constitutional right to be heard. YES.
W13. The debt enforcement supervisory authority decided against me on 2026-06-01. Last day to file: 2026-06-11. Filing on 2026-06-12: NO.
W14. In a bill-of-exchange enforcement the supervisory authority decided on 2026-09-10. Last day: 2026-09-15. NO on 2026-09-16.
W15. I was convicted to 18 months of imprisonment, unconditional. Does my appeal stop the sentence? YES. A conditional sentence of 18 months? NO. The damages I must pay to the victim in the same judgment? NO.
W16. My sentence includes a closed therapeutic measure (deprivation of liberty). Does my appeal suspend it? YES.
W17. The cantonal court has not decided my criminal appeal for two years. Can I complain to the Federal Supreme Court and is there a time limit? YES (any time).
W18. I say the court got the facts wrong but not obviously so. Can I complain about the facts? NO. It got them obviously wrong and it is decisive. YES.
W19. The decision I got does not tell me how to appeal. Can the Federal Supreme Court send it back or set it aside? YES.
W20. I am the accused in an extradition case. I appeal the order for extradition detention; the detention causes me an irreparable disadvantage. Can I appeal this interim order? YES. A different interim order (hearing a witness) with an irreparable disadvantage? NO.
W21. Seam to chapter 3: I am a worker; my employment case is worth CHF 15,000; the cantonal high court was the last instance and I took part. May I appeal? YES. At CHF 14,999 and no other ground? NO. For a non-labour, non-tenancy dispute at CHF 29,999: NO; at CHF 30,000: YES.
W22. Seam to chapter 3: the Federal Supreme Court is considering not entering my CHF 20,000 sales dispute because it raises no legal question of fundamental importance; may a panel of three judges decide that? YES.
W23. Seam to chapter 3: my case is a CHF 15,000 employment dispute; may a panel of three judges decline to enter because no question of fundamental importance arises? NO.
W24. In my criminal case, the cantonal decision has no operative part (Dispositiv). Is it compliant? NO. It lacks a statement of the value in dispute. Compliant? YES (no value threshold in criminal matters).
