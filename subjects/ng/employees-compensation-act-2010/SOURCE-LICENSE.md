## Why the text may be deposited

Section 3 of the **Copyright Act, 2022** (Act No. 8 of 2023; Official Gazette No. 56, Lagos, 27 March 2023, Vol. 110) reads, in part: "The following shall not be eligible for copyright -- ... (b) official texts of a legislative or administrative nature as well as any official translations, except their compilations".
Checked 2026-10-19 in the gazette copy published by the Nigerian Copyright Commission at https://www.copyright.gov.ng/CopyrightAct/CopyrightAct2023FinalPublication1.pdf (robots.txt at https://www.copyright.gov.ng/robots.txt read first: Joomla defaults, `/CopyrightAct/` not disallowed).
The text of an Act is therefore free to copy. What may be protected is a *compilation* (for example the arrangement of a consolidated revision) and the host site's own material; this deposit carries only the words of the instrument, re-typed as plain text, with none of the host's pages, layout or commentary.
