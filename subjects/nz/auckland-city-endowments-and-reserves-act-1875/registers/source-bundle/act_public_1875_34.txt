Auckland City Endowments and Reserves Act 1875
1 Short Title
The Short Title of this Act shall be the Auckland City Endowments and Reserves Act 1875.
2 Land in Schedules vested in Corporation
All the land described in Schedule 1 are hereby vested for an estate in fee simple in The Mayor, Councillors, and Citizens of the City of Auckland, and their successors, as an endowment for the improvement and benefit of the said city.
3 Powers of Corporation in respect of lands in Schedule 1
The Mayor, Councillors, and Citizens, in respect of the lands described in Schedule 1, and any other lands for the time being held by them under the provisions of this Act, may from time to time exercise all or any of the powers following—
May raise money by mortgage or debenture, without power of sale, and subject to and with such powers covenants and stipulations as they may think fit.
May demise or lease for any term not exceeding 99 years, without fine or foregift, or let from year to year or otherwise, and with or without special conditions.
May lay out roads through the said lands.
May dedicate any part or parts of such lands for public or for recreation purposes.
Provided that no person shall be liable to see to the application of any money paid by him, or accountable for the mis-application or non-application thereof.

5 Nothing to affect prior lease, &c
Nothing in this Act contained shall prejudicially affect any lease, mortgage, or other encumbrance heretofore made and now subsisting in respect of the said lands or any of them, nor shall impair or prejudicially affect the provisions of an Act of the Provincial Council of Auckland intituled the City of Auckland Loan Empowering Act 1874, whereby the said Corporation is authorized to borrow the sum of $100,000, nor any thing whatever done or authorized to be done under the said Act or under the powers thereby conferred.
