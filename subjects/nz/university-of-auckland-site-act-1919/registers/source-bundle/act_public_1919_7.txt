University of Auckland Site Act 1919
1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the University of Auckland Site Act 1919.
2 Land vested in Council of the University of Auckland
The land described in the Schedule hereto is hereby vested in the Council of the University of Auckland in trust as a site for a University, subject to the provisions of this Act.
3 Council to erect university buildings
The said Council shall erect on the said land buildings suitable for a University college to the satisfaction of the Minister of Education.
No building shall be erected on the said land except according to plans and in the manner approved by the said Minister.
Unless within ten years after the passing of this Act the said Council has erected buildings as aforesaid, the Governor-General may, by Proclamation, resume possession of such land, and the land shall as from the date of that Proclamation revert to the Crown.
4 Council may take adjacent land for purposes of University
The said Council is hereby empowered to take, with the consent of the Minister of Education and in accordance with the provisions of the Public Works Act 1908, any lands adjacent to the land hereby vested in the Council, and for that purpose the said Council shall be deemed to have all the powers and authorities by that Act conferred upon a local authority with respect to the taking of lands for public works.
All lands acquired by the Council under this section shall be held by it in trust for the purposes of a University.
