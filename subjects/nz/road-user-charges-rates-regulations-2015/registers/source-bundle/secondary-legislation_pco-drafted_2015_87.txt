Road User Charges (Rates) Regulations 2015
Regulations
1 Title
These regulations are the Road User Charges (Rates) Regulations 2015.
2 Commencement
These regulations come into force on 1 July 2015.
3 Interpretation
In these regulations,—
Act means the Road User Charges Act 2012
additional weight means,—
for a single heavy RUC vehicle, the difference between the RUC weight for the vehicle and the permit weight for the vehicle:
for a combination vehicle, the difference between the maximum allowable mass for the combination vehicle as determined by section 4 of the VDAM Rule 2016 and the permit weight for the combination vehicle
single large-tyred axle has the same meaning as in Part 2 of the VDAM Rule 2016
twin-tyred axle has the same meaning as in Part 2 of the VDAM Rule 2016.
In these regulations, the following terms have the same meaning as in regulation 3(1) of the Road User Charges Regulations 2012:
all-terrain crane:
axle:
close:
combination vehicle:
leading trailer:
motor caravan:
overweight:
permit weight:
spaced:
specialist RUC vehicle:
towing vehicle:
type H vehicle:
VDAM Rule 2016:
vintage powered vehicle.
A reference in any of Schedules 1 to 8 to a type in respect of a RUC vehicle is a reference to the RUC vehicle type number for that vehicle.
Other terms or expressions used and not defined in these regulations but defined in the Act have, in these regulations, the same meaning as in the Act.
4 Rates of road user charges
The rates of road user charges for a distance licence for powered RUC vehicles (except type H vehicles, all-terrain cranes, and overweight specialist RUC vehicles) are the rates specified in Schedule 1.
The rates of road user charges for a distance licence for all-terrain cranes are the rates specified in Schedule 2.
The rates of road user charges for a distance licence for unpowered RUC vehicles are the rates specified in Schedule 3.
The rates of road user charges for a distance licence for type H vehicles are the rates specified in Schedule 4.
The rates of road user charges for a distance licence for overweight specialist RUC vehicles are the rates specified in Schedule 5.
The rates of road user charges for each 1,000 kg of additional weight carried by a powered RUC vehicle (except an all-terrain crane) operating under an additional licence are the rates specified in Schedule 6.
The rates of road user charges for each 1,000 kg of additional weight carried by an all-terrain crane operating under an additional licence are the rates specified in Schedule 7.
The rates of road user charges for each 1,000 kg of additional weight carried by a combination vehicle (except an all-terrain crane) operating under an additional licence are the rates specified in Schedule 8.
5 Rates inclusive of GST
The rates of road user charges specified in Schedules 1 to 8 are inclusive of goods and services tax.
6 Revocation
The Road User Charges (Rates) Regulations 2014 (LI 2014/146) are revoked.
