Home and Community Support (Payment for Travel Between Clients) Settlement Act 2016
1 Title
This Act is the Home and Community Support (Payment for Travel Between Clients) Settlement Act 2016.
2 Commencement
This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.
 Part 1 — Preliminary provisions
3 Purposes
The purposes of this Act are—
to facilitate the settlement of civil proceedings about the payment of wages to HCS employees at the minimum rate prescribed under the Minimum Wage Act 1983 for travel between clients; and
to provide for payment by HCS employers to HCS employees for travel between clients from 1 July 2015.
To those ends, this Act—
extinguishes claims by HCS employees for wages payable under the Minimum Wage Act 1983 for travel between clients before the commencement of this Act; and
amends the Minimum Wage Act 1983 to disapply the requirement to pay wages at the minimum rate prescribed under that Act for travel between clients; and
provides a minimum level of required payments to be made to HCS employees for travel between clients.

5 Meaning of exceptional travel and related terms
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—
exceptional travel means—
travel by an HCS employee between clients where the distance travelled is more than the maximum travel distance; and
exceptional first client travel by an HCS employee; and
exceptional last client travel by an HCS employee
maximum travel distance means, except for the purposes of section 16(2), the maximum travel distance specified in clause 4 of Schedule 4.
For the purposes of subsection (1),—
an HCS employee’s travel to the first client visit of the day is exceptional first client travel if the location of the client visit is more than the maximum travel distance from the employee’s home; and
an HCS employee’s travel from the last client visit of the day is exceptional last client travel if the location of the client visit is more than the maximum travel distance from the employee’s home.

The transitional, savings, and related provisions (if any) set out in Schedule 1 have effect according to their terms.

 Part 2 — Payment for travel between clients
 Extinguishing claims for travel payments and statutory amendment to employment agreements
8 Certain claims extinguished
This section applies to—
any claim by or on behalf of an HCS employee for wages payable under the Minimum Wage Act 1983 for travel between clients lodged but not determined before the commencement of this Act; and
any potential claim by or on behalf of an HCS employee against an HCS employer, a former HCS employer, Health New Zealand, ACC, or the Crown for wages payable under the Minimum Wage Act 1983 for travel between clients before the commencement of this Act.
The claim or potential claim cannot be pursued and must be treated as if it had been withdrawn or is incapable of being lodged.
Without limiting subsection (2), an HCS employee (or any person acting on his or her behalf) cannot—
name, join, or seek to name or join an HCS employer, a former HCS employer, Health New Zealand, ACC, or the Crown in any civil proceedings relating to liability for wages relating to travel between clients by the employee before the commencement of this Act; or
apply in any civil proceedings for any remedy or relief from an HCS employer, a former HCS employer, Health New Zealand, ACC, or the Crown for wages relating to travel between clients by the employee before the commencement of this Act.
This section is subject to section 9.
9 Act does not affect enforcement of employment agreements requiring payment for travel before commencement of Act
Nothing in section 8 limits or prevents an HCS employee from making a claim or lodging any proceedings in relation to the enforcement of a term or condition in the employee’s employment agreement that required the HCS employer or former HCS employer to pay the employee for travel between clients before the commencement of this Act.
10 Act overrides existing terms and conditions in employment agreements requiring payment of greater amounts
This section applies to an employment agreement containing either or both of the following terms or conditions agreed before the commencement of this Act:
a term or condition that requires an HCS employee to be paid a greater amount for travel between clients than that required by this Act:
a term or condition that requires an HCS employee to be paid for—
travel by the employee to the first client visit of the day that is not exceptional first client travel; or
travel by the employee from the last client visit of the day that is not exceptional last client travel.
To the extent that the term or condition exceeds the requirements of this Act, the term or condition has no effect, and is not enforceable, for travel on and from the commencement of this Act.
11 Act does not prevent new terms and conditions in employment agreements requiring payment of greater amounts
This Act does not prevent an HCS employer from paying an HCS employee a greater amount for travel between clients than that required by this Act or for travel described in section 10(1)﻿(b) (whether specified in an employment agreement or otherwise) on a basis agreed with the employee after the commencement of this Act, and any such agreement has full effect and is enforceable.
12 No HCS employee to be financially disadvantaged
Subsection (2) applies if, because of the operation of section 10, an HCS employee’s post-commencement travel entitlement is less than the employee’s pre-commencement travel entitlement.
The HCS employer must compensate the employee for the reduced entitlement by inserting a term in the employee’s employment agreement that has the effect of requiring the employer to pay the employee an amount for travel between clients greater than that required by this Act.
The amount must be no less than the amount that is equal to the employee’s total entitlement before 1 July 2015.
An HCS employer and an HCS employee—
may agree to enhance a term inserted in the employee’s employment agreement under subsection (2); but
may not agree to otherwise amend or remove the term at any time while the employee is employed by the employer.
In this section,—
post-commencement travel entitlement means an HCS employee’s total entitlement under this Act for travel between clients in the pay period immediately after 1 March 2016
pre-commencement travel entitlement means the total amount an HCS employee would have been entitled to for the following travel under the terms of his or her employment agreement at 30 June 2015 were section 10 not in force:
travel between clients in the pay period immediately after 1 March 2016; and
travel described in section 10(1)﻿(b) in the pay period immediately after 1 March 2016.
13 Payment of lesser amounts prohibited
An employment agreement that contains a term or condition that excludes, restricts, or reduces an HCS employee’s entitlements under this Act—
has no effect to the extent that it does so, regardless of whether the term or condition was agreed before or after the commencement of this Act; but
is not an illegal contract under subpart 5 of Part 2 of the Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017.

 Relationship with Employment Relations Act 2000
22 Compliance dispute is employment relationship problem
To avoid doubt,—
a dispute about whether an HCS employer is complying, or has complied, with this Act is an employment relationship problem within the meaning of that term in section 5 of the Employment Relations Act 2000; and
the dispute must be resolved in accordance with that Act.
23 HCS employee may recover arrears
The failure by an HCS employer to pay an HCS employee an amount required by this Act, either in whole or in part, constitutes payment of wages or other money at a rate lower than that legally payable for the purposes of section 131 of the Employment Relations Act 2000.
 Record keeping
24 Record of amounts paid, qualifying travel time, and qualifying travel distance
An HCS employer must keep a record, for each HCS employee employed by the employer, of—
the amounts paid to the employee under this Act for each pay period; and
the qualifying travel times, qualifying distances, deemed travel times, and deemed distances used as the basis for calculating the amounts.
A record kept for the purposes of subsection (1) is deemed, for the purposes of the Employment Relations Act 2000, to form part of the wages and time record of the employee (as that record is described in section 130 of that Act).
 Penalty for non-compliance
25 Penalty for non-compliance
An HCS employer who fails to comply with an obligation imposed on the employer by this Act is liable to a penalty imposed by the Employment Relations Authority under the Employment Relations Act 2000.
 Other employers and employees unaffected
26 Other employers and employees unaffected
Nothing in this Act limits or affects the rights, duties, or obligations relating to payment for employment-related travel of any employer or employee (within the meaning of those terms in the Employment Relations Act 2000) to whom this Act does not apply.
This section is to avoid doubt.
 Amendments to Act by Order in Council
27 Power to amend Schedule 3
The Governor-General may, by Order in Council made on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, amend Schedule 3 to—
replace the name of an HCS employer in recognition of a change in the employer’s name:
insert the name of an employer who begins providing home and community-based support services after 1 December 2015.
Before making a recommendation under subsection (1)﻿(b), the Minister of Health must—
consult ACC and the Minister for ACC; and
obtain the consent of—
any unions representing 1 or more HCS employees of the employer; and
any HCS employees of the employer who are not represented by a union.
An order made under subsection (1)﻿(b) may apply retrospectively from the date that the HCS employer started providing home and community-based support services but only if the parties listed in subsection (2)﻿(b) agree.
An order under this section is secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
28 Power to amend Schedule 4
The Governor-General may, by Order in Council made on the recommendation of the Minister of Health and the Minister for ACC,—
adjust the figure specified in clause 1 of Schedule 4 upwards:
adjust the figure specified in clause 2(1) of Schedule 4 upwards or downwards, provided that the figure is no less than 3.7 kilometres:
adjust the figure specified in clause 3(1) of Schedule 4 upwards or downwards, provided that the figure is no less than 8 minutes and 30 seconds:
adjust the figure specified in clause 4 of Schedule 4 downwards.
Before making a recommendation under subsection (1), the following parties must be consulted:
ACC:
Health New Zealand:
all HCS employers listed in Schedule 3:
any unions representing 1 or more HCS employees.
Before making a recommendation under subsection (1)﻿(b), the Minister of Health and the Minister for ACC must be satisfied that the adjusted figure is a fair approximation of the actual distance HCS employees would travel between clients.
Before making a recommendation under subsection (1)﻿(c), the Minister of Health and the Minister for ACC must be satisfied that the adjusted figure is a fair approximation of the actual amount of time HCS employees would spend travelling between clients.
An order under this section is secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
