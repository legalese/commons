Poverty Bay Land and Deeds Registration Districts Act 1896
1 Short Title
The Short Title of this Act is The Poverty Bay Land and Deeds Registration Districts Act 1896.
2 Interpretation
In this Act, if not inconsistent with the context,—
Register-office means and includes Land and Deeds Register Office
Registrar includes Registrar of Deeds and District Land Registrar
Register means and includes all registers, record-books, indices, and books of reference required to be kept under the provisions of The Land Transfer Act 1952, and of The Deeds Registration Act 1868, or of any regulation under the said Acts respectively.
3 Land in Schedule constituted a new land and deeds registration district
On and after the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and ninety-seven, the district the boundaries whereof are defined in the Schedule hereto (hereinafter called the said district) shall cease to be within the limits of the Hawke's Bay and Auckland Land and Deeds Registration Districts, and shall be a separate land registration district within the meaning of The Land Transfer Act 1952, and shall also be a separate deeds registration district within the meaning of The Deeds Registration Act 1868, under the name of The Poverty Bay Land and Deeds Registration District.
4 Governor may appoint register-office for new district
There shall be within the said district, at such place as the Governor shall from time to time appoint, a register-office for the purposes of this Act, and, upon the appointment of a Registrar for the said district, all records relating exclusively to land within the said district shall be transferred to the custody of such Registrar, and shall be deposited in the office aforesaid.
5 Portion of existing Hawke's Bay Land and Deeds Registration District to be included in Auckland District
So much of the present Hawke's Bay Land and Deeds Registration District north of the thirty-ninth parallel of latitude as is not included in the district hereby created shall, on and after the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and ninety-seven, cease to be within the Hawke's Bay Land and Deeds Registration District, and shall be included in and form part of the Auckland Land and Deeds Registration District.
6 Copies of existing registers made available for new district
Copies shall be made of such or so much of the registers in the register-offices at Napier and Auckland respectively as are necessary for the purposes of this Act in respect of the district hereby created, and shall be deposited in the register-office for the said district.
The like copies shall be made of such or so much of the registers in the register-office at Napier as are necessary for the purposes of this Act in respect of that portion of the present Hawke's Bay Land and Deeds Registration District which is hereby included in the Auckland District, and shall be deposited in the register-office for the Auckland District.
All such copies shall, when so deposited, have the force and effect of the original registers, and shall for all purposes of this Act be deemed to be the original registers, and be admissible in evidence for all purposes for which such original registers would be admissible.
