Health Benefits (Reciprocity with the United Kingdom) Act 1982
1 Short Title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Health Benefits (Reciprocity with the United Kingdom) Act 1982.
This Act shall come into force on a date to be fixed by the Governor-General by Order in Council. The date so fixed may be a date before the date on which the Order in Council is made.
An order under this section is secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
2 Agreement to have full force and effect
The provisions contained in the agreement set out in the Schedule shall have full force and effect so far as they relate to New Zealand.
3 Revocation
The Medical, Hospital, and Related Benefits (Reciprocity with United Kingdom) Notice 1960 is hereby revoked.
