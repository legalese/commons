Meteorological Services Act 1990
1 Short Title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Meteorological Services Act 1990.
This Act shall come into force on 1 September 1990.
2 Meteorological services
The Minister of Transport shall ensure the provision of the meteorological warning service referred to in section 3.
The Minister of Transport shall arrange for the provision of—
the making and issuing of forecasts of the weather; and
the collection and recording of such meteorological information as is necessary for the purposes of paragraph (a).
3 Minister to designate meteorological warning service
The Minister of Transport shall from time to time designate a person who shall be responsible to provide the authorised meteorological warning service in New Zealand.
