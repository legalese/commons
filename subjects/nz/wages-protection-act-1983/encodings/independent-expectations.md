# Independent expectations: Wages Protection Act 1983 (NZ), version as at 27 November 2025

Written from `BRIEF.md` and `../source/WPA1983.txt` alone, before any `.l4` file or `NOTES.md` was opened.
"REFUSE" means the Act does not give the answer and the encoding should refuse.

Conventions taken as read: "within 2 weeks of receiving" a notice received on day D ends at the end of D+14; "not later than N days after" day P ends at the end of P+N; "6 years from" date D ends on the anniversary of D (Legislation Act 2019: a period after a day excludes that day); "2 months after" day D ends on the same day-number two months on, or the last day of that month if it has no such day. Default scenario date: after 27 November 2025 unless stated.

## Gaps where the Act gives no answer (expect REFUSE)

- Lawfulness of a deduction before 1 April 2016 that turns on the replaced s 5(1) (old text not in this version) (D15).
- Whether a consent withdrawal was implemented "as soon as is practicable" is a judgement, so it is an input, not computed.
