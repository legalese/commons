Health Benefits (Reciprocity with Australia) Act 1999
1 Short Title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Health Benefits (Reciprocity with Australia) Act 1999.
This Act comes into force on a date to be appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.
An order under this section is secondary legislation (see Part 3 of the Legislation Act 2019 for publication requirements).
2 Reciprocity agreement to have effect
The provisions contained in the agreement set out in the Schedule have effect so far as they relate to New Zealand.
