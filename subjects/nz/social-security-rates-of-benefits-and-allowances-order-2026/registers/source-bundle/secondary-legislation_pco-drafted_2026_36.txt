Social Security (Rates of Benefits and Allowances) Order 2026
Order
1 Title
This order is the Social Security (Rates of Benefits and Allowances) Order 2026.
2 Commencement
This order comes into force on 1 April 2026, but clause 7 comes into force immediately after clauses 4, 5, and 6.
3 Interpretation
In this order, commencement date means 1 April 2026.
4 Application
In accordance with section 453(5) of the Social Security Act 2018, clause 5 applies to benefits (as defined in that Act) payable on and after the commencement date.
In accordance with section 15(6) of the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001, clause 6 applies to New Zealand superannuation payable on and after the commencement date.
In accordance with section 452(4) of the Social Security Act 2018, clause 7 applies to benefits, allowances, and entitlements payable on and after the commencement date.
5 Mandatory adjustments under section 453 of Social Security Act 2018
In each provision of the Social Security Act 2018 listed in the first column of Part 1 of Schedule 1 of this order, replace the amount set out opposite to that provision in the second column with the amount set out opposite to that provision in the third column.
6 Mandatory adjustments under sections 15 and 16 of New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001
In each provision of the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001 listed in the first column of Part 2 of Schedule 1 of this order, replace the amount set out opposite to that provision in the second column with the amount set out opposite to that provision in the third column.
7 Discretionary increases under section 452 of Social Security Act 2018
In each provision of the Social Security Act 2018 listed in the first column of Part 1 of Schedule 2 of this order, replace the amount set out opposite to that provision in the second column with the amount set out opposite to that provision in the third column.
In the provision of the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001 listed in the first column of Part 2 of Schedule 2 of this order, replace the amount set out opposite to that provision in the second column with the amount set out opposite to that provision in the third column.
In the provision of the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001 listed in the first column of Part 3 of Schedule 2 of this order, replace the amount set out opposite to that provision in the second column with the amount set out opposite to that provision in the third column.
8 Revocation
The Social Security (Rates of Benefits and Allowances) Order 2025 (SL 2025/9) is revoked.
