# Independent expectations: Minimum Wage Act 1983 (NZ)

Written from BRIEF.md and source/MWA1983.txt alone, before any .l4 file or NOTES.md was opened.

## The invented Minimum Wage Order (inputs)

- Adult rate (s 4): $25.00 per hour.
- Starting-out class A (s 4A(1)(c)(ii)(B)): aged 16 or 17, less than 6 months' continuous employment with the current employer: $20.00 (a monetary amount; exactly 80%).
- Starting-out class B (s 4A(1)(c)(i)): aged 16 to 19, continuously paid a specified social security benefit for at least 6 months: 85% of the adult rate = $21.25.
- Starting-out class C (s 4A(1)(c)(iii)): aged 16 to 19, employed under a training contract: $21.00.
- Training rate (s 4B): $20.00.
