Births, Deaths, Marriages, and Relationships Registration (Fees) Regulations 1995
Regulations
1 Title and commencement
These regulations may be cited as the Births, Deaths, Marriages, and Relationships Registration (Fees) Regulations 1995.
These regulations shall come into force on the day after the date of their notification in the Gazette.
1A Interpretation
In these regulations, certificate means a birth certificate, death certificate, marriage certificate, civil union certificate, or name change certificate.
2 Fees
There shall be paid to the Registrar-General or, as the case requires, to a Registrar, for each of the matters specified in the Schedule, the fee specified for it in that schedule.
3 Amount of goods and services tax included
The fees prescribed by these regulations are exclusive of goods and services tax under the Goods and Services Tax Act 1985.
4 Revocation
The Births and Deaths Registration (Fees) Regulations 1989 (SR 1989/319) and Births and Deaths Registration (Fees) Regulations 1989, Amendment No 1 (SR 1993/220) are hereby revoked.
 Schedule 1 — Fees
Fees payable for various matters on or after 18 June 2026 and before close of 15 June 2027
Fees payable for various matters on or after 16 June 2027 and before close of 15 June 2028
Fees payable for various matters on or after 16 June 2028
