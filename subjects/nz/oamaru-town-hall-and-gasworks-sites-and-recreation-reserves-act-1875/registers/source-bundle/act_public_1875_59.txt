Oamaru Town Hall and Gasworks Sites and Recreation Reserves Act 1875
1 Short Title
The Short Title of this Act shall be The Oamaru Town Hall and Gasworks Sites and Recreation Reserves Act 1875.
2 Governor may grant lands in Schedules A to E to Corporation of Oamaru
It shall be lawful for the Governor to cause grants from the Crown of the several parcels of land described in Schedules A, B, C, D, and E of this Act to be issued to the Municipal Corporation of the Town of Oamaru, to be held by the said Corporation in trust for the purposes following, that is to say,—
All that parcel of land described in Schedule A, as and for a site for a town hall;
All that parcel of land described in Schedule B, for purposes of public recreation;
All that parcel of land described in Schedule C, for purposes of public utility for the Town of Oamaru and the inhabitants thereof;
All those two parcels of land respectively described in Schedule D, for purposes of public recreation;
All that parcel of land described in Schedule E, for a road and right of way to Drill Shed Reserve, School Reserve, and Recreation Reserve.
3 Corporation may lease lands in Schedule C But lands in Schedules A, B, D, and E may not be leased, etc
It shall be lawful for the said Corporation from time to time to demise the lands specified in Schedule C, or any part or parts thereof, at such annual rent or rents and for such term of years, not exceeding twenty-one years, as the said Corporation may think fit, and the rents issues and profits thereof shall form part of the ordinary municipal revenue of the said Town of Oamaru.
But it shall not be lawful for the said Corporation to demise or lease, or apply to any other purpose than the purpose in each case particularly expressed, any of or any part of the lands described in Schedules A, B, D, and E respectively.
4 Corporation may use lands in Schedule F as a site for gasworks
It shall be lawful for the said Corporation to apply and use for the purpose of erecting gasworks thereon, for the supply of the said Town of Oamaru with gas, all that piece or parcel of land described in the Schedule F of this Act.
