Property Speculation Tax Repeal Act 1979
1 Short Title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Property Speculation Tax Repeal Act 1979.
This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on 22 June 1979.
2 Abolition of Property Speculation Tax
The Property Speculation Tax Act 1973 is hereby repealed.
The following Orders in Council are hereby revoked:
the Property Speculation Tax Exemption Order 1973:
the Property Speculation Tax Exemption Order 1974:
the Property Speculation Tax Exemption Order 1978.
3 Savings
In this section expressions defined in the Property Speculation Tax Act 1973 have the meanings so defined.
Notwithstanding section 2, the enactments thereby repealed and the Orders in Council thereby revoked shall continue to apply to all dispositions of land made before 22 June 1979.
