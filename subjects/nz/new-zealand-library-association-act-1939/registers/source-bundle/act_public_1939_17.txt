New Zealand Library Association Act 1939
1 Short Title
This Act may be cited as the New Zealand Library Association Act 1939.
2 Incorporation of New Zealand Library Association
The society heretofore known as the New Zealand Library Association (hereinafter referred to as the Association) is hereby constituted a body corporate by the name of the New Zealand Library Association (Incorporated), having perpetual succession and a common seal, and being capable of holding real and personal property and of doing and suffering all that bodies corporate may do and suffer.
The society referred to in the last preceding subsection is the society originally constituted as the Libraries Association of New Zealand at a meeting of representatives of public libraries that was held in Dunedin in March 1910, pursuant to a resolution adopted by the Dunedin City Council on 7 February 1910.
3 Provisions of Incorporated Societies Act 2022 to apply
The following provisions of the Incorporated Societies Act 2022 apply with all necessary modifications to the Association as if it were a society incorporated under that Act:
subpart 2 of Part 3 (financial gain):
section 77 (members have no right to property of society):
section 78 (liability of members):
sections 110 and 111 (registered office):
section 123 (method of contracting):
section 125 (service of documents).
4 Rules of Association to be amended
As soon as conveniently may be after the passing of this Act, the rules of the Association shall be amended so as to make provision for such additional matters as would have to be provided for in its rules if the Association were a society incorporated under the Incorporated Societies Act 1908.
Nothing in the last preceding subsection shall preclude the revocation of the existing rules and the making of new rules in substitution therefor.
The Association may make provision in its rules for any matter in respect of which it could make rules if it were incorporated under the Incorporated Societies Act 2022.
The adoption of new rules by the Association shall not affect its corporate identity.
5 Local authorities may become members of Association
Any local authority that has established or has power to establish a public library may be admitted to and become a member of the Association in the name and on behalf of the corporation represented by it.
Any local authority, being a member of the Association as aforesaid, may pay any subscriptions, dues, or other moneys payable by it to the Association (including donations to the funds of the Association) out of any of its moneys available for library purposes.
6 Corporate bodies may become members of Association
Any corporate body, whether incorporated under the Incorporated Societies Act 2022 or in any other manner, may be admitted to and become a member of the Association, unless the objects of the Association, as defined in its rules, are ultra vires of such corporate body.
7 Exercise of rights of membership by corporate and unincorporated bodies
The rules of the Association may define the rights of membership of any corporate or unincorporated bodies, being members of the Association, and may prescribe the manner in which such rights may be exercised.
8 Winding up of Association
The Association shall not be wound up except by leave of the Governor-General in Council.
