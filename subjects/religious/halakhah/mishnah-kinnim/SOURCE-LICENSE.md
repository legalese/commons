# Source license

The deposited texts for this subject are the Hebrew text of Mishnah Kinnim and four classical aids, as Hebrew Wikisource prints them.
They are in `registers/source-bundle/`, one file per page slice, each with a `.meta.json` naming the page revision it came from:

- `mishnah-kinnim-1`, `-2`, `-3`: the Mishnah, the printed text (נוסח הדפוסים), standard numbering. **The primary text.**
- `mishnah-kinnim-1-rambam-recension`, `-2-…`, `-3-…`: the same chapters in Maimonides' recension (נוסח הרמב"ם): a textual witness, used where a word differs.
- `bartenura-kinnim-1`, `-2`, `-3`: R. Ovadiah of Bertinoro (d. c. 1515) on Kinnim: an aid.
- `tiferet-yisrael-kinnim-3`: R. Israel Lipschutz (1782–1860), Tiferet Yisrael on chapter 3: an aid, from an OCR page the site marks as needing proofreading.
- `rambam-pesulei-hamukdashin-8`: Maimonides, Mishneh Torah, Hilkhot Pesulei ha-Mukdashin ch. 8 (Vilna-Warsaw print): an aid.

## Not deposited, and why

- **Sefaria** (Hebrew and English): both its API and its site answered with a Cloudflare human-verification challenge. Recorded as a no; not worked around (ENCODING-GUIDE §8).
- **Mechon Mamre**: its `robots.txt` carries `Content-Signal: search=yes,ai-train=no,use=reference` and disallows `ClaudeBot`, and its home page says the texts are "free to use, subject to our copyright". It was read once, for orientation, and not deposited.
- **Philip Reiss**, "A Mathematical Proof of Kinnim 3:2", *The Torah u-Madda Journal* 9 (2000) 58–75 (JSTOR 40914641), and **William Gewirtz**, "Kinnim (3:2): An Intuitive Mathematical Approach", *Ḥakirah* 30 (2021) 269–279: copyrighted articles, cited by page and not copied.
- **Higayon**: Moshe Koppel, "משפט קומבינטורי במסכת קינים", *Higayon* 2, pp. 96–102 (not obtained); Eliyahu Beller, "משנת קנים כפשוטה", *Higayon* 5, pp. 155–157 (read).
