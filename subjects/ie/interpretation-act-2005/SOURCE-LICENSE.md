## Language

English is not the only authoritative language. Under Article 25.4 of the Constitution, where the President signs a Bill in only one official language, an official translation is issued in the other, and where both texts are enrolled and conflict the text in the national language, Irish, prevails (as recorded in the sibling subject `ie/sick-leave-act-2022`, checked 2026-09-22).
Only the English text is deposited. This Act's signed language was not checked.
