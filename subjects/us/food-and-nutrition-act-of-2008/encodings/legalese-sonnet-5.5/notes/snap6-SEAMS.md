# SEAMS (snap6)

Two lists, as PLAN.md rule 9 asks: ASKS (the answers snap6 takes as inputs because another part computes them) and ANSWERS (what snap6's sections give to others).
Generated by `work/seams.py` from the layer-1 modules `snap6-s2026.l4`, `snap6-s2031.l4`, `snap6-s2034.l4`, `snap6-s2035.l4`, `snap6-s2036.l4` and `snap6-s2036a.l4`; s 2033 is repealed and has no module.
A seam input is always a `GIVEN` parameter of the asking function (naming rule 8), never a top-level constant.
Where PLAN.md says an input is `MAYBE BOOLEAN`, it is: `NOTHING` means the answer has not been supplied, and a `NOTHING` input gives a `NOTHING` answer unless another limb decides without it.
