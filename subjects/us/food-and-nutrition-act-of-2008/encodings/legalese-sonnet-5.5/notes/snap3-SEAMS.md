### Facts another section decides, named in the text's own words

PLAN.md Group C says to name such an input for the answering section and to type it `MAYBE BOOLEAN` unless it is a number or a date.
These inputs do not follow that: they are not named for the answering section; each is a fact about the household, the claim, the plan or the State agency, phrased as the Act phrases it, and its type is the one in the table (mostly `BOOLEAN`, one `MAYBE BOOLEAN`, two `NUMBER`).
That is a deviation I took (assumed, not ruled).
If the answering part returns `NOTHING` for one of the `BOOLEAN` ones, the lead must decide whether to rename and retype it as `MAYBE BOOLEAN`; until then a caller who has no answer cannot say so.
