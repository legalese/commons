# Source license

The quoted text in this folder's `.l4` files, and the tables built from it, is taken from `../../registers/source-bundle/usc07-ch51.txt` and `../../registers/source-bundle/fna-snap-cola-fy2027.txt`, plain-text renderings of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 made by converter scripts.
They are not the official text.
Their terms, their attribution wording, the conditions of the grant and their unofficial status are recorded in `../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md`, the subject-level licence note written when the text was deposited.
That note is not repeated here.

What this encoding adds to it:

- Every quotation is a line copied out of the text by script, carrying its line number, so that the accuracy of the quotation can be checked by a reader who holds the text beside the code.
  `srcrefs.py` re-checks each quotation against the text.
- Nothing in this folder claims to be verified against the official text.
- The encoding itself (the code, the tests, the notes) is Apache-2.0 like the rest of canon; that does not widen the terms of the quoted text.
