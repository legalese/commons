## Language

English is the only authoritative language. Congress enacts statutes only in English, and there are no official versions in other languages. (The US has no statutory official language, but federal legislation exists only in English.)

## Status of this deposit

Deposited 2026-10-19 from the OLRC USLM XML at release point Public Law 119-121 (current through 9 October 2026). Each file is plain text converted from the XML: title, chapter, subchapter, part and section numbers and headings, the section text at every level, tables (one row per line, cells separated by ` | `) and each section's source credit are kept; tables of contents and the editorial and statutory notes are omitted. Each `.meta.json` beside a file gives the download URL, the sha256 of the downloaded zip and of the text, the USLM identifiers converted, and a section count checked against the XML.

`subject.json` was left as it was indexed on 2026-09-22 (its source is still govinfo's 2024 edition); this section, not `subject.json`, records what is deposited. The earlier index record in `registers/source-bundle/` (`text_deposited: false`) is also unchanged.
