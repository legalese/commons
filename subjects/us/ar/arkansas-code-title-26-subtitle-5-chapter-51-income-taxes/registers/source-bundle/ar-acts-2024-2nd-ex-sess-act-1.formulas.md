# Acts 2024 (2nd Ex. Sess.), No. 1, s 1 — the tables of Ark. Code 26-51-201(a)(3) as enacted

Transcribed from the enrolled Act (PDF), https://arkleg.state.ar.us/Acts/FTPDocument?path=%2FACTS%2F2024S2%2FPublic%2F&file=1.pdf&ddBienniumSession=2023%2F2024S2, pages 2 and 3, retrieved 2026-10-24.
The Act is in amendment form ("Stricken language would be deleted from and underlined language would be added to present law"). The plain-text deposit `ar-acts-2024-2nd-ex-sess-act-1.txt` loses the strike-through and underlining, so each changed figure appears twice there.

**How the enacted figures were read.** The PDF could not be rendered as an image in this session, so strike-through could not be seen. The figures were read by the Act's own drafting order, in which the stricken figure comes first and the underlined figure follows it (for example "eighty-seven thousand dollars ($87,000) eighty-nine thousand six hundred dollars ($89,600)"). The reading is checked three ways: the thresholds in the words of (A), (B) and (C) agree with the tables; the (C) table's new rows run without gap from $89,601 to $92,700, the end of the range (C) states in words; and the new (C) rows fall by $10 for each $100, ending at $0 for "$92,701 and up". A human should still check the figures against the PDF before relying on them.
