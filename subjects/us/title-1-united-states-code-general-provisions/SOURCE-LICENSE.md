## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding.
Title 1 is enacted into positive law, so its text is legal evidence of the law under 1 U.S.C. 204.

## Language

English is the only authoritative language. Congress enacts statutes only in English, and there are no official versions in other languages.
