# HB 463 (2026), Act 465: what is struck and what is inserted

The deposited text `ga-2026-act-465-hb-463-ap.txt` is `pdftotext -layout` output of the bill as passed (HB 463/AP, https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20252026/249080, retrieved 2026-10-24). Georgia bills that revise a Code section print the whole revised text, with deleted words **struck through** and new words **underlined**. Plain text loses both marks, so the deposited file prints old and new figures side by side ("$24,000.00 $30,000.00") and keeps paragraphs that the bill deletes.

This sidecar records the marks. They were read from the PDF itself: each mark is a thin filled rectangle drawn in the page content stream, about 4 points above a line's baseline for a strike and about 2 points below it for an underline. The rectangles were matched to the text lines (bill page and line numbers below) with a short script kept outside commons. Nothing in the deposited text was changed.

Only the parts of Part II that the encoding uses are given. Square brackets mark struck text, braces mark inserted text.

## Section 2-1: O.C.G.A. 48-7-20(a.1)

Bill page 2, lines 39-43, and page 3, lines 44-65:

> (a.1)(1) On and after January 1, [2025] {2026}, the tax imposed pursuant to subsection (a) of this Code section shall be [5.19 percent] {4.99 percent} for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, [2025] {2026}; provided, however, that such rate shall be reduced by [0.10] {0.125} percent annually beginning on January 1, [2026] {2027}, until the rate reaches [4.99 percent,] {3.99 percent}; provided that such annual reductions in the tax rate shall be [subject to delays as provided in paragraph (2) of this subsection] {delayed by one year for each year that prospective annual reductions in the standard deduction are delayed for any of the reasons provided in paragraph (1.1) of subsection (a) of Code Section 48-7-27}.
>
> [(2) Each prospective annual reduction in the tax rate ... (A) ... (B) ... (C) ... year.]
>
> [(3) The Office of Planning and Budget shall make the determinations ... This paragraph shall not be applicable after the final reduction to the rate of 4.99 percent occurs.]

Paragraphs (2) and (3), lines 47-65, are struck through in full. Their delay tests reappear, unchanged in substance, as new 48-7-27(a)(1.1).

The underline on line 42 covers "0.125" only. The struck "4.99" on line 42 and "percent," on line 43 are separate rectangles. "3.99 percent;" on line 43 is underlined.

## Section 2-3: O.C.G.A. 48-7-27(a)

Bill page 4, line 82: "as defined in [the United States] Internal Revenue Code of 1986". The strike runs from about x=245 to x=315 on that line.
