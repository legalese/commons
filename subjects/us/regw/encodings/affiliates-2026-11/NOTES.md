# Regulation W (12 CFR Part 223) — the affiliate definition

## Scope

This encoding covers the definition of "affiliate" in 12 CFR 223.2(a)–(c), and the definitions it rests on in 12 CFR 223.3: company, person, subsidiary and control.
It does not cover investment-fund affiliates (223.2(a)(6)), merchant-banking affiliates (223.2(a)(9)(i)), covered transactions, the quantitative limits, valuation, the exemptions or the sequence provisions.
The provision numbers below come from a first reading of a secondary mirror of the regulation; they are checked against the deposited text (`../../source/`) when each row is encoded, and the table is corrected if they differ.

## Seat

The seat is the member bank to which the regulation is applied (a company, not an official): "Is this company my affiliate?"
No seat is written for the Board of Governors, which is the regulator.
