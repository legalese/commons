- **Verdict: conditions, met.** The text of a statute is not copyrightable in the United States: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A site-wide rights notice, or a claim to the compilation, can at most cover the publisher's arrangement and editorial matter. So only the unannotated statutory text and its history line are deposited. The Revisor's Notes, cross references, law review citations and case annotations on the pages are left out, and so is the site's own arrangement (the sections are a selection, in statute order). No term requires permission or forbids modification.
