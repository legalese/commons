- **Verdict: permission required, so not deposited.** The State claims copyright by statute and requires "the express authorization of the Code Commission" to print or distribute, on pain of a misdemeanour. The claim reaches only what federal law permits, and under *Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020) the enacted text itself is probably outside copyright, so the claim may well not reach it.
