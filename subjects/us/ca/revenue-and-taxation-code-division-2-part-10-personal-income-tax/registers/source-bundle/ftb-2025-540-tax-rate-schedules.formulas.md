# 2025 California Tax Rate Schedules — transcribed

From the Franchise Tax Board's PDF, https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-tax-rate-schedules.pdf, retrieved 2026-10-24 (deposited as `ftb-2025-540-tax-rate-schedules.txt`, where the columns lost their alignment). Each row reads: if taxable income (Form 540, line 19) is over the first figure but not over the second, the tax is the base amount plus the rate of the amount over the first figure. Each base amount was checked by hand against the row above (base + rate × width of the row above).

The FTB says to use the schedules only for taxable income over $100,000; at or below, its Tax Table (in the booklet deposit) applies, which works in $100 steps.
