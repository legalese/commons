## Why this subject is a cluster of sections

California has no single interpretation act.
Gov. Code § 9603 says: "The general rules for the construction of statutes are contained in the preliminary provisions of the different codes."
The scope below is an assumption made when this subject was stubbed on 2026-10-21, not a ruling, and it is the nearest analogue to the other jurisdictions' Interpretation Acts.

- Government Code, General Provisions, §§ 1-26 (definitions, "shall"/"may", the singular and plural, number and gender, and the like).
- Civil Code, Preliminary Provisions, §§ 1-23.6.
- Code of Civil Procedure, Preliminary Provisions, §§ 1-34, which include computation of time (§ 12) and holidays (§§ 12a-12c).
- Government Code, Title 1, Division 7, Chapter 7 (Holidays), §§ 6700-6736, and Chapter 8 (Computation of Time), §§ 6800-6808.
- Government Code, Title 2, Division 2, Part 1, Chapter 6 (Operation of Statutes and Resolutions), §§ 9600-9612: when statutes take effect and the effect of repeal.

Other codes' preliminary provisions (for example the Penal Code and the Evidence Code) are not included.
Sections that appear in square brackets in the table of contents, which mark repealed sections, are not included.
