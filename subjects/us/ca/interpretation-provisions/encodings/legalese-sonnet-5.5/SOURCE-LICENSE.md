**What is quoted.**
Sections of the California Government Code (§§ 1-26, 6700-6736, 6800-6808, 9600-9612), Civil Code (§§ 1-23.6) and Code of Civil Procedure (§§ 1-34).
The modules reproduce them verbatim in `-- src:ID:N | text` comment lines, copied by script from the deposited plain-text files `GOV-ss-1-26.txt`, `CIV-ss-1-23.6.txt`, `CCP-ss-1-34.txt`, `GOV-ss-6700-6736.txt`, `GOV-ss-6800-6808.txt` and `GOV-ss-9600-9612.txt`.
Those files are a converter-made plain-text rendering, not the official text; the `current_to` field of their `.meta.json` files (in `../../registers/source-bundle/`) says the sections are current to 2026-10-19.

**Terms.**
Public domain by statute, Gov. Code § 10248.5, which the deposit's stub licence (`../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md`) quotes as: "Notwithstanding subdivision (g) of Section 10248, the information that the Legislative Counsel makes available to the public pursuant to Section 10248 is within the public domain and the State of California retains no copyright or other proprietary interest in that information."
Subdivision (a)(9) of § 10248 lists "The California Codes" among that information, according to the same stub.
Attribution required: none under § 10248.5, according to the stub.
