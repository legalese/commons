## What was read, and when

**Gov. Code § 10248.5**, read on 2026-10-24 in the same bulk export as the deposited text (`LAW_SECTION_TBL_13255.lob`, history "Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 441, Sec. 2. (AB 884) Effective September 22, 2016."), says in full:

> Notwithstanding subdivision (g) of Section 10248, the information that the Legislative Counsel makes available to the public pursuant to Section 10248 is within the public domain and the State of California retains no copyright or other proprietary interest in that information.

**Gov. Code § 10248**, read the same day (`LAW_SECTION_TBL_13254.lob`), lists that information in subdivision (a): it includes "(2) The text of each bill introduced in each current legislative session, including each amended, enrolled, and chaptered form of each bill" and "(9) The California Codes".
Its subdivision (g), which § 10248.5 overrides, reads: "An action taken pursuant to this section does not alter or relinquish any copyright or other proprietary interest or entitlement of the State of California relating to any of the information made available pursuant to this section."

**A copyright line in the bill XML.** The XML of the chaptered bill (`BILL_VERSION_TBL_8965.lob`) carries the comment `<!-- Copyright © Legislative Counsel Bureau, State of California. -->`.
The comment is not part of the bill's text and was dropped in conversion.
Gov. Code § 10248.5 is read here as governing it, because it speaks of the information made available under § 10248, which includes the chaptered bill text; that reading is ours, not a statement by the Legislature about this file.

**The bulk host's own terms.** https://downloads.leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/ is a plain directory index with no robots.txt (HTTP 404 on 2026-10-24).
Its `pubinfo_Readme.pdf` (dated 05/21/2021, read 2026-10-24) describes the tables and how to load them and says "The leginfo data is made available to the public via: http from https://downloads.leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/"; it states no licence, terms of use or restriction.

www.leginfo.ca.gov answered HTTP 403 to its robots.txt.
