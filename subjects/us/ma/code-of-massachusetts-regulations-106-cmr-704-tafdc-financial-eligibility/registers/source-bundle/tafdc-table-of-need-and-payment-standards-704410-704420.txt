(and likewise for the Payment Standards). That address answered HTTP 404 on 2026-10-24; this Mass.gov page, offered by the
Department of Transitional Assistance and titled for 704.410-704.420, is where the figures are posted.

From September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026, inclusive, the appropriate Need and Payment Standard shall be increased by $500 for each eligible applicant and client under age 19.
Note: Due to a nonrecurring $500 clothing allowance in September 2026 for each eligible applicant or client under age 19. The Need and Payment Standards specified in 106 CMR 704.410 and 106 CMR 704.420 are therefore increased by $500.
