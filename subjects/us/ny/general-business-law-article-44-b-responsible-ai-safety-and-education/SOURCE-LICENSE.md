## Terms: UNDETERMINED

- **What was read, 2026-10-24.** nyassembly.gov's `robots.txt` reads, in full: `User-agent: GPTBot` / `Disallow: /`. It does not address this fetcher.
- **What was searched for and not found.** No terms of use, copyright notice, licence or disclaimer page was found on nyassembly.gov: the home page and the site map (https://nyassembly.gov/sitemap/) were searched for links on privacy, disclaimer, terms, policy or copyright, and none was listed. The bill pages carry no copyright notice.
- **What could not be read.** The New York State Senate's site (www.nysenate.gov), whose policies page the other New York subjects in this repository cite for a CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 US licence with a "CC+" waiver, answered HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare challenge on 2026-10-24 and was not read in this session. That page's terms, as recorded in this repository on 2026-09-22 (for example `../general-business-law-article-22-a-protection-from-unfair/SOURCE-LICENSE.md`), were not re-read and are not relied on here; they cover the Senate's site, not the Assembly's.
- **The statute text itself.** The other New York subjects in this repository record that statute text is not copyrightable in the United States (the government edicts doctrine, *Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). That statement was not re-checked in this session and is carried here only as what this repository already records. Whether a bill print on a legislature's site is covered in the same way was not checked.

## Consequence for this subject

The two bill texts are deposited as plain-text conversions and quoted in the encoding, on the footing the repository already applies to New York statute text. Because the host's own terms could not be established, the original HTML pages are NOT deposited (only their sha256, in the `.meta.json` files). A reviewer should confirm the terms before this subject leaves `draft`.
