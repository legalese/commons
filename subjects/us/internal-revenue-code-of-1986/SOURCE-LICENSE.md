## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding, subject to the attribution above.

## Language

English is the only authoritative language. Congress enacts statutes only in English, and there are no official versions in other languages. (The US has no statutory official language, but federal legislation exists only in English.)

Each file is plain text converted from the XML: title, chapter, subchapter, part and section numbers and headings, the section text at every level, tables (one row per line, cells separated by ` | `) and each section's source credit are kept; tables of contents and the editorial and statutory notes are omitted.
