## How the files are made
- Templates live in `../../../work/src/*.l4.in` (that is `<run>/work/src/`), outside the deposit.
  A template line `@@Q a b` expands to the quote lines for raw lines a..b, copied by `srcrefs.py quote` (so quotations are mechanical).
  `python3 -I <run>/work/gen.py` rewrites every deposit `.l4` from its template. Edit the template, never the deposit `.l4`.
- Binary: `L4_BIN` = <scratchpad>/bin/l4-main-110 (1.6.110 main); second stable build `L4_MAIN` = l4-main-109.

## Decisions taken (each with the text that licenses it; full register goes in NOTES.md)
- Facts about time are DATE spans (inclusive first and last day); "N years" = the days in N calendar years ending on a date (computed with `Date`).
- (a) read as TWO requirements, each aggregating 2 years in the 5-year period: ownership (owned) and use (used as principal residence), not necessarily at the same time (licensed by (b)(2)(A)(i)-(ii), (d)(4)(A)-(B)); (c)(1)(B)(i)(I) needs ONE number and takes the days both owned and used.
- FALSE/NOTHING: failed "if" = JUST FALSE; deeming/including provisions whose condition fails = NOTHING; scope chapeau ("In the case of ...") not met = NOTHING.
