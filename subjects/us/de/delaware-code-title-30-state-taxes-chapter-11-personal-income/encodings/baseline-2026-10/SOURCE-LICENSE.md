# Source license

The text of **Delaware Code, Title 30 (State Taxes), Chapter 11 — Personal Income Tax** is published on the [The Delaware Code Online (Delaware General Assembly, Division of Research)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/).

- **Terms**: The register makes no copyright claim at all: there is no terms-of-use or copyright page on delcode.delaware.gov, and the only policy link in the site chrome is the Delaware.gov portal privacy policy. Delaware has instead legislated for the electronic Code's official status by adopting the Uniform Electronic Legal Material Act, so the online text is the designated official record and is authenticated. Combined with the government edicts doctrine (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)), under which statutory text is uncopyrightable, reuse and adaptation are unrestricted. The printed Delaware Code is produced commercially by LexisNexis, but nothing in that arrangement is asserted over the free online text.

- **Attribution required**: None required. Cite as '<title> Del. C. § <section>'; § 101(b) of Title 1 prescribes that form.
- **Source**: https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c011/index.html

In the United States the text of a statute is not copyrightable: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A register's copyright notice can still cover a publisher's annotations, headnotes and numbering, which this corpus does not reproduce.

### Statute text deposited 2026-10-20

Deposited from the Delaware Code Online, current through 85 Del. Laws, c. 518 (acts enacted as of 10 September 2026), fetched 2026-10-19 (UTC). Each file is plain text converted from the site's subchapter or chapter HTML pages, joined in order. Where the Code prints two versions of a section with different effective dates (for example 14 Del. Each `.meta.json` lists every page converted, with its retrieval time (UTC), sha256 and size, and gives the sha256 of the text and its character count.
