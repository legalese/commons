## Decision applied (2026-10-24)

**Ruling of 24 Oct 2026 (Michael):** Canadian texts whose terms permit reproduction but are silent on adaptation may be quoted in L4 encodings in Commons, keeping the attribution each publisher requires and a "not an official version" notice in the text header and here. Commons counts as non-commercial use (ruling of 20 Oct 2026).

The House of Assembly's terms were read under that ruling: they permit excerpting and citing for non-commercial purposes such as research, and they do not forbid modification; their closing sentence reserves adaptation rather than prohibiting it. On that reading the deposits here are **excerpts** (selected sections of the Act; two short regulations whole), each opening with the attribution and a not-an-official-version notice, and the encoding quotes short passages with their citations. **Point for Michael to confirm:** whether the closing reservation ("No permission to reproduce, adapt or distribute ... is to be implied") takes these terms outside the 24 October ruling. If it does, the deposits and the quotations in the encoding should be withdrawn and the subject returned to metadata only.

The earlier note on this subject ("Metadata only until resolved ... No human has recorded a licence decision") is superseded by the paragraph above, subject to that confirmation.
