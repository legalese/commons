# Source license

This row quotes the text of the Interpretation Act (British Columbia), RSBC 1996, c. 238, in the comments of its modules (each quotation is a `-- src:N | ...` line).
The terms the text carries, the conditions of the King's Printer Licence - British Columbia, the language of the source, and the status of the deposit are recorded once for the subject in `../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md`; this file does not repeat them.

**What the quoted text is.**
It is `RSBC-1996-c.-238.txt`, a plain-text rendering made by a converter script from the consolidation published by the King's Printer, Province of British Columbia (BC Laws), current to 2026-10-13.
**It is not the official text, and it is not an official version.**
Nothing in this row has been verified against the official text.

**The attribution statement the licence requires.**
It is copied here mechanically from line 3 of the text file, and the same statement is in the header comment of every module of this row that quotes the text:

> These materials contain information that has been derived from information originally made available by the Province of British Columbia at: http://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca and this information is being used in accordance with the King's Printer Licence – British Columbia available at: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/standards/Licence.html. They have not, however, been produced in affiliation with, or with the endorsement of, the Province of British Columbia and THESE MATERIALS ARE NOT AN OFFICIAL VERSION.

**Whether an encoding that quotes the text is within clause 2.2 of the licence** ("modify the medium or format of the Information and include all or any portion of the Information in Your own product or application") is a human decision that `../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md` says must be recorded before an encoding quotes the text.
It is **undetermined** here: no one has made it, and this row does not grant itself the answer.
The licence of the encoding itself is Apache-2.0 (see `encoding.json`).
