# nunavut/

Nunavut (Canada) legislation, one subject-id per body of law, following the subject-sidecar shape described in [`subjects/README.md`](../README.md).

The subjects here were laid out on 2026-09-23 for the legislation governing twelve everyday topics. Each was checked against the Nunavut Legislation website (Consolidated Law of Nunavut) - Legislation Division, Department of Justice, Government of Nunavut; official consolidations are published by authority of the Nunavut Territorial Printer under the Legislation Act; the check is recorded in each subject's `registers/source-bundle/*.meta.json`. No statute text has been deposited and nothing is encoded yet.

**Licence category: unclear.** **Metadata only until resolved.** No human has recorded a licence decision for this jurisdiction in `tools/topics/round3/licence-decisions.json`. The register's own terms, quoted above, are the evidence to decide on.

## Register access

Fetched over plain HTTPS with an honest, self-identifying user agent ('canon-legislation-indexer/1.0 (legislation metadata indexing; contact address removed)'). The site is Drupal 10 behind Cloudflare. About 37 requests in total, spaced roughly 2 seconds apart, and all but one returned 200: the home page, the 'Welcome to the Nunavut Legislation website' page (which carries the reproduction terms), the 'Current Consolidated Statutes and Regulations' index and its per-letter pages (?title=C,E,H,I,L,N,O,P,Q,R,S,U), the per-Act pages for the nine Acts below, five 'Notice under subsection 65(2) of the Legislation Act' PDFs, and the consolidation PDFs themselves (served from /en/file-download/download/public/<id>). ONE BLOCK to record: the letter-W page, https://www.nunavutlegislation.ca/en/consolidated-law/current?title=W, returned a Cloudflare interstitial ('Just a moment...', noindex/nofollow, Turnstile challenge) instead of the listing. I did not retry it and did not attempt to solve the challenge; nothing in my final selection depends on a W-letter Act (the Workers' Compensation Act was therefore not checked). Everything else was reachable. The register gives, for each Act, an English page plus Francais and, for some Acts, Inuktitut and Inuinnaqtun versions; the consolidation PDF's cover page prints the C.S.Nu. chapter citation and the consolidation date, and where the consolidation has been overtaken the Act's page carries a red 'Note: This consolidation is not up to date. See subsequent amendments: ...' with links to the amending chapters. CanLII was not used (it refuses this network).

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Passenger compensation**: Federal, not territorial. Air carriage and passenger compensation are governed by the Canada Transportation Act, S.C. 1996, c. 10 and the Air Passenger Protection Regulations, SOR/2019-150, administered by the Canadian Transportation Agency. This bites harder in Nunavut than anywhere else in Canada - every community is fly-in - but it remains entirely federal; the register's Public Highways Act and Motor Vehicles legislation do not touch compensation for delayed or denied carriage.
- **Customs/duties**: Federal and exclusive. Constitution Act, 1867, s. 91(2)-(3); Customs Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (2nd Supp.) and Customs Tariff, S.C. 1997, c. 36. A territory cannot legislate customs duties.
- **Work permits/immigration**: Federal. Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, S.C. 2001, c. 27 and the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations, SOR/2002-227 govern entry, status and work authorisation. Nunavut has no territorial immigration statute and, unlike the other territories, has historically not run a provincial/territorial nominee program of its own.

## Update 2026-10-24: not sourced (licence and robots.txt)

The tax and benefits sample was approached on 2026-10-24: the Income Tax Act and the Income Assistance Act with its regulations. **Nothing was deposited, for two reasons. Either one alone would be enough.**

1. **The terms are "personal use" terms, which go to Michael.** The register's terms, recorded on 2026-09-23 from <https://www.nunavutlegislation.ca/en/welcome-nunavut-legislation-website> and quoted in each subject's `SOURCE-LICENSE.md`, say that the statutes and regulations "may be copied freely for personal use". They continue: "There is no requirement to seek permission and there are no fees to be paid for reproductions of the statutes and regulations for personal use. The electronic versions of the statutes and regulations may not be copied for the purpose of resale in this or any other form without the written consent of the Territorial Printer." Personal-use terms are excluded from both owner rulings. The 2026-10-20 ruling (that Commons counts as non-commercial) and the 2026-10-24 ruling (that texts whose terms permit reproduction but are silent on adaptation may be quoted) do not cover them. Written consent from the Territorial Printer (Territorial.Printer@gov.nu.ca) or a decision by Michael is needed first.
2. **robots.txt now excludes AI agents.** `https://www.nunavutlegislation.ca/robots.txt`, read 2026-10-24 (HTTP 200, behind Cloudflare), starts with a group headed "Block AI Training Bots, AI Search Indexers, and SEO Scrapers". That group gives `Disallow: /` to, among others, `Claude-User`, `ClaudeBot`, `Claude-Web`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `GPTBot`, `PerplexityBot` and `CCBot`. The `User-agent: *` group disallows only Drupal paths. The fetching session was a Claude agent, so the site's exclusion of `Claude-User` was taken to apply whatever user-agent string the fetcher sent. Using a different string to fall under `*` would be working around a refusal. Nothing beyond robots.txt was fetched, and the terms page was not re-read.

**What it would take:** written consent from the Territorial Printer, or Michael's ruling on the personal-use terms. A human would also need to download the consolidations, since the site excludes Claude agents. Nunavut publishes Acts in English and French (Official Languages Act s 5(1), equally authoritative), and Inuktitut or Inuinnaqtun versions for some. The English version would be deposited and the others noted.

The CRA page "Current year tax rates and income brackets (2026)", deposited in `../nt/income-tax-act/registers/source-bundle/cra-tax-rates-brackets-2026.txt`, prints the 2026 Nunavut brackets. They are 4% to $55,801, 7% to $111,602, 9% to $181,439, and 11.5% above that. The page is not law, and nothing here is encoded from it.
