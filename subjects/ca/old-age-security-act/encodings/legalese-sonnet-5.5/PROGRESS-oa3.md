## Decisions taken (with the provision that licenses each)

- Quote lines are written by a placeholder expander over `srcrefs.py quote`, so every quotation is copied mechanically from the source file.
- No enactment or making date is in lines 1-418 (the text has none); reg 48(2) refers to the day "this section comes into force", which is not stated, and reg 2(2) to the "date of union of Newfoundland with Canada", which is not stated: both are inputs. Reg 22.1(2) names 7 March 1996, carried as a value inside that rule.
- Duties are encoded as BOOLEAN or DATE rules (does the duty arise, by what day, was it met), because on stable 1.6.98 an `#ASSERT` over a DEONTIC value fails even against itself (reg 20(1), 24, 25 and Part II are the provisions concerned).
- Rounding: the keel's `rounded half up to the number of decimal places` is the dropped-digit rule of regs 7, 8(2), 8(3) and 9(2): a decision on one dropped digit is a half-up decision (the tests of `oa3-part1-amounts.l4` assert the 5 edge in each).
- The forks are listed in NOTES.md with the reading taken and the text that licenses it.
- The Schedule is a list of records, one for each country, built by a generator script from the printed table, each agreement cell kept as printed (regulation 22.01 and the Schedule heading).
