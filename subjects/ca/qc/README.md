## Tax and benefits, 24 October 2026: not sourced (blocked, and the terms need permission)

The Taxation Act (RLRQ c. I-3) and the Individual and Family Assistance Act (RLRQ c. A-13.1.1) with its regulation were to be sourced and encoded on 2026-10-24. Nothing was deposited, for two independent reasons:

1. **The register still refuses.** On 2026-10-24 (04:4x UTC) `https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/robots.txt` and `https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/I-3` each answered HTTP 403 with the same 919-byte CloudFront "Request blocked" body, to curl with the User-Agent `legalese-commons-source-fetcher/1.0 (+https://github.com/legalese/commons)`, two requests 3 seconds apart. A 403 is a no: no other client, user agent, proxy or mirror was tried, and no unofficial aggregator was used.
2. **The terms need permission.** The Government of Quebec's copyright page, re-read on 2026-10-24 at https://www.quebec.ca/droit-auteur (HTTP 200; robots.txt allows it), still says the government holds exclusive rights over everything it publishes, "que ces documents soient des textes officiels ou administratifs, des lois et règlements", and: "Il est interdit de reproduire, télécharger, stocker, traduire, adapter, publier ou représenter en public les contenus du gouvernement du Québec sans autorisation préalable." Authorisation is by request form. The ruling of 24 Oct 2026 covers texts whose terms *permit* reproduction and are silent on adaptation; these terms forbid reproduction without prior authorisation, so they go to Michael and nothing is deposited until he decides (or an authorisation is obtained).

So both Quebec subjects stay metadata only. Quebec computes its own taxable income (the Taxation Act is not a tax-on-federal-taxable-income statute like the other provinces'), so a Quebec personal income tax encoding cannot be built from the federal Income Tax Act encoding either.

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Passenger compensation**: Federal matter: compensation for denied boarding, delay, cancellation and lost baggage is set by the Air Passenger Protection Regulations, SOR/2019-150, made under the Canada Transportation Act, S.C. 1996, c. 10; interprovincial rail and bus carriage is federal too. No provincial statute provides a passenger-compensation scheme.
- **Customs/duties**: Federal matter: customs and tariffs are exclusively federal under s. 91(2)-(3) of the Constitution Act, 1867 - the Customs Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (2nd Supp.) and the Customs Tariff, S.C. 1997, c. 36. No provincial statute governs it.
