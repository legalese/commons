## Required notice and attribution

Every deposited text opens with:

    © King's Printer for Ontario, <year>.
    NOT AN OFFICIAL VERSION. This is an unofficial plain-text reproduction, made by legalese commons, of the consolidated law published on e-Laws ...

The year used is the year the instrument was first published (enacted, or filed as a regulation): S.O. 2007, c. 11, Sched. A: 2007, O. Reg. 37/09: 2009. The terms ask for "the year of first publication of the legal materials"; whether a consolidation should instead carry its consolidation year is a point to confirm. Any encoding or projection that quotes the text must keep both the copyright line and the not-official statement.

## Consequence for this subject

Reproduction, including commercial reproduction, is permitted on the conditions above, but the terms do not expressly grant a right to ADAPT. An L4 encoding that quotes the statute is arguably an adaptation. **Confirm before quoting the text in an encoding** -- a human decision recorded here.

**Decided 24 October 2026 (Michael):** Canadian texts whose terms permit reproduction but are silent on adaptation, as the King's Printer for Ontario's terms are, MAY be quoted in L4 encodings in Commons, keeping the attribution the publisher requires and a not-an-official-version notice in the text header and here. Every deposited text keeps its "© King's Printer for Ontario, <year>." line and its NOT AN OFFICIAL VERSION notice, and so does every encoding that quotes it (`encodings/baseline-2026-10/`).

## Language

English is not the only authoritative language. Legislation Act, 2006 (S.O. 2006, c. 21, Sched. F), s. 65: 'The English and French versions of Acts and regulations that are enacted or made in both languages are equally authoritative.' The Acts here, and O. Reg. 134/98 and O. Reg. 222/98, are bilingual; O. Reg. 37/09, R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 874 and O. Reg. 357/96 are made in English only. Only the English text is deposited; e-Laws publishes the French at /laws/loi/<id> and /laws/reglement/<id>.

## Supporting publications: the indexed amounts (deposited 2026-10-24)
