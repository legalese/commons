# Source license

The text of **The Income Tax Act** is published on the [Manitoba Laws (King's Printer for Manitoba / Legislative Counsel)](https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/index.php).

- **Terms**: Crown copyright, Province of Manitoba - and there are two notices that do not say the same thing, which matters here. The Manitoba Laws-specific notice is purpose-limited: copies of all or part of any Act or regulation may be made free and without permission only for study or research, or for use in legal proceedings or for providing legal advice, and copies for any other purpose require the prior written consent of the King's Printer. Adaptation is not permitted under that notice at all. The government's general OpenMB Information and Data Use Licence is far broader - it says most material covered by Crown copyright can be copied, modified, published, translated, adapted, distributed or otherwise used without seeking additional permissions, and is described as similar to CC BY 4.0 - but the legislation register carries its own narrower notice, so for the Acts below the purpose-limited notice is what governs. Recorded as not open on that basis. Whether OpenMB is intended to reach the consolidated statutes is genuinely ambiguous and is worth a written question to copyright@gov.mb.ca (King's Printer for Manitoba, 204-945-3744).
- **Terms checked**: 2026-09-23, at https://manitoba.ca/legal/mb_laws.html
- **From the terms**: "You may, without charge and without requesting permission, make copies of all or part of any Act or regulation for study or research, or for use in legal proceedings or for providing legal advice. You must not make copies for any other purpose without first obtaining the written consent of King's Printer."
- **Reuse and adaptation permitted, per the index check**: no, or not established
- **Attribution required**: No wording is prescribed by the Manitoba Laws notice itself. The OpenMB licence, if it were relied on, prescribes: 'Contains information from the Manitoba government, licensed under the OpenMB Information and Data Use Licence (Manitoba.ca/OpenMB)'.
- **Source**: https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/i010.php?lang=en

## Language

English and French are both official for Manitoba legislation (s. 23 of the Manitoba Act, 1870), and the register says so expressly: 'Only the bilingual version of an Act on the Manitoba Laws website is an official copy of that Act.' The English-only HTML versions linked below are convenience versions; the bilingual PDF is the official one. Some older Acts are still marked '(English version only)' in the consolidation and remain to be re-enacted bilingually.
