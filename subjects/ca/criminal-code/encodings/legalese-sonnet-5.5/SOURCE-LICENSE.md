# Source license

The quoted text in this folder's `.l4` files, and the tables built from it, is taken from `../../registers/source-bundle/R.S.C.-1985-c.-C-46.txt`, a plain-text rendering of the Criminal Code (R.S.C., 1985, c. C-46), current to 2026-09-30, made by a converter script from the Department of Justice Canada's XML.
It is not the official text. Its terms, its attribution wording, the conditions of the grant and its unofficial status are recorded in `../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md`, the subject-level licence note written when the text was deposited. That note is not repeated here.

**Required attribution** (from the subject-level note): Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. **Not an official version:** this is an unofficial reproduction of the Department of Justice Canada consolidation; the Department assumes no responsibility for the accuracy or reliability of any reproduction derived from its legislative material.

What this encoding adds to it:

- Every quotation is a line copied out of the text by script, carrying its line number, so that the accuracy of the quotation can be checked by a reader who holds the text beside the code. `srcrefs.py` re-checks each quotation against the text.
- Nothing in this folder claims to be verified against the official text.
- The encoding itself (the code, the tests, the notes) is Apache-2.0 like the rest of this repository; that does not widen the terms of the quoted text.
