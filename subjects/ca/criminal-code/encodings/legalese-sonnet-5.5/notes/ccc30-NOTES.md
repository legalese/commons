## What is encoded and what is not

The range is **FORM 1 to FORM 23 and their sub-forms** (5.001 to 5.3, 6.1 to 6.4, 7.1, 10.1, 11.1, 16.1): 60 Forms, 953 non-blank lines, 15,934 words; Forms 3, 13 and 14 are shown repealed. The Schedule tariff (items 1 to 29) ends at line 17449 in ccc29's range; Form 24 begins at line 18464 in ccc31's.
A Form is the prescribed wording of a document. Its identification blocks, recitals ("Whereas ..."), signature lines, blank conditions and commands to officers are **prescribed wording** and are carried as `inert (reason 2)`: the rule behind each is in the section that prescribes the Form, which another part owns.
What is **encoded** is what a Form says beyond its wording, to the person it reaches: a duty to attend, appear, preserve, produce or keep; the warnings (an offence, a warrant for arrest, cancellation, detention, forfeiture); the exception that an inaccurate description is not a lawful excuse; the limits on what may be put on an undertaking ($500, 200 km, "reasonable ... and necessary"); how conditions are varied and how long they last; that the person need not accept them and what follows if they do not; what a surety may do; what a keeper is to do and what ends it; the periods (90 and 60 days, "during a period of"); the quoted text of ss 487.0551(1), 487.0552(1) and the definitions of "record" and "therapeutic record"; and the table of sections each Form cites.

## Seat

The seat is a person who is, or may be, accused of an offence under the Criminal Code (the accused), asking on facts they can state what the Forms they are handed or served say: what they must do, what follows if they do not, what may be put on them, how long it lasts, and how to get out of it (assumed, not ruled: Singapore's Penal Code is the model, as the maintainer instructed on 2026-11-09, but this is a plain seat layer; no charge-generator contract).
No question is asked for an official: an officer's command, a keeper's precept and a justice's recital are layer 1 only (Jural relations, below). Forms that are only prescribed wording have no question. Each question is `@export` with `@desc`, takes plain inputs (one per fact the person can state), and calls the layer-1 rule; none restates a rule.
An answer's scope is what the FORM says: where the Form points at a section ("an offence under subsection 145(3)", "a warrant for your arrest (section 512)"), the section itself is another part's, and the merged seat layer answers it there.

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (ccc30)

Every decision function answers a `MAYBE BOOLEAN` (the exceptions: `MAYBE` of a core type where the answer is a standard, a list of sections or a punishment; a `DATE` where the Form fixes a day; the regulative rules, run with `#TRACE`).
- **`JUST TRUE`**: on the facts given the Form says so in its own words ("the person is required to preserve", "the summons may be cancelled", "you may obtain a copy").
- **`JUST FALSE`, a "no"**: the text says no in its own words. In this range that is: an "If" that is unmet ("If you commit an offence", "If you contravene this order", "If you do not comply with this undertaking"), unless the sentence is a "may" that gives a power or liberty (see the next bullet); a bar met ("until they are cancelled or changed", "until (insert date)", "until they are delivered by due course of law"); an "unless" met ("unless you have a lawful excuse", "unless they sooner consent"); a list closed by "only" ("check only those that are reasonable ... and necessary", "You are only required to bring the things"); a cap ("not more than $500", "up to six dates"); a flat negative ("It is not a lawful excuse", "this declaration does not apply", "you may not enter the dwelling-house unless"); a fixed period outside its end ("for 90 days after the day on which the order is made"); a "where"-condition failed ("in the case where charges have been laid", "on being served", "when you receive"). Every such branch carries `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "<the quoted words>"`, and `work/verify-quotes.py` checks that each quoted fragment occurs in the text.
- **`NOTHING`, "not covered"**: the Form lists without closing the list ("a fine, to imprisonment or to both" names three; a fourth consequence is not covered), is silent, or limits a grant or prohibition in its own wording and the case falls outside the limit (a "may" that gives a power or liberty whose "If" is unmet, such as "this summons may be cancelled" and "you may obtain a copy" (F-W); "without lawful excuse", "the person ... who takes the samples", "because you are not ordinarily resident", "as a result, you may be detained", "or other persons"). Every such branch carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)` with <why> one of the six the brief names.
- **A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`.** In particular "without lawful excuse" with a lawful excuse is NOT "no liability"; an instrument the quoted sentence does not list is NOT "no warrant may issue"; a Form that says what is necessary for release (Form 11 item 4) is NOT saying that compliance is enough.
- `REFUSE` does not exist on the stable binary: "not covered" is `NOTHING` or a constructor, asserted with `EQUALS NOTHING`.
- Counts: 110 FALSE-kind and 137 NOTHING-kind comments in the layer-1 modules and the seat (`python3 -I work/verify-quotes.py` also checks that every `JUST FALSE` line carries a FALSE-kind comment, every `NOTHING` branch a NOTHING-kind comment, and every name begins with a Form label).

## Jural relations (ccc30)

One row per provision that creates a relation, from the Form's words (Hohfeld's correlatives: a claim and a duty; a privilege and a no-right; a power and a liability; an immunity and a disability). A Form that only prescribes the wording of a document creates its relation only through the section that prescribes it, and is listed once as `none (prescribed wording)`. Each relation is stated once, in one L4 rule, named neutrally for the Form.
