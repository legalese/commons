### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (ccc9)

Nearly every layer-1 function and every seat question answers a `MAYBE BOOLEAN`; the punishment questions answer a `Punishment` or `MAYBE Punishment`, and the class questions a `Class of offence`.
- **`JUST TRUE`**: on the facts given, the text's words are met. For an offence question: the limbs hold. For a bar ("is barred", "is not admissible"): the bar applies. For a duty or power: the duty or power is engaged.
- **`JUST FALSE`, a "no"**: the text says no in its own words. In this range that means:
  (i) an offence section's "who" condition ("Every one commits an offence who ...") that no limb meets: "this section's offence is not committed on these facts". It says nothing of any other offence: the same facts may be an offence under another section (a marginal reading, below);
  (ii) a flat negative ("is not a compellable witness", "must not be made to a judge ... presiding at any other proceedings", "need not be supported by any affidavit", "An order for costs must not be made", "does not affect");
  (iii) an "unless" or "except" that is met ("Except in accordance with sections 278.12 to 278.19", "shall not be adduced unless the judge ... determines", "unless the judge ... orders"): the bar is lifted;
  (iv) a duty whose "if" gate fails ("If evidence is admitted at trial on the basis of a determination ..., the judge shall instruct the jury"): the duty does not arise on these facts (this is not a finding that the judge may not);
  (v) a bare "must" or "shall" requirement that is not met (form and content, copies, service, reasons, the 60 days): the requirement is not met;
  (vi) a "means" definition ("record means", "therapeutic record means", "weapon means", "third party means") that the facts do not meet;
  (vii) a period the text fixes in its own words ("no later than 30 days after", "at least 60 days before", "until the later of").
  A "no" never means the conduct is lawful, or that a record is producible: it answers only the section's own question.
- **`NOTHING`, "not covered"**: the text does not answer this question on these facts. In this range that means:
  (i) the case is outside the scope the provision limits itself to ("a person who is ordinarily resident in Canada and who is (a) under the age of 16 years ...", "an offence under section 151, 152, 153 ...", "that is in the possession or control of a third party"): a prohibition or grant limited by its own wording, ruling (c) of 2026-11-05 and the status quo of (b);
  (ii) a "may" function (a judge's or prosecutor's power) whose "if" gate is unmet (ruling of 2026-11-12, limit (1)): the text gives the power and says nothing of what happens when the gate fails;
  (iii) a list the text does not close ("includes", subsection 273.1(3) after the list of 273.1(2), the eleven assertions of 278.12(4), "including, for example, the following conditions");
  (iv) the text is silent (no time for the copy to the complainant; two convictions on the same day; two overlapping limbs of the punishment);
  (v) a borrowed answer is itself not covered (section 265, section 2, section 84(1), section 445.01(4)), or "sexual assault" (not defined in the text) is not something the person can state.
- A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`. The seat questions return `NOTHING` unchanged.
- `REFUSE` is not used: "not covered" is `NOTHING`, asserted with `EQUALS NOTHING`.
- Every branch that answers a "no" carries `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "<the quoted words>"`; every branch that answers "not covered" carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)`, with <why> one of silent, list not closed, includes, undefined term, points outside the text, limited by its own wording. `work/lint-kinds.py` checks both mechanically and finds every quoted fragment in lines 5032 to 5568 (116 FALSE-kind and 126 NOTHING-kind comments, 0 problems).
  The helpers of `ccc9-support.l4` carry a `HELPER` tag instead, because they quote no text.
  One line carries a `COMPARISON only` tag instead (`s 273.1(1)`: it compares a borrowed answer with `JUST FALSE` to choose which of two results to give, and returns neither `JUST FALSE` nor `NOTHING` itself); `lint-kinds.py` accepts that tag, the `HELPER` tag and nothing else in place of a kind comment.

## Marginal readings for a lawyer (ccc9)

Two readings are taken as the status quo (the brief, "For a lawyer, not for you to decide"): (a) a failed "if", "who" or "where" counts as the text's no, clause by clause, within the provision that holds it, and a flat negative counts as the text saying no; (b) a prohibition or grant limited by its own wording does not (`NOTHING` outside the limit).

### What the stable binary does not accept

Measured on 1.6.110 (main, e51181d, L4_BIN) and 1.6.109 (main, 4c887e0, L4_MAIN); probes are in `work/probes/`; the keel's list of the same heading (its NOTES.md) holds for this range too and is not repeated.
- **`NOT a OR b` without brackets** is read as `NOT (a OR b)`: an assertion that expects `(NOT a) OR b` fails (probe `p1.l4`: both assertions failed with `eval/assertion-failed`). Every `NOT` in layer 1 that sits inside `AND` or `OR` is bracketed, and the five assertions that exposed it in module 3 were repaired by brackets, not by changing an expected value.
- **`BEFORE` / `AFTER` on two `DATE` values** do not typecheck on the stable binary (probe `p2.l4`: `(which is not a function) to 2 arguments here`); dates are compared with `BELOW`, `ABOVE`, `AT LEAST`, `AT MOST`, or by the day difference (`Day later MINUS Day earlier`).
- **A `DEONTIC` value cannot be the subject of `#ASSERT`** (probe `p3.l4`: `assertion could not be evaluated`). The regulative rules are run by `#TRACE`, and what each trace should return is written in a `-- TRACE-EXPECT: <text>` comment above it; `work/check-traces.py` runs the file on `L4_BIN` and `L4_MAIN` and compares each result with its comment: 148 traces, 148 matched on each (see "What check.sh printed"). `check.sh` does not see a trace outcome (its own last line says so), so this check is the guard for the 148 traces.
- **`AFTER n WITHIN m` windows** are rejected on the stable binary according to the skill's dialect page (`dialects.md`, probes 32 and 33: `You are trying to apply order ... (which is not a function)`); not re-probed here. No rule in this range uses one: where the text gives a start and an end of a period (`s 278.16(7)`, `s 278.25(6)`), a decision function over the dates carries the period and no rule is written (fork F19).
- **A `MAY` rule with a `LEST` clause**: not used. A `MAY` rule is written without `LEST` (the rules `s 278.12(6)`, `s 278.15(2)`, `s 278.2(2)`, `s 278.21(6)`, `s 278.24(2)`), because a power that is not used is not a breach.
- **`IMPORT` from another folder** is not used; the modules import their neighbours in this folder only.
- **A section `GIVEN` shared by several rules** is not used (the keel measured it: the `GIVEN` is the signature of the next rule only).
- **Genitive precedence**: `f facts's \`x\`` is read as `(f facts)'s \`x\``, because the genitive binds more weakly than function application, so a field of an argument is bracketed (`f (facts's \`x\`)`). Found as a type error in module 2 (`s 273.2`) and repaired by brackets (observed while writing, not kept as a probe).
- **A multi-line `OTHERWISE JUST (` expression** did not parse (observed while writing module 3, not kept as a probe); a `WHERE` local and `concat (LIST ...)` carry such values instead.
- Accepted, and not used (the brief forbids them): `REFUSE`, `#ASSERT REFUSED`, `YMD`.
