### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (ccc26)

Every function here that can be silent answers `MAYBE BOOLEAN`, or `MAYBE` of a number, a date or a small type of its own.
`JUST TRUE` is the text's yes.
`JUST FALSE` is the text's no in its own words, and in this part that means one of: a definition that says "means" (an `s 716` definition); a condition the text introduces with "if", "only if", "unless" or "until" on a bar, a duty or the provision's own question; a "where", "when", "upon", "on", "after", "with" or "during" gate on a grant (listed, not decided, see the sweep); a flat negative ("shall not", "does not apply"); a period the text fixes ("ninety days or less"); a list closed with "only".
`NOTHING` is "not covered": the text lists without closing the list, says "includes", leaves a term undefined, is silent, points to a regulation, rate, program or authority outside the text (the provincial minimum wage of s 734(5), the periods of s 732.1(11), whether an offence carries a minimum punishment), or limits a grant in its own wording and the case falls outside the limit (an "if" gate on a grant, a cap such as "up to 240 hours").
A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into false: a sentence the text says nothing about is not a sentence the text forbids.
The seat questions pass the answer through unchanged.

## Seat layer (ccc26)

The seat is the person accused of an offence, who may be found guilty and sentenced.
Each question begins `Under section N,`, takes plain inputs with an `@desc` worded for that person, and calls the layer-1 function of the same name; it restates no rule.
A length of time is asked as three numbers (years, months, days) and a yes or no for "less a day"; imprisonment as a yes or no for a term, a yes or no for life, and a length of time as above; a provision of the Code as a section number and a subsection as printed.
One test decided what is left out of layer 2: a provision is in layer 1 only when its subject is a victim's, a community representative's or a government's own conduct, or an inquiry about victims (s 722(2), s 722(4), s 722(5), s 722(6)(a), s 722(6)(b), s 722(7), s 722.2(1.1), s 722.2(2), s 722.2(3), s 722.2(4), s 725(1.1), s 726.3, s 734.4(3)(a), s 734.4(3)(b)).
What an official must or may do to, for or about the accused is asked: the clerk's copies to the offender (s 721(5), 722.1, 722.2(5)), the endorsements (s 726.11, 726.21, 726.22), the adjournments (s 722(3), 722.2(1.2)) and the court's powers.
There is never a seat for an official as such.

## What the stable binary does not accept (ccc26)

1. **A multi-line `#ASSERT`**: `#ASSERT 1` then `EQUALS 1` on the next line is a parse error (`parse/unexpected-EQUALS`); every assertion is one line. Loud.
