## How the files are made

- Layer-1 and test files are generated from templates in `../../../work/src/*.l4.tpl` by `../../../work/build.py`; the schedule module and its tests come from `../../../work/gen_sched.py` and `gen_sched_tests.py`, the seat layer and its cases from `gen_seat.py` and `gen_seat_cases.py`.
  Edit the template or the generator, never the generated `.l4`.
- `@@Q <row id>[ .. <row id>]@@` in a template writes the verbatim quote lines (`-- src:N | text`) and the `@ref`; `@@R ...@@` writes the `@ref` only.
- Row ids are in `../../../work/rows.tsv` (527 rows, from `coverage.py`; all 741 non-blank lines of the range fall in a row).
- `NOTES.md` is generated by `../../../work/gen_notes.py` from the deposit and the hand-written text in `../../../work/notes_text.py`; edit those, not `NOTES.md`.
- `../../../work/runtest.sh <name-fragment>` builds and runs one module or test file.
