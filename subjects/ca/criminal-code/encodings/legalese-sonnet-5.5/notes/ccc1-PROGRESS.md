## Conventions taken (see NOTES.md fork register for the reading and the other reading)
- Result type of a layer-1 rule: MAYBE BOOLEAN (or MAYBE NUMBER / MAYBE of a core type). JUST FALSE only where the text says no in its own words (means; if/where/when/while/until/unless/except; flat negative); NOTHING elsewhere (scope lead-in, "who" clause, "includes", silent, grant limited by its own wording).
- A "who ..." relative clause or a scope lead-in that fails gives NOTHING; an "if", "where", "while", "until" gate on the effect that fails gives JUST FALSE, except in a function named for a grant ("may") with an "if" gate: NOTHING (ruling of 2026-11-12).
- Defences ("is justified", "is protected", "is excused", "is not guilty"): JUST FALSE when an "if" gate fails, worded "this section gives no defence on these facts" (ruling 2026-11-05 (b)).
- Bars ("no person shall", "may not ... unless", "without the consent"): function named as the bar; JUST TRUE = barred.
- Answers from section 2 and from another part are INPUTS typed MAYBE BOOLEAN, named `s 2 <term>: ...`; NOTHING is carried through.
- Offences named by section number: core `Provision of the Code` (section decimal; subsection string, a paragraph of a section written "(a)").
