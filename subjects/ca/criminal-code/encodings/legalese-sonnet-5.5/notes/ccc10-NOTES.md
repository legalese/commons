### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (ccc10)

Every decision function of ccc10 answers `MAYBE BOOLEAN` (a penalty answers `Punishment`, `MAYBE Punishment` where a defined-term input can be "not covered").
- `JUST TRUE`: on the facts given, the case is within the rule on the text's own words. For an offence: every element of the offence holds. For a bar (a prohibition): the thing is prohibited. For a defence or immunity: the defence or immunity applies.
- `JUST FALSE`, a "no": the text says no in its own words, quoted in the branch's comment `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "<the words>"`. In this row that means: a flat negative ("does not apply", "No person shall be convicted ... if", "shall not be taken into account"); an "unless" that is met; a condition the text introduces with "if", "where", "only if" or "applies only" that fails; a period the text fixes in its own words that was missed. For a defence it is worded "this section gives no defence on these facts" and never "no defence at all".
- `NOTHING`, "not covered": the text is silent, leaves a term undefined, points outside the sources (the s 2, s 2.1, s 84, s 467.1 and s 787 answers), lists without closing the list, or limits a prohibition or grant in its own wording (a named act, a named intent, a named complement, a named place) and the case falls outside the limit. For an offence: an element is missing, which the section does not say anything about (F1). Every such branch carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)`, with <why> one of: silent, list not closed, includes, undefined term, points outside the text, limited by its own wording.
- A caller never turns `NOTHING` into `FALSE`: the seat layer wraps a person's own yes/no with `JUST`, and passes a function's answer on unchanged.
- `REFUSE` is not used (it does not exist on the brief's stable line): "not covered" is `NOTHING`, asserted with `EQUALS NOTHING`, or a named "not covered" constructor.

### What the text turns out to say (numbered)

Each finding was checked against the converter text, by command, on 2026-11-13; the lines are lines of `R.S.C.-1985-c.-C-46.txt`.

1. **Mixed pronouns.** A regex count of `he`, `his`, `him` against `they`, `their`, `them` over lines 5658-5780 (ss 279-286) gives 2 against 17; over lines 5855-5990 (ss 297-317) it gives 31 against 2; lines 6150-6176 (ss 320.101-320.104) use neither. 2. **s 279.04(2) says "complainant", s 279.04(1) says "another person".** Line 5714: "In determining whether an accused exploits a complainant under subsection (1), the Court must consider all the circumstances"; subsection (1) is written for "another person". The encoding reads them as the same person (the one said to be exploited); the function is a duty of the court with a closed enumeration (a) to (i) plus the nature of the relationship (the circumstance is an input, the list is read as non-exclusive: "including").

Lines 5743-5746 (279.1(2.1)): an earlier offence under s 279.1 always counts; one under subsection 279(1) counts only if a firearm was used. The encoding therefore never answers `JUST FALSE` to "is it a defamatory libel" from an immunity: an applied immunity makes the offence functions answer `JUST FALSE` (fork F44), and no immunity answers `NOTHING`.
11. **Four sections carry an explicit "if" that turns an element into a limit** (s 304(1) "if, at the time of the sale, he does not know", s 308(a) to (d), s 310(b) "if the comments are confined to criticism", s 312 to s 315 conditions): they answer `JUST FALSE` ("this section gives no defence on these facts") when the immunity's act is shown and the "if" fails (fork F48).
12. **s 286.2(4) then (5) then (6) form a chain**: the exception (4) is lifted by any of (5)(a) to (e), and (6) makes a commercial enterprise an aggravating factor for sentencing; (5)(e) and (6) use the same words ("a commercial enterprise that offers sexual services for consideration"). The module reads (5)(e) as a limit on the exception and (6) as a duty of the court on conviction.

### What the stable binary does not accept

Measured on 2026-11-13 on 1.6.110 (main, e51181d, 2026-11-11), each a small file run with `l4 run` (the second stable build, 1.6.109, is compared by `check.sh`):
- **`#ASSERT` on a regulative rule's value is an Error**: `#ASSERT <rule> EQUALS FULFILLED` prints `Trying to check equality on types that do not support it` (`eval/unsupported-equality`, Severity Error), so a regulative rule cannot be asserted on the stable binary. Every regulative rule in ccc10 (SHANT, MUST) is therefore run with `#TRACE` and its outcome is stated in a comment, and each duty is also encoded as a decision function over the facts, which is asserted. (`check.sh` does not see a `#TRACE` outcome; the outcomes were read by eye in this run and are stated beside each trace.)
- **`NOT a AND b` without brackets** parses as `NOT (a AND b)` and returns a wrong answer without any diagnostic: found twice by failing assertions in `ccc10-records.l4` (`AND NOT (x) AND NOT (y)` and `NOT a OR b` inside brackets). Every `NOT` in ccc10 is bracketed, `(NOT a) AND b`.
- **A strict `@ref` must cite lines inside the nearest quote run above it** (`srcrefs.py check --strict`): a regulative rule that cites a paragraph run placed after another run is rejected, so each later declaration sits directly below the run it cites.
- `WAITUNTIL 5` in a `#TRACE` (spelled without a space on the stable line) and `SHANT ... WITHIN`-less rules (a prohibition with no deadline stays standing, "MUST NOT") work as the skill describes.
- A section `GIVEN` is not used anywhere (the dialects page: rejected on the stable line with a misleading message).
- **`IF a THEN x` / `ELSE IF b THEN y` / `ELSE z`, with the `ELSE IF` and the final `ELSE` at the same indent as the first `IF`, is a parse error on 1.6.110** (measured 2026-11-13 on a three-line probe: `l4 run` prints an Error with an empty message). It parses when the nested `IF` carries its `THEN` and `ELSE` on following lines indented under it (`ELSE IF b` then `THEN 2` and `ELSE 3` indented deeper than `ELSE`), and a chain of three or more branches is written with `BRANCH`.
