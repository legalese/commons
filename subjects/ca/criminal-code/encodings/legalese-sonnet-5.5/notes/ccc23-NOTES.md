### F-SUBJECT (11 functions)

- What: A rule that is "subject to" another subsection, or that applies "despite" it.
- Reading taken: `NOTHING` where the other subsection's answer is itself `NOTHING` or `JUST FALSE`, so the limit is not turned into a no; otherwise the rule's own answer.
- Other reading: Ignore the "subject to" and answer the rule alone (`JUST TRUE` or `JUST FALSE`).
- Text that licenses it: "subject to subsection (2)", "despite subsection (1)".

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (ccc23)

The answers are `MAYBE BOOLEAN` (`JUST TRUE`, `JUST FALSE`, `NOTHING`), a number, or a small type of this part's own whose constructors are prefixed with the section.

- `JUST TRUE` says the provision gives the person the thing the function is named for. Where the function is named as a bar ("the section bars ...", "is barred from ..."), `JUST TRUE` means the bar applies, and a duty function's `JUST TRUE` means the duty arises on those facts.
- `JUST FALSE` is the text saying no in its own words: a definition that says "means" (an offence, a hospital, a party is outside the definition); a condition the text opens with "if", "where", "when", "until" or "unless" that is not met (the duty or effect does not arise); a limit with "only" (the court "may only order" an assessment if); a stated maximum or period the case exceeds (a Review Board hearing is held within twelve months; the 24- and 36-month extensions stop at those figures); a flat negative ("A party may not compel the attendance of witnesses", "shall not be permitted"). For a function named as a grant ("may ...") `JUST FALSE` is given only for "where/when/upon"-kind gates, closed gates and maxima, and each such function is in the fork register (F-GRANT-WHERE, F-CAP) for a lawyer.
- `NOTHING` is "not covered": the text lists without closing the list ("including"), leaves a term undefined ("significant threat" is defined by 672.5401, but "serious bodily harm"-type judgments are inputs), is silent, points outside this text (the terms of an interprovincial agreement, a regulation, the Interpretation Act), or limits a grant in its own wording and the case falls outside the limit (a Review Board "may extend" to 24 months "if" the accused is represented by counsel and consents, and without counsel the text does not say the Board may not). A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`.
- The not-covered constructors of this part (for example `s 672.54 not covered`, `s 672.851(9) not covered`) say the same thing in a type: the text does not choose.
- In the accused's seat the answer to "may the Review Board extend the time to 24 months" is therefore three-valued: yes on these facts, no (above the maximum), or the text does not say.

7. 672.81(1.3) defines "serious personal injury offence" for subsection (1.2), but 672.64(1) and 672.84 use it by reference to that definition; the encoding takes the definition as one function and each user takes its answer as an input `by s 672.81(1.3)`.
8. 672.54(a): the "where" clause covers both the verdict of not criminally responsible and the opinion that the accused is not a significant threat; `JUST FALSE` answers a missing verdict or a threat, and `NOTHING` the case where the opinion is not stated (an undefined judgment, the input of 672.5401).
9. 672.47 has two extensions on different words: (2) "Where the court is satisfied that there are exceptional circumstances" (a "where" gate, listed under F-GRANT-WHERE) and (5) "If the court is satisfied that there are exceptional circumstances" (an "if" gate: `NOTHING` outside it, F-GRANT-IF); each has a 90-day maximum (counted from the verdict in (2), from the disposition in (5)), and above it the answer is `JUST FALSE` in both.
10. In 672.86 an accused who is not detained in custody is transferred under (2.1) and one detained in custody under (1); the two descriptions are scope limits, so an accused outside both is `NOTHING`, not `JUST FALSE`, and the "where" conditions (the Review Board's recommendation and both Attorneys General's consent) are `JUST FALSE` when unmet (F-GRANT-WHERE).

## What the stable binary does not accept (ccc23)
