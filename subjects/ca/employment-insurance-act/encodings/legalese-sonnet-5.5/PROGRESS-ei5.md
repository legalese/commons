## Build method (so a resumed session can continue)

- The layer-1 modules are written as templates `*.l4.in` in the scratchpad folder `.../scratchpad/ei5-work/tpl/` and expanded into the `.l4` files in this folder by `.../ei5-work/expand.py` (run through `.../ei5-work/build.sh` or `run.sh`).
  The expander inserts the `-- src:N | text` quotation lines and the `@ref` lines by calling `srcrefs.py quote` on the Act's raw file, so quotations are mechanical.
  A directive `@@Q 2351-2368 | s 152.07(1)` becomes those quote lines and `@ref Employment Insurance Act s 152.07(1) (src:2351-2368)`.
- Always edit the template, then rebuild; an edit to a generated `.l4` is lost at the next build.
- The test files `ei5-tests-*.l4` are written directly in this folder.
- The seat `ei5-seat.l4` is joined by `.../ei5-work/seatbuild.sh` from `.../ei5-work/seat-parts/*.part`, and `ei5-cases-seat.l4` by `.../ei5-work/casebuild.sh` from `.../ei5-work/cases-parts/*.part`; edit a part and rebuild.
- `NOTES.md` is assembled by `.../ei5-work/mknotes.py` from `.../ei5-work/coverage.tsv` and `.../ei5-work/notes-parts/`; the Hohfeld table is generated by `.../ei5-work/jural-gen.py` from the coverage table's rule names.
- Probes of the binary use copies of the core in `.../ei5-work/probe/`, never this folder.

## Convention

An ei5 layer-1 module never imports another ei5 module.
Every value that crosses a section boundary (the date a benefit period begins, the maximum benefit period of s 152.11(16), the weeks of s 152.14, the weekly rate) is a plain `GIVEN` with a comment naming the rule that computes it, and the seat layer composes them.
This avoids import cycles (s 152.07(1)(d) needs s 152.11(1); s 152.11(16) needs s 152.14(1); s 152.14(8) needs s 152.11(16)).
A module imports only the keel (`eia-core*`).
