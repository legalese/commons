CHAPTER I-2.01 REG 7 The Income Tax Act, 2000
Title
1  These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Low-income Tax Credit Regulations.
Definition
2  In these regulations, “Act” means The Income Tax Act, 2000.
24 Dec 2025 cI-2.01 Reg 7 s2.
Determination of C re subsection 39(6.3) of the Act
3  For the purposes of determining the variable C in the formula set out in subsection 39(4) of the Act in accordance with subsection 39(6.3) of the Act for the 2026 taxation year, the amount C is equal to 3.029% of the amount, if any, by which the individual’s adjusted income for that taxation year exceeds the amount D calculated in accordance with the following formula:
