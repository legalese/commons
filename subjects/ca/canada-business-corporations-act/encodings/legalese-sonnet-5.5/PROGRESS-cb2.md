## How the work is organised

- The deliverable is this folder.
  All `cb2-*.l4` files here are generated by a macro expander from sources in `inputs/work/src/*.l4.in`, in which a line `%Q N M` becomes the verbatim quote lines `-- src:N | ...`.
  The sources and the helper scripts are outside this folder, under `inputs/work/`; edit the `.l4.in` files and the generators in `inputs/work/gen/`, never the generated `.l4` here.
- `inputs/work/run.sh [modules]` rebuilds and runs the named modules, prints errors, warnings, assertions written and satisfied per module, then runs `srcrefs.py check --refs` and a lint that fails any rule that declares a `GIVEN` its body never reads.
- `cb2-seat.l4` and `cb2-cases-seat.l4` are generated by `inputs/work/gen/seat_build.py` from the compiled signatures of the layer-1 modules, so the seat's inputs and call order cannot drift from the rules.
- NOTES.md is assembled by `inputs/work/assemble_notes.py` from hand-written parts in `inputs/work/notes/` and the coverage and jural rows generated beside them.
