The text of **The Income-tax Act, 2025** is published on the [India Code (Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/).

- **Terms**: No open licence found. India Code publishes no copyright licence; the Wayback copy of its disclaimer.jsp has only an 'as is' disclaimer and an English-prevails clause. The statutory exception is Copyright Act 1957 s.52(1)(q)(ii): reproducing or publishing an Act of a Legislature is not infringement only if it comes with commentary or other original matter. Gazette matter other than Acts is freely reproducible under s.52(1)(q)(i). Bare reproduction and adaptation of Act text is therefore not clearly permitted without that condition.

## Consequence for this subject

Reproduction is permitted on the conditions above, but the terms do not expressly grant a right to ADAPT. An L4 encoding that quotes the statute is arguably an adaptation. **Confirm before depositing text or quoting it in an encoding** -- a human decision recorded here.

## Language

English is authoritative. Under Article 348(1)(b) of the Constitution, the authoritative texts of Acts of Parliament are in English. Under the Authoritative Texts (Central Laws) Act 1973 and s.5 of the Official Languages Act 1963, Hindi translations published under the President's authority are deemed authoritative Hindi texts. Constitutionally, though, the English text is the authoritative one. India Code's disclaimer (Wayback copy of disclaimer.jsp) says that in any discrepancy 'the English version of the Acts shall prevail'. Article 348 itself was cited from knowledge and not re-fetched this run.

**The statutory position.** India publishes no open licence for Gazette matter or Acts. Under the Copyright Act 1957, s.52(1)(q)(i), reproducing or publishing "any matter which has been published in any Official Gazette except an Act of a Legislature" does not infringe copyright. Under s.52(1)(q)(ii), an Act of a Legislature may be reproduced or published only "together with any commentary thereon or any other original matter". These provisions are cited from the 2026-09-22 record (made from India Code's PDF, via the Wayback Machine) and from knowledge; they were not fetched again on 2026-10-19, because India Code refuses access.

**The rule applied.**

- **Deposited:** Gazette matter other than Acts, which s.52(1)(q)(i) clearly permits. These are the Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026 and the two notifications under Schedule I to the National Food Security Act.
- **Not deposited:** Acts of Parliament, because reproducing them is conditional rather than clearly permitted. They are converted and staged outside the repository, and their meta files record the checksums. Whether a deposit that sits beside commentary and encodings meets the s.52(1)(q)(ii) condition is a **human decision**.
- **Attribution:** none is required. The Gazette source is acknowledged at the top of each text.
