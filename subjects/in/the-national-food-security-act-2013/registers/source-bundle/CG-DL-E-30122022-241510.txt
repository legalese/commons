MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION
(Department of Food and Public Distribution)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 30th December, 2022
S.O. 6148(E).—In pursuance of the provisions of Schedule I to the National Food Security Act, 2013 (20 of 2013), the Central Government hereby decides that the rice, wheat and coarse grains shall be provided free of cost for all eligible households under section 3 of the National Food Security Act, 2013, for the period 1st of January, 2023 to 31st of December, 2023.
Provided that the prices of foodgrains for benefits under section 4, 5 & 6 of the National Food Security Act, 2013 shall continue as per notification S.O. 3546(E) dated 30th September, 2019, until further orders.
[F.No. 15-33/2022-NFSA]
MAMTA SHANKAR, Sr. Economic Adviser
Note:
The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number S.O. 3577(E), dated the 29th November, 2016 and subsequently amended vide numbers S.O. 1003(E), dated the 30th March, 2017, S.O. 2041(E), dated the 29th June, 2017, S.O. 3127(E) dated the 27th June, 2018 and S.O. 3546(E) dated the 30th September, 2019.
