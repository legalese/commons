NO. S 776

                  CRIMINAL PROCEDURE CODE 2010
                         (ACT 15 OF 2010)

                 CRIMINAL PROCEDURE CODE
              (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2010

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2) of the Criminal
Procedure Code 2010, the Minister for Law hereby makes the following
Notification:

  1. This Notification may be cited as the Criminal Procedure Code
(Commencement) Notification 2010.

  2. The Criminal Procedure Code 2010 shall come into operation on
2nd January 2011.

   Made this 20th day of December 2010.
                                                                   CHAN LAI FUNG
                                                                   Permanent Secretary,
                                                                     Ministry of Law,
                                                                       Singapore.
