NO. S 496

        SINGAPORE BROADCASTING AUTHORITY ACT
                     (CHAPTER 297)

            SINGAPORE BROADCASTING AUTHORITY
                (CLASS LICENCE) (AMENDMENT)
                      NOTIFICATION 2001

  In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 21 and 23 of the
Singapore Broadcasting Authority Act, the Singapore Broadcasting
Authority hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Singapore Broadcasting
Authority (Class Licence) (Amendment) Notification 2001 and
shall come into operation on 10th October 2001.

Amendment of Schedule
  2. The Schedule to the Singapore Broadcasting Authority
(Class Licence) Notification (N 1) is amended —
     (a) by inserting, immediately after paragraph 2, the following
         paragraph:
                 ‘‘2A.—(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), an Internet Content
               Provider who is or is determined by the Authority to be a political
               party registered in Singapore providing any programme on the
               World Wide Web through the Internet shall —
                     (a) in the case of an Internet Content Provider who was
                         registered with the Authority under the provisions of this
                         Notification in force before 10th October 2001, re-register
                         with the Authority within 14 days of that date; or
                    (b) in any other case, register with the Authority within 14 days
                        of the commencement of its service.
                  (2) Notwithstanding sub-paragraph (1), the Authority may permit
               the Internet Content Provider to register or re-register, as the case
               may be, with the Authority within such longer time as the Authority
               may permit.’’;

      (b) by deleting sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph 3;
S 496/2001                              2

    (c) by deleting paragraph 5 and substituting the following
        paragraph:
                ‘‘5. An Internet Content Provider who is required to register or
             re-register, as the case may be, under paragraph 2A, 3 or 4 shall —
                  (a) register or re-register in such form and manner as the
                      Authority may determine; and
                  (b) provide the Authority with such particulars and undertakings
                      as the Authority may require in connection with the
                      provision of the Internet Content Provider’s service.’’; and

    (d) by deleting the words ‘‘register with the Authority under
        paragraph 2, 3 or 4’’ in paragraph 6 and substituting the
        words ‘‘register or re-register, as the case may be, under
        paragraph 2, 2A, 3 or 4’’.

  Made this 5th day of October 2001.


                                            NIAM CHIANG MENG
                                                   Chairman,
                                        Singapore Broadcasting Authority.
