The quoted text of the **Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001** (Singapore), 2020 Revised Edition, is reproduced in the `-- src:N |` lines of the `crtpa-*.l4` modules and in the case comments.
It comes from the text file `CRTPA2001.txt`, a plain-text rendering made by a converter script from Singapore Statutes Online (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CRTPA2001, retrieved 2026-10-01).
It is **not** the official text, and nothing in this encoding is verified against the official text.

The terms the quoted text carries are the subject-level note written when the text was deposited (`STUB-SOURCE-LICENSE.md` in the encoder's inputs; the subject's own `SOURCE-LICENSE.md` once filed).
That note is not repeated here.
It records that the text is not under an open licence, that reproduction rests on the standing grant in the SSO Terms of Use clause 13 with its attribution and no-endorsement conditions, and that SSO reproduces an unofficial consolidation.
I did not find any other grant, and I make none.
