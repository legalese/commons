The terms on which the quoted text of the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 (Singapore) may be reproduced are in the subject-level note written when the text was deposited, `STUB-SOURCE-LICENSE.md`; this file points at it and does not repeat it.

What this encoding quotes: `-- src:N-M | text` comment lines in the `.l4` modules, copied mechanically from `UCTA1977.txt`, and short phrases of the Act in comments and in the names of functions and inputs.

What the quoted text is: a plain-text rendering, made by a converter script, of the consolidation published on Singapore Statutes Online (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/UCTA1977, retrieved 2026-10-01).
It is not the official text, and nothing in this encoding is verified against the official text.
The attribution notice that note requires applies to the quoted text.

The licence of the encoding itself (the `.l4`, `NOTES.md` and the scripts) is Apache-2.0, as stated in `encoding.json`.
