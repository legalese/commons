NO. S 212

        SINGAPORE BROADCASTING AUTHORITY ACT
                     (CHAPTER 297)

            SINGAPORE BROADCASTING AUTHORITY
               (BROADCASTING AND TELEVISION)
                (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 1999

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 79 of the Singapore
Broadcasting Authority Act, the Singapore Broadcasting Authority,
with the approval of the Minister for Information and the Arts,
hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
  1. These Regulations may be cited as the Singapore Broadcasting
Authority (Broadcasting and Television) (Amendment) Regulations
1999 and shall come into operation on 14th May 1999.

Amendment of regulation 8
  2. Regulation 8 of the Singapore Broadcasting Authority
(Broadcasting and Television) Regulations (Rg 1) is amended by
deleting the words ‘‘, grant an exemption from’’ and substituting the
words ‘‘and subject to such conditions as it may think fit, waive or
reduce’’.
