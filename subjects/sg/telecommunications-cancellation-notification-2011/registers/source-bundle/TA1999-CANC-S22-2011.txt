                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

                            TELECOMMUNICATIONS
                               (CANCELLATION)
                              NOTIFICATION 2011

                        ARRANGEMENT OF PARAGRAPHS

Paragraph
    1.   Citation and commencement
    2.   Cancellation



  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(5) of the
Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Development
Authority of Singapore hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Cancellation) Notification 2011 and shall come into operation on
21st January 2011.

Cancellation
   2. The following Notifications are cancelled:
      (a) the Telecommunications (Exemption from Telecom
          Competition Code — 1-800/1-900 Number Portability)
          Notification 2005 (G.N. No. 407/2005);
      (b) the Telecommunications (Exemption from Telecom
          Competition Code — Service Quality Information Disclosure
          Requirements) Notification 2005 (G.N. No. 409/2005);
      (c) the Telecommunications (Exemption from Telecom
          Competition Code — Co-Location at Bukit Timah Exchange)
          Notification 2005 (G.N. No. 410/2005);
