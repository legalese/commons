## Method
- Layer-1 modules are generated from `work/src/*.l4.tpl` by `work/gen.py`; quotes and `@ref` locators are copied from `inputs/CoA1967.txt` by the script, never typed. Edit the template, then run `python3 gen.py`; never edit the generated `.l4`.
- `NOTES.md` is generated by `work/gennotes.py` from the layer-1 modules, the raw text and three hand-written files (`work/notes_forks.py`, `work/notes_jural.txt`, `work/notes_prose.py`); the coverage table by `work/coverage.py`. The script stops on a fork tag with no register entry (or the reverse), a subsection without a jural row, a grant-named function without a gate class, a unit without a locator, or a regulative rule whose breach path disagrees with its traces.
- `work/run.sh` counts assertions, `work/chk.sh` runs `l4 check`, `srcrefs --strict` and the quote checker, `work/traces.py` compares `-- EXPECT` comments with trace output, `work/checknotes.py` checks quotations in NOTES.md.
- The work folder is not shipped.
