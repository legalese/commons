## Decisions taken (and the text that licenses each)
- A duty is encoded as a function named "... is in default of the duty ...": JUST TRUE = default, JUST FALSE = no default on these facts (the provision's own question, fork F-D1); plus a regulative rule. "Immediately" and "at the meeting" carry no number: no deadline invented, rule listed under "Rules with no breach path".
- "within N days after X": a duty uses `period: the date is not later than the end of a period after` / the last day (an early act is in time); the keel's `within a period after` excludes the day of the event (P2) and would call an act on the day itself late (fork F-P2).
- "within 14 days beginning on the date ...": the date counts as day 1, so the last day is the date plus 13 (205AA(3), 205AC(2), 205AD(3), 205AD(4)) (fork F-P3).
- A "may" with a "where" gate fails -> JUST FALSE (periods ruling (b), left as the guide gives, fork F-W1); a "may" with an "if"/"while" gate fails -> NOTHING (grant rule 2026-11-12, fork F-G1); a grant limited by a named complement (3/4 majority, "in writing") fails -> NOTHING; a stated period -> JUST FALSE (periods ruling (a), fork F-P1).
- Thirteenth Schedule: "any 2 of 3 criteria for each of the 2 preceding years" read per year (reading A); the same-two-criteria reading is also coded, labelled "other reading". Ceasing (paras 5, 10): fails in BOTH preceding years (reading C1), the either-year reading also coded. A company's/group's small status carries the previous year's status as an input (para 13 "until it ceases").
- No section GIVEN, no REFUSE, no TYPICALLY, no EVERY; the stable build only.
- Scope limit with no "only" (a relative clause such as "a company which is exempt ... are exempt"; "a company that is a small company ... is exempt"): outside the scope NOTHING (periods ruling (c), fork F-S4). 205B(1), which the text introduces with "if", answers JUST FALSE (marginal reading (a), fork F-M1).
- An "entitled" bar ("the company is not entitled", 205B(7), 205C(2) as applied) is named for the bar: JUST TRUE where the notice was given, JUST FALSE where no notice (a "where" gate, periods ruling (b)); counted in fork F-N1 with the other reading NOTHING.
- An "unless" once met (205C(3), (4)): NOTHING for "subsection (1) applies" (fork F-U1). "before the next annual general meeting": the last day is the day before the meeting (fork F-P4).
