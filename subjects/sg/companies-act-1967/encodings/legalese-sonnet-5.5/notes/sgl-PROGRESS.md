## Method (how the files are made)
- Sources of the .l4 files are templates `work/src/NAME.l4t` (outside the deposit); `python3 work/build.py NAME` expands `{{SRC cite | N-M}}` into verbatim `-- src:N | text` lines plus an exact `@ref cite (src:a-b)`, `{{REF cite | N-M}}` into an `@ref` only. The text is read from inputs/CoA1967.txt, never retyped.
- `python3 work/run.py FILE.l4` runs the stable build 1.6.110 and prints unique errors/warnings and assertion counts.
- `python3 work/traces.py sgl-tests-*.l4` compares every `#TRACE` with its `-- EXPECT` line on both stable builds.
- NOTES.md is generated by `python3 work/notes_gen.py` from `work/notes/*.md` and from the files themselves (counts, tables, the check log); it refuses to run if a `.l4` file is newer than `work/check-full.log`.
- Policy for FALSE/NOTHING is in work/policy.md and in NOTES.md ("Reading a result").
- `srcrefs.py check --strict`: a locator is checked against the quote runs above it in its own block, so a regulative rule after a function or a `§` heading needs its own `{{SRC}}`.
