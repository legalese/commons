# Phase 3, type (ii): a "may" (or a relief or a power) gated by "where", "when", "upon", "on application" and the like

Reader group 3. Propose only: no `.l4` file, `ANSWERS.md`, `triage.md` or `tests-independent.l4` was edited.
Sources: the five agents' own "Split sites" sections (`phase3/g3-split-sites.md`), the text of `inputs/CoA1967.txt` (line numbers below are lines of that file), and my own `ANSWERS.md`, `tests-independent.l4` and `triage.md`.

How I read the text. The text alone never says "may not" outside a gate at any of these sites, so the question at each row is which convention applies to a power or relief conferred in stated circumstances.
My reading column gives my answer for the gate the agent listed, with the words that lead me to it.
I use these four lines of the Guide, quoted as the agents quote them:
- **Line 87:** the answer is `NOTHING` where the text "is silent ... or limits a grant or prohibition in its own wording and the case falls outside the limit".
- **Line 93 (a) and (b):** "(a) a period the text fixes in its own words ...: outside the period the answer is `JUST FALSE`; (b) a "where ..." condition: when it fails the answer is `JUST FALSE`".
- **Line 94 (c):** ""when", "upon" and "whenever" count as "where" under rule (b)".
- **Lines 97 and 99 (grant ruling, 2026-11-12, "not confirmed by the maintainer"):** limit 1, "answer `NOTHING` ... only for a function named for a grant or a power ... whose `if` gate failed"; limit 3, "do not change, but list ... a "may" function whose gate is "where", "when" or "upon"".

DECIDED means the text's own words close the gate (a period or date it fixes, an "unless" that withdraws the power, "only", "not otherwise").
OPEN means the text reads both ways and the Guide's two lines (93 (b) / 94 (c) against 87 / 97) conflict; the Guide itself parks these in limit 3.

Column 7 gives my scenario number in `ANSWERS.md` and whether the assertion passed; "none" means I wrote no assertion for the function at that site.
"Gate-fails case" means the case in which the listed gate is not met.

## G. Closing

Of these, **3 are DECIDED** (D5 s215(1) Court, D7 s215H(1), E3 s362(3)) and **25 are OPEN**.

In all, 36 rows: 3 DECIDED, 33 OPEN.
Inside OPEN rows, the period or date limbs (A2, D4, D6, X1, X4 to X6) are DECIDED by the text's own words and the encodings already agree with me there; only the "Where", "on application" or "unless" limb is open.

**Proposed changes for DECIDED sites where the agent differs from the text: none.**
At all three DECIDED sites the agent's answer is the one I read from the text: D5 JUST FALSE where no timely application is made; D7 JUST FALSE for an application after the effective date; E3 JUST FALSE before the time in the notice has expired or where cause to the contrary has been shown.
One consequence for my own files, not a change to the encoding: my assertion #235 (D7) expects NOTHING for a late application and is out of line with my reading of "at any time before the date on which the amalgamation becomes effective"; I would move triage 235 from AMBIGUOUS to SCENARIO (the assertion stays as written).

**The OPEN rows: where I lean and what the other reading costs.**
I lean NOTHING for a pure "where", "upon", "on application" or consent gate on a power or relief, and JUST FALSE for a period or date the text fixes and for an "unless" that withdraws the power.
My leans: NOTHING at A1, A2 ("Where" limb), A3, A5, A6, A7, D1, D2, D3, D4 ("Where" limb), D6 ("Where" limb), F1, E1, E2, E4, E5, G1 to G7 and X1 to X8 (except the period limbs); JUST FALSE at A4 and F2 (not grants), and at the DECIDED D5, D7 and E3.
The agents answer JUST FALSE at 26 of the 28 listed sites and NOTHING at 2 (A6, A7), so my leans differ from the encoded answers at 21 sites (A1, A2, A3, A5, D1, D2, D3, D4, D6, F1, E1, E2, E4, E5, G1 to G7) and agree at 7 (A4, A6, A7, F2, D5, D7, E3).
- **Why NOTHING:** a power conferred "where" or "on application of" a person states when it exists; it does not say it is withheld elsewhere, and the text sometimes says the opposite (s210(10) "in addition to any of its powers"; s205(8) gives the Registrar another route to the same appointment as s205(10)). Line 87 treats silence as `NOTHING`, and the grant ruling of 2026-11-12 was written for exactly these "may" functions.
- **Cost of NOTHING:** it gives up the clean "no" that lines 93 (b) and 94 (c), both typed by the maintainer on 2026-11-05, give for "where", "when" and "upon"; limit 3 of the grant ruling says "do not change" these sites, so switching 21 branches in five modules goes against the Guide's own instruction to leave them; the user of the answer loses a "no" for cases a lawyer would also call no.
