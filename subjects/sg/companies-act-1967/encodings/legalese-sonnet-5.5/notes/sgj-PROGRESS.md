## Workflow
Layer-1 and test sources are `work/src/<name>.in.l4` (macros `@@Q a b@@`, `@@QR label a b@@`, `@@REF label a b@@` quote the text mechanically); `python3 work/build.py [name...]` writes the deposit `.l4`.
The seat and its cases are generated by `work/mkseat.py` (the spec and every expected value are in that file, written from the text).
`work/coverage.py` builds the coverage table; `work/mkmarginal.py` extracts the tagged branches and rules; `work/mknotes.py` assembles NOTES.md.
Checks: `work/summ.py FILE` (assertions satisfied or not), `work/traces.py FILE` (every `#TRACE` against its `-- EXPECT` word on 1.6.110 and 1.6.109), `work/mutate.sh` (mutation).
