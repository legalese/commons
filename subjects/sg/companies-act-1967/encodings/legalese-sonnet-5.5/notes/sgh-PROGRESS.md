## How the files are built
- Templates in `RUN/work/src/sgh-*.l4.tpl` (RUN = the directory above deposit/); `python3 -I RUN/work/gen.py` expands `@@R <label> | a-b[, c-d]` into quote lines (`-- src:N | text`, furniture and pure amendment-mark lines skipped) plus the `@ref` line, and writes the `.l4` into the deposit folder.
  Edit the template, never the generated `.l4` of a module with a template.
- `work/genseat.py` writes `sgh-seat.l4` and `sgh-cases-seat.l4` from `work/seatspec.py` (the 206 questions), the layer-1 function list (`work/listfn.py`) and the `@@F` / `@@T` lines of the test templates.
- `work/coverage.py` (with `work/units.py` and `work/coverage-overrides.tsv`) partitions the ranges into rows and checks that every non-blank line is in exactly one row.
- `work/checkquotes.py` checks every `-- FALSE-kind` and `-- NOTHING-kind` quote against the text.
- `work/traces.py FILE...` runs each test file on both builds and compares every `#TRACE` with the `-- EXPECT` comment above it.
- `work/mknotes.py` writes NOTES.md from the encoding, the coverage files, `work/check.log` (the output of check.sh) and the hand-written `work/notes_text.py` and `work/notes_jural.py`.
- Run order for a full rebuild: gen.py, listfn.py, genseat.py, coverage.py, check.sh > work/check.log, mknotes.py.

## Conventions taken (each has a fork line in NOTES.md)
- "where"/"when"/"upon"/"whenever" gate failing: JUST FALSE (Guide: periods ruling (b), (c)); on a "may" it is split site (ii) (fork F-H-S1).
- "if" gate on a function NAMED for a grant or a power failing: NOTHING (grant ruling 2026-11-12, limit 1); "on payment", "on application", "with the consent of": NOTHING (fork F-H-S3).
- A duty or a bar named as such: gate unmet gives JUST FALSE (the provision's own question, limit 2); a scope noun phrase absent gives NOTHING.
- An offence function: element unmet gives JUST FALSE (split site (i), fork F-H-S2).
  A penalty function returns `Penalty OF fine, months, bothAllowed, defaultPenalty`, or NOTHING.
- "within N days after X" in a duty or a right: X itself is in time (fork F-H-P2).
- A MAY is never encoded as a regulative rule: on both builds a MAY with a LEST lapses to FULFILLED, never BREACH (probe `work/scratch/probes/p9.l4`); permissions are decision functions.
- Fork ids F-H-D1 to F-H-D4 mark single sites (s 12(1), s 13(1), s 19(2), s 21(4)(b) with (4B)).
