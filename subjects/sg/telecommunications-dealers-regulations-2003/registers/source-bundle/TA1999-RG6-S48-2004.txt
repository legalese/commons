NO. S 48

                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

                           TELECOMMUNICATIONS
                          (DEALERS) (AMENDMENT)
                             REGULATIONS 2004

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 74 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Development
Authority of Singapore, with the approval of the Minister for
Information, Communications and the Arts, hereby makes the
following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
  1. These Regulations may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Dealers) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into
operation on 3rd February 2004.

Amendment of regulation 9
  2. Regulation 9 of the Telecommunications (Dealers) Regulations
2003 (G.N. No. S 185/2003) is amended by deleting ‘‘$400’’ and
substituting ‘‘$250’’.

Amendment of regulation 10
  3. Regulation 10 (1) of the Telecommunications (Dealers)
Regulations 2003 is amended by deleting ‘‘$400’’ in sub-paragraphs (a)
and (b) and substituting in each case ‘‘$250’’.

   Made this 29th day of January 2004.
                                                             LAM CHUAN LEONG
                                                                    Chairman,
                                                               Info-communications
                                                             Development Authority of
                                                                    Singapore.
