# Cradle to grave, abridged — Jun Hao in twenty beats

The same life as [`SCENARIO.md`](SCENARIO.md), cut to two beats a chapter. Each is a scene: where he is, how old he looks, what is asked, and what the law in this repository says back. The beats are flagged `"abridged": true` in [`facts.json`](facts.json), which also carries his age at every event.

Where Simone's beats had the voice stop because it lacked the law, his have it stop because a person must decide: the Youth Court, the Official Assignee, the exclusion Committee, the doctor with the percentage, the assessor with the activities of daily living. The rule is there every time. What is missing is a human, and the voice says so.

## Chapter 0 — Born (2006)

**Beat 1 · 30 August 2006 · KKH.** The same ward where Simone was born three years before. Boon Keng is at the counter again, two weeks faster this time.

- *Citizen?* — Constitution Art 121. `ENC`. *By when?* — Registration of Births and Deaths Act ss 7, 8. `ENC`.

**Beat 2 · October 2006 · the kitchen table.** A second child paid better than a first, and an account was opened for him.

- *What did a 2006 second child get?* — the Child Development Co-Savings Act's Scheme, `ENC`; the rate, which the child-support row refuses to state for a birth before 2015, by design. `PART`. The first honest stop of his life happens before he can talk.

## Chapter 1 — Primary school (2013–2018)

**Beat 3 · 20 May 2016 · the Ministry's letter.** Thirty-one days absent in Primary 4. The letter is addressed to his parents.

- *Whose duty, whose offence?* — Compulsory Education Act s 3: the child must attend; each parent commits the offence if he does not; the Board may direct first. `ENC`. Nothing follows the letter but the letter.

**Beat 4 · October 2018 · a results slip.** Posted to Normal (Technical).

- *Who decides the stream?* — a policy, not a law. `ADMIN-POLICY`.

## Chapter 2 — The mall and the Youth Court (2019–2022)

**Beat 5 · 20 November 2021 · a mall at 9 p.m.** Fifteen, two friends, a phone case and earbuds, and a security officer at the door.

- *What is this?* — Penal Code ss 378-379, and s 380 because a shop is a building for the custody of property. `ENC`. *Arrestable?* — Criminal Procedure Code First Schedule. `ENC`. *Which court?* — the Youth Court, Children and Young Persons Act Part 3: the row stops at the door. `PART`.

**Beat 6 · 14 February 2022 · the Youth Court.** A probation order for twelve months, community service, no conviction recorded. The voice could say what probation is; it could not say whether he would get it. A magistrate decided.

- *What does probation do to the conviction?* — Probation of Offenders Act s 11. `ENC`. *And if he breaks it?* — s 7. `ENC`.

## Chapter 3 — ITE, a vape, and national service (2023–2027)

**Beat 7 · 22 September 2025 · a camp gate.** Two days late from leave.

- *Is this absence without leave?* — Singapore Armed Forces Act s 22. `ENC`. A summary trial; seven days confined to camp.

**Beat 8 · 20 November 2026 · a bunk.** Five years to the day since the mall. The voice tells him before he asks.

- *Is it spent?* — Registration of Criminals Act s 7A: yes. *What may I say about it?* — s 7E: nothing, and nobody may hold it against you. `ENC`.

## Chapter 4 — The platform years (2027–2032)

**Beat 9 · 6 March 2027 · a power-assisted bicycle.** Thirty-three days after ORD, a delivery bag on his back.

- *Am I a worker or a business?* — Platform Workers Act ss 3-6: a platform worker, and the app is a platform operator. `ENC`. *Does it pay my CPF?* — Part 3 of the same Act, outside the row. `PART`. The voice says which half it holds.

**Beat 10 · 12 April 2029 · a car door.** A fractured wrist, six weeks off, a cast.

- *Am I covered?* — Work Injury Compensation Act ss 34A-34P, the platform-worker Part. `ENC`. *How much?* — medical leave wages, s 14; the lump sum for a three per cent permanent incapacity from the Table. `ENC`. The three per cent is the doctor's.

## Chapter 5 — Cheryl, Rui En, and a marriage that came after (2032–2033)

**Beat 11 · 22 January 2033 · KKH, the other side of the curtain.** A daughter. Her parents are not married.

- *Is she a citizen?* — Art 121: yes. `ENC`. *Is she legitimate?* — Legitimacy Act s 2: not yet. `ENC`. *May I be on the certificate?* — the provision on a father's particulars outside marriage, outside the row. `PART`. *Do I get paternity leave?* — CDCSA ss 12H(4), 12I: not while unmarried. `ENC`.

**Beat 12 · 16 September 2033 · the Registry.** Eight months later, with Rui En present.

- *May we marry?* — Women's Charter ss 9, 11, 22. `ENC`. *What does it do for her?* — Legitimacy Act s 3: she is legitimated; the Baby Gift and the account follow; his four weeks of leave are still inside the year. `ENC`.

## Chapter 6 — The security job, the claim, and the debts (2034–2037)

**Beat 13 · 10 March 2036 · a tribunal waiting room.** Thirteen months of overtime paid at the basic rate.

- *Must I mediate first?* — Employment Claims Act s 3: yes, and within the time limits. *What is owed?* — Employment Act s 38(4): one and a half times, $4,100. *What can the tribunal order?* — ss 12-16, 21-24. All `ENC`. The order is paid.

**Beat 14 · 20 April 2037 · the Official Assignee's office.** A creditor has filed for bankruptcy over $41,000.

- *Is there another way?* — Insolvency, Restructuring and Dissolution Act ss 289-291: the Debt Repayment Scheme, if the Official Assignee finds him suitable. `ENC`. The voice lists what the Assignee must consider and stops: the finding is his. He is found suitable. Five years at $690.

## Chapter 7 — Separation, divorce, and a flat (2038–2045)

**Beat 15 · 1 June 2038 · a doorway with a suitcase.** Cheryl and Rui En, five, leave for her mother's.

- *Who has her?* — Guardianship of Infants Act ss 3, 5: the court, on the welfare principle; care and control to Cheryl. `ENC`, the principle `CASE`. *What do I pay?* — Women's Charter s 69: maintenance for the child; $600 a month is the court's figure. `ENC`.

**Beat 16 · 10 July 2041 · the Family Court.** Three years apart.

- *Is the marriage old enough to end?* — s 94: yes, past three years. *On what ground?* — s 95A(1)(d): three years apart, with consent; no fault needed. `ENC`. *Anything to divide?* — Part 10 Chapter 4A, outside the row; there is nothing. `PART`.

## Chapter 8 — The exclusion order, the stairwell, and the retrenchment (2045–2060)

**Beat 17 · 8 May 2046 · a form with his sister's name on it.** Simone applies for a family exclusion order.

- *Can she do that?* — Casino Control Act ss 159-161, outside the row. `PART`. *What can the order say?* — s 162. `ENC`. *Who decides?* — a Committee, exercising a discretion the Act gives it. The voice says what it must weigh and does not say what it will do. The order is made for a year.

**Beat 18 · 5 October 2053 · a wet stairwell.** Forty-seven, on patrol, eleven weeks off, a doctor's letter with a percentage.

- *What is the employer liable for?* — WICA s 7. *How much?* — the Table for an eight per cent permanent incapacity, `ENC`, computed to the dollar. *And the eight?* — the doctor's, at the edge of a band; one point either way changes the sum. The voice shows both sums and names the one thing it cannot decide.

## Chapter 9 — The stroke, Silver Support, and the end (2061–2079)

**Beat 19 · 28 April 2063 · a rehabilitation ward.** A stroke at fifty-six. Two activities of daily living clearly lost; a third at the margin.

- *What is severe disability?* — CareShield Life and Long-Term Care Act First Schedule: unable to perform three of six. `ENC`, exact. *Do I meet it?* — the assessor's finding, not the voice's. It is found that he does. Two years later, that he does not.

**Beat 20 · 2 May 2079 · a very small flat.** He has died at seventy-two; his kidneys have been donated under a presumption he never objected to. Rui En, forty-six, and Simone, seventy-six, at the table.

- *No will.* — Intestate Succession Act s 7: his daughter takes all. `ENC`. *Is there a grant?* — Public Trustee Act ss 6-8: an estate under the limit goes to the Public Trustee without one; the limit itself is in rules. `ENC`, `REGS`. *His CPF?* — s 25: to Rui En as nominee, outside the estate. `ENC`. *The flat?* — the unexpired lease is refunded on HDB's terms. `ADMIN-POLICY`. *Duty?* — Estate Duty Act s 2A: none. `ENC`.

## Notes for the screenplay

- **The judgement stops.** Five beats stop for a person rather than for a missing law: the magistrate (6), the Official Assignee (14), the Committee (17), the doctor (18), the assessor (19). Two of them are the Act's own discretion (14, 17); two are a clinical judgement at the edge of an exact rule (18, 19); one is a court (6). They are the model for the two human-judgement stops Simone's screenplay is to gain.
- **One missing-law stop.** Beat 9, the platform's CPF, is the only place the voice lacks the law. Keep it to one.
- **The net, not the wall.** Every unlawful act in this life ends in a process: composition, probation, a summary trial, a tribunal, a scheme. He is never imprisoned. If a producer wants prison, that is a different character.
- **The sister.** Simone appears three times: the exclusion order, their mother's estate, his death. She is the thread that ties the two films.
- **Figures on screen.** Seven: $89, $300, $4,100, $41,000, $690, $600, and the two WICA sums side by side in beat 18.
