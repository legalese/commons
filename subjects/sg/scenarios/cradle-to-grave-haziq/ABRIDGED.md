# Cradle to grave, abridged — Haziq in twenty beats

The same life as [`SCENARIO.md`](SCENARIO.md), cut to two beats a chapter. Each is a scene. The beats are flagged `"abridged": true` in [`facts.json`](facts.json), which also carries his age at every event.

Where Simone asked the voice what she would get and Jun Hao asked what would happen to him, Haziq asks what he owes: to employees, to users, to investors, to the Registrar, to the electorate, to his heirs. The voice answers from the other side of the same rows. And twice in this life it stops for a reason the other two never met: the law it is asked about is Muslim law, which the statute applies by name and does not restate.

## Chapter 0 — Born (1996)

**Beat 1 · 9 April 1996 · KKH.** A taxi driver's son.

- *Citizen?* — Art 121. `ENC`.

**Beat 2 · May 1996 · a payslip.** The first deduction his father's payslip showed for him was not CPF.

- *What is the Mosque Building and Mendaki Fund, and can one opt out?* — Administration of Muslim Law Act ss 78-79, Third Schedule. `ENC`.

## Chapter 1 — School (2003–2014)

**Beat 3 · November 2008 · a prize-giving.** An Edusave scholarship after the PSLE.

- *What is it, and who decides?* — Education Endowment and Savings Schemes Act ss 7-9, outside the row; the criteria are policy. `PART`, `ADMIN-POLICY`.

**Beat 4 · November 2014 · A-levels.** Nothing legal happens. The voice is quiet; it is noticed for the first time that it has been quiet for years.

## Chapter 2 — Officer and law student (2015–2021)

**Beat 5 · 9 February 2015 · a parade square.** Enlisted; by November, commissioned.

- *What law governs me in uniform?* — Singapore Armed Forces Act ss 3-4, 11. `ENC`.

**Beat 6 · 7 August 2017 · a bursar's counter.** Law school, fees partly from his own account.

- *May the account pay for a degree?* — EESS Act ss 20, 22: yes. `ENC`.

## Chapter 3 — The Bar, a Kadi, London (2021–2029)

**Beat 7 · 30 November 2024 · the Registry of Muslim Marriages.** Nadia's father as wali.

- *Who may solemnise; whose consent?* — AMLA ss 94B-97, Fourth Schedule. `ENC`. The marriage is under a second personal law, and the voice says so.

**Beat 8 · 15 March 2028 · a show-flat.** Citizen and permanent resident, buying together.

- *Is a condominium restricted?* — Residential Property Act ss 2-4: no. *Whose profile sets the additional duty?* — Art 3(bi): the PR's, five per cent of the whole price, $80,000; BSD $44,600. `ENC`.

## Chapter 4 — Founding (2030–2034)

**Beat 9 · 15 March 2031 · ACRA's portal at 11 p.m.** Seventy, ten, twenty.

- *What is a company; what is an interest in shares; who may audit it?* — Companies Act ss 4-7, 10. `ENC`. *How do I incorporate, and what do I owe as a director?* — ss 19, 157: outside the row. `PART`. The voice says which half of the Act it holds.

**Beat 10 · 1 June 2031 · three desks.** The first payroll.

- *What do I owe them from day one?* — Employment Act; CPF s 7; the levy; WICA s 24; the reservist release. `ENC`. The voice lists six Acts in a sentence, and the sentence is the point.

## Chapter 5 — A round, a breach, a house (2035–2039)

**Beat 11 · 3 April 2036 · 2 a.m.** Twelve hundred email addresses.

- *Do I tell the Commission?* — PDPA Part VIA: yes, within three days; and the users. `ENC`.

**Beat 12 · 20 May 2038 · a terrace house.** Two citizens now.

- *May we own landed property?* — Residential Property Act s 3: yes. *Duty?* — $191,600; no additional duty, because the condominium went first; no seller's duty after ten years. `ENC`.

## Chapter 6 — The candidate and the acquirer (2040–2045)

**Beat 13 · 23 June 2040 · a nomination centre.** A deposit paid.

- *Who may stand; when is the deposit lost?* — PEA s 28: forfeited below one eighth of the vote; Constitution Arts 44-45. `ENC`. He takes forty-one per cent and keeps it.

**Beat 14 · 15 June 2043 · a signing room.** Formly acquired.

- *Duty on the shares?* — Art 3(c). *Must we notify the merger?* — Competition Act s 54: not at this size. `ENC`.

## Chapter 7 — Exit, foundation, his father (2048–2052)

**Beat 15 · 30 June 2050 · a completion dinner.** Forty million; Simone's options paid out at the next table.

- *Duty?* — Art 3(c), $80,000. *Is my gain taxed?* — ITA s 10(1)(g): only if it is income, and whether it is income is a fact the row takes from the cases. `ENC`, with the line drawn by `CASE`.

**Beat 16 · 3 November 2052 · his father's flat.** No will. His mother, his sister, himself.

- *Who inherits, and how much?* — AMLA s 112: according to Muslim law. The row has the section and not the law it points at; the voice reads the section and stops. `PART`, `CASE`. *The flat?* — survivorship to his mother. *The CPF?* — the nomination. `CASE`, `ENC`.

## Chapter 8 — Investor, elder (2053–2069)

**Beat 17 · 5 October 2053 · a stairwell he has never used.** The night officer falls in his building.

- *Is it ours?* — WICA s 7: the agency's, as employer. *And the occupier?* — WSH Act s 11, outside the row. `ENC`, `PART`.

**Beat 18 · 9 April 2065 · a study.** A lawyer writes his own will, and finds it is not his to write freely.

- *May I leave a third to the Foundation?* — AMLA s 111 and the one-third rule: outside the row; Muslim law. `PART`, `CASE`. The second stop of its kind.

## Chapter 9 — The end (2074–2078)

**Beat 19 · 21 October 2078 · a hospital room.** He dies at eighty-two.

- *Does the organ presumption apply to a Muslim?* — HOTA s 5: yes, since 2008, unless he opted out. `ENC`.

**Beat 20 · 15 November 2078 · a kitchen table.** A certificate from the Syariah Court.

- *In what order?* — the wasiat's third to the Foundation; the house by survivorship; the CPF by nomination; the residue by faraid, an eighth to Nadia, two parts to Imran, one to Aisyah; no duty. `PART`, `CASE`, `ENC`.

## Notes for the screenplay

- **The adjudicator's side.** Beats 9, 10, 11, 13, 14 and 15 are the same rows the other films used, asked from the counter. If the three lives become one film, these are the agency scenes Simone's screenplay is to gain.
- **The two religious-law stops.** Beats 16 and 18 are stops of a kind the other lives never meet: the Act is in the library and the law it applies is not. They are honest and should be kept.
- **Figures on screen.** Six: $80,000 twice (the ABSD and the share duty, which is the film's quiet joke), $191,600, forty million, two million, and the fractions on the certificate.
- **The quiet beat.** Beat 4 has no law in it. Keep it; it is the overachiever's version of Simone's evening with the noodles.
