                          TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                               (CHAPTER 323)

               TELECOMMUNICATIONS
     (EXEMPTION FROM SECTIONS 33, 34 (1) (b) AND 35)
           (AMENDMENT) NOTIFICATION 2008

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 73 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Minister for Information,
Communications and the Arts hereby makes the following
Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Exemption from sections 33, 34 (1) (b) and 35) (Amendment)
Notification 2008 and shall come into operation on 2nd January 2008.
