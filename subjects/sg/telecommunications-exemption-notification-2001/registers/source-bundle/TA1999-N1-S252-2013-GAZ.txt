No. S 252

                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

                TELECOMMUNICATIONS
      (EXEMPTION FROM SECTIONS 33, 34(1)(b) AND 35)
           (AMENDMENT) NOTIFICATION 2013

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 73 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Minister for Communications and
Information hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Exemption from sections 33, 34(1)(b) and 35) (Amendment)
Notification 2013 and shall come into operation on 25th April 2013.

Amendment of Schedule
  2. The Schedule to the Telecommunications (Exemption from
sections 33, 34(1)(b) and 35) Notification (N 1) is amended ––
     (a) by deleting item 5 and substituting the following item:
              “ 5.   Induction            0.016 – 0.150 MHz        66 dBµA/m @ 10m
                     Loop                 0.150 – 5.000 MHz        13.5 dBµA/m @ 10m
                     Communication
                     Systems              6.765 – 6.795 MHz        42 dBµA/m @ 10m
                                          7.400 – 8.800 MHz        9 dBµA/m @ 10m         ”; and

      (b) by deleting item 8 and substituting the following item:
              “ 8.   Medical and           9.000 – 315.000 kHz          30 dBµA/m @ 10m
                     Biological            40.500 – 41.000 MHz          0.01 mW ERP
                     Telemetry
                     Devices               216.00 – 217.00 MHz          100 mW ERP
                                           454.000 – 454.500 MHz        2 mW ERP
                                           1,427.00 – 1,432.00 MHz      100 mW ERP
                                           All other frequencies        25 µW ERP               ”.
