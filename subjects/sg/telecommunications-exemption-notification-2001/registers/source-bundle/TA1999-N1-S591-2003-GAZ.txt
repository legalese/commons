                          TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                               (CHAPTER 323)

               TELECOMMUNICATIONS
     (EXEMPTION FROM SECTIONS 33, 34 (1) (b) AND 35)
           (AMENDMENT) NOTIFICATION 2003

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 73 of the Telecom-
munications Act, the Minister for Information, Communications and
the Arts hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Exemption from sections 33, 34 (1) (b) and 35) (Amendment)
Notification 2003 and shall come into operation on 15th December
2003.

Amendment of Schedule
  2. The Schedule to the Telecommunications (Exemption from
sections 33, 34 (1) (b) and 35) Notification (N 1) is amended —
