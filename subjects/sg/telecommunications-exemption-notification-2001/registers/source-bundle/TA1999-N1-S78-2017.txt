                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

                TELECOMMUNICATIONS
      (EXEMPTION FROM SECTIONS 33, 34(1)(b) AND 35)
           (AMENDMENT) NOTIFICATION 2017

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 73 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Minister for Communications and
Information makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement

  1. This Notification is the Telecommunications (Exemption from
sections 33, 34(1)(b) and 35) (Amendment) Notification 2017 and
comes into operation on 1 April 2017.

Amendment of First Schedule

   2. The First Schedule to the Telecommunications (Exemption
from sections 33, 34(1)(b) and 35) Notification (N 1) is amended
by inserting, immediately after item 17, the following item:
      “
          18. Intelligent Transport
              System ––
              (a) Vehicular On-Board Unit 5,875.00 –– 5,925.00 MHz                  2 W EIRP

              (b) Roadside Infrastructure 5,885.00 –– 5,895.00 MHz                  2 W EIRP
                  (also known as Road Side
                  Unit or Non-Vehicular
                  Installation)      using
                  Dedicated Short-Range
                  Communication Control
                  Channel
                                                                                                ”.
