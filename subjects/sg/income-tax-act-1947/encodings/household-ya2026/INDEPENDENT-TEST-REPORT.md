## Failures

One assertion failed, and it is a supplementary case, not one of the 70.

### S7 (`tests-independent-supplement.l4` line 472): a foreign employee, neither a citizen nor a permanent resident

- **My reading, with the words**: the BRIEF's scope says the CPF covers "an employee who is a citizen or a permanent resident past the second anniversary month"; so a household whose adult is neither is outside the scope, and (the brief's rule: "the answer is 'not covered', never a guess") the position should be NOTHING.
  I did not use the Exemption Order; the CPF Act's definition of "employee" (s 2, "any person who ... is employed in Singapore by an employer") does not itself exclude a foreigner, and First Schedule para 5(dc) defines "foreign employee" for the graduated-rate paragraphs.
- **The encoding's number**: a position, not NOTHING: income tax 1,880, employee CPF 0, net income 58,120 for wages of 5,000 a month (`#EVAL` of `HH77`).
- **Where it diverges**: `cpf-wages-year.l4:186`, `WHEN \`neither a citizen nor a permanent resident\` THEN JUST 0`, with the comment "Exemption Order para 3: the employer is exempt; nothing is recoverable"; the module header (`cpf-wages-year.l4:27`) lists "Exemption Order classes other than 'neither a citizen nor a permanent resident'" among what is NOT encoded, so the encoder meant this class to be covered.
  The BRIEF's "Not covered" list does not name a foreign employee.
- **Verdict**: a genuine fork between the brief's scope sentence and the encoder's choice, not a computation error; my expectation rests on the brief's wording and on a source (the Exemption Order) that I did not read, so I cannot call the encoding wrong.
  Commons should decide which is meant and make the BRIEF and the encoding say the same: either add "an employee who is neither a citizen nor a permanent resident" to the BRIEF's Not covered list and return NOTHING, or state in the BRIEF that the household is covered with employee CPF nil under the Exemption Order.
  The assertion is left failing as written.
