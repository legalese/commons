No. S 485

                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

           TELECOMMUNICATIONS (CLASS LICENCES)
              (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2016

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 74 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Media
Development Authority, with the approval of the Minister for
Communications and Information, makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
  1. These Regulations are the Telecommunications (Class
Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 and are deemed to have
come into operation on 1 October 2016.

Amendment of regulation 2
  2. Regulation 2 of the Telecommunications (Class Licences)
Regulations (Rg 3) is amended by deleting the definition of
“Authority”.
