# Source licence

This file says how the text in `registers/source-bundle/` came to be here, and no more than that.

1. **What the text is.** It is the text of the Telecommunications (Class Licences) Regulations (Cap. 323, Rg 3), Singapore subsidiary legislation: the Law Revision Commission's Revised Edition (or the gazetted instrument), then the gazetted instruments that amend it, one file each. The header line as printed on each is recorded in that file's `.meta.json` (`agc_header_line_as_printed`).
2. **Where the copy came from.** Each file was extracted from a PDF that IMDA hosts on its own site (https://www.imda.gov.sg/regulations-and-licensing-listing/telecommunications-act-1999). The PDF's URL, the date it was retrieved (2026-11-03) and its sha256, and the sha256 of the extracted text, are in the file's `.meta.json`. These files have no text layer and are tesseract OCR of the page images, unchecked against the images beyond what the encoder read: TA1999-RG3-S685-2011.
3. **What the reproduction relies on.** It relies on the grant in Singapore Statutes Online's Terms of Use cl. 13, with the cl. 13(a) attribution reproduced as in the Act-level file (`subjects/sg/telecommunications-act-1999/SOURCE-LICENSE.md`):

> This Singapore legislation is subject to the copyright of the Singapore
> Government and is reproduced here with the permission of the
> Attorney-General's Chambers of Singapore. Readers may check Singapore Statutes
> Online (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/) for the latest version of this legislation.

4. **IMDA's own terms.** IMDA's Terms of Use (https://www.imda.gov.sg/terms-of-use, last updated 25 May 2026) paras 6 and 7 restrict the contents of IMDA's site. This deposit relies on the text being legislation rather than IMDA content. That is a reliance, not a clearance obtained from IMDA or from the Attorney-General's Chambers.
