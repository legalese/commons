NO. S 128

 STATUTES (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT 2008
                 (ACT 4 OF 2008)

                          STATUTES
               (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT
               (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2008

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Statutes
(Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2008, the Minister for Finance
hereby makes the following Notification:

 1. This Notification may be cited as the Statutes (Miscellaneous
Amendments) Act (Commencement) Notification 2008.

  2. The Statutes (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2008 shall come
into operation on 18th March 2008.

   Made this 17th day of March 2008.
                                                                    TEO MING KIAN
                                                                    Permanent Secretary,
                                                                    Ministry of Finance,
                                                                        Singapore.
