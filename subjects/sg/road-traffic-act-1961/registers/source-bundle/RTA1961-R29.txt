Methods of payment
3.—(1) When an of fer of composition of any of fence is made and
accepted, payment must be made —
(a) by an electronic funds transfer system to a bank account
designated by the Commissioner of Police or the Authority
to collect the payment, who or which must account for the
payment;
(b) to a person appointed by the Authority to collect the
payment on the Authority’ s behalf, who must account for
and issue a receipt for the payment; or
(c) if the payer is unable to make payment according to
sub‑paragraph (a) or (b) — by cash or cheque to a relevant
authorised of ficer (within the meaning given by
section 135(1B) of the Act) authorised by the Deputy
