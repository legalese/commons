## Why this Act, and why scoped

This row answers **REQ-0211** in `subjects/sg/requirements.jsonl`: Tier 3 of the
remaining Singapore Acts, ordered by everyday-life relevance. The requirement asks
what the Act decides for a person or business it applies to; no scenario has asked
a sharper question yet.

The Act is the constitution of a statutory body, so what it decides for a person is
what it decides for a union member's child seeking a bursary, a member, a director,
or someone the Foundation pays. This row takes the bursary object (s 4(c)),
resignation and termination of membership (s 6(4), (5)), removal of directors (s 7),
Board meetings (s 10), requisitioning an extraordinary general meeting (s 13), notice
(s 14), quorum (s 16), virtual meetings hit by a disruption (s 16B), inspection of

protection (s 23). Incorporation, the Board's powers, admission of members,

## What the Act turns out to say

### 1. A member may see the Foundation's books only with a director's written permission

s 17 opens the register of members, the minutes and the books of account "to
inspection by members, provided that the written permission of the Chairperson or of
a director is obtained prior to the inspection". The member's right of inspection is
therefore in the Board's hands, and a non-member has none. Asserted.

### 2. Membership can be ended for any reason the Board thinks just

s 6(5) lists grounds for termination (mental incapacity, bankruptcy, insolvency,
unpaid subscriptions, loss of NTUC affiliation, a general meeting's resolution), then
adds (g) "for any other reason that to the Board may seem just or proper". The list
is illustrative. A resigning member still owes what was due on the date of
resignation (s 6(4)); that later sums are not owed is an inference. Asserted.

### 3. Members cannot be paid out, and of the directors only one may be remunerated

s 19(1) forbids any dividend, bonus, profit "or otherwise" to members or directors.
s 19(2) saves good-faith payment of employees' remuneration, remuneration of the one
director given general administrative control under s 9, and directors' out-of-pocket
and travelling expenses. Treating a fee to an ordinary director as barred, and not
saved by "other proper charges and expenses", is an inference. Every saved payment
must be made in good faith. Asserted.

### 4. The Minister can remove five of the seven directors without a reason

The Board is 3 directors appointed by the Minister on NTUC's advice, 2 appointed by
the Minister, and 2 elected at the AGM (s 7(1)). The Minister may revoke any of the
five appointments "without giving any reason". The two elected directors can be
removed only by a resolution of a general meeting (s 7(4)). Asserted.

### 5. A small number of members can force a meeting, and an adjourned meeting needs no quorum

An extraordinary general meeting must be convened on a written requisition by "at
least 100 members or one-quarter of the total number of members, whichever is less"
(s 13(1)): 50 members of a 200-member Foundation, 100 of a 1,000-member one. The
quorum is one-quarter of the register (s 16(1)); a meeting without one stands
adjourned a week, and at the adjourned meeting those present constitute a quorum after
half an hour (s 16(2)). That at least one voter must be present is an inference.
Notice must be sent at least 14 days ahead (s 14(1)). Asserted.

### 6. A court's finding of injustice is not enough to undo a virtual meeting

s 16B: a hybrid or virtual meeting is not invalidated by a technological disruption
unless the court both finds substantial injustice that no order can remedy **and**
by order declares the meeting invalid. A finding without the declaration leaves the
meeting standing. Asserted.

### 7. The bursary object is a class, not an entitlement

s 4(c) makes bursaries, scholarships and fellowships an object for children of
members of NTUC-affiliated unions and (since Act 30 of 2024) platform work
associations, or of associate affiliates; workers studying or training in Singapore
in relevant fields; and persons NTUC nominates to study in Singapore or elsewhere. A
worker studying abroad without NTUC's nomination is outside it. Nothing in the Act
gives anyone a right to an award. Asserted.

### 8. Board decisions need four directors and a majority, with a casting vote

s 10(2): four directors form a quorum and a simple majority of those present and
voting decides, the person presiding having a casting vote on a tie. The Board must
meet at least once in 3 months (s 10(1)). Asserted.
