            TELECOMMUNICATION AUTHORITY OF
                     SINGAPORE ACT
                 (CHAPTER 323, SECTION 130)

             TELECOMMUNICATION AUTHORITY OF
            SINGAPORE (EXEMPTION IN RESPECT OF
                SPECIFIED EQUIPMENT) ORDER

                     ARRANGEMENT OF PARAGRAPHS

Paragraph
    1.   Citation
    2.   Exemption



                                                [12th November 1998]

Citation
  1. This Order may be cited as the Telecommunication Authority of
Singapore (Exemption in respect of Specified Equipment) Order.

Exemption
 2. All persons shall be exempted from section 30 (1) to (5) of the
Act in respect of —
    (a) any telecommunication equipment that has been approved
         for sale by the Authority under regulation 24 (1) of the
         Telecommunication (Dealers) Regulations (Rg 7); and
    (b) any telecommunication equipment that is set out in the
         Fifth Schedule to the Telecommunication (Dealers)
         Regulations and that meets the standards and specifications
         approved by the Authority and published by the Authority
         from time to time.
