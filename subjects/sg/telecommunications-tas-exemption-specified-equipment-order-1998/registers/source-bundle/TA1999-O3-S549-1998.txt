              TELECOMMUNICATION AUTHORITY OF
                      SINGAPORE ACT
                       (CHAPTER 323)
              TELECOMMUNICATION AUTHORITY OF
             SINGAPORE (EXEMPTION IN RESPECT OF
               SPECIFIED EQUIPMENT) ORDER 1998
                        ARRANGEMENT OF PARAGRAPHS
Paragraph
  1.   Citation and commencement
  2.   Exemption


  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 130 of the
Telecommunication Authority of Singapore Act, the Minister for
Communications hereby makes the following Order:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Order may be cited as the Telecommunication Authority of
Singapore (Exemption in respect of Specified Equipment) Order
1998 and shall come into operation on 12th November 1998.

Exemption
  2. All persons shall be exempted from section 30 (1) to (5) of the
Act in respect of —
    (a) telecommunication equipment that has been approved for
         sale by the Authority under regulation 24 (1) of the
         Telecommunication (Dealers) Regulations (Rg 7); and
    (b) telecommunication equipment that is set out in the Fifth
         Schedule to the Telecommunication (Dealers) Regulations
         and that meets the standards and specifications approved by
         the Authority and published by the Authority from time to
         time.

   Made this 11th day of November 1998.

                                                            TEO MING KIAN
                                                           Permanent Secretary,
                                                        Ministry of Communications,
                                                                 Singapore.
