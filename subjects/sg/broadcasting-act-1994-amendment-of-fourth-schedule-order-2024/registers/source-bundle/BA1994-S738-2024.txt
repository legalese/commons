                           BROADCASTING ACT 1994

                     BROADCASTING ACT 1994
                (AMENDMENT OF FOURTH SCHEDULE)
                          ORDER 2024

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 2B(1) of the
Broadcasting Act 1994, the Minister for Digital Development and
Information makes the following Order:

Citation and commencement
 1. This Order is the Broadcasting Act 1994 (Amendment of
Fourth Schedule) Order 2024 and comes into operation on
25 September 2024.

Replacement of Fourth Schedule
 2. In the Broadcasting Act 1994, replace the Fourth Schedule
with —
                                   “FOURTH SCHEDULE
                                                              Sections 2A(1), 2B(1), 2C and 2D(1)
                                                                                           and (4)


                                                PART 1
                          ONLINE COMMUNICATION SERVICES
       1. Social media service.
       2. App distribution service.
S 738/2024                            2

                                    PART 2
                                 DEFINITIONS
    1. In this Schedule —
       “app” means an application software package that includes content
          accessible by end‑users, or allows end‑users to access content on the
          Internet through the installed application software;
       “app distribution service” means an electronic service (that is accessible
          by Singapore end‑users) that is provided for the purpose of enabling
          end‑users to download an app where the download of the app is by
          means of access to the Internet.”.

 Made on 18 September 2024.
