NO. S 374

                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

   TELECOMMUNICATIONS (RADIO-COMMUNICATION)
         (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2001

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 74 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Development
Authority of Singapore, with the approval of the Minister for
Communications and Information Technology, hereby makes the
following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
  1. These Regulations may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Radio-communication) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall
be deemed to have come into operation on 9th July 2001.

Amendment of First Schedule
  2. Part II of the First Schedule to the Telecommunications
(Radio-communication) Regulations 2001 (G.N. No. S 114/2001) is
amended by inserting, immediately after item 10, the following item:

       ‘‘
            11.   Fixed Wireless Broadband Network                  $500 per group
                                                                    of 50 stations or
                                                                    part thereof.
                                                                                           ’’.


   Made this 31st day of July 2001.


                                                     LAM CHUAN LEONG
                                                           Chairman,
                                                Info-communications Development
                                                      Authority of Singapore.
