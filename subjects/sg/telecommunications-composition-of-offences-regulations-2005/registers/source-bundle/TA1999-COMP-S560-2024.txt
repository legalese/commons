No. S 560
                    TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT 1999

                         TELECOMMUNICATIONS
                      (COMPOSITION OF OFFENCES)
                    (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2024

 In exercise of the powers conferred by section 84(2) of the
Telecommunications Act 1999, the Info‑communications Media
Development Authority, with the approval of the Minister for
Communications and Information, makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
 1. These Regulations are the Telecommunications (Composition of
Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 and come into operation
on 1 July 2024.

Amendment of regulation 2
 2. In the Telecommunications (Composition of Offences)
Regulations 2005 (G.N. No. S 706/2005), in regulation 2 —
         (a) in paragraph (f), replace “; and” at the end with “as in force
             before 1 July 2024;”;
         (b) in paragraph (g), replace the full‑stop at the end with
             “; and”; and
         (c) after paragraph (g), insert —
                              “(h) any offence under regulation 6 of the
                                   Telecommunications     (Wiring    Work)
                                   Regulations 2024 (G.N. No. S 559/2024).”.
