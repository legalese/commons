No. S 000

TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
(CHAPTER 323)

TELECOMMUNICATIONS
(COMPOSITION OF OFFENCES)
(AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2011

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 64 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Development
Authority of Singapore, with the approval of the Minister for
Information, Communications and the Arts, hereby makes the
following Regulations:

Citation and commencement

J. These Regulations may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Composition of Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and
shall come into operation on 28 December 2011.

Amendment of regulation 2

2. Regulation 2 of the Telecommunications (Composition of
Offences) Regulations 2005 (G.N. No. S$ 706/2005) is amended —

(a) by deleting the word “and” at the end of paragraph (e); and

(b) by deleting the full-stop at the end of paragraph (f) and
substituting the word “; and”, and by inserting immediately
thereafter the following paragraph:

“(g) any offence under the Telecommunications
(Class Licences) Regulations (Rg 3).”.
2

Made this 6th day of December 2011.

YONG YING-I
Chairman,
Info-communications Development
