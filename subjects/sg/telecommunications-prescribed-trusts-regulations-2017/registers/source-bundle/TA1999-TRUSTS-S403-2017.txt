                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

       TELECOMMUNICATIONS (PRESCRIBED TRUSTS)
                 REGULATIONS 2017

                       ARRANGEMENT OF REGULATIONS
Regulation
     1.   Citation and commencement
     2.   Prescribed class of trusts


  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 74 of the
Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Media
Development Authority, with the approval of the Minister for
Communications and Information, makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
  1. These Regulations are the Telecommunications (Prescribed
Trusts) Regulations 2017 and come into operation on 19 July 2017.

Prescribed class of trusts
   2. The class of trusts prescribed for the purposes of the definition
of “designated trust” in section 32A(1) of the Act is the class of
trusts that meets all of the following conditions:
     (a) the trust is established wholly or partly in respect of a
         telecommunication system (or any part thereof);
     (b) the telecommunication system mentioned in paragraph (a)
         is operated by a designated telecommunication licensee.
