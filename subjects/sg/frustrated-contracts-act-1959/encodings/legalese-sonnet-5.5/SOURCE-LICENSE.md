# Source licence: Frustrated Contracts Act 1959 (Singapore)

The terms the quoted legal text carries are recorded in the subject-level note written when the text was deposited: `inputs/STUB-SOURCE-LICENSE.md` of this run.
This file points at it and does not repeat it, and it adds no terms.

What this encoding adds to that note:

- The text in `FCA1959.txt` is an unofficial plain-text rendering made by a converter script from Singapore Statutes Online (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/FCA1959, retrieved 2026-10-01, current version as at 01 Oct 2026, 2020 Revised Edition).
  It is not the official text, and nothing in this folder is "verified against the official text".
- The `.l4` files quote the Act in `-- src:N | ...` comment lines, generated mechanically from `FCA1959.txt` with `srcrefs.py quote`; the line numbers refer to that file.
  They are quotations of the Act's text under the conditions in the note above.
- `sgia-p1-definitions.l4` is a copy of an encoding of the Interpretation Act 1965 held in the Commons repository, copied unchanged; its own quotations are of that Act and its terms are those that encoding's row carries.
- The licence of the encoding itself (the L4 code and `NOTES.md`) is Apache-2.0, like the rest of the corpus (`encoding.json`).
