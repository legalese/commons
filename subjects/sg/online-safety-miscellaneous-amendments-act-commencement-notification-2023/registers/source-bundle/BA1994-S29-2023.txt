                       ONLINE SAFETY
            (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT 2022

                       ONLINE SAFETY
            (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT 2022
              (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2023

 In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Online
Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2022, the Minister for
Communications and Information makes the following Notification:
 1. This Notification is the Online Safety (Miscellaneous
Amendments) Act 2022 (Commencement) Notification 2023.
 2. The Online Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2022
comes into operation on 1 February 2023.

 Made on 30 January 2023.
