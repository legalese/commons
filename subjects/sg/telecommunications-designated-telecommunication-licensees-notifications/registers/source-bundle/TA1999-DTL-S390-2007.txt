                        TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                             (CHAPTER 323)

              TELECOMMUNICATIONS
   (DESIGNATED TELECOMMUNICATION LICENSEES —
            SERVICES-BASED OPERATOR)
                NOTIFICATION 2007

                        ARRANGEMENT OF PARAGRAPHS

Paragraph
    1.   Citation and commencement
    2.   Designated telecommunication licensees



   In exercise of the powers conferred by the definition of ‘‘designated
telecommunication licensee’’ in section 32A (1) of the Telecom-
munications Act, the Info-communications Development Authority
of Singapore hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Designated Telecommunication Licensees — Services-based
Operator) Notification 2007 and shall come into operation on
20th July 2007.

Designated telecommunication licensees
  2. The Authority hereby declares Syniverse Technologies Private
Limited to be a designated telecommunication licensee for the
purposes of Part VA of the Act and subsection 10.2 of the Code of
Practice for Competition in the Provision of Telecommunication
Services 2005 (G.N. No. S 87/2005).
