NO. S 23

                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

               TELECOMMUNICATIONS
   (DESIGNATED TELECOMMUNICATION LICENSEES —
     FACILITIES-BASED OPERATOR) (AMENDMENT)
                 NOTIFICATION 2011

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 32A(3) of the
Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Development
Authority of Singapore hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Designated Telecommunication Licensees — Facilities-based
Operator) (Amendment) Notification 2011 and shall come into
operation on 21st January 2011.

Amendment of paragraph 2
  2. Paragraph 2 of the Telecommunications (Designated
Telecommunication Licensees — Facilities-based Operator)
Notification 2005 (G.N. No. S 86/2005) is amended by deleting the
words ‘‘and subsection 10.2 of the Code of Practice for Competition
in the Provision of Telecommunication Services 2005 (G.N. No.
S 87/2005)’’.

   Made this 6th day of January 2011.
                                                         YONG YING-I
                                                           Chairman,
                                                Info-communications Development
                                                      Authority of Singapore.
