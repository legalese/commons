                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT

                  TELECOMMUNICATIONS
            (DESIGNATED TELECOMMUNICATION
         LICENSEES — FACILITIES-BASED OPERATOR)
                    NOTIFICATION 2005

                        ARRANGEMENT OF PARAGRAPHS

Paragraph
    1.   Citation and commencement
    2.   Designated telecommunication licensees



   In exercise of the powers conferred by the definition of ‘‘designated
telecommunication licensee’’ in section 32A (1) of the Telecommu-
nications Act, the Info-communications Development Authority of
Singapore hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Designated Telecommunication Licensees — Facilities-based
Operator) Notification 2005 and shall come into operation on
4th March 2005.

Designated telecommunication licensees
  2. The Authority hereby declares all telecommunication licensees
granted a licence to provide facilities-based operations under
section 5 of the Act to be designated telecommunication licensees
for the purposes of Part VA of the Act and subsection 10.2 of the
Code of Practice for Competition in the Provision of Telecommuni-
cation Services 2005 (G.N. No. S 87/2005).
