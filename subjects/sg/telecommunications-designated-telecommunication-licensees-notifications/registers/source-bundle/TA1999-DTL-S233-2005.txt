NO. S 233
                        TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                             (CHAPTER 323)

              TELECOMMUNICATIONS
   (DESIGNATED TELECOMMUNICATION LICENSEES —
            SERVICES-BASED OPERATOR)
                NOTIFICATION 2005

                        ARRANGEMENT OF PARAGRAPHS

Paragraph
    1.   Citation and commencement
    2.   Designated telecommunication licensees



   In exercise of the powers conferred by the definition of ‘‘designated
telecommunication licensee’’ in section 32A (1) of the Telecom-
munications Act, the Info-communications Development Authority
of Singapore hereby makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Designated Telecommunication Licensees — Services-based
Operator) Notification 2005 and shall come into operation on 7th April
2005.

Designated telecommunication licensees
  2. The Authority hereby declares Pacific Internet Limited to be
a designated telecommunication licensee for the purposes of Part VA
of the Act and subsection 10.2 of the Code of Practice for
Competition in the Provision of Telecommunication Services 2005
(G.N. No. S 87/2005).

   Made this 7th day of April 2005.
                                                          YONG YING-I
                                                         Deputy Chairman,
                                                 Info-communications Development
                                                       Authority of Singapore.
