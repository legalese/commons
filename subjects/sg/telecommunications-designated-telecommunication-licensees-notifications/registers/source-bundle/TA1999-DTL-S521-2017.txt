No. S 521

                       TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                            (CHAPTER 323)

         TELECOMMUNICATIONS (DESIGNATED
    TELECOMMUNICATION LICENSEES) (AMENDMENT)
                NOTIFICATION 2017

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 32A(2) of
the Telecommunications Act, the Info-communications Media
Development Authority makes the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Notification is the Telecommunications (Designated
Telecommunication Licensees) (Amendment) Notification 2017 and
comes into operation on 22 September 2017.

Amendment of paragraph 2
   2. Paragraph 2(2) of the Telecommunications (Designated
Telecommunication Licensees) Notification 2012 (G.N. No. S 35/2012)
is amended by deleting sub-paragraph (a) and substituting the
following sub-paragraph:
    “(a) M1 Net Ltd;”.
