  STATUTES (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT 2008

  STATUTES (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT 2008
        (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2024

 In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Statutes
(Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2008, the Minister for Finance
makes the following Notification:
 1. This Notification is the Statutes (Miscellaneous Amendments)
Act 2008 (Commencement) Notification 2024.
 2. The Statutes (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2008, except
sections 9 and 12, comes into operation on 1 April 2024.

 Made on 27 March 2024.
