## Seat

**The person who has operational control of a business facility** — the
operator, who must apply to register, report emissions, pay the carbon tax and
answer for the offences. The Act puts every duty on that person (s 7(1A): the
person "having operational control over the business facility on 31 December of
the trigger year"; s 16(4): the tax accrues as a liability of the registered
person having operational control). The Agency, the Minister and the auditors are
the officials; what they may do appears only as what it means for the operator.
Decisions are named as the operator would ask them: `Must I apply to register the
facility as a taxable facility`, `What carbon tax am I charged for the year`, `By
when must I pay the tax`, `Can I appeal to the Minister`, `What is the most I can
be made to pay or serve for`.

The 2026-10-18 defaults:

- **The Act alone, with the instruments that set its numbers or dates.** Followed.
  The thresholds and rates are in the Act's own Schedules and are encoded. The
  regulations that prescribe the industry sectors (s 5(1)), the reporting dates,
  the prescribed circumstances in s 3 and the end date of Division 1A (s 20B) were
  not deposited; the sector is assumed, and the rest are inputs, said so.
- **The actors the text names.** Followed: the registered person, the person who
  took over (Y) and the person taken over from (X), the Agency, the Minister.
- **A judgment handed to someone else answered conditionally.** Followed. The
  Minister's award of allowances (ss 20D, 20E) is an input amount; whether
  Division 1A still applies is an input; the Agency's and the Minister's relief
  under s 18 is not applied. One departure in kind: s 9(1)(c)(ii) asks whether
  emissions are "unlikely to attain" a threshold without saying who judges it, so
  it is a fact the operator states.
- **The Act's own definitions worked out from facts, borrowed terms asked.**
  Followed for reckonable emissions (s 2), a single site (s 3(3)), operational
  control (s 4) and the thresholds. Departure: Part 2 of the Second Schedule (the
  non-reckonable emissions table) is not transcribed, so the operator states the
  non-reckonable and excluded amounts in tCO2e.
- **One case is the text's natural unit.** Followed: one facility for one
  emissions year (`Facility year`).
- **The Act's own terms, each with a plain gloss.** Followed, with the statutory
  words quoted in the comment above each rule.
- **The operative sections in scope; machinery out.** Followed, and narrowed:
  see below.

## Why this scope

The Act is long (79 sections, five Schedules). This encoding keeps to what an
operator meets: whether and by when to register (ss 3, 4, 7, 8), when it may
deregister (s 9), what it must report and have verified (ss 11, 12), what tax it
is charged (ss 16, 20C, Third Schedule), when and how it pays and what it costs to
pay late (s 17), whether it can appeal (s 34) and what each offence can cost it
(ss 54-59, 62(1)). Left out: the Agency's administration and investigation powers
(ss 6, 46-51), monitoring plans (s 13) beyond their place in s 12, directions
(s 14), refunds and recovery (ss 19, 20), assessments and revision (ss 21-25)
beyond the payment deadline, the credits registries (ss 26-33D) beyond the carbon
price, international credits, appeal procedure (ss 35-39), registers (ss 40-44),
Part 9's evidence and service rules, offences by officers (s 60) and in appeals
(s 61), composition (s 71), and regulation-making (s 76).

## What the Act turns out to say

1. **Excluded emissions count towards the thresholds but are not taxed.** The s 2
   definition makes every GHG emission reckonable unless it is non-reckonable, so
   emissions from vehicles and AFOLU activities count towards the s 7 thresholds.
   But the emissions report leaves out "excluded GHG emissions" (s 11(2)), and the
   tax is charged on the total "as set out in the emissions report" (s 16(1A)). This
   is an inference from reading the two together. On the tax side: a facility with
   26,000 tCO2e of which 2,000 are excluded is not charged. Asserted.
2. **An allowance does not lift the threshold.** s 16(2) tests the reckonable
   emissions before any allowance; s 20C then charges (A - C) x B. A 2026 facility
   at 30,000 tCO2e with a 10,000 allowance is charged on 20,000, $900,000. Asserted.
3. **The carbon price trails the tax rate by one year.** The rate is $25 for 2024
   and 2025 emissions and $45 from 2026; a credit costs $5 if bought in 2024 or
   earlier, $25 in 2025 or 2026, $45 from 2027. Because tax falls due in the next
   year (s 17(1)(a)(i): 30 September), a year's tax bought with that next year's
   credits is a whole number of them. Asserted for 2025 tax of $750,025 (30,001
   credits) and 2026 tax of $2,025,000 bought in 2027 (45,000).
4. **s 17(3) does not say how to round a fraction of a credit.** s 17(3B) rounds
   down, but only for a revised assessment. A tax that is not a whole number of
   credits gives NOTHING. Asserted.
5. **Two deadlines can apply on a takeover.** s 8(1)(a)(ii)(A) (emissions in the
   year before the transfer below the second threshold: 30 June of the next year)
   and (C) (the facility was the transferor's taxable facility: 30 days after the
   transfer) both fit a taxable facility whose emissions fell, and the text does not
   rank them. The encoding answers NOTHING there. Asserted, as are the three cases
   where only one limb applies.
6. **The verified-report fines are fixed multiples, not maxima.** s 56(2) is "a fine
   that is equal to the tax undercharged"; (3) double plus up to $5,000 or 3 years;
   (4) triple plus up to $10,000 or 3 years; (5) quadruple plus up to $50,000 or 5
   years. s 59 is triple the tax in the demand note. Asserted.
7. **Only a refusal to deregister a taxable facility can be appealed.** s 34(1)(a)
   names refusing to deregister "as a taxable facility"; a refusal to deregister a
   reportable facility is not in the list. The appeal must be lodged within 30 days
   of service. Asserted.
8. **The 1% late penalties are capped at triple the unpaid tax.** s 17(4): 5% at
   once, then 1% for each completed month once 60 days pass, "not exceeding triple
   the amount of tax remaining unpaid in total". Read as capping the 1% penalties;
   whether "in total" also takes in the 5%, and from when months are counted, is
   left open. On $100,000 unpaid for 400 months: $5,000 + $300,000. Asserted.
9. **An inaccuracy notified under s 15(1) is not an offence.** s 55(3), 56(6),
   57(3). Asserted.
10. **Years before 2024 are not covered.** Act 37 of 2022 rewrote s 16 from 1
    January 2024 and the earlier text was not deposited, so the tax for a 2023
    year is NOTHING, although the Third Schedule still prints $5 for it. Asserted.
11. **The Act does not say when an emissions report is due.** s 11(3) says only
    that the obligation "arises at the end of the reporting period"; the due date
    is left to regulations, which were not deposited.

## What would need doing before this is worth anything

- The regulations: the prescribed industry sectors (s 5(1)), reporting dates, the
  s 3 prescribed circumstances, the Division 1A end date, the ICC rules.
- Part 2 of the Second Schedule transcribed, so non-reckonable emissions are worked
  out from gas and circumstance rather than stated.
- The pre-2024 text, if earlier years matter.
- Monitoring plans, assessments and their revision, refunds, and the credits
  registries, none of which is encoded.
- A reading of forks 5 and 8 above from the Agency's guidance or any decision.
- An independent test pass.
