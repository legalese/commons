No. S 30 Broadcasting Act 1994 Broadcasting (Service of Documents Website) Notification 2023
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 64(5)( b ) of the Broadcasting Act 1994, the Minister for Communications and Information makes the following Notification:
Citation and commencement
1. This Notification is the Broadcasting (Service of Documents Website) Notification 2023 and comes into operation on 1 February 2023.
Website
2. The website maintained by the competent authority at https://www.imda.gov.sg/broadcasting‑act‑directions is prescribed for the purposes of section 64(5)( b ) of the Act.
Made on 30 January 2023.
JOSEPH LEONG WENG KEONG Permanent Secretary, Ministry of Communications and Information, Singapore.
