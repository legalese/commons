NO. S 33

      TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2011
                  (ACT 19 OF 2011)

          TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT
            (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2012

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the
Telecommunications (Amendment) Act 2011, the Minister for
Information, Communications and the Arts hereby makes the following
Notification:

  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Amendment) Act (Commencement) Notification 2012.

  2. The Telecommunications (Amendment) Act 2011 shall come
into operation on 1st February 2012.

   Made this 10th day of January 2012.
                                                          CHAN YENG KIT
                                                          Permanent Secretary,
                                                         Ministry of Information,
                                                       Communications and the Arts,
                                                                Singapore.
