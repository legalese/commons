             VARIABLE CAPITAL COMPANIES ACT 2018

                    VARIABLE CAPITAL COMPANIES
                  (CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS TO
                      OTHER ACTS) ORDER 2022

 In exercise of the powers conferred by section 167(1) of the
Variable Capital Companies Act 2018, the Minister for Finance
makes the following Order:

Citation and commencement
 1.—(1) This Order is the Variable Capital Companies
(Consequential Amendments to Other Acts) Order 2022 and,
except for paragraph 12, comes into operation on 13 January 2022.
 (2) Paragraph 12 comes into operation on 30 June 2022.
