  STATUTES (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT 2022

  STATUTES (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT 2022
        (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2022

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Statutes
(Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2022, the Minister for Law makes
the following Notification:
 1. This Notification is the Statutes (Miscellaneous Amendments)
Act 2022 (Commencement) Notification 2022.
 2. The Statutes (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2022, except
sections 4(1)(a) to (d) and (f), 10, 12(2) to (6), 16(b), 20(2) and 22(3),
comes into operation on 1 November 2022.

 Made on 28 October 2022.
