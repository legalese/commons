                 info-communications media
               development AUTHORITY Act 2016
              (Commencement) Notification 2016

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the
Info-communications Media Development Authority Act 2016, the
Minister for Communications and Information makes the following
Notification:

  1. This Notification is the Info-communications Media
Development Authority Act 2016 (Commencement) Notification
2016.

  2. The Info-communications Media Development Authority Act
2016, except sections 89, 90(1) and 96(v), comes into operation on
1 October 2016.

  3. Sections 89, 90(1) and 96(v) of the Info-communications
Media Development Authority Act 2016 come into operation on
2 October 2016.

   Made on 28 September 2016.
