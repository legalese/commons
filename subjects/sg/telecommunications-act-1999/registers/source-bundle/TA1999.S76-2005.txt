NO. S 76

      TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2005
                  (ACT 10 OF 2005)

          TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT
            (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2005

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the
Telecommunications (Amendment) Act 2005, the Minister for
Information, Communications and the Arts hereby makes the
following Notification:

  1. This Notification may be cited as the Telecommunications
(Amendment) Act (Commencement) Notification 2005.

  2. The Telecommunications (Amendment) Act 2005 shall, with
the exception of sections 10 and 11, come into operation on
16th February 2005.

   Made this 7th day of February 2005.
                                                           TAN CHIN NAM
                                                          Permanent Secretary,
                                                         Ministry of Information,
                                                       Communications and the Arts,
                                                                Singapore.
