     TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2016
                 (ACT 30 OF 2016)

     TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2016
         (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2017

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the
Telecommunications (Amendment) Act 2016, the Minister for
Communications and Information makes the following Notification:

 1. This Notification is the Telecommunications (Amendment)
Act 2016 (Commencement) Notification 2017.

  2. The Telecommunications (Amendment) Act 2016 comes into
operation on 1 February 2017.

   Made on 11 January 2017.
