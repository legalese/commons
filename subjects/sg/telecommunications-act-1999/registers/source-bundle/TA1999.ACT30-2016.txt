                         EXPLANATORY STATEMENT
        This Bill seeks to amend the Telecommunications Act (Cap. 323) for the
     following purposes:
           (a) to provide the Info‑communications Media Development Authority
               (the Authority) with the powers to regulate and facilitate the use of and
               access to rooftop space for mobile deployment;
           (b) to clarify the notification and objection process for public
               telecommunication licensees’ and telecommunication licensees’ entry
               to land and buildings;
           (c) to enhance and strengthen the Authority’s oversight of the
               telecommunication industry;
           (d) to provide the Authority with the power to establish or approve one or
               more alternative dispute resolution schemes for the telecommunication
               sector;
           (e) to provide clarity to certain provisions of the Act;
