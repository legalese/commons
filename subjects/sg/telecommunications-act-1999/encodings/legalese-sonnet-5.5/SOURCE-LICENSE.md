# Source licence

This encoding's licence file points at the subject-level one: [`../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md`](../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md).
It governs the quoted text of the Telecommunications Act 1999 (Singapore Statutes Online, standing grant in SSO's Terms of Use cl. 13, with the attribution that file requires).

## The instruments made under the Act

The text of each instrument made under the Act, with the gazetted instruments that amend it, is deposited in its own subject under `subjects/sg/telecommunications-*/registers/source-bundle/`.
Each subject has its own `SOURCE-LICENSE.md`, and each text file has a `.meta.json` giving the URL of the PDF it was read from, the retrieval date (2026-11-03), the sha256 of the PDF and of the text, the header line as printed, and how it was obtained.
The copies were fetched from IMDA's own site, not from Singapore Statutes Online.
The reproduction relies on SSO's Terms of Use cl. 13, with the cl. 13(a) attribution as in the Act-level file, because the text is Singapore legislation.
It is a reliance, not a clearance obtained from IMDA or from the Attorney-General's Chambers, and IMDA's own Terms of Use (paras 6 and 7) restrict the contents of IMDA's site.
The quotations in `sgta-i-*.l4` and in `TV-QUESTION.md` are taken from those deposited texts.
Two amending instruments are scans read by OCR (S 685/2011 and S 686/2011); the OCR is not checked against the page images beyond what the encoder read.
