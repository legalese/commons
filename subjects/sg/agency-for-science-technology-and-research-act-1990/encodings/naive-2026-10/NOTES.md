## Why this Act, and why scoped

Requirement **REQ-0155** in `subjects/sg/requirements.jsonl`: Tier 3 of the remaining
Singapore Acts, ordered by everyday-life relevance. The requirement asks what the Act
decides for a person or business it applies to; no scenario has asked a sharper
question yet.

The Act sets up a statutory board, so most of it is constitutional. This row takes
what an outsider, a grant-seeker, a member or an employee actually meets: the two
offences (ss 19, 20), the bar on personal suits (s 17), the financial-assistance power
(s 7(1)(d)), executing a deed (s 4(2)), the financial year (s 14), the metrology staff
transferred in 2008 (s 23), and the Schedule rules on membership, disqualification,
vacation, quorum, voting and requisitioned meetings (s 5; Schedule paras 3(1), 9, 11,
13). Not encoded: the functions (s 6) beyond s 6(4), the rest of s 7, money matters
(ss 8-12), Ministerial directions (s 15), the chief executive (s 16), the 2002 renaming
saving (s 21), the 2008 transfer of property and contracts (ss 22, 24), pre-transfer
misconduct (s 25), and the remaining Schedule paragraphs.

## What the Act turns out to say

### 1. A composition with creditors disqualifies for life; bankruptcy only while undischarged

Schedule para 11(b) bars anyone who "is an undischarged bankrupt or has made any
arrangement or composition with his or her creditors". The bankruptcy limb ends on
discharge; the composition limb has no time limit in the text. So on the words, a
discharged bankrupt may sit on the Agency but someone who compounded with creditors
thirty years ago may not. A conviction for dishonesty disqualifies unless there is a
free pardon (para 11(c)); a mental disorder disqualifies only with incapacity to manage
oneself or one's affairs (para 11(a)). Asserted.

### 2. Using the A*STAR symbol is an offence with no stated exception

s 20(2) makes it an offence for "any person" to use a symbol identical to the
Agency's, or so like it as to be likely to deceive or confuse. The text states no
exception for use with the Agency's consent and no mental element; whether one is
implied is not something the deposit answers. The maximum is $2,000 or 6 months,
the same fine as the secrecy offence but half its imprisonment (s 19(2): $2,000 or
12 months). Asserted.

### 3. The secrecy duty binds insiders only

s 19(1) binds "a member, officer, employee or agent of the Agency", and only for
information about a person's affairs obtained in the course of duties. Disclosure
for those duties, when lawfully required by a court, or where a written law requires
or allows it, is not an offence. An outsider repeating the same information commits
no offence under this Act. Asserted.

### 4. Individuals are shielded from suit; the Agency is not, but its functions cannot be enforced

s 17: no suit lies "personally" against a member, officer, employee or person acting
under the Agency's direction for what is done in good faith in executing the Act. The
Agency itself remains capable of "suing and being sued" (s 3(1)(a)). But s 6(4) says
nothing in s 6 imposes "any form of duty or liability enforceable by proceedings
before any court", so a person cannot sue the Agency for failing to promote science
or fund research. Asserted for s 17; s 6(4) is not asserted.

### 5. Financial assistance is a power, in areas the Agency itself defines

s 7(1)(d) says the Agency "may" provide financial assistance (including scholarships)
for training manpower in Singapore for research and development or commercial
application, or to any public- or private-sector person for research or commercial
application "in areas defined by the Agency". s 2 defines financial assistance
widely: grants, loans, guarantees, bonds and equity. Nothing gives an applicant a
right to it. Asserted (within or outside the power, not entitlement).

### 6. The quorum can be a fraction, so it effectively rounds up

Schedule para 13(2): the quorum is the higher of one-third of all members and 3. With
13 members, one-third is 4⅓, so 4 present is not enough and 5 is (an inference that
"one-third" must be met in full; the text does not round). s 2 counts the Chairperson
as a member. A tie is broken by the presiding member's casting vote, in addition to
the original vote (para 13(3)). Four members' written requisition obliges the
Chairperson to convene within 7 days (para 13(6)). Asserted.

### 7. A stale cross-reference after the 2020 renumbering (not asserted)

s 22(3) defines "Board" for "this section and sections 23, 24 and 27". The deposit's
comparative table shows that old ss 25 and 26 were omitted as spent and old s 27 is
now s 25; there is no s 27 in the 2020 Revised Edition. The reference reads as if it
should be to s 25 (an inference from the table).

### 8. The s 21(3) window may be empty (not asserted)

s 21(3) validates contracts made in the Agency's name "on or after 1 January 2002 but
prior to the commencement of" the 2002 amendment Act. The legislative history gives
that Act's commencement as 1 January 2002 (except ss 6, 8(b), 9 and 10, from
1 October 2002). If the relevant commencement is 1 January 2002, the window is empty;
which commencement s 21(3) means is not stated (inference).

### 9. Fewer than 8 members: constituted wrongly, but the Agency may still act (not asserted)

s 5(1) requires 8 to 20 members besides the Chairperson (asserted), but Schedule para
13(7) says "The Agency may act despite any vacancy in its membership", and para 14
saves acts despite a defect in appointment. The text does not say whether a
membership below 8 is a "vacancy".
