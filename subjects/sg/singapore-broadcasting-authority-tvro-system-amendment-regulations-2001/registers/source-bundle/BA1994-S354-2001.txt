NO. S 354

        SINGAPORE BROADCASTING AUTHORITY ACT
                     (CHAPTER 297)

            SINGAPORE BROADCASTING AUTHORITY
                (TVRO SYSTEM) (AMENDMENT)
                     REGULATIONS 2001

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 79 of the
Singapore Broadcasting Authority Act, the Singapore Broadcasting
Authority, with the approval of the Minister for Information and the
Arts, hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
  1. These Regulations may be cited as the Singapore Broadcasting
Authority (TVRO System) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and
shall come into operation on 1st August 2001.

Amendment of regulation 2
  2. Regulation 2 of the Singapore Broadcasting Authority (TVRO
System) Regulations (Rg 3) (referred to in these Regulations as the
principal Regulations) is amended —
      (a) by deleting the definition of ‘‘broadcast television receiver’’
          and substituting the following definition:
              ‘‘ ‘‘broadcast television receiver’’ means any apparatus
                     used for the visual and aural reception of any
                     broadcasting service;’’; and
      (b) by deleting the definitions of ‘‘licence’’ and ‘‘licensee’’ and
          substituting the following definitions:
              ‘‘ ‘‘licence’’ means a licence to install or operate one
                      or more TVRO systems;
                 ‘‘licensee’’ means a person to whom a licence is
                      granted;’’.
S 354/2001                       2

Deletion and substitution of regulations 3, 4 and 5
  3. Regulations 3, 4 and 5 of the principal Regulations are deleted
and the following regulations substituted therefor:
    ‘‘Application and qualifications
      3.—(1) An application for a licence —
        (a) shall be made in such form and manner as the Authority
            may determine; and
        (b) shall contain a full and true account of such particulars
            as the Authority may require.
       (2) No person other than an institution may be granted with
    a licence.

    Licence fee
       4.—(1) The Authority may refuse to grant or renew any
    licence unless such fee as the Authority may determine to be
    levied in respect of the licence is paid.
      (2) No fee or part thereof paid in respect of any licence
    granted by the Authority shall be refunded in respect of the
    unexpired period of any licence which has been cancelled or
    suspended in accordance with section 33, read with section 24,
    of the Act.

    Period of licence
     5. A licence shall be valid for such period as the Authority
    may determine unless it is sooner cancelled or suspended.’’.

Deletion and substitution of regulations 8 and 9
  4. Regulations 8 and 9 of the principal Regulations are deleted
and the following regulations substituted therefor:
    ‘‘Changes to or inaccuracy in particulars
      8. A licensee shall inform the Authority in writing of any
    change to or inaccuracy in any of its particulars furnished to
    the Authority under regulation 3 (1) (b).

    Surrender of licence
      9. A person whose licence is cancelled or suspended under
    section 33, read with section 24, of the Act shall, if required by
    the Authority, surrender the licence to the Authority within
    such time as the Authority may specify.’’.

Miscellaneous amendments
  5. The principal Regulations are amended —
    (a) by deleting the word ‘‘issued’’ in regulations 6 (1), 10 and
        14 and substituting in each case the word ‘‘granted’’;
    (b) by deleting the word ‘‘worked’’ in the 1st line of
        regulation 11 (1) and substituting the word ‘‘operated’’;
    (c) by deleting the word ‘‘work’’ in regulations 11 (2) (1st line)
        and 14 and substituting in each case the word ‘‘operate’’; and
    (d) by deleting the word ‘‘working’’ wherever it appears in
        regulations 11 (1) (3rd line and marginal note), 12 (1st line)
        and 14 (marginal note) and substituting in each case the
        word ‘‘operation’’.

  Made this 17th day of July 2001.


                                      NIAM CHIANG MENG
                                             Chairman,
                                  Singapore Broadcasting Authority.
