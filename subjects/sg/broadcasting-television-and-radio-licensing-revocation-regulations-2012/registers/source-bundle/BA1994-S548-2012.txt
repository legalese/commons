NO. S 548
                              BROADCASTING ACT
                                 (CHAPTER 28)

                  BROADCASTING
  (TELEVISION AND RADIO LICENSING) (REVOCATION)
                REGULATIONS 2012

                       ARRANGEMENT OF REGULATIONS

Regulation
    1.   Citation and commencement
    2.   Revocation and savings



  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 66 of the
Broadcasting Act, the Media Development Authority of Singapore,
with the approval of the Minister for Information, Communications
and the Arts, hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
  1. These Regulations may be cited as the Broadcasting (Television
and Radio Licensing) (Revocation) Regulations 2012 and shall be
deemed to have come into operation on 1st January 2011.

Revocation and savings
 2.—(1) The Broadcasting (Television and Radio Licensing)
Regulations (Rg 1) (referred to in these Regulations as the revoked
Regulations) are revoked.
  (2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), the revoked Regulations shall
continue to apply to and in relation to —
     (a) every licence granted under section 20(1) of the Act, in respect
         of any broadcast television receiver or broadcast sound
         receiver, for any period before 1st January 2011;
    (b) every broadcast television receiver or broadcast sound
         receiver in respect of which any licence referred to in
         sub-paragraph (a) has been granted, in respect of any matter
S 548/2012                        2

          concerning that broadcast television receiver or broadcast
          sound receiver which occurred before 1st January 2011; and
      (c) every person to whom any licence referred to in
          sub-paragraph (a) has been granted, in respect of any thing
          which he is required, before 1st January 2011, to do under
          the revoked Regulations,
as if these Regulations had not been made.
  (3) Any fee for the grant or renewal of a licence, and any additional
fee for the variation of a licence, that has been paid in advance under
regulation 5 of the revoked Regulations, for any period commencing on
or after 1st January 2011, shall be refunded to the licensee in full.

  Made this 30th day of October 2012.
