# Source licence note: past editions of the Broadcasting Act taken from the e-Gazette

This note covers `BA1994.20030731.txt` and `BA1994.20120831.txt` and their `.meta.json` files in this directory.
It does not replace or change `../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md`, which covers `BA1994.txt` (the Singapore Statutes Online rendering), and that file was not edited.

## What the texts are

- `BA1994.20030731.txt`: the Broadcasting Act (Chapter 28), **Revised Edition 2003 (31st July 2003)**, as the Gazette PDF prints it ("Prepared and Published by the Law Revision Commission ... Printed by the Government Printer, Singapore 2003").
- `BA1994.20120831.txt`: the Broadcasting Act (Chapter 28), **Revised Edition 2012 (31st August 2012)**, as the Gazette PDF prints it.
- Each is the output of `pdftotext -layout` on a PDF that has a text layer, unedited (no OCR); the PDF is the source and the text is an aid for quotation.
  The URL, the retrieval time, the sha256 of the PDF and of the text, and how each was obtained are in the `.meta.json` beside it.

## Where they came from

Each PDF was fetched on 2026-11-03 from the e-Gazette's static file host (`assets.egazette.gov.sg`), by one request with the User-Agent `legalese-commons-source-fetcher/1.0 (+https://github.com/legalese/commons)`, at least four seconds after the previous request.
The e-Gazette's `robots.txt` (`https://www.egazette.gov.sg/robots.txt`) says `Allow: /` and `Disallow: /search`; no search path was used.

## Status of the texts

The e-Gazette FAQ (`https://www.egazette.gov.sg/faq/`, read 2026-11-03) says: "Before 9 March 2025, only copies of the Gazette printed by the Government Printer are regarded as prima facie evidence of their contents. These printed copies prevail over all other forms of the Gazette if there is any inconsistency."
Both editions here were published before that date.
So these copies are an aid and are not stated to be prima facie evidence of their contents.

## What this is not

The deposit is a **reliance** on the e-Gazette's clause 11 permission, not a clearance from the Government or the e-Gazette.
Whether a Revised Edition printed as a legislation supplement is an "e-Gazette" within clause 11 is read here in the way the terms' own heading ("Reproduction of legislation-Gazettes") suggests; no one has asked the Government.
