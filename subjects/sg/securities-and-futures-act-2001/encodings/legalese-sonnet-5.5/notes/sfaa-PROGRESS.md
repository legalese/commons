## Decisions (with the licence)

The fork register in NOTES.md is the full list (A1-A7, B2-B18, C1, D1, D2); the ones that shape the code:

- A1 s 130(6): "becomes aware" is the earlier of the actual day and the day reasonable diligence would have given awareness (the text of s 130(6)).
- A2 s 133(6): "below the age of 21 years" limits the children, not the spouse.
- A3 notices: a notice given on or before the last day is in time (`AT MOST`), not the keel window "after the event".
- "immediately" (s 137C(1)) is `WITHIN 0` in a trace.
- Split sites: every site of kind (i) (an unmet element of an offence) and (ii) (a gated "may") answers `NOTHING`, each tagged `-- SPLIT-SITE (i)` or `(ii)` on its branch and listed with its own words in NOTES.md `## Split sites (sfaa)`.
- Function named for a grant or a power whose "if" gate fails: `NOTHING` (the grant ruling of 2026-11-12, limit 1); bars, duties and the provision's own question keep `JUST FALSE`.
- Duty "where" gates (s 136(1), 137A, 137B): `JUST FALSE` (periods ruling (b)); class-limited duties ("A person who ...", "Every director ..."): `NOTHING` outside the class.
- Seat: `s 137J(1)` and `s 137U(1)` are answered by calling `s 135(1)` with the unitholder's facts and "ceased" FALSE (fork C1).
