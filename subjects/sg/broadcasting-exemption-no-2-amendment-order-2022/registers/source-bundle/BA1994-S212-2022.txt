                           BROADCASTING ACT 1994

                 BROADCASTING (EXEMPTION) (NO. 2)

Broadcasting Act 1994, the Minister for Communications and
Information makes the following Order:

Citation
 1. This Order is the Broadcasting (Exemption) (No. 2)
(Amendment) Order 2022.

Amendment of paragraph 2
  2. Paragraph 2 of the Broadcasting (Exemption) (No. 2) Order (O 2)
is amended by deleting sub-paragraph (a).
