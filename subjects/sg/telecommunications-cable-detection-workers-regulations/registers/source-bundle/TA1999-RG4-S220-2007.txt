                        TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT
                             (CHAPTER 323)

    TELECOMMUNICATIONS (CABLE DETECTION WORKERS)
            (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2007

        In exercise of the powers conferred by section 74 of the Telecommunications Act,
the Info-communications Development Authority of Singapore, with the approval of the
Minister for Information, Communications and the Arts, hereby makes the following
Regulations:

Citation and commencement
1. These Regulations may be cited as the Telecommunications (Cable Detection
Workers) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2007.

Amendment of regulation 8
2. The Telecommunications (Cable Detection Workers) Regulations (Rg 4) (referred to
in these Regulations as the principal Regulations) are amended by renumbering
regulation 8 as paragraph (1) of that regulation, and by inserting immediately thereafter
the following paragraphs:
        “(2) It shall be a condition of every licence that the licensee shall carry out his
        duties as a telecommunication cable detection worker in a safe, careful and proper
        manner.
        (3) A licensee shall not cause any telecommunication cable detection work to be
        carried out by any person, whether employed by him or otherwise, who is not a
        licensee.”.

Amendment of regulation 10
3. Regulation 10 of the principal Regulations is amended by deleting paragraph (3) and
substituting the following paragraph:
        “(3) Where the Authority is satisfied in any case that a licensee has failed to
        comply with the condition referred to in regulation 8 (2), the Authority may,
        without prejudice to any other action that it is entitled to take under the Act or
        these Regulations, order the licensee to undergo such retraining as the Authority
        may specify for a period not exceeding 12 months.”.
Amendment of regulation 14
4. Regulation 14 of the principal Regulations is amended by deleting the words
“regulation 8 (b), (c), (d) or (e)” in paragraph (a) and substituting the words “regulation 8
(1) (b), (c), (d) or (e) or (3)”.
