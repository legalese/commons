NO. S 750

             BROADCASTING (AMENDMENT) ACT 2004
                      (ACT 55 OF 2004)

               BROADCASTING (AMENDMENT) ACT
              (COMMENCEMENT) NOTIFICATION 2004

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the
Broadcasting (Amendment) Act 2004, the Minister for Information,
Communications and the Arts hereby makes the following
Notification:

 1. This Notification may be cited as the Broadcasting (Amendment)
Act (Commencement) Notification 2004.

  2. The Broadcasting (Amendment) Act 2004 shall come into
operation on 1st January 2005.

   Made this 9th day of December 2004.
                                                          TAN CHIN NAM
                                                         Permanent Secretary,
                                                        Ministry of Information,
                                                      Communications and the Arts,
                                                               Singapore.
