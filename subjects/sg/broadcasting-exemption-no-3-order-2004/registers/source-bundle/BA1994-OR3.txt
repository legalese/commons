Broadcasting Act (CHAPTER 28, Section 60( b )) Broadcasting (Exemption) (No. 3) Order O 3 G.N. No. S 378/2004 REVISED EDITION 2007 (2nd July 2007) [1st October 1994]
Citation
1. This Order may be cited as the Broadcasting (Exemption) (No. 3) Order.
Exemption
2. The Minister hereby exempts from section 43 of the Act any person who receives funds from loans, bank guarantees, letters of credit or other credit facilities granted to or in favour of it, by banks or other financial institutions licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore, provided that such funds are intended for bona fide commercial purposes.
[G.N. No. S 378/2004]
