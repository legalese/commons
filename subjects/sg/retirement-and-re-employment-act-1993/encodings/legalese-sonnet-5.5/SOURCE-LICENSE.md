# Source licence

This file points at the subject-level licence note and records what this encoding quotes.
It does not repeat the terms.

- The terms the quoted legal text carries are in the subject-level licence note written when the text was deposited: `inputs/STUB-SOURCE-LICENSE.md` in the run, which becomes the subject's `SOURCE-LICENSE.md`. Read the grant, its conditions and the required attribution there.
- The quoted text is the Retirement and Re-employment Act 1993 (Singapore), 2020 Revised Edition, informal consolidation, version in force from 1/7/2022, from Singapore Statutes Online (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/RRA1993).
- The file the quotations were taken from is `RRA1993.txt`, sha256 `f3034e0effbc40f5aa42e8367eff9114f926676bbed7fe0379c3ad26a792fee5`.
- `RRA1993.txt` is a plain-text rendering made by a converter script. It is not the official text, and nothing in this encoding is verified against the official text.
- The licence note and `RRA1993.meta.json` (`text_deposited: false`) were written before the text file was deposited, and do not describe it. The text file named above was deposited for this run, and every `-- src:` line in the `.l4` files is copied from it mechanically by `srcrefs.py quote`.
- The `.l4` files quote the Act in `-- src:` lines, so they reproduce the text; that reproduction is subject to the terms in the licence note.
- The licence of the encoding itself (the L4, the tests, `NOTES.md`) is Apache-2.0 like the rest of the Commons repository.
