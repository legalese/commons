No. S 852
                    TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT 1999

                         TELECOMMUNICATIONS
                      (WIRING WORK) (AMENDMENT)
                            REGULATIONS 2024

 In exercise of the powers conferred by section 97 of the
Telecommunications Act 1999, the Info‑communications Media
Development Authority, with the approval of the Minister for
Digital Development and Information, makes the following
Regulations:

Citation and commencement
 1. These Regulations are the Telecommunications (Wiring Work)
(Amendment) Regulations 2024 and come into operation on
11 November 2024.

Amendment of First Schedule
 2. In the Telecommunications (Wiring Work) Regulations 2024
(G.N. No. S 559/2024), in the First Schedule, in item 1, in the
second column —
         (a) in paragraph (a), delete “(CoC)”;
         (b) in paragraph (e), delete “or” at the end;
         (c) in paragraph (f), replace the full-stop at the end with “; or”;
             and
         (d) after paragraph (f), insert —
                           “(g) The Certificate of Competency for Fibre Optics
                                  Cabling    Installer         conducted         by      Republic
                                  Polytechnic.”.
