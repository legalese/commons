NO. S 878

                COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2005
                        (ACT 21 OF 2005)

   COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT (COMMENCEMENT)
                NOTIFICATION 2005

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Companies
(Amendment) Act 2005, the Minister for Finance hereby makes the
following Notification:

 1. This Notification may be cited as the Companies (Amendment)
Act (Commencement) Notification 2005.

  2. The Companies (Amendment) Act 2005 shall come into operation
on 30th January 2006.

   Made this 21st day of December 2005.
                                                                   LIM SIONG GUAN
                                                                   Permanent Secretary,
                                                                   Ministry of Finance,
                                                                       Singapore.
