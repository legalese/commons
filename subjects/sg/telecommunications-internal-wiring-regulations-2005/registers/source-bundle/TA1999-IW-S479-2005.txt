        In exercise of the powers conferred by section 74 of the Telecommunications Act,
the Info-communications Development Authority of Singapore, with the approval of the
Minister for Information, Communications and the Arts, hereby makes the following
Regulations:
                                      PART I
                                   PRELIMINARY

Citation and commencement
1. These Regulations may be cited as the Telecommunications (Internal Wiring)
Regulations 2005 and shall come into operation on 21st July 2005.

Definitions
2. In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires —
       "Code of Practice" means the Code of Practice published by the Authority under
               regulation 4 (1);
       "interface point" means any point of interconnection between a user’s
               telecommunication equipment and any telecommunication system of a
               public telecommunication licensee;
       "internal telecommunication wiring" means any telecommunication line, optical
               fibre, conduit or other physical medium connecting a user’s
               telecommunication equipment and any interface point but does not include
               the use of extension cords with built-in connectors and sockets;
       "licence" means a telecommunication wiring contractor’s class licence granted
               under regulation 5 or a telecommunication wiring installer’s licence
               granted under regulation 6;
       "licensee" means any person to whom a licence has been granted under these
               Regulations;
       "telecommunication equipment" means any appliance, apparatus or accessory
               used or intended to be used for telecommunication;
       "telecommunication wiring contractor" means any person who —
               (a) in the course of or in connection with or for the purposes of any trade
               or business carried on by him, performs or carries out any
               telecommunication wiring work or who offers to perform or carry out such
               work or who causes or arranges for such work to be performed or carried
               out; or
               (b) for profit or reward, offers his services as a telecommunication wiring
               contractor;
       "telecommunication wiring installer" means any person whose trade or
               occupation requires or includes the personal performance by him of any
               telecommunication wiring work;
       "telecommunication wiring work" includes the installation, maintenance or repair
               of a user’s internal telecommunication wiring;
       "user" means a person who has subscribed to any telecommunication service of a
               public telecommunication licensee.
                                 PART II
                      GENERAL LICENSING CONDITIONS

Licensing authority
3. The licensing authority in respect of every licence granted under these Regulations
shall be the Authority with the consent of, or under the terms of a general authority given
by, the Minister.

Code of Practice
4. —(1) The Authority shall publish a Code of Practice setting out the technical
standards and specifications for the performance or carrying out of telecommunication
wiring work, which the Authority may, from time to time, modify.
(2) The Code of Practice may be made available to any person upon request and upon
payment of the fee specified in the First Schedule.

Telecommunication wiring contractor’s class licence
5. —(1) Subject to regulation 8, no person other than a holder of a telecommunication
wiring contractor’s class licence shall —
       (a) act as a telecommunication wiring contractor;
       (b) advertise, notify or state that he carries on or is willing to carry on the business
       of a telecommunication wiring contractor; or
       (c) in any way hold himself out as ready to undertake for payment or other
       remuneration (whether monetary or otherwise) any of the functions of a
       telecommunication wiring contractor.
(2) A person shall be deemed to have been granted a telecommunication wiring
contractor’s class licence if he has —
       (a) registered with the Authority in such form and manner as may be specified by
       the Authority;
       (b) submitted to the Authority upon registration such information in writing as the
       Authority may require; and
       (c) paid to the Authority the fee specified in the First Schedule.
(3) A telecommunication wiring contractor’s class licensee shall —
       (a) comply with the conditions of class licence set out in the Second Schedule;
       and
       (b) notify the Authority in writing of any change in any information furnished to
       the Authority under this regulation within 14 days of such change.
(4) The Authority may issue a written acknowledgment in such form as the Authority
may determine to indicate that a telecommunication wiring contractor has been granted a
telecommunication wiring contractor’s class licence in accordance with these
Regulations.
Telecommunication wiring installer’s licence
6. —(1) Subject to regulation 8, no person other than a holder of a telecommunication
wiring installer’s licence shall —
        (a) act as a telecommunication wiring installer;
        (b) advertise, notify or state that he carries on or is willing to carry on the business
        of a telecommunication wiring installer; or
        (c) in any way hold himself out as ready to undertake for payment or other
        remuneration (whether monetary or otherwise) any of the functions of a
        telecommunication wiring installer.
(2) An application for a telecommunication wiring installer’s licence shall be made to
the Authority in such form and manner as the Authority may require and shall be
accompanied by —
        (a) the appropriate fee specified in the First Schedule; and
        (b) such particulars, information and documents as the Authority may require.
(3) The Authority may refuse to grant an application for a telecommunication wiring
installer’s licence if the Authority is not satisfied as to the educational qualification or
experience of the applicant, having regard to the nature of the duties of a
telecommunication wiring installer.
(4) A telecommunication wiring installer’s licence shall be in such form as may be
provided by the Authority and shall be subject to such conditions as the Authority may,
from time to time, determine.
(5) The Authority may at any time, by notice in writing, vary all or any of the conditions
upon which a telecommunication wiring installer’s licence is granted or impose
additional conditions and the licensee shall, at his own expense, comply with the varied
or additional conditions.
(6) A telecommunication wiring installer’s licensee shall notify the Authority in writing
of any change in any information furnished to the Authority under this regulation or as
specified in his licence within 14 days of such change.

Duration
7. A licence shall remain valid unless cancelled or suspended in accordance with the
provisions of the Act or these Regulations.

Wiring work within own premises
8. —(1) Regulations 5 (1) and 6 (1) shall not apply to any person who performs or
carries out any telecommunication wiring work in any premises of which he is the owner
or occupier.
(2) No owner or occupier of any premises shall perform or carry out any
telecommunication wiring work in that premises unless such work is done in accordance
with the Code of Practice.
No assignment, transfer, etc., of benefits of licence
9. A licensee shall not assign, transfer, sublet or otherwise dispose of the benefit of any
licence granted under these Regulations, or permit another person to participate in any of
the benefits, powers or authority granted under the licence except with the consent in
writing of the Authority.

Replacement licence
10. —(1) If the Authority is satisfied that any licence has been lost, destroyed or
defaced, the Authority may, upon payment of the fee specified in the First Schedule,
issue a replacement licence.
(2) No replacement licence shall be issued in place of a defaced licence unless the
defaced licence is first surrendered to the Authority for cancellation.

Cancellation and suspension of licence
11. —(1) The Authority may, by notice in writing, cancel or suspend, for such period as
it thinks fit, a licence if the licensee —
        (a) has performed or carried out any telecommunication wiring work otherwise
        than in accordance with the Code of Practice;
        (b) has caused or allowed any telecommunication wiring work to be performed or
        carried out by an unlicensed telecommunication wiring installer;
        (c) has made, or caused, or allowed to be made or produced in or in connection
        with any application by him, or on his behalf, for a licence, any false, misleading
        or inaccurate information, whether in writing or not;
        (d) has, in the opinion of the Authority, contravened any of the provisions of the
        Act or these Regulations or with any of the conditions of the licence;
        (e) gives notice to the Authority in such form and manner as the Authority may
        determine that he has ceased to be a telecommunication wiring contractor or
        telecommunication wiring installer, as the case may be;
        (f) has ceased to carry on any business or trade as a telecommunication wiring
        contractor or telecommunication wiring installer and his registration, if any, of the
        business under the Business Registration Act (Cap. 32) is cancelled; or
        (g) being a company incorporated under the Companies Act (Cap. 50) or a limited
        liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2005
        (Act 5 of 2005), has gone into liquidation other than for the purpose of
        amalgamation or reconstruction.
(2) Upon the cancellation or during the period of suspension of any licence, the licensee
shall for the purposes of the Act and these Regulations be deemed to be not licensed.
(3) The licensee whose licence is cancelled or suspended shall, if required by the
Authority, surrender the licence to the Authority within such time as the Authority may
specify.
(4) A licensee whose licence has been cancelled or suspended by the Authority, or
terminated at his own request, shall not be entitled to any compensation or refund of fees
by the Authority in respect of the unexpired period of the licence.
(5) A cancellation of a telecommunication wiring contractor’s class licence of a person
shall not operate so as to prevent such person from subsequently being deemed a holder
of such a licence in accordance with regulation 5.

Inspection of telecommunication wiring work at user’s request
12. —(1) The Authority may, at the request of any user, inspect the telecommunication
wiring work performed or carried out by a licensee to ensure that such work is in
accordance with the Code of Practice.
(2) Upon inspection, the Authority may —
       (a) advise the user that the telecommunication wiring work has been carried out in
       accordance with the Code of Practice; or
       (b) where any telecommunication wiring work has not been carried out in
       accordance with the Code of Practice, direct the licensee in writing to regularise,
       within such period as the Authority may determine and to its satisfaction, such
       telecommunication wiring work at his own expense.
(3) Any licensee who fails to comply with the directions issued by the Authority under
paragraph (2) (b) shall be guilty of an offence.
(4) The Authority may impose the fee specified in the First Schedule for the second or
any subsequent inspection requested by the user under paragraph (1).

Register of licensees
13. —(1) The Authority may, from time to time, cause the names of all persons who
hold valid licences and such other information as the Authority thinks fit to be entered in
a register kept for that purpose.
(2) The Authority may make available the list of licensees to any person upon request.

Cessation or refusal by licensees to perform or carry out telecommunication wiring
work
14. A licensee may, with the approval of the Authority, cease or refuse to perform or
carry out any telecommunication wiring work for any user if he is of the opinion that —
       (a) the user's internal telecommunication wiring is not in accordance with the
       Code of Practice; or
       (b) such telecommunication wiring work is likely to threaten the safety or proper
       functioning of any telecommunication system of a public telecommunication
       licensee.

                                     PART III
                                 MISCELLANEOUS
Offence
15. Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of these Regulations shall be
guilty of an offence.

Waiver
16. The Authority may, in its discretion —
      (a) waive all or any conditions in any licence; and
      (b) subject to such conditions as it thinks fit, waive or reduce the payment of the
      fee payable for a licence.

Revocation and transitional provisions
17. —(1) The Telecommunication (Internal Wiring) Regulations (Rg 9) are revoked.
(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), any licence granted under the revoked
Telecommunication (Internal Wiring) Regulations shall, unless revoked, cancelled or
suspended, and in so far as it is not inconsistent with these Regulations —
       (a) continue and be deemed to be a licence under these Regulations; and
       (b) be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the Second Schedule.

                                SECOND SCHEDULE
                                                               Regulations 5 (3) and 17 (2)

 CONDITIONS OF TELECOMMUNICATION WIRING CONTRACTOR’S
                     CLASS LICENCE

1. The holder of a telecommunication wiring contractor’s class licence (referred to in
this Schedule as the class licensee) shall at all times have in his employment a
telecommunication wiring installer who holds a valid telecommunication wiring
installer’s licence.
2. The class licensee shall ensure that only a holder of a valid telecommunication wiring
installer’s licence carries out any telecommunication wiring works in the course of the
class licensee’s trade or business.
3. The class licensee shall comply with the provisions of the Act, these Regulations and
any code of practice issued under the Act.
