No. S 478
                                BROADCASTING ACT
                                   (CHAPTER 28)

                            BROADCASTING
                     (CLASS LICENCE) (AMENDMENT)
                           NOTIFICATION 2020

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 9 of the Broadcasting
Act, the Info-communications Media Development Authority makes
the following Notification:

Citation and commencement
 1. This Notification is the Broadcasting (Class Licence)
(Amendment) Notification 2020 and comes into operation on
22 June 2020.

Amendment of paragraph 3A
 2. Paragraph 3A of the Broadcasting (Class Licence) Notification
(N 1) is amended by inserting, immediately after sub-paragraph (3),
the following sub-paragraph:
        “(4) This Notification also does not apply to or in relation to a
       person who is a class licensee by virtue of the Broadcasting
       (Class Licence — Broadcasting to Digital Display Panels)
       Notification 2020 (G.N. No. S 477/2020).”.
