DEFINITIONS
In this Schedule —
“applicant” means an applicant for the grant or renewal of a cleaning
business licence;
“company” means a company registered under the Companies Act 1967;
“financial statements”, in relation to an applicant, means —
(a) where the applicant is a company — the financial statements of
the applicant that are prepared in accordance with the
Companies Act 1967;
(b) where the applicant is a limited liability partnership — the
accounts and records of the applicant that are kept in accordance
with the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2005;
(c) where the applicant is a society — the accounts and records of
the applicant that are kept in accordance with the Societies Act
1966; or

PRESCRIBED REQUIREMENTS FOR APPLICA TION FOR GRANT
OR RENEW AL OF CLASS 1 CLEANING BUSINESS LICENCE
1. The applicant has a paid-up capital of at least $250,000 or (where the
applicant is not a company) the applicant has a net worth of not less than $250,000,
for the financial year immediately preceding the date of the application.
2. The applicant holds a bizSAFE Certification of level 3 or a higher level.
3. During the period of 24 months immediately before the date of application,
the applicant has not —
(a) contravened or breached any provision of the Act;
(b) been convicted of any off ence under the Central Provident Fund Act
1953, the Employment Act 1968, the Employment of Foreign
Manpower Act 1990 or the W orkplace Safety and Health Act 2006; or
(c) failed to comply with any order made by an Employment Claims
T ribunal under section 22 of the Employment Claims Act 2016.
4. Where the application is for the grant of a class 1 cleaning business licence,
during the period of 12 months immediately before the date of the application —
