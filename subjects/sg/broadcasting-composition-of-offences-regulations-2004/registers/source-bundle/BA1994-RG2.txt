Broadcasting Act (CHAPTER 28, Section 57(2)) Broadcasting (Composition of Offences) Regulations Rg 2 G.N. No. S 332/2004 REVISED EDITION 2007 (2nd July 2007) [21st June 2004]
Citation
1. These Regulations may be cited as the Broadcasting (Composition of Offences) Regulations.
Compoundable offence
2. The offence under section 47 of the Act may be compounded by the Authority in accordance with section 57 (1) of the Act.
[G.N. No. S 332/2004]
