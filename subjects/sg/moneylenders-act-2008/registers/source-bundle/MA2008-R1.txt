Principal place of business
6.—(1) An applicant for the issue of a licence which intends to
conduct its business of moneylending at 2 or more places of business
must, when applying to the Registrar under section 1 1 of the Act for
approval of the places of business for moneylending, notify the
Registrar in writing which of those places will be its principal place of
business.
(2) A licensee which intends to conduct its business of
moneylending at any place of business in addition to any place or
places of business already approved under section 1 1 of the Act must,
when applying to the Registrar under that section for approval of the
additional place of business for moneylending, notify the Registrar in
writing which of those places will be its principal place of business.
(3) A licensee which intends to change its principal place of
business for the conduct of its business of moneylending must notify
the Registrar in writing of this before carrying out the change.
