                                   PART I
                                PRELIMINARY

Citation
  1. These Regulations may be cited as the Broadcasting (Television
and Radio Licensing) Regulations.

Definitions
  2. —(1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise
requires —
        ‘‘broadcast sound receiver’’ means any apparatus used for the
            aural reception of broadcast matter transmitted by radio
            waves, wire or cable;
        ‘‘broadcast television receiver’’ means any apparatus used for
            the aural reception, and the visual reception in monochrome
            or colour, of broadcast matter transmitted by radio waves,
            wire or cable but does not include any Television Receive-
            Only Satellite Receiving System;
        ‘‘hotel’’ means any premises registered under the Hotels Act
            (Cap. 127) for use as a hotel;
        ‘‘Television Receive-Only Satellite Receiving System’’ or
            ‘‘TVRO system’’ means any apparatus (including a dish
            antenna) or any combination of apparatus capable of
            direct reception of broadcast matter emitted from or passing
            through any communication or broadcast satellite in
            extra-terrestrial space;

                                                                 29.2.2004
                    Broadcasting (Television and Radio
p. 4    2004 Ed.]        Licensing) Regulations             [CAP. 28, Rg 1

       ‘‘vessel’’ includes any aircraft, ship or boat not propelled by oars.
    (2) Unless the context otherwise requires, the words ‘‘licence’’ and
‘‘licensee’’ shall, when used in or with reference to the following
regulations, be construed respectively as meaning a licence or the
person to whom is granted a licence of the type specified against
such regulations, as the case may be:
       (a) regulations 17 to 22 — licence for dealing in broadcasting
           apparatus (other than a TVRO system);
       (b) regulations 23 to 26 — licence for broadcast sound receivers;
       (c) regulations 27 to 35 — licence for broadcast television
           receivers; and
       (d) regulations 36 and 37 — block licence for broadcast sound
           receivers and broadcast television receivers.


                                 PART II
                       GENERAL PROVISIONS
                      IN RESPECT OF LICENCES

Licence authority
  3.—(1) The Authority shall be the licensing authority in respect
of licences granted under these Regulations.
 (2) In this Part, ‘‘licence’’ means a licence granted under these
Regulations.

Operation of apparatus
   4.—(1) All apparatus for the reception of broadcast matter
licensed under these Regulations shall be operated in such manner
as to cause as little radiation as practicable from the aerial or from
the apparatus itself, and shall not in any case exceed the values laid
down in any regulations made under the Telecommunications Act
(Cap. 323) governing interference from electrical apparatus.
  (2) Where any broadcasting apparatus is situated within 800
metres of the boundary of any aerodrome, or within the approach
funnel (as specified by the chief executive officer of the Civil Aviation
Authority of Singapore) to any aerodrome, the aerial, or any mast
                  Broadcasting (Television and Radio
CAP. 28, Rg 1]         Licensing) Regulations              [2004 Ed. p. 5

supporting it, shall not exceed such height as may be approved by the
chief executive officer of the Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore.
  (3) Any aerial which crosses above or is liable to fall upon or to be
blown on to any overhead telecommunication, power wire or power
apparatus shall be adequately guarded.

Duration of and fee for licence
  5.—(1) The fee for any licence shall be payable in advance and
shall be —
     (a) in the case of a licence for 12 months, the fee set out in the
         First Schedule; and
     (b) in the case of a licence for any other period, the pro-rata rate
         of the appropriate annual licence fee set out in the First
         Schedule.
  (2) Subject to regulation 37 (1), a licence (other than a licence for
a broadcast sound receiver installed in a vehicle) shall be valid from
the first day of the month in which it is issued, and remain valid for
such period as the licensing authority may determine unless it is
sooner determined in accordance with these Regulations.
   (3) A licence for a broadcast sound receiver installed in a vehicle
shall, as from the date of its issue, be valid for such period as the
licensing authority may determine unless it is sooner determined in
accordance with these Regulations.
  (4) Where a licence has expired, it shall, subject to regulation 37 (1),
be renewable for such period as the licensing authority may determine
by the issue of a fresh licence commencing on the day following the
date of expiry of the previous licence.
   (5) Any person in possession of a current broadcast sound receiver
licence in respect of a vehicle or a vessel may, if the person so desires
and upon payment of an additional fee, apply to have the licence
varied to enable him to operate a broadcast television receiver until
the date of the expiration of the licence.
   (6) The additional fee referred to in paragraph (5) would be the
difference in the fee payable per annum for each unexpired year of
the licence between a licence for a broadcast sound receiver and a
licence for a broadcast television receiver as set out in the First
Schedule, subject to a minimum of —
                                                                  29.2.2004
                    Broadcasting (Television and Radio
p. 6    2004 Ed.]        Licensing) Regulations           [CAP. 28, Rg 1

       (a) $13 in a case falling within item 3 of the First Schedule; or
       (b) $83 in a case falling within item 4 of the First Schedule.
  (7) Any person in possession of a current licence to operate a
broadcast television receiver in monochrome may, upon payment of
an additional fee, apply to have the licence varied to enable him to
operate a broadcast television receiver in colour until the date of the
expiration of the licence.
  (8) The additional fee referred to paragraph (7) would be the
difference in the fee payable per annum for each unexpired year of
the licence between a licence for a broadcast television receiver in
monochrome and a licence for a broadcast television receiver in
colour as set out in the First Schedule, subject to a minimum of —
       (a) $52.50 in a case falling within item 6 of the First Schedule;
       (b) $35 in a case falling within item 8 of the First Schedule; or
       (c) $70 in all other cases.

Late payment fee
  6. A late payment fee for the grant or renewal of a licence for a
broadcast sound receiver or a broadcast television receiver shall be
in accordance with the Second Schedule.

Prosecution
  7. In any prosecution against a person holding an expired licence
in respect of a contravention of section 20 (1) of the Act or of
regulation 17, 27 or 36 —
       (a) the subsequent renewal of the expired licence under
           regulation 5 (4) shall not in any way affect or prejudice such
           prosecution; and
       (b) unless the licensing authority has been notified in writing by
           that person that the broadcasting apparatus is no longer in
           his possession, the onus of proving the same shall lie upon
           that person.

Exemptions
  8. The licensing authority may, in its discretion and subject to
such conditions as it thinks fit, waive or reduce the payment of the
                 Broadcasting (Television and Radio
CAP. 28, Rg 1]        Licensing) Regulations             [2004 Ed. p. 7

fee payable for a licence for any broadcast sound receiver or any
broadcast television receiver —
    (a) to a person who is required to use his broadcasting apparatus
        for Government purposes;
    (b) to a blind person for operating a broadcast sound receiver;
    (c) to a deaf person for operating a broadcast television
        receiver; or
    (d) in such other circumstances as may appear to the licensing
        authority to be reasonable.

Termination of licence
  9.—(1) The licensing authority may, by notice in writing,
determine any licence if the holder of the licence fails to comply with
the Act or these Regulations or with any condition of the licence, and
no fee or part thereof paid for such licence shall be refundable.
  (2) Where a licence is determined before its expiry for any reason
other than those specified in paragraph (1), the licensing authority
may, if it thinks fit, make a refund of the appropriate pro rata amount
for the unexpired period of the licence.

Transfer, assignment or disposal of licence
   10.—(1) Except as provided in paragraph (2), the holder of a
licence shall not transfer, assign or otherwise dispose of the licence.
   (2) Where the registered owner of a vehicle transfers the
registration of the vehicle to another person under the provisions of
the Road Traffic Act (Cap. 276), any licence which may have been
issued to him under these Regulations in respect of a broadcast sound
receiver installed in the vehicle shall be deemed to have been
transferred to the person to whom he has transferred the registration
of the vehicle.

Change of particulars
  11.—(1) The holder of a licence shall, subject to paragraph (2),
notify the licensing authority of any change in his address or in any
other of the particulars stated in the licence within 28 days of the
change.
                                                               29.2.2004
                   Broadcasting (Television and Radio
p. 8   2004 Ed.]        Licensing) Regulations          [CAP. 28, Rg 1

  (2) Where the holder of a licence who has changed his residential
address makes a report of the change under section 8 of the National
Registration Act (Cap. 201) within 28 days thereof, he shall be
deemed to have notified the licensing authority of the change in his
residential address in compliance with paragraph (1).

Telecommunications apparatus
  12.—(1) No broadcasting apparatus shall be used in a manner
which interferes or may interfere with the efficient or convenient
working or maintenance of any telephone, telegraph or power line
which already existed at the time the broadcasting apparatus was
installed.
  (2) No person shall use any broadcasting apparatus in such manner
as knowingly to cause interference or disturbance to reception by any
other broadcasting apparatus licensed under these Regulations or
the Telecommunications Act (Cap. 323).
   (3) No unauthorised person shall intercept any sign, signal, writing,
image, sound or intelligence of any nature transmitted by wire, radio,
visual or other electromagnetic system, which is not intended for the
general public.

Power to examine apparatus
  13. Any officer authorised in that behalf by the licensing authority
shall have power, on production of proof of identity, to enter at a
reasonable hour any vehicle, vessel, aircraft, premises or other place
where broadcasting apparatus is installed or is in the course of being
installed to examine or test that broadcasting apparatus.

Power to examine licence
  14. Any officer authorised in that behalf by the licensing authority
or any police officer shall be entitled, on production of proof of
identity, to examine any licence issued under the Act or these
Regulations.

Information
  15. The licensing authority may, by notice in writing, require any
person to furnish within a reasonable time such information as may

that person, for the purposes of the Act or these Regulations.

                              PART III
                       DEALERS IN
                 BROADCASTING APPARATUS

Broadcasting system not to include TVRO system
  16. In this Part, a reference to any broadcasting apparatus shall not
include a reference to any TVRO system or part thereof.

Dealers in apparatus
  17.—(1) No person shall offer for sale, sell or have in his
possession with a view to sale in the course of his business any
broadcasting apparatus without a licence to deal in such apparatus
and in respect of such place of business.
  (2) A person to whom a licence is granted shall, in respect of all
broadcasting apparatus sold by him, keep at every place of his
business a register in such form as the licensing authority may require.
  (3) For the purposes of this regulation, ‘‘sale’’, with its grammatical
variations, includes all transactions whatsoever as the result of which
the ownership of or an interest in goods is or may be transferred or
created.
   (4) For the purposes of this regulation, any sale made by a
travelling salesman in the employ of a licensee shall be deemed to
have been made at the premises specified in the licence of that
licensee.

Application for dealers’ licence
   18. An application for a dealer’s licence shall be made to the
licensing authority in writing.

Demonstration of apparatus
  19.—(1) The possession of a licence shall entitle the licensee to
demonstrate broadcasting apparatus at his place of business and at


                                                                 29.2.2004
                    Broadcasting (Television and Radio
p. 10   2004 Ed.]        Licensing) Regulations          [CAP. 28, Rg 1

the premises of prospective customers, but shall not entitle him to
operate the apparatus for the transmission of broadcast matter or
radiocommunication apparatus for demonstration.
  (2) In this regulation, ‘‘radiocommunication apparatus’’ means
any apparatus capable of transmitting radio waves.

Exhibition of licence
  20. The licensee shall exhibit his licence conspicuously in his place
of business.

Purchaser to furnish particulars
  21. Every purchaser of broadcasting apparatus from a licensee
shall furnish his name and address to the licensee.

Inspection of register
  22.—(1) A licensee shall at all reasonable times permit any officer
authorised in that behalf by the licensing authority to inspect the
register kept in accordance with regulation 17 (2).
  (2) The licensee shall —
    (a) supply copies of entries in that register or make a nil return
        at the end of every calendar month to the licensing authority
        or to any other officer whom the licensing authority may
        direct; and
    (b) furnish to the licensing authority or such officer such
        information as may be required with reference to sales of
        broadcasting apparatus.
  (3) Every such register shall be preserved by the licensee so that at
any time full particulars of transactions in the preceding calendar year
are available for inspection.
  (4) Dealers shall notify every purchaser of a broadcasting apparatus
of the obligation to obtain a licence for the operation of the set in
Singapore.

                              PART IV
               BROADCAST SOUND RECEIVERS

Broadcast sound receivers for vehicles or vessels
   23.—(1) A licence shall be required for every broadcast sound
receiver installed in a vehicle or in a vessel in respect of which a ship
station licence has not been granted under the Telecommunications
Act (Cap. 323).
   (2) An application for a licence may be made at any post office or
at such other places as the licensing authority may require.
  (3) A licence in respect of a broadcast sound receiver installed on
board a vessel shall be granted only in the name of the owner or
charterer of the vessel.
  (4) A licence issued in a place other than Singapore for any
broadcast sound receiver shall permit the use of the receiver in
Singapore for a period not exceeding 2 months.
  (5) For the purposes of this regulation, a broadcast sound receiver,
other than one which is merely carried in a vehicle or vessel, shall be
deemed to be installed therein if it is affixed in that vehicle or vessel
by means of brackets or other mountings whether temporarily or
permanently.

Exemption for temporary use of broadcast sound receiver
  24. A licence shall not be required for the temporary use of a
broadcast sound receiver in a public place or at an official or social
function or a function organised for charitable purposes.

Dimensions of external aerial
   25. The combined height and length of an external aerial for a
fixed broadcast sound receiver shall not, except with the written
consent of the licensing authority, exceed 30 metres.

Licensee may be required to cease operating broadcast sound receiver
   26. A licensee shall, if so required by the licensing authority, cease
to operate a broadcast sound receiver for any period specified by the
licensing authority.

                                 PART V
            BROADCAST TELEVISION RECEIVERS

Licence for broadcast television receiver
  27.—(1) No person shall operate a broadcast television receiver
without a licence.
   (2) An application for a licence may be made at any post office or
at such other places as the licensing authority may require.
   (3) A licence shall permit the use of one or more broadcast
television receivers and one or more broadcast sound receivers by the
licensee, members of his family and domestic servants resident at the
address specified therein.
   (4) A separate licence shall be required for a broadcast television
receiver used by a lodger, boarder, resident guest or any person
occupying a portion of the premises under a separate tenancy,
irrespective of whether or not such broadcast television receivers are
connected to a common aerial, wire or cable.

Broadcast television receiver in vehicle or vessel
  28.—(1) If any person wishes to mount a broadcast television
receiver in a vehicle or vessel, the owner of the vehicle or vessel shall
ensure that the receiver is so mounted that it is not possible for the
viewing screen to be seen from the normal driving position or from
the helmsman’s position.
   (2) A separate licence shall be required for every broadcast
television receiver installed in a vehicle or in a vessel in respect
of which a ship station licence has not been granted under the
Telecommunications Act (Cap. 323).
  (3) A licence in respect of a broadcast television receiver installed
on board a vessel shall be granted only in the name of the owner or
charterer of the vessel.
  (4) A licence issued in a place other than Singapore for any
broadcast television receiver shall permit the use of the receiver in
Singapore for a period not exceeding 2 months.
  (5) For the purposes of this regulation, a broadcast television
receiver, other than one which is merely carried in a vehicle or vessel,
                  Broadcasting (Television and Radio
CAP. 28, Rg 1]         Licensing) Regulations        [2004 Ed. p. 13

shall be deemed to be installed therein if it is affixed in that vehicle
or vessel by means of brackets or other mountings whether
temporarily or permanently.

Exemption for temporary use of broadcast television receiver
  29. A licence shall not be required for the temporary use of a
broadcast television receiver in a public place or at an official or social
function or a function organised for charitable purposes.

Broadcast television receivers in schools or institutions
  30.—(1) Subject to paragraph (2), in the case of a school or such
other institution as the licensing authority may for the purposes of
this regulation approve, a licence shall be regarded as permitting the
use therein of one or more broadcast television receivers and one or
more broadcast sound receivers.
   (2) A separate licence shall be required in respect of any broadcast
television receiver used by any member of the staff in residential
quarters, whether or not the quarters form part of the main premises
of the school or institution referred to in paragraph (1).

Broadcast television receivers in hospitals
  31.—(1) A separate licence shall be required —
    (a) for every broadcast television receiver installed in a hospital
        and for every extension from that broadcast television
        receiver; and
    (b) in respect of every broadcast television receiver used by a
        member of the staff in residential quarters, whether or not
        the quarters form part of the main premises.
 (2) A licence under paragraph (1) (a) shall permit the use of one or
more broadcast sound receivers.

Broadcast television receivers in hotels
   32.—(1) A licence taken out by the proprietor of a hotel shall be
regarded as permitting the use therein of one or more broadcast
television receivers and one or more broadcast sound receivers by the
proprietor, members of his family and resident staff, or by any person
in any common room at the disposal of paying guests.
                                                                   29.2.2004
                    Broadcasting (Television and Radio
p. 14   2004 Ed.]        Licensing) Regulations          [CAP. 28, Rg 1

   (2) A separate licence shall be required in respect of any broadcast
television receiver used in each and every apartment of any paying
guest in a hotel.
  (3) For the purposes of paragraph (2), any extension from a
broadcast television receiver shall require a separate licence.

Restricted monochrome licence
  33. For the purposes of regulations 27, 30 and 32, a licence to
operate a broadcast television receiver in monochrome shall not be
regarded as permitting the use of any broadcast television receiver in
colour.

Dimensions of broadcast television receivers aerials
  34. The licensing authority may, in its discretion, require that the
aerial for any broadcast television receiver shall comply with the
authority’s specifications with regard to dimensions, height and
method of support.

Licensee may be required to cease operating broadcast television
receiver
  35. A licensee shall, if so required by the licensing authority, cease
to operate a broadcast television receiver for any period specified by
the licensing authority.

                                PART VI
                  BLOCK LICENCE FOR
            BROADCAST SOUND RECEIVERS AND
            BROADCAST TELEVISION RECEIVERS
               IN HOTELS AND HOSPITALS

Block licence
   36. Notwithstanding the provisions of Parts IV and V, in the case
of a hotel or hospital, the licensing authority may permit the owner of
the hotel or hospital to apply for a block licence for all the broadcast
television receivers installed in the hotel or hospital, as the case may
be.
                     Broadcasting (Television and Radio
CAP. 28, Rg 1]            Licensing) Regulations        [2004 Ed. p. 15

Licensing authority empowered to vary expiration dates
   37.—(1) Where the existing licences for the broadcast television
receivers in any hotel or hospital have different expiry dates, the
licensing authority shall be empowered to vary the different expiry
dates of those licences to one common expiry date in issuing a block
licence.
   (2) The licensing authority, in varying the different expiry dates
of the licences under paragraph (1), shall take into account any
unexpired portion of the period of the licences and make appropriate
adjustments to the amount of licence fees payable for the block
licence.

                                   PART VII
                             MISCELLANEOUS

Licences
  38. The licences granted under these Regulations shall be in such
forms as the licensing authority may determine.

Authority not liable
   39.—(1) Nothing in these Regulations shall be construed as
rendering the Authority liable or responsible for any infringement by
a licensee in the exercise of his licence of copyright in any work or
of any patent for an invention or for any breach of any written law
arising out of the exercise of the licence.
   (2) Nothing in these Regulations shall affect the liability of the
licensee in respect of any such act done by him.
