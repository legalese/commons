## Marginal readings for a lawyer (roc12)

Two readings are the status quo here and are for a lawyer, not for the encoder, to decide (the rule of 2026-10-29).

- **(a)** A failed "if" or "where" counts as the text's no, clause by clause within the provision that holds it, and a flat negative counts as the text saying no. **Other reading:** the answer is `NOTHING` ("if A then B" does not say "if not A then not B"; a flat negative states a prohibition, not that its opposite is permitted).
- **(b)** A prohibition or grant limited by its own wording answers `NOTHING` outside the limit. **Other reading:** `JUST FALSE` outside the limit, as for (a).

A `FALSE-kind` branch whose quoted words are a closed "means" is not listed: the rule of 2026-10-29 itself makes that a no.

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (roc12)

Each function that answers a question about these Rules answers `JUST TRUE`, `JUST FALSE` or `NOTHING`.
`JUST FALSE` is the Rules themselves saying no on those facts, in words: the "where" or "if" of the rule is not met (the process is not for a foreign tribunal, the Minister did not send the letter, the occupier is a tenant holding over), a period the Rules fix has run out (14 days, 3 months, 2 clear days), a flat negative applies ("must not", "may not", "no person may") or a closed "means".
`NOTHING` is the Rules not answering: a scope limit without "only" and the case is outside it, a "must" that has not been done and for which the Rules state no consequence in the rule itself, an exception that lifts a bar (which grants nothing), "includes", a power or a Form or a fee that sits outside these Orders, or a Fourth Schedule row whose printed cells do not number its columns.
**A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`:** "the Rules do not say" is not "the Rules say no".
Where an answer is a number, a date or a text and so cannot carry `JUST FALSE`, the rule has a yes/no twin that does (for example `o 66 r 7(1) a final order made on that date is barred` beside `o 66 r 7(1) the final order for possession may be made`).
For a bar lifted by an exception, the permission twin answers `NOTHING` and the "barred" twin answers `JUST FALSE` (QA).
For a duty whose trigger is unmet, the companion "the duty arises" answers `JUST FALSE` and the compliance function answers `NOTHING` (QD).
