No. S 600

                              BROADCASTING ACT
                                 (CHAPTER 28)

        BROADCASTING (EXEMPTION) (AMENDMENT)
                     ORDER 2014

  In exercise of the powers conferred by section 60 of the
Broadcasting Act, the Minister for Communications and Information
hereby makes the following Order:

Citation and commencement
  1. This Order may be cited as the Broadcasting (Exemption)
(Amendment) Order 2014 and shall come into operation on
15 September 2014.

Amendment of paragraph 1
  2. Paragraph 1 of the Broadcasting (Exemption) Order (O 1) is
amended by inserting, immediately after the words “Notification
(N 1)” in sub-paragraph (f), the words “or subject to a notice under
paragraph 3A(1) of that Notification”.

Deletion of paragraph 2
   3. Paragraph 2 of the Broadcasting (Exemption) Order is deleted.

   Made on 12 September 2014.

                                                            AUBECK KAM
                                                          Permanent Secretary,

                                                            and Information,
