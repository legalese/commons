INCREASE IN RESPECT OF SOCIAL GRANTS

The Minister of Social Development has, in terms of the Social Assistance Act, 2004
(Act No. 13 of 2004) as amended, with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance,
announced the increase in social grants.

The new maximum monthly amounts are as follows:

| Type of Social Grant | Current Amount | New Amount 01 April 2026 | October 2026 |
| Older Persons | R 2 320 | R 2 400 | 0 |
| Older Persons 75 yrs+ (Additional payment) | R 2 320 + R 20 | R 2 400 +R20 | 0 |
| War Veterans’ (including Additional payment) | R 2 320 + R20 | R 2 400 +R20 | 0 |
| Disability | R 2 320 | R 2 400 | 0 |
| Care Dependency | R 2 320 | R 2 400 | 0 |
| Foster Child | R 1 250 | R 1 290 | R 1 300 |
| Child Support | R 560 | R 580 | 0 |
| Child Support (Top-Up) | R 560 + R 280 | R 580 + R290 | 0 |
| Grant-in-Aid | R 560 | R 580 | 0 |

The increases will be effected on 1 April 2026 and 1 October 2026 respectively.
