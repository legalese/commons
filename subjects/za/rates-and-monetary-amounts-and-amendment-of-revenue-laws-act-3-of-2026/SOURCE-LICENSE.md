### Copyright position relied on

Section 12(8)(a) of the Copyright Act 98 of 1978, read on 2026-10-19 in the Government Gazette copy of the Act on gov.za
(https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201504/act-98-1978.pdf, OCR text layer), provides that no copyright
subsists "in official texts of a legislative, administrative or legal nature". Acts, regulations and Gazette notices are such
texts. The deposits rely on that provision. The sites' non-commercial terms are recorded above and are **flagged for a
decision before commit** (see the subject README): they are website terms, not a copyright licence, and gov.za's own terms
disclaim creating any agreement, but they are not a permission for commercial use either.
