# Source license

**Affordable Housing Act, 2024** is indexed here, not sourced: **no text is deposited.** See `registers/source-bundle/act-2024-2.meta.json`.

**Licence category: the law is free of copyright, but the only host found restricts access.** Under the Copyright Act s. 2
the Act itself carries no copyright. The only source found for a current, consolidated text is Kenya Law, whose access
conditions (below) exclude collection for AI input and prohibit scraping, and whose content licence is NonCommercial.
**A human decision is needed** before any text is deposited: ask Kenya Law for permission or bulk access
(info@kenyalaw.org, as its Terms of Use invite), or find the Act on a host whose terms permit it.

### The law on copyright in Kenyan legislation

Copyright Act, 2001 (No. 12 of 2001), s. 2, definition of "literary work": "... but does not include a written law or a
judicial decision". Read 2026-10-19 in the Kenya Law expression of 31 December 2022, through the Legal Data Hunter index
(KE/KenyaLaw), not on Kenya Law's site.
