Written from BRIEF.md and `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A02884.txt` only, before any L4 was read.

## Prescribed period (s 3(1))

"The period of 16 years that commenced on 13 October 1983": first day 13 October 1983, last day 12 October 1999.

| # | Scenario | Expected | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 13 Oct 1983 | in the prescribed period | s 3(1) |
| 2 | Day 12 Oct 1983 | not in the period | s 3(1) |
| 3 | Day 12 Oct 1999 | in the period | s 3(1) |
| 4 | Day 13 Oct 1999 | not in the period | s 3(1) |
