## Status of this deposit

Text deposited on 2026-10-24: `registers/source-bundle/GG2026S363.txt` (50,090 characters), converted from Special
Gazette No. S 363 of 30 June 2026, retrieved 2026-10-24T04:42:30Z. It holds three notices: the Accident Compensation
Act 1985 notice (Gazette pages 1-8), the Workers Compensation Act 1958 notice (pages 9-10) and the Workplace Injury
Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2013 notice (pages 11-18). The text gives each amount before and from 1 July 2026.
It is an unofficial plain-text version, not the published Gazette. The download and text hashes, the line range of each
notice, and how the text was converted are in `registers/source-bundle/GG2026S363.meta.json`.

The copyright notice, the Government Printer's attribution and the unofficial-version notice head the deposited file.
The Gazette's own imprint and copyright notice are kept at its end.

**Currency.** These amounts apply from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027. The next notice, for 1 July 2027, should be
deposited beside this one as a new file. The notice says weekly payments "are indexed on the anniversary of the
entitlement to weekly payments", so which figure applies to a worker's weekly payment depends on that date as well.
