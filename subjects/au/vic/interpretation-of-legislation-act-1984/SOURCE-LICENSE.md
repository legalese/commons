## Owner's ruling, 24 October 2026

The Victorian terms were put to Michael (the owner of Legalese Commons), with the warning that nothing could be deposited without permission.
The terms were the Government Printer's notice at https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/copyright (no reproduction except in accordance with the Copyright Act 1968; authorised electronic versions published "for personal use only").
On 24 October 2026 he answered, in chat:

> "On NT, Vic and SA, yes please go ahead."

The ruling: Legalese Commons may deposit Victorian legislation and quote it in L4 encodings, keeping the copyright notice, the Government Printer's attribution and a notice that the version is unofficial.
The ruling is about the licence only.
It does not lift robots.txt: nothing is fetched from a path that robots.txt disallows.
