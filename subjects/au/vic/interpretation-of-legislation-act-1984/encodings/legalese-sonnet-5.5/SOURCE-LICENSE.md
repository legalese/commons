# Source license

The terms the quoted text of the Interpretation of Legislation Act 1984 (Victoria) carries are recorded once, in the subject folder's `SOURCE-LICENSE.md` (`../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md` from this folder).
That file is the record, and this one does not repeat it.

What this encoding adds:

- The `-- src:` comment lines in the `.l4` files quote the text word for word, so the copyright notice and the Government Printer's attribution in the header of `registers/source-bundle/interpretation-of-legislation-act-1984.txt` travel with them.
- The text is a plain-text conversion made by a converter script from the Word file the Victorian Legislation website publishes.
  It is not the official text and not an authorised version, and nothing in this encoding claims to have been checked against the official text.
  The header of the text file says that only the authorised version published on that website is admissible as evidence under s 64.
- The licence of this encoding's own code, tests and notes is Apache-2.0, as for the rest of the collection.
