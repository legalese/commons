# queensland/

Queensland Acts, one subject-id per body of law, following the subject-sidecar shape described in [`subjects/README.md`](../README.md).

The subjects here were laid out on 2026-09-22 for the Acts that govern twelve everyday topics -- payroll, leave, termination, pensions, government benefits, personal tax, insurance claims, utility billing, passenger compensation, consumer and tenancy, customs and duties, and work permits. The mapping and the check of each Act against the official register were made with tools that now live outside this repository. ## Social security and benefits, and personal income tax (sourced 2026-10-20)

A Priority 3 sample: the Queensland law a resident meets on these two topics, chosen so that a member of the public could get useful determinations from it. Eleven instruments: two whose text was already here, nine added on 2026-10-20.

### How the set connects to the Commonwealth

Personal income tax and the main social security payments are **Commonwealth** law, already sourced one level up: [`income-tax-assessment-act-1997`](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/) and [`income-tax-assessment-act-1936`](../income-tax-assessment-act-1936/) (what is taxable income), [`income-tax-rates-act-1986`](../income-tax-rates-act-1986/) and [`medicare-levy-act-1986`](../medicare-levy-act-1986/) (the rates), [`taxation-administration-act-1953`](../taxation-administration-act-1953/) (PAYG withholding, assessments, objections), [`social-security-act-1991`](../social-security-act-1991/) and [`social-security-administration-act-1999`](../social-security-administration-act-1999/) (pensions, allowances, means tests), and [`a-new-tax-system-family-assistance-act-1999`](../a-new-tax-system-family-assistance-act-1999/). Queensland levies no income tax and pays no general income support. What a Queensland resident meets **on top of** the Commonwealth layer is:

- **State benefits** that pay money to an individual: financial assistance to victims of violence, workers' compensation weekly payments and lump sums, and the first home owner grant.
- **State taxes that fall on individuals** rather than businesses: land tax (with the home exemption) and transfer duty (with the home and first home concessions), both administered under one Act.

The two layers, and the Queensland instruments among themselves, touch at named points that an encoding of either side will need:

- **Workers' compensation and social security.** Weekly compensation is "compensation" for the Social Security Act's compensation preclusion and income tests (Social Security Act 1991, Part 3.14), and is assessable income under the ITAA 1997. The Queensland weekly rate is capped and indexed by QOTE (Workers' Compensation Act, s 10A), which the QOTE Notice sets each financial year.
- **Victims assistance and the other schemes.** The Victims of Crime Assistance Act 2009 deals with its relationship to workers' compensation and to motor accident insurance in its Chapter 3 Parts 3 and 3A (pointing at [`workers-compensation-and-rehabilitation-act-2003`](workers-compensation-and-rehabilitation-act-2003/) and [`motor-accident-insurance-act-1994`](motor-accident-insurance-act-1994/)), and reduces assistance by other payments received for the same harm (s 86).
- **The home.** One fact, "this is my home", drives several rules: the land tax home exemption (Land Tax Act 2010, Part 6 Division 1), the transfer duty concessions for homes and first homes (Duties Act 2001, Chapter 2 Part 9), the first home owner grant's residence requirement (FHOG Act, s 15), the Commonwealth CGT main residence exemption (ITAA 1997, Subdivision 118-B) and the principal home's place in the social security assets test (Social Security Act 1991, s 11). The Queensland and Commonwealth definitions are not the same, and an encoding should keep them apart.
- **One revenue administration.** The Taxation Administration Act 2001 names the Duties Act and the Land Tax Act as revenue laws (s 6) and governs their assessments, interest, penalty tax, objections and appeals: it is the Queensland counterpart of the Commonwealth Taxation Administration Act 1953 for these taxes. The first home owner grant is paid by the same Commissioner of State Revenue but carries its own objection and review provisions (FHOG Act, Part 5).

### Gaps

- **Concessions for pensioners and seniors.** Queensland's electricity, water and council-rates rebates for pensioners and seniors, and the Seniors Card, are administrative schemes: no Act or regulation sets their amounts or eligibility, so there is nothing to source. The registration-fee concessions for pensioners sit in the Transport Operations (Road Use Management—Vehicle Registration) Regulation 2021 (sl-2021-0113), not sourced in this pass.
- **Victims assistance amounts.** No regulation under the Victims of Crime Assistance Act 2009 is in force; the special assistance amounts and the caps are in the Act itself.
- **Social housing.** The Housing Act 2003 (act-2003-052) and the Housing Regulation 2026 (sl-2026-0120) govern public housing assistance, but eligibility and rent are set by policy rather than by the instruments; not sourced.
- **Older deposits.** The Duties Act 2001 and Workers' Compensation and Rehabilitation Act 2003 texts are from 2026-09-22 and were converted by an earlier tool: their meta files are in an older shape (no retrieval time, no conversion note) and their texts carry no notice at the top. A check on 2026-10-20 found both still at the version they were taken from.
- The Taxation Administration Regulation 2022 (sl-2022-0108) is left out as administrative.

### Gaps

- **Aboriginals Protection and Restriction of the Sale of Opium Act 1897.** Not on the register: neither the in-force nor the repealed collection holds it. It is a repealed historical Act, and nothing was deposited.
- **The 2024 anti-discrimination reforms.** The register holds no separate Anti-Discrimination Act 2024 and no such Bill. The 2024 reform is the Respect at Work and Other Matters Amendment Act 2024 (act-2024-047), which amends the 1991 Act and is deposited as passed. Its provisions due on 1 December 2024 are in the current 1991 Act. Those due on 1 July 2025 (among them new ss 10 and 11, the positive duty in chapter 5C and the prohibition of harassment on the basis of sex) are not in the 1991 Act version in force from 2025-05-19, which was still current on 2026-11-06, and the 1991 Act's s 285AA refers to the commencement of s 7B of the amending Act. Their commencement was therefore deferred or changed by later legislation, not deposited here, which should be found before the reforms are encoded.
- **Civil Liability Regulation 2014.** Ended on 1 September 2025 and replaced by the Civil Liability Regulation 2025, which is deposited. Only the 2026 Civil Liability Indexation Notice is deposited, so the 2025-26 and earlier amounts are not.
- **Not sourced in this pass:** the Motor Accident Insurance Indexation Notice 2026 (sl-2026-0062) and the Uniform Civil Procedure (Fees) Regulation 2019 (sl-2019-0168).

### Still inputs, because they are not deposited

Shared by several encodings:

- **The Acts Interpretation Act 1954 (Qld)**, which says how days, months, years and business days are counted. Each encoding states its reading in `NOTES.md`. For example, the land tax s 77 notice is taken as due by 31 July (it might be 1 August), and workers' compensation counts business days as Monday to Friday, leaving out the Queensland public holidays the person says fall in the period.
- **The value of a penalty unit** (Penalties and Sentences Act 1992). The duties, land tax and first home owner grant offences are stated in penalty units.
- **The Taxation Administration Regulation 2022**, left out of the 2026-10-20 sourcing as administrative. It sets the prescribed interest rate and weekday for unpaid tax interest on duty and land tax. Both encodings take the rate as an input and accrue interest daily, as the Act's own s 54(7) example does, although s 54(3)(b) makes late payment interest accrue weekly.

By encoding:

- **Duties Act.** The s 153(2) clawback formula did not survive conversion of the deposit, so its TD is not computed (finding 9). It needs transcribing from the authorised PDF.
- **Land Tax Act.** The s 18(2) averaging factor, whose formula was also lost in conversion (finding 1), and the Valuer-General's values under the Land Valuation Act 2010.
- **First Home Owner Grant Act.** The Reserve Bank's 90-day bank bill yield for May, for instalment interest (Regulation r 4(3)). The version of the Act in force before 1 July 2026 and the Revenue (Cost of Living Relief Locked-in Law) and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2026 are not deposited, so a purchase or build that commenced before 20 November 2023 answers `not covered` (finding 2).
- **Workers' Compensation Act.** QOTE for 2025-26 and earlier: only the 2026 Notice ($1,994.50 for 2026-27) is deposited, so a week outside 2026-27 answers `not covered` without it. Three Commonwealth figures: the single pension rate (the maximum single disability support pension, weekly), the maximum carer payment (weekly), and what a pensioner may earn before the pension is reduced (s 154). The ss 163(2) and 166(2) partial incapacity formula was lost in conversion, so partial incapacity answers "at most $X a week" (finding 1).
- **Victims of Crime Assistance Act.** The table of costs (s 132) and the scheme guidelines (s 131), so each expense is the applicant's own figure, taken as reasonable. The NIISQ facts, since the NIISQ Act 2016 is not deposited. The commencement days of the 2017 and 2023 amending Acts, so an act of violence before 2 September 2024 answers `not covered` (finding 20). The QCAT Act's time limit for external review is left out.

### The Commonwealth encodings each links to

The stable `l4` cannot IMPORT across encoding folders, so each link is recorded in the encoding's `NOTES.md`, not in code.

- **Duties Act** links to [`income-tax-assessment-act-1997/encodings/baseline-2026-10/`](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/encodings/baseline-2026-10/): the CGT main residence exemption (Subdivision 118-B; ss 118-110 and 118-150, and s 118-185(2)). The Queensland "home" (s 86) is tested at acquisition, the Commonwealth main residence over the whole ownership period, so the two can come apart: a home let out 13 months after moving in keeps its duty concession but its CGT exemption is only partial unless the absence rule is chosen.
- **Land Tax Act** links to [`social-security-act-1991/`](../social-security-act-1991/) (s 11 homeowner and s 11A principal home, with its absence rules in s 11A(8) and (9), for the assets test) and to [`income-tax-assessment-act-1997/`](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/) (Subdivision 118-B: ss 118-140, 118-145, 118-150). "Used as the home" under s 36 is a different test from both: a person in aged care can have the former home exempt from land tax under s 37, and counted or not under the Social Security Act's own rules.
- **First Home Owner Grant Act** links to [`social-security-act-1991/`](../social-security-act-1991/): s 8(8)(ha) excludes a State payment to help buy or build a home from income (not yet encoded in the Commonwealth baseline), and the home bought is disregarded in the assets test as the principal home (s 1118(1)(a), (b)). The deposited [`income-tax-assessment-act-1997/`](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/) does not mention the grant; the first home super saver scheme (Division 313) is separate from it.
- **Workers' Compensation Act** links to [`social-security-act-1991/`](../social-security-act-1991/) (weekly compensation is "compensation" for the preclusion and income tests of Part 3.14; the single pension rate, the carer payment and the s 154 earnings figure come from it), [`income-tax-assessment-act-1997/`](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/) (weekly compensation is assessable income; s 87-60 personal services business determinations), [`taxation-administration-act-1953/`](../taxation-administration-act-1953/) (s 11(1)(a): an employee for PAYG withholding under Schedule 1 Part 2-5) and [`fair-work-act-2009/`](../fair-work-act-2009/) (s 11(1)(b): regulated workers under chapter 3A, of whom the deposited Regulation prescribes none, finding 3).
- **Victims of Crime Assistance Act** links to [`social-security-act-1991/encodings/baseline-2026-10/`](../social-security-act-1991/encodings/baseline-2026-10/): under s 17(2B) and (2C) a payment of compensation for a criminal injury is not "compensation", so the Part 3.14 preclusion does not reach it, though assistance for a domestic violence act that is not an offence, and funeral expense assistance, are not plainly covered; its place in the income test (s 8(8), (11)) is not settled by the deposit. It also links to [`income-tax-assessment-act-1997/encodings/baseline-2026-10/`](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/encodings/baseline-2026-10/): s 118-37(1)(a)(ii) disregards a capital gain from compensation for a personal injury, but whether the loss-of-earnings component is ordinary income (s 6-5) or falls under s 15-30 is not answered. Within Queensland, its Part 3 (ss 31-34) takes as inputs facts that the workers' compensation encoding decides, and workers' compensation is a relevant payment under its s 86.
