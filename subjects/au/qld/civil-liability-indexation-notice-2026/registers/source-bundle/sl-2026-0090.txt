Civil Liability Indexation Notice 2026
1	Short title
This notice may be cited as the Civil Liability Indexation Notice 2026.
2	Commencement
This notice commences on 1 July 2026.
3	Fixed amount of damages for loss of consortium or loss of servitium—Act, s 75
For section 75(1)(a) of the Act, the fixed amount for section 58(1)(b) of the Act, for the 2026–2027 financial year, is $59,300.
4	Fixed amounts for the calculation of general damages—Act, s 75
(1) For section 75(1)(b) of the Act, the base amount fixed for section 62(2)(a) of the Act, for the 2026–2027 financial year, for an injury scale value stated in column 1 of the following table is the amount (if any) stated in column 2 of the table opposite the injury scale value.
(2) Also, for section 75(1)(b) of the Act, the variable amount fixed for section 62(2)(b) and (3) of the Act, for the 2026–2027 financial year, for an injury scale value stated in column 1 of the following table is the amount worked out in the way stated in column 3 of the table opposite the injury scale value.

5	Fixed amount of award for future loss—Act, s 75
For section 75(1)(c) of the Act, the fixed amount for section 64(2) of the Act, for the 2026–2027 financial year, is $197,770.
6	Repeal
The Civil Liability Indexation Notice 2025, SL No. 54 is repealed.
