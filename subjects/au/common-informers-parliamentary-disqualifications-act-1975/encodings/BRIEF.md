# Encoding brief: the Common Informers (Parliamentary Disqualifications) Act 1975 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Common Informers (Parliamentary Disqualifications) Act 1975** (Act No. 28 of 1975), Commonwealth of Australia, compilation prepared on 8 July 2008 (C2008C00320), deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A00250.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The senator or member sued** for having sat while incapable under the Constitution (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"How much can I be made to pay to the person suing me; and can I be sued under s 46 of the Constitution instead?"* The operative ss 3-4 (s 5, jurisdiction, as a note).

- s 3(1): $200 for having so sat on or before the day the originating process was served, plus $200 for every later day on which the person is proved to have so sat.
- s 3(2): no sitting earlier than 12 months before the day the suit was instituted counts.
- s 3(3): no order penalising the person more than once for any period or day of sitting.
- s 4: no liability and no suit under s 46 of the Constitution.

## Dates

- "a time earlier than 12 months before the day on which the suit is instituted": a sitting counts if it is on or after the corresponding date 12 months before that day (or the last day of the month where there is none).
- The $200 in s 3(1)(a) is due only if at least one counted sitting falls on or before the day of service.

## Inputs

- Whether the person was, on the sitting days, incapable of sitting under the Constitution (a finding).
- The days on which the person is proved to have sat; the day the suit was instituted; the day the originating process was served; the days already penalised in an earlier suit.

## Rules that matter

- A failing assertion is a finding. Never edit an expected value to match the code.
