# Encoding brief: the United States Naval Communication Station (Civilian Employees) Act 1968 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**United States Naval Communication Station (Civilian Employees) Act 1968**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1968A00054.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The civilian employee of the United States Government at the Station** (or a dependant after death) (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Does this Act apply to me; am I covered by the Commonwealth Employees' Compensation Act; and can I (or my dependant) recover damages from the Commonwealth for injury, disease or death?"* Sections 3(2), 4, 5(1) and 6(1)-(2). The Schedule's modifications and s 6(3)-(4) are out of scope.

- s 4: a person (not a member of the US forces) employed by the US Government (or an instrumentality, s 3(2)) in or in connexion with the Station, who is (a) not a US citizen or national, or (b) a US citizen or national employed on terms agreed with a registered Australian organisation or WA union.
- s 5(1): the Commonwealth Employees' Compensation Act applies as if they were an employee.
- s 6(1): for injury, disease or death after commencement, where s 9 or 10 of that Act applies and they (or another) would, had everyone at the Station been employed by the Commonwealth, have been entitled to damages from the Commonwealth independently of that Act, they may recover like damages from the Commonwealth.
- s 6(2): the same for injury, disease or death on or after 29 August 1966 and before commencement.

## Inputs

The person's employer and role, citizenship, the terms of employment, whether ss 9-10 of the compensation Act apply, whether damages would be recoverable on the hypothesis of Commonwealth employment, and the date of the injury, disease or death.
