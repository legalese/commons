# Independent expectations: Federal Proceedings (Costs) Act 1981

Written from BRIEF.md and `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A02401.txt` only, before reading any L4.

Conventions:
- "Can a certificate be granted" means the court *may* grant one: a route in ss 6-10A exists and s 14 does not bar the applicant.
- "Maximum payable" (s 16(2), s 18) is the lesser of my costs and the prescribed maximum for the court that heard the appeal or new trial, or for the Tribunal (s 18(2A)). For a s 10(2) or (3) certificate, s 18(2) does not apply, so the maximum is my costs. When no certificate can be granted, nothing is payable (0).
- Prescribed maximum (s 18(1)): the Schedule amount, or "such higher amount as is prescribed". A prescribed amount lower than the Schedule is not a "higher amount" and leaves the Schedule amount in place.
- "Can the A-G pay now" (s 16(2), s 17): a certificate can be granted, it is not vacated, and for a certificate referred to in s 15 (ss 6(1), 6(2), 7(1), 9(1)) the A-G is entitled to presume under s 17(2) that it will not be vacated: no appeal or leave application, and a notice given. (The "satisfied" limb of s 17(1) is discretionary and not modelled.)
- Defaults: an individual; route s 6(1); High Court; costs $8,000; no prescribed higher amount; no further appeal by the other side; no appeal or leave sought; notice given.
