# Independent expectations: Not-for-profit Sector Freedom to Advocate Act 2013

Written from BRIEF.md and `C2013A00056.txt` only, before reading any `.l4` file.

Commencement: s 2, "the day after this Act receives the Royal Assent"; assent 13 June 2013, so commencement is **14 June 2013**.

## Reading of the Act

- **Commonwealth agreement** (s 3): "a legally binding agreement between an agency (on behalf of the Commonwealth) and a not-for-profit entity". Agency = Privacy Act s 6(1) "agency" paras (a)-(f) or (h); para (g) (and anything else) is excluded. NFP = (a) entitled to ACNC registration, or (b) non-profit body under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999. Either limb suffices.
- **Prohibited content** (s 5(1)): "any requirement that restricts or prevents a not-for profit entity (including staff ...) from commenting on, advocating support for or opposing a change to any matter established by law, policy or practice of the Commonwealth". Any one of the three activities suffices; restricting staff suffices.
- s 5(2): "not prohibited content to the extent that it restricts or prevents ... from disclosing information that (a) is confidential information; or (b) is personal information". A clause that restricts *only* such disclosure is not prohibited content.
- s 4(1): agency "must not include prohibited content" — but s 7(2) confines s 4(1) to agreements "entered into on or after the commencement". So a breach by the agency only for agreements entered on or after 14 June 2013.
- s 4(2): prohibited content is void. s 7(4)(a): for an agreement that included it immediately before commencement, it "becomes void on the commencement of this Act" (14 June 2013). For an agreement entered on/after commencement, void from the outset (i.e. from the day the agreement was entered into).
- s 7(4)(b): voiding "does not affect any right, privilege, obligation or liability acquired, accrued or incurred under the prohibited content before the commencement". Only relevant to pre-commencement agreements.
- If the arrangement is not a Commonwealth agreement (other party not an agency of the listed kinds, or we are not an NFP), nothing in ss 4-5 applies: not void, no breach.

Differences from the brief: none of substance. Note the brief's input "whether it does so only to stop disclosure of confidential or personal information" collapses s 5(2)'s "to the extent that" into an all-or-nothing flag; a clause that partly restricts advocacy and partly protects confidential information would be prohibited content to the extent of the advocacy restriction. I test the all-or-nothing input as given.
