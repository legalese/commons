# Encoding brief: the Not-for-profit Sector Freedom to Advocate Act 2013 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Not-for-profit Sector Freedom to Advocate Act 2013** (No. 56, 2013), Commonwealth of Australia, as made, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2013A00056.txt` (CC BY 4.0). Assented to 13 June 2013; commenced the next day, 14 June 2013 (s 2).

## Seat

**The not-for-profit entity that signed a Commonwealth agreement** (and its staff) (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Is this clause in our agreement with the Commonwealth prohibited content; is it void, and from when; did the agency break the Act by including it; and does voiding it disturb what accrued before 14 June 2013?"* The operative ss 3-5 and 7. Section 6 (compensation for acquisition of property) is out of scope.

## Inputs

- Whether the other party is an agency (paragraphs (a)-(f) or (h) of the Privacy Act 1988 definition) acting for the Commonwealth, and whether we are a not-for-profit entity (entitled to ACNC registration, or a non-profit body under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999).
- Whether the clause restricts or prevents us (or our staff) from commenting on, advocating support for, or opposing a change to any matter established by Commonwealth law, policy or practice; and whether it does so only to stop disclosure of confidential or personal information.
- The day the agreement was entered into.
