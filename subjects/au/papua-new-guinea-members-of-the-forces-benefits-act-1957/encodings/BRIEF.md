# Encoding brief: the Papua New Guinea (Members of the Forces Benefits) Act 1957 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Papua New Guinea (Members of the Forces Benefits) Act 1957**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1957A00089.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The member of the Forces, or a dependant** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Am I a member of the Forces under this Act; is the Commonwealth liable for my incapacity (or the member's death); what benefits am I eligible for; and can my pension be taken?"* Sections 3 (member of the Forces, the war), 4, 5, 6 and 8. Rates are fixed by the regulations (not in the text) and are not computed.

- s 3: a male indigenous inhabitant of Papua New Guinea or a Pacific island (not part of a State) who served in the Defence Force during the war (from 3 September 1939; other wars counted only if entered before 3 September 1945; service begun earlier counts if it continued into the war) at less than the minimum pay of an Australian Military Forces male member, and whose service ended by discharge or death.
- s 5(1)-(2): liable if the incapacity or death resulted from an occurrence during war service, or arose out of or is attributable to it, including a pre-existing cause materially contributed to or aggravated by service conditions.
- s 5(3): not liable for serious default or wilful act, intentionally self-inflicted injury, or a serious breach of discipline.
- s 4: the benefits are those under this Act and the War Gratuity Act 1945-1947, and no others.
- s 8: a pension is absolutely inalienable.
