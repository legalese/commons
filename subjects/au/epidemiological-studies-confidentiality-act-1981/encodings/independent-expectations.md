# Independent expectations: Epidemiological Studies (Confidentiality) Act 1981

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Seat: a person who conducts, supervises or assists in a study. Questions: may I record or pass on the information? max penalty? can a court compel production? must I take an oath?

Default facts unless stated: study = Vietnam Veterans Study; my part = conducting; info acquired by assisting in the study; info concerns another person's affairs; act = divulge/communicate; not for the purpose of the study; recipient = an unrelated third party; no s 5 authorisation; not a s 11 publication; Minister has not required an oath.
