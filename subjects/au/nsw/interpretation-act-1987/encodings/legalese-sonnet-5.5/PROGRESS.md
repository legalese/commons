## Decisions taken, and the section that licenses each

- The first day of a period dating from a given day is the next day: 36(1).
- A period in months is a run of calendar months, each ending as Schedule 4 "calendar month" says: 36(1), Schedule 4 (fork F1).
- The last day of a period is inside the period: 36(2) (fork F2).
- A period in weeks or in years is not covered; a number of calendar years counts only from 1 January: Schedule 4 has no entry for "week" or "year" (fork F3).
- The rule for a last day on a Saturday, a Sunday, or a public or bank holiday applies to months as to days: 36(2) (fork F4).
- When paragraphs (b) and (c) of 15(2), (3) or (4) both apply, the Act does not say which prevails: 15 (fork F5).
- Repealed provisions are in force on every day before the new provisions commence, not on the commencement day: 29, 24, 30D (fork F6).
- 23(3) and 23(5) are each encoded as printed and are not combined: 23 (fork F7).
- A 29 February birthday in a year that is not a leap year is not covered: 37 (fork F8).
- A necessary condition gives `JUST FALSE` when unmet and `NOTHING` when met: 39(3), 43(3), 49(3), 50(2), 80(2) (fork F9).
- The 15 sitting days of 41(1)(b) run from the first sitting day after the laying: 41(1), 18 (fork F10).
- The end of a day and the beginning of the next day are one instant: Schedule 4 "midnight", 24, 30D, 37, 71 (fork F11).
- A condition introduced by "if", "where", "only if", "as long as" or "unless" gives `JUST FALSE` when it fails; an open list, a scope limit with no "only", and silence give `NOTHING`: the Guide's rulings, applied at each site (shapes in the fork register).
- The days a House sat and the public and bank holidays of a place are borrowed facts and are inputs: Schedule 4, 18, 36(2), 76.
