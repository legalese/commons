# Independent expectations: Explosives Act 1961

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

| # | Scenario | Expected | Licensed by |
|---|----------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Explosives the property of / in the possession or control of the Commonwealth | Part II (handling rules) applies | s 9(a) |
| 2 | Commonwealth-manufactured, being exported under an arrangement with the Commonwealth | Part II applies | s 9(ab) |
| 3 | A foreign government's / forces' explosives, here with Commonwealth approval for a defence purpose | Part II applies | s 9(b) |
| 4 | Anyone else's (e.g. a private quarry's) | Part II does not apply | s 9 |
| 5 | I contravened the regulations, not continuing, no lesser penalty prescribed | Offence; max 6 months' imprisonment and/or fine of 10 penalty units; no daily fine | s 20(a) |
| 6 | As 5, continuing for 1 day | Additional daily fine 2 penalty units; total fine 12 penalty units; imprisonment still max 6 months | s 20(a) |
| 7 | As 5, continuing for 5 days | Daily fine 10 penalty units; total fine 20 penalty units | s 20(a) |
| 8 | As 5, continuing 30 days | Daily 60; total fine 70 penalty units | s 20(a) |
| 9 | I contravened an order made under the regulations (not the regulations themselves), not continuing | Same as 5: offence, 6 months / 10 penalty units | s 20 |
| 10 | No contravention | No offence; no penalty (0 months, 0 units) | s 20 |
| 11 | Contravention where the regulations prescribe a lesser penalty | Offence; the maximum is the prescribed lesser penalty, which the Act does not quantify (the s 20(a) figures do not bind) | s 20(b) |
| 12 | Contravened the regulations, explosives are anyone else's (e.g. Part III explosives-area regulations under s 14) | Still an offence under s 20: s 20 covers "a provision of the regulations" generally, not only Part II regulations (medium confidence) | ss 14, 20 |
