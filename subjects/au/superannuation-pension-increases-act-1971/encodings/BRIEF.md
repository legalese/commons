# Encoding brief: the Superannuation (Pension Increases) Act 1971 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Superannuation (Pension Increases) Act 1971** (No. 73 of 1971), Commonwealth of Australia, compilation of 1 July 2014, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1971A00073.txt` (CC BY 4.0). It commenced on Royal Assent, 1 October 1971 (the commencing date).

## Seat

**The eligible pensioner under the Superannuation Act 1922, or the widow or widower** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"By how much does my annual pension increase under the 1971 Act?"* Sections 4-9, with the s 3 definitions they use (as inputs where they turn on the Superannuation Act's scales and the Treasurer's determinations).

- s 4: where the adjusted unit entitlement exceeds the actual unit entitlement, the increase is $65 × the additional units.
- s 5: for the listed pensioners (not a deferred pension), the increase × (the annual pension payable immediately before the commencing date ÷ the annual pension that would have been payable outside s 5).
- s 6: for a deferred pension, the increase × (the annual pension payable ÷ $91 × the units applicable when the pensioner ceased to be a contributor, less any s 6(2) units).
- s 7: reduced by the increases under the 1957 Act s 11, the 1961 Act s 4, the 1963 Act s 26 and the 1967 Act s 4.
- s 8: a widow or widower gets five-eighths of the late spouse's increase (one-half where the husband elected that s 26 of the 1922-1959 Act should not apply).
- s 9: no increase for the excluded pensions.
- Section 10 (pensions for persons named in the Schedule) and ss 11-13 are out of scope.

## Inputs

The unit figures; the scaling case and its pension figures; the earlier increases; the spouse's case; whether the pension is excluded.
