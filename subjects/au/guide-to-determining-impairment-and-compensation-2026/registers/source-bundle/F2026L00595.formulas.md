# Formulas lost in conversion: F2026L00595

Guide to Determining Impairment and Compensation 2026, register id F2026L00595 (as made, unamended).

The authorised rendering of the Guide (the Word document served by the Federal Register of Legislation at
`https://api.prod.legislation.gov.au/v1/Documents/Find(titleid='F2026L00595',asatspecification='Current',type='Primary',format='Word',uniquetypenumber=0,volumenumber=0,rectificationspecification='Latest')`,
retrieved 2026-10-23; sha256 of the download as recorded in `F2026L00595.meta.json`) sets its formulas as images. The deposited text `F2026L00595.txt` keeps every paragraph and every table cell (one per line), but not the images. The deposited text is not altered; this file sits beside it.

**Transcribed** (the one formula an encoding in Commons uses):

| Where in the text | Image | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter 23, "Combined ratings", after "If the combined impairment is C (see Chapter 18) and the lifestyle effect is L, this can be expressed as:" | `word/media/image49.png` (its alternative text: "Start formula, compensation factor final equals, start fraction, warlike rating times, open parentheses, compensation factor warlike, close parentheses, plus peacetime rating, ...") | CF^final(C, L) = (A × CF^wl(C, L) + B × CF^pt(C, L)) / (A + B) |

where, as the text around it says, A is the impairment rating from warlike or non-warlike service, B the rating from peacetime service (each the sum of the points of the conditions from that kind of service), C the combined impairment rating (Chapter 18), L the lifestyle rating, CF^wl the factor from Table 23.1 and CF^pt the factor from Table 23.2. The Guide's worked examples check it: (20 × 0.478 + 30 × 0.268) / (20 + 30) = 0.352 and (30 × 0.478 + 20 × 0.268) / (30 + 20) = 0.394.

**Not transcribed:** the Guide's other image formulas, among them the lung function formulas for Table 1.4 (Chapter 1), the nomogram formula in Chapter 1, the body mass index formula (Chapter 6), the conductive deafness and Formula 7.1 hearing formulas (Chapter 7), the creatinine clearance formulas (Chapter 9) and the Combined Values Chart formula and its worked example (Chapter 18). No Commons encoding uses them yet; they would be transcribed here, from the same rendering, when one does. Tables 23.1 and 23.2 (the compensation factors) are text, not images, and are complete in the deposited text (lines 11474 to 12900: impairment ratings 0 and 5 to 80, lifestyle ratings 0 to 7).
