# Independent expectations: Diplomatic and Consular Missions Act 1978

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Seat: a person who displays a sign, flag or insignia, or makes a representation. Questions: is my conduct caught (s 4(1)), can the Federal Court restrain me by injunction (s 4(5), (6)), can a removal warrant issue (s 5(1)), and until when may it be in force (s 5(5))?

Default facts unless stated: mission = a consented diplomatic or consular mission; conduct = displaying a sign; the premises are NOT the mission's office or a member's residence; the sign implies another mission representing that country; I do not act for a designated overseas mission (DOM) or for a consented mission; Court finds I have engaged in the conduct; no warrant issued.
