# Encoding brief: the Defence Forces Retirement Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1967 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Defence Forces Retirement Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1967** (No. 91 of 1967), Commonwealth of Australia, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1967A00091.txt` (CC BY 4.0). It commenced on Royal Assent, 8 November 1967 (the commencing date).

## Seat

**The eligible DFRB pensioner, or the widow** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"By how much does my pension increase under the 1967 Act?"* Sections 4-10 and 13, with the s 3 definitions they use (as inputs where they turn on the DFRB Act's scales).

- s 3: an eligible pensioner's pension must have begun to be payable before 30 June 1967.
- s 4: where the pension at current rate exceeds the actual pension entitlement, the increase is five-sevenths of (the pension at current rate less the basic pension entitlement).
- s 5: a pensioner who commuted part of the pension under s 74, or elected under ss 61A or 61B, gets the increase × (the rate payable immediately before the commencing date ÷ the rate that would have been payable without the commutation or election).
- s 6: the increase is reduced by any increase under the 1961 Act or Part III of the 1963 Act.
- s 7: a pensioner in the listed cases gets a rate the Finance Minister determines: the Act gives no figure, so NOTHING.
- s 8: a widow gets five-eighths of her husband's increase (one-half where he made the s 47(6) or s 48(4) election), computed with s 5 disregarded so far as it applies because of a commutation under s 74. s 9 widows get a determined rate (NOTHING).
- s 10: a suspended pension, when it again becomes payable, gets the increase it would have had.
- s 13: no increase (except a widow's or a child's) for a person also entitled to a pension under the Superannuation Act 1922-1967.
- Sections 11, 12, 14 and 15 are out of scope.

## Inputs

The class of pensioner; the day the pension began to be payable; the pension at current rate, the actual and basic pension entitlements; the commutation or election and the two rates; the earlier increase; for a widow, her husband's figures and whether the one-half election applies; whether I am also entitled to a Superannuation Act 1922 pension.
