# Independent expectations: Customs Securities (Penalties) Act 1981

Written from BRIEF.md and the Act only, before reading any .l4 file.

## Scenarios

| # | Facts | Expected | Working / words of the Act |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tender accepted; Collector required security under s 42; person failed; undertaking prescribed percentage 10%; goods value $50,000 | penalty $5,000 | s 4: "an amount equal to the prescribed percentage of the value of the goods" = 10% x 50,000 |
| 2 | As 1 but refused (rather than failed) | $5,000 | s 4(b) "refuses, or fails" |
| 3 | As 1 but tender not accepted | no penalty (0) | s 4(a) "has been accepted" |
| 4 | As 1 but Collector never required security | no penalty | s 4(b) "when required by a Collector under section 42" |
| 5 | As 1 but the person gave the security | no penalty | s 4(b) |
| 6 | Unit value in undertaking $20; 1,000 units; 15% | $3,000 | s 3(2) value calculated from the unit value: 20 x 1000 = 20,000; 15% = 3,000 |
| 7 | Unit value $2.50; 3 units; 10% | $0.75 | s 3(2); fractional arithmetic |
| 8 | Percentage 0% | $0 | s 4 |
| 9 | Percentage 100%, value $12,345 | $12,345 | s 4 |
| 10 | Goods value $0 | $0 | s 4 |

## Where I read the Act differently from the brief / gaps

- s 3(2) says the value "shall be calculated in accordance with" the unit value; it does not literally say "x the units". The brief's "value x units" is the natural reading; I follow it.
- The Act gives no rounding rule; I expect exact values.
