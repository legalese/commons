# Encoding brief: the Customs Securities (Penalties) Act 1981 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Customs Securities (Penalties) Act 1981**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A02424.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The tenderer whose tender under a Part XV Customs Act scheme was accepted** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"What penalty do I owe for not giving the security a Collector required?"* Sections 3 and 4.

- s 4: where the tender was accepted and the person refuses or fails, when required by a Collector under s 42 of the Customs Act, to give security for any penalty under the Customs Undertakings (Penalties) Act 1981, the penalty is the prescribed percentage (set out in the Customs undertaking) of the value of the goods.
- s 3(2): where the undertaking sets a value per unit, the value is that value × the units.
