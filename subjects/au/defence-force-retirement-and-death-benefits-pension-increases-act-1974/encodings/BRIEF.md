# Encoding brief: the Defence Force Retirement and Death Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1974 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Defence Force Retirement and Death Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1974**, Commonwealth of Australia, as made, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A00163.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The DFRB pensioner or DFRDB pension-benefit recipient, or the widow or eligible child** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"By how much does my annual pension (or pension benefit) increase from the pay-day of 4 July 1974?"* Sections 3, 7-9, 13 (Part II, DFRB pensions) and 16-18, 22 (Part III, DFRDB pension benefits). Sections 10-12, 19-21 (other modifications, suspension, reclassification, commutation) are out of scope.

- s 3(1): the prescribed percentage is the lower of 1.4 × ((A − B) ÷ B) × 100 and ((C − D) ÷ D) × 100, with A, B the CPI for the March quarters of 1974 and 1973 and C, D the AWE for the same quarters; s 3(3): rounded to a tenth (a half rounds up).
- ss 7, 16: only if A > B and C > D; s 7 excludes a child's pension.
- s 8(2): DFRB pension payable before 1 July 1973 (or a widow's after it, of a contributor who retired before 1 October 1972): the prescribed percentage of (the annual pension before 1 July 1974 less 22.5% of the annual pension before 1 July 1973).
- s 9: s 82ZB pension payable from 1 July 1973 (not after 15 June 1974): the prescribed percentage of 77.5% of the annual pension before 1 July 1974, × the months from the day it became payable to 30 June 1974 ÷ 12.
- s 17: DFRDB pension benefit payable before 1 July 1973 (or to a widow or child of a member who retired before then): the prescribed percentage of 71.43% of the annual benefit before 1 July 1974 (for a child's pension, of the additional pension).
- s 18: retirement or death on or after 1 July 1973 (benefit not payable after 15 June 1974), or a s 78 benefit from 1 July 1973: the s 17 amount × the months from the day after the retirement or death (or from the day the benefit became payable) to 30 June 1974 ÷ 12.
- Months (ss 9, 18): a part month of half or more counts as a month, less is dropped; under a month counts as one.

## Inputs

A, B, C, D; which case applies; the annual amounts before 1 July 1974 (and 1973 for s 8); for a child's DFRDB pension, the additional pension; the first day of the counted period for ss 9, 18.
