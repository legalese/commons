# Independent expectations: Commonwealth Entities (Payment Surcharges) Act 2024

Written from BRIEF.md and `C2024A00101.txt` only, before reading any `.l4` file.

Commencement 3 December 2024 (s 2 table).

## Reading of the Act

- **payment surcharge** (s 4): (a) an amount charged, in addition to the payment, for processing it; or (b) an amount (however described) charged for one payment method rather than another. Either limb suffices. If neither, it is not a payment surcharge and s 5 does not authorise it.
- s 5(1): entity "may charge and collect" a surcharge if (a) under a Commonwealth/State/Territory law it has the power, function or duty of collecting the base payment; **and** (b) apart from s 5 the base payment is not already a surchargeable payment.
- s 5(2): "must not be such as to amount to taxation". A surcharge that amounts to taxation is not authorised by s 5.
- If (b) fails (another law already lets the entity surcharge), s 5 does not authorise; the entity may still surcharge under that other law, but that is outside this Act — s 5's permission answer is "no", and s 6 refund is not available under this Act (s 6 covers only surcharges charged "under section 5").
- s 6: may refund the whole or part "if the Commonwealth entity believes that the refund would be appropriate" — discretion; a refund is *possible* (not owed) for a s 5 surcharge, and not available under s 6 otherwise.
- s 7(1): "must take all reasonable steps to comply" with the policies. s 7(2): "A failure to comply with subsection (1) does not affect the validity of a decision to charge, collect or refund". So non-compliance never makes the charge invalid.

Differences from the brief: the brief's input is "whether the entity complied with the policies"; s 7(1)'s duty is to take all reasonable steps to comply, not to comply. Either way, s 7(2) makes validity unaffected, so the answer to "is the charge invalid?" is always no on this ground.

## Scenarios

Defaults: entity has power to collect the base payment; not already surchargeable; amount is a processing charge (limb (a)); does not amount to taxation; complied with policies.

| # | Facts | May charge under s 5? | Refund under s 6 possible (discretionary)? | Invalid for policy breach? | Act's words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Defaults | yes | yes (discretion) | no | s 5(1), s 6 |
| 2 | Limb (b) only: charge for card rather than other method | yes | yes | no | s 4 (b) |
| 3 | Neither limb (not a payment surcharge) | no | no | no | s 4 |
| 4 | No power to collect base payment | no | no | no | s 5(1)(a) |
| 5 | Already a surchargeable payment under another law | no | no | no | s 5(1)(b) |
| 6 | Amounts to taxation | no | no | no | s 5(2) |
| 7 | Defaults, but entity did not comply with policies | yes | yes | no (validity unaffected) | s 7(2) |
| 8 | No power and did not comply | no | no | no | s 5(1)(a), 7(2) |
| 9 | Both limbs (a) and (b) | yes | yes | no | s 4 |
| 10 | Taxation and non-compliance | no | no | no | s 5(2), 7(2) |
