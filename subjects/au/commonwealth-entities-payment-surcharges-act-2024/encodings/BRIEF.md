# Encoding brief: the Commonwealth Entities (Payment Surcharges) Act 2024 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Commonwealth Entities (Payment Surcharges) Act 2024** (No. 101, 2024), Commonwealth of Australia, as made, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2024A00101.txt` (CC BY 4.0). Commenced 3 December 2024.

## Seat

**The person making a payment to a Commonwealth entity** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"May the Commonwealth entity charge me a surcharge for how I pay; can I get it refunded; and if the entity did not follow the surcharging policies, is the charge invalid?"* The operative ss 4 (definitions of payment surcharge and surchargeable payment), 5, 6 and 7(1)-(2). Section 7(3)-(7) (the Minister's policies and directions) is out of scope.

## Inputs

- Whether the entity has the power, function or duty, under a law of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory, of collecting the base payment.
- Whether, apart from s 5, the payment is already a surchargeable payment (another law gives the entity power to surcharge it).
- What the amount is: a charge for processing the payment in addition to it, or a charge for one payment method rather than another; and whether it amounts to taxation (a finding).
- Whether the entity complied with the Commonwealth payment surcharging policies.

## Rules that matter

- s 6: a refund is in the entity's discretion ("may ... if [it] believes ... appropriate"). The encoding says so; it does not decide it.
- s 7(2): a failure to follow the policies does not affect the validity of the decision.
- A failing assertion is a finding. Never edit an expected value to match the code.
