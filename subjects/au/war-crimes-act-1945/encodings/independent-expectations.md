# Independent expectations: War Crimes Act 1945

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Base facts unless varied: the act is murder, which was an offence under the law then in force in part of Australia (s 6(1)(a), 6(3)); done in Europe on 1 June 1942; in the course of hostilities in a war; involved wilful killing; the accused is an Australian citizen; not permitted by the laws of war; a crime against humanity.

Notes on reading:
- "war" is confined to Europe and 1 Sep 1939 - 8 May 1945 (s 5). Hostilities outside Europe are not in a "war".
- s 9(1)(a) window is inclusive at both ends.
- s 10(1) life only where the offence "involv[es] the wilful killing of a person"; otherwise 25 years max (s 10(2)).
- s 17(2) needs both limbs.
- s 16: superior orders never a defence (only relevant to sentence).
