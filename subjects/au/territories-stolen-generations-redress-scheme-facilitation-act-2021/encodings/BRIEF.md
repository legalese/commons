# Encoding brief: the Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme (Facilitation) Act 2021 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme (Facilitation) Act 2021** (No. 126, 2021), Commonwealth of Australia, as made, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2021A00126.txt` (CC BY 4.0). It commenced on 1 March 2022 (F2021N00322).

## Seat

**The survivor who receives a payment under the Territories Stolen Generations Redress Scheme** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Does my redress payment affect my Commonwealth pension or benefits; is it counted as compensation; is it counted in an assets test; and can anyone take it from me?"* The operative s 4.

## Inputs

- The question asked of the payment: eligibility or entitlement to a Commonwealth pension, benefit, payment or service; whether it is compensation or damages; the value of assets; or whether it can be sold, assigned, charged, taken in execution or bankruptcy.
- The law asking: the Social Security Act 1991, the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986, another Commonwealth law, a State or Territory law prescribed by the regulations, or another State or Territory law.
- Whether that Commonwealth law expressly provides otherwise (s 4(5)), and whether the regulations exclude it (s 4(6)).

## Rules that matter

- Where the Act does not answer (for example, a State law not prescribed, on the compensation question), give NOTHING.
- A failing assertion is a finding. Never edit an expected value to match the code.
