## Where I read the Act differently from the brief, or the brief is silent

- Counting "within 90 days beginning on" day X includes day X, so the last day is X + 89, as the brief says. I agree.
- s 9 is a discretion ("may"); s 9 is in Part 3, not Part 2, so s 10 does not speak to it, but s 9 confers no right either.
- s 7(3) commitment is to the "Repatriation Commission" deciding MRC Act claims only; for other covered Acts only the s 7(2)(b) proportionate-time commitment applies.
- The Act does not say whether s 7(3) reaches claims lodged before 31 October 2019; not asserted.
- Family member is undefined in the Act.
