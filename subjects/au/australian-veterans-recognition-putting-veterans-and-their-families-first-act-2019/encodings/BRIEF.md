# Encoding brief: the Australian Veterans' Recognition (Putting Veterans and their Families First) Act 2019 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Australian Veterans' Recognition (Putting Veterans and their Families First) Act 2019**, Commonwealth of Australia, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2019A00096.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The veteran, or a family member** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Am I a veteran under this Act; by when has the Commonwealth committed to decide my MRC Act claim; and can I enforce any of Part 2?"* Sections 4 (veteran), 7(3), 9 and 10.

- s 4: a veteran is a person who has served, or is serving, in the Permanent Forces or the Reserves.
- s 7(3): the Commonwealth is committed to the Repatriation Commission deciding an MRC Act claim within 90 days beginning on the later of the day it receives the claim and the day it receives information or a document it requested under s 330 of that Act. The last day is that later day + 89.
- s 9: the Commonwealth may issue pins, cards or artefacts to veterans and their families.
- s 10: Part 2 creates no legally enforceable rights or obligations, does not affect the validity of decisions, and no proceeding lies on it.
