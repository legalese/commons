# Independent expectations: Parliamentary Retiring Allowances (Increases) Act 1967

Written from BRIEF.md and `C1967A00092.txt` only, before reading any `.l4` file.

Commencement: s 2, the day of Royal Assent, **8 November 1967**.

## Reading of the Act

- s 3(1): (a) entitled immediately before commencement to a pension under the 1948-1955 Act; or (b) under the 1948-1959 Act and s 18 of the 1964-1966 Act applied (to the person, or for a widow-pensioner, to her deceased husband). Weekly increase by table: 30.00 -> 18.16; 33.00 -> 23.89; 36.00 -> 21.79; 42.00 -> 21.79.
- s 3(2): widow of a man whose pension was increased under s 3(1), entitled under s 19(2)(a): "at the rate of Forty-eight dollars sixteen cents a week" = $48.16 weekly.
- s 4(1): 1948-1959 Act pensioner to whose pension a provision of s 17 of the 1964-1966 Act applied: annual increase 2,291.67 -> 437.50; 2,750.00 -> 525.00; 3,062.00 -> 525.00.
- s 4(2): widow of a man whose pension was increased under s 4(1): "at the rate of Two thousand seven hundred and twenty-nine dollars seventeen cents" = $2,729.17. The Act does not say "a year" in s 4(2); the brief does. Reading it as annual is consistent with the s 4 table being annual, so I accept the brief.
- s 7: increases under ss 3(1) and 4(1) "have effect from and including the first fortnightly payment of pensions made after the date of commencement". "After" 8 Nov 1967 — a payment on 8 Nov 1967 itself is not after.
- s 7 does not mention ss 3(2)/4(2); the widow's rates have no s 7 start date. I do not test an effective date for widows.
- Amount not in the table: the Act gives no increase (brief: NOTHING). Same for a person in no class.
- Tables are keyed by exact amount; a 3(1) pensioner on $2,291.67 a year (an s 4 amount) gets nothing under s 3, and vice versa.

Differences from the brief: s 4(2) "a year" is supplied by the brief, not the text (accepted). The brief's top-level "from when" applies only to s 3(1)/4(1) increases.
