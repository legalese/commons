# Encoding brief: the Parliamentary Retiring Allowances (Increases) Act 1967 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Parliamentary Retiring Allowances (Increases) Act 1967** (No. 92 of 1967), Commonwealth of Australia, as made (assented to and commenced 8 November 1967), deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1967A00092.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The former member, or the widow, entitled to a parliamentary pension** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"What increase in my pension am I entitled to, and from when; and as a widow, what is my pension?"* The operative ss 3, 4 and 7. Sections 5-6 concern the Fund and are out of scope.

- s 3(1): pensioners under the 1948-1955 Act, or under the 1948-1959 Act to whom s 18 of the 1964-1966 Act applied, get a weekly increase by the table: $30.00 → $18.16; $33.00 → $23.89; $36.00 → $21.79; $42.00 → $21.79. (2): the widow's pension is $48.16 a week.
- s 4(1): pensioners under the 1948-1959 Act to whom s 17 of the 1964-1966 Act applied get an annual increase by the table: $2,291.67 → $437.50; $2,750.00 → $525.00; $3,062.00 → $525.00. (2): the widow's pension is $2,729.17 a year.
- s 7: increases have effect from the first fortnightly payment after 8 November 1967.
- A pension amount not in the table gets no increase under this Act: NOTHING.

## Inputs

- Which class I am in (s 3(1)(a), s 3(1)(b), s 4(1), the widow of a man whose pension was increased under s 3 or s 4, or none); the amount of my pension immediately before 8 November 1967; the date of the first fortnightly payment after that day.
