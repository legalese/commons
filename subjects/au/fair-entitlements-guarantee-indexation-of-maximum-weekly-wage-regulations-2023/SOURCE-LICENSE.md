## The Australian Statistician's estimates (deposited beside the Regulations)

Section 6(3) of the Regulations indexes by "the last assessment of current average weekly ordinary time earnings published by the Australian Statistician before 1 July". The five releases that fix the maximum weekly wage for 2022-23 to 2026-27 are deposited beside the instrument, as published, as `registers/source-bundle/abs-awe-nov-2021.txt` to `abs-awe-nov-2025.txt` (the release pages' main content, converted to text; each with a `.meta.json` giving its URL, release date, sha256 and the figure read).
