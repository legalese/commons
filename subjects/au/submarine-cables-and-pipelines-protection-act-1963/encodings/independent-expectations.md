Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Default facts unless stated: the cable or pipeline lies beneath the high seas or in the EEZ and is not a Telecommunications Act cable in a protection zone; the ship is Australian-registered; what was damaged = a submarine pipeline; fault = my conduct resulted in it (s 7(1)); not acting to save lives or ship; prosecuted on indictment; not laying or repairing my own cable; no gear sacrificed.
