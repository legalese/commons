# Encoding brief: the Defence Force Retirement and Death Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1976 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Defence Force Retirement and Death Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1976** (No. 34, 1976), Commonwealth of Australia, compilation No. 1 of 10 December 2015, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A01465.txt` (CC BY 4.0). Part II (other than s 16) is deemed to have commenced on 1 July 1973; Part III on 1 July 1975.

## Seat

**The DFRB pensioner or DFRDB pension benefit recipient, or the widow or child** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"By how much did my annual pension increase under the 1976 Act, for 1973-74, 1974-75 or 1975-76?"*

- Part II (DFRB pensions): ss 5(4), 6, 7, 10 and 11. The increase is the prescribed percentage (the lower of 1.4 times the CPI rise and the average weekly earnings rise, each as a percentage, rounded to a tenth of 1%) of the non-contributory portion of the annual pension (s 8, an input), where both indices rose; none for a child's pension. Section 10 scales the 1973-74 increase for pensions from 1 October 1971 by months to 30 June 1973 over 21; s 11 scales a later year's increase for a recent s 82ZB pension by months over 12. An s 82ZB pension that became payable after 15 June before the period ends gets none.
- Part III (DFRDB pension benefits): ss 18-21. The prescribed percentage is the CPI rise from March 1974 to March 1975, rounded, applied to the annual benefit (for a child, the additional pension), scaled by months to 30 June 1975 over 12 for a retirement or death from 1 July 1974 (s 21).
- Months: whole months, a part month of at least half its days counted as one, and at least 1.
- Out of scope: s 8's computation of the non-contributory portion (an input), ss 9, 12-16, 22-25.

## Inputs

The scheme and year; the index numbers A, B, C, D as the relevant section defines them; the amount the percentage applies to; whether the increase is scaled, from which day, and the day the pension became payable.
