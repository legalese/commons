5 Definitions
In this instrument:
Act means the Social Security (Administration) Act 1999.
job search: a person makes a job search if the person makes contact (whether by telephone, in writing or in person) with another person or a body in relation to, and for the purposes of, obtaining paid work with that other person or body:
(a)	whether or not the contact is in relation to particular paid work; and
(b)	whether or not the person or body has advertised any paid work.
suitable work for a person means paid work that is not unsuitable to be done by the person.
Note:	Section 40X of the Act sets out when particular paid work is unsuitable to be done by a person.
	Part 2 — Adequate job search efforts
6 How to work out whether a person has undertaken adequate job search efforts
(1)	For the purposes of paragraph 42AC(1)(e) and subsection 42AC(2) of the Act, to work out whether a person has undertaken adequate job search efforts in relation to a particular period, the Secretary:
(a)	must assess whether, during that period, the person made the number of job searches that the person was required to make; and
(b)	if the person made the required number of job searches—may do any of the following:
(i)	assess the quality of the job searches made by the person in relation to that period;
(ii)	consider any other matter that the Secretary considers relevant.
(2)	The Secretary may have regard to the following matters for the purposes of subparagraph (1)(b)(i) in relation to the job searches made by the person in relation to the period:
(a)	whether they were made in good faith;
(b)	whether they were in relation to a variety of levels of seniority or remuneration that were suitable work for the person;
(c)	whether they were in a variety of fields and occupations, including fields and occupations other than those in which the person has qualifications or experience, if that work is suitable work for the person;
(d)	whether they were made using a variety of methods.
