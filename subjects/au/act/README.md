## Social security and benefits, and personal income tax (Priority 3 sample, 2026-10-20)

A connected set of fifteen ACT instruments, seven Acts and the eight instruments that set their current numbers. With them a member of the public can work out what the Territory pays them, and what it taxes them, on top of what the Commonwealth does.

### How the ACT set connects to the Commonwealth

Personal income tax and the main social security payments are Commonwealth law, already indexed or sourced under `subjects/au/`: [Income Tax Assessment Act 1997](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/), [Income Tax Rates Act 1986](../income-tax-rates-act-1986/), [Medicare Levy Act 1986](../medicare-levy-act-1986/), [Taxation Administration Act 1953](../taxation-administration-act-1953/), [Social Security Act 1991](../social-security-act-1991/), [Social Security (Administration) Act 1999](../social-security-administration-act-1999/), [A New Tax System (Family Assistance) Act 1999](../a-new-tax-system-family-assistance-act-1999/) and [Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986](../veterans-entitlements-act-1986/). The ACT levies no income tax and pays no general income support. So this sample is the territory law a Canberra resident meets on top of them, in two halves.

- **Territory benefits and support payments.** These are the payments the Territory, or a scheme it compels, makes to an individual: financial assistance to victims of crime; weekly compensation and other benefits for injured workers; and defined benefits for people injured in motor accidents. They meet the Commonwealth in the text: workers compensation stops weekly payments by reference to "pension age" in the Social Security Act 1991 (Cwlth), s 23, and sets its minimum by the national minimum wage under the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cwlth). The motor accident scheme uses Social Security Act health care and pensioner concession cards and the same pension age. Commonwealth law in turn decides how these payments are taxed and how compensation reduces a social security payment.
- **Territory taxes that fall on individuals.** General rates on the home, stamp duty on buying one, and land tax on a home that is rented out. Several of their concessions are tied to Commonwealth status. The pensioner rates rebate (Rates Act 2004, pt 7, div 7.3) is open to a holder of a pensioner concession card under the Social Security Act 1991 (Cwlth), and to a service pensioner or card holder under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 (Cwlth). The Pensioner Duty Concession Scheme turns on receiving a Services Australia or DVA age pension, the disability support pension, a DVA Gold Card or a service pension. The Duties Act's own disability exemption (s 109) refers to the age pension and the disability support pension under the Social Security Act 1991 (Cwlth).
- **The two halves connect to each other.** The Taxation Administration Act 1999 administers rates, duty and land tax alike. Under its s 139 the Minister fixes, every 1 July, the rates, fixed charges, rebate cap and concession schemes, and the 2026-27 determinations made under s 139 are deposited here. The same people (pensioners, home buyers, injured workers) appear on both sides: a Commonwealth pension opens the rates rebate and the duty concession, and a territory compensation payment affects the Commonwealth pension.

Total: 1,928,391 characters. Why each one is here:

- **Victims of Crime (Financial Assistance) Act 2016** and its **Regulation**: who counts as a primary or related victim or a homicide witness, and what can be paid (immediate-need, economic-loss and recognition payments). The Regulation holds the dollar amounts.
- **Workers Compensation Act 1951**: weekly compensation for injured private-sector workers (average pre-incapacity weekly earnings for the first 26 weeks, then a step-down to 65% with the statutory floor as a minimum), medical costs, and lump sums for permanent injury. ACT public servants are under the Commonwealth's Comcare scheme instead.
- **Motor Accident Injuries Act 2019** with the **Indexation of Benefits Declaration 2026 (No 2)**: the defined-benefits CTP scheme (income replacement, treatment and care, quality of life, death and funeral benefits). The Declaration sets the AWE-indexed dollar figures in the Act's formulas from 1 October 2026.
- **Rates Act 2004** with the **Amounts Payable—Rates Determination 2026** and the **Age 65 or Over—Rates Deferral Determination 2026**: rates on the home, the pensioner rebate (rebate cap $750 for 2026-27) and deferral for older owners (75%).
- **Duties Act 1999** with the **Duty**, **Home Buyer Concession Scheme** and **Pensioner Duty Concession Scheme** Determinations 2026: stamp duty on buying a home, the owner-occupier scale, nil duty for eligible first-time buyers and pensioners from 1 July 2026.
- **Land Tax Act 2004** with the **Land Tax Determination 2026**: the tax an individual pays on a residential property that is not their home.
- **Taxation Administration Act 1999**: assessments, objections, interest and penalty tax for all of the above, and s 139, the power under which the determinations are made.

### Currency and gaps

- All texts are the versions current on the register on 2026-10-20. The Acts' latest republications are dated 26 November or 16/26 December 2025, because no later amendment has commenced. The determinations are the 2026-27 ones (from 1 July 2026). The MAI figures apply from 1 October 2026; the Declaration's signature reads "31 August 2025", which looks like a slip in the source (it was notified 3 September 2026).
- **Pending**: the register lists proposed amendments to the Victims of Crime (Financial Assistance) Act 2016 by the Victims of Crime Legislation Amendment Bill 2026, the Crimes (Coercive Control) Amendment Bill 2026 and the Family, Personal and Sexual Violence Legislation Amendment Bill 2025, and to the Regulation by the first of these.
- **Not statutory, so not here**: the Electricity, Gas and Water Rebate ($800 in 2025-26) and other ACT concessions (Services Access Card, transport concessions) are programs run by the ACT Revenue Office and directorates. No Act or instrument setting their eligibility or amount was found on the register. There is no ACT first home owner grant: the Home Buyer Concession Scheme replaced it, and the A2000-21 number belongs to an unrelated Act. The old Rates and Land Rent (Relief) Act 1970 (pensioner rebate) is repealed; the rebate is now Rates Act 2004 pt 7.
- **Left out, could be added**: Workers Compensation Regulation 2002 (SL2002-20); Motor Accident Injuries (Premiums and Administration) Regulation 2019 and the 2026 Adjustment of Pre-injury Income Declarations; Duties (Pensioner Duty Deferral Scheme) Determination 2026 (DI2026-141); the Disability Duty Concession Scheme and motor vehicle duty determinations 2026; the MAI guidelines (DI2025-158 to -160, -308).

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Termination/severance**: Commonwealth matter. Unfair dismissal, notice and redundancy pay are governed by the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). The ACT does not legislate general termination rights. Payment of long service leave on termination sits under the Long Service Leave Act 1976, listed under Leave/holiday pay.
- **Pensions/social security**: Commonwealth matter: Social Security Act 1991 (Cth) and the superannuation guarantee legislation.
- **Government benefits**: Commonwealth matter: social security and family assistance legislation. ACT concessions are mostly administered under subordinate instruments or programs rather than a principal Act.
- **Personal tax/withholding**: Commonwealth matter: Income Tax Assessment Acts and Taxation Administration Act 1953 (Cth), which include PAYG withholding. The ACT has no personal income tax.
- **Passenger compensation**: Commonwealth matter: aviation passenger rights, the Civil Aviation (Carriers' Liability) Act 1959 (Cth), and the Australian Consumer Law.
- **Work permits/immigration**: Commonwealth matter: Migration Act 1958 (Cth).
