## Social security and benefits, and personal income tax (Priority 3 sample, 2026-10-20)

A connected set of fifteen ACT instruments, seven Acts and the eight instruments that set their current numbers. With them a member of the public can work out what the Territory pays them, and what it taxes them, on top of what the Commonwealth does.

### How the ACT set connects to the Commonwealth

Personal income tax and the main social security payments are Commonwealth law, already indexed or sourced under `subjects/au/`: [Income Tax Assessment Act 1997](../income-tax-assessment-act-1997/), [Income Tax Rates Act 1986](../income-tax-rates-act-1986/), [Medicare Levy Act 1986](../medicare-levy-act-1986/), [Taxation Administration Act 1953](../taxation-administration-act-1953/), [Social Security Act 1991](../social-security-act-1991/), [Social Security (Administration) Act 1999](../social-security-administration-act-1999/), [A New Tax System (Family Assistance) Act 1999](../a-new-tax-system-family-assistance-act-1999/) and [Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986](../veterans-entitlements-act-1986/). The ACT levies no income tax and pays no general income support. So this sample is the territory law a Canberra resident meets on top of them, in two halves.

- **Territory benefits and support payments.** These are the payments the Territory, or a scheme it compels, makes to an individual: financial assistance to victims of crime; weekly compensation and other benefits for injured workers; and defined benefits for people injured in motor accidents. They meet the Commonwealth in the text: workers compensation stops weekly payments by reference to "pension age" in the Social Security Act 1991 (Cwlth), s 23, and sets its minimum by the national minimum wage under the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cwlth). The motor accident scheme uses Social Security Act health care and pensioner concession cards and the same pension age. Commonwealth law in turn decides how these payments are taxed and how compensation reduces a social security payment.
- **Territory taxes that fall on individuals.** General rates on the home, stamp duty on buying one, and land tax on a home that is rented out. Several of their concessions are tied to Commonwealth status. The pensioner rates rebate (Rates Act 2004, pt 7, div 7.3) is open to a holder of a pensioner concession card under the Social Security Act 1991 (Cwlth), and to a service pensioner or card holder under the Veterans' Entitlements Act 1986 (Cwlth). The Pensioner Duty Concession Scheme turns on receiving a Services Australia or DVA age pension, the disability support pension, a DVA Gold Card or a service pension. The Duties Act's own disability exemption (s 109) refers to the age pension and the disability support pension under the Social Security Act 1991 (Cwlth).
- **The two halves connect to each other.** The Taxation Administration Act 1999 administers rates, duty and land tax alike. Under its s 139 the Minister fixes, every 1 July, the rates, fixed charges, rebate cap and concession schemes, and the 2026-27 determinations made under s 139 are deposited here. The same people (pensioners, home buyers, injured workers) appear on both sides: a Commonwealth pension opens the rates rebate and the duty concession, and a territory compensation payment affects the Commonwealth pension.

- **Victims of Crime (Financial Assistance) Act 2016** and its **Regulation**: who counts as a primary or related victim or a homicide witness, and what can be paid (immediate-need, economic-loss and recognition payments). The Regulation holds the dollar amounts.
- **Workers Compensation Act 1951**: weekly compensation for injured private-sector workers (average pre-incapacity weekly earnings for the first 26 weeks, then a step-down to 65% with the statutory floor as a minimum), medical costs, and lump sums for permanent injury. - **Motor Accident Injuries Act 2019** with the **Indexation of Benefits Declaration 2026 (No 2)**: the defined-benefits CTP scheme (income replacement, treatment and care, quality of life, death and funeral benefits). ### Currency and gaps

- All texts are the versions current on the register on 2026-10-20. The Acts' latest republications are dated 26 November or 16/26 December 2025, because no later amendment has commenced. The determinations are the 2026-27 ones (from 1 July 2026). The MAI figures apply from 1 October 2026; the Declaration's signature reads "31 August 2025", which looks like a slip in the source (it was notified 3 September 2026).
- **Pending**: the register lists proposed amendments to the Victims of Crime (Financial Assistance) Act 2016 by the Victims of Crime Legislation Amendment Bill 2026, the Crimes (Coercive Control) Amendment Bill 2026 and the Family, Personal and Sexual Violence Legislation Amendment Bill 2025, and to the Regulation by the first of these.
- **Not statutory, so not here**: the Electricity, Gas and Water Rebate ($800 in 2025-26) and other ACT concessions (Services Access Card, transport concessions) are programs run by the ACT Revenue Office and directorates. No Act or instrument setting their eligibility or amount was found on the register. There is no ACT first home owner grant: the Home Buyer Concession Scheme replaced it, and the A2000-21 number belongs to an unrelated Act. The old Rates and Land Rent (Relief) Act 1970 (pensioner rebate) is repealed; the rebate is now Rates Act 2004 pt 7.
- **Left out, could be added**: Workers Compensation Regulation 2002 (SL2002-20); Motor Accident Injuries (Premiums and Administration) Regulation 2019 and the 2026 Adjustment of Pre-injury Income Declarations; Duties (Pensioner Duty Deferral Scheme) Determination 2026 (DI2026-141); the Disability Duty Concession Scheme and motor vehicle duty determinations 2026; the MAI guidelines (DI2025-158 to -160, -308).

## Encodings (2026-10-24)

Five baseline encodings were made on 2026-10-24, each at `encodings/baseline-2026-10/` in its subject's folder. Between them they use all fifteen instruments of the sample. The other ten are brought into an Act's encoding rather than encoded on their own:

- the Victims of Crime Regulation into the victims of crime encoding;
- the Indexation of Benefits Declaration into the motor accident encoding;
- the Duty, Home Buyer Concession Scheme and Pensioner Duty Concession Scheme Determinations into the Duties Act encoding;
- the Rates and Rates Deferral Determinations, the Land Tax Act 2004 and the Land Tax Determination into the Rates Act encoding;
- the Taxation Administration Act 1999 (objections, interest, penalty tax and the offence of not notifying) into both tax encodings.

Land tax is encoded in the Rates Act's folder because it is measured by the Rates Act's average unimproved value (Land Tax Act s 9(4)), and the stable `l4` cannot IMPORT across encoding folders. So [`land-tax-act-2004/`](land-tax-act-2004/) has no encoding folder of its own.

Each encoding is a seat-neutral core in the text's own actors and terms, with its duties and deadlines as regulative rules, and seat layers that ask the questions and restate no rule. Every rate, fixed charge and threshold for duty, rates and land tax, the rebate cap and levies, the deferral percentage, and every condition of the home buyer and pensioner duty concessions, is read from the 2026 determinations, not from the Acts. They commence on 1 July 2026 and revoke earlier ones that are not deposited. So a duty transaction, a rates year or a land tax quarter outside 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 answers `not covered`.

Each was checked with the stable `l4` of VS Code extension 1.6.98 (`JL4_LIBRARY_PATH` unset). Every `.l4` file of all five was run again for this README on 2026-10-24: 0 errors, 0 warnings, every assertion satisfied. The Rates Act encoding was independently checked on 2026-10-24. All six issues raised were real and were fixed the same day (its `NOTES.md`, Corrections, and findings 25-28). The other four have had no independent test pass. There was no pipeline, coverage table or human gate. They are drafts, not for public use as advice. Each `NOTES.md` gives the method, edition, scope, every finding, and what would be needed before relying on it.

### Still inputs, because they are not deposited

- **Duties Act.** The market rate component of the interest on unpaid duty (Taxation Administration Act s 26(2)) and the value of a penalty unit (Legislation Act 2001 s 133; the republication notes give $160 for an individual). Whether a buyer receives a Commonwealth pension or holds a Gold Card, and any family law order or agreement, are asked as facts. Answering `not covered` because their instruments are not deposited: the Pensioner Duty Deferral Scheme (DI2026-141), the Disability Duty Concession Scheme, motor vehicle duty, and the 2025 and 2027 determinations.
- **Rates Act and Land Tax Act.** The interest rate for overdue rates and land tax (Rates Act s 21, Land Tax Act ss 19 and 19A; a Taxation Administration Act s 139 determination that is not deposited), the s 26 market rate component for deferred rates (Rates Act s 48(3)) and the value of a penalty unit. DI2026-150 determines no s 18(3) discount rate, so none is applied (finding 5). The unimproved values are inputs; valuation (pt 2) is not encoded. The Legislation Act 2001's rules for counting periods are not deposited (finding 27).
- **Victims of Crime (Financial Assistance) Act.** The CPI and WPI figures for indexation (ss 25, 97) are taken as already reflected in R11. Asked as facts, because their Acts are not deposited: whether Crimes Act 1900 s 66(1)(b) applies, domestic partner and periods of time (Legislation Act 2001), intimate partner (Family Violence Act 2016), the age of criminal responsibility (Criminal Code s 25), the ACAT's time limits (ACT Civil and Administrative Tribunal Act 2008) and the commissioner's guidelines (s 87).
- **Workers Compensation Act.** The Workers Compensation Regulation 2002 and the indexation notices are not deposited, so no current dollar figure is encoded. These are all inputs: the indexed single loss amount, maximum loss amount, death lump sum, weekly payment for a dependent child and funeral maximum; AWE; the statutory floor (the national minimum wage) and the pre-incapacity floor; and the index numbers. The Act's base figures and its indexing arithmetic are encoded ($100,000, s 49; $150,000, s 50; $539,002.37, $148.23 and $11,828.87, s 77), and the cases use them at an index factor of 1. Average pre-incapacity weekly earnings and hours (ss 21-26) and pension age are inputs.
- **Motor Accident Injuries Act.** The MAI guidelines (the receipt notice period, s 60; the funeral expenses that count, s 182), the Premiums and Administration Regulation 2019 (the interim weekly payment percentage, s 103(3)) and the 2026 Adjustment of Pre-injury Income Declarations (s 95). So pre-injury income, already AWE adjusted, is an input. So are post-injury earning capacity (the insurer's s 100 decision), the superannuation guarantee charge percentage (finding 20), the date the person reaches pension age, and ACT public holidays (the Holidays Act 1958 is indexed but not deposited). Only NI2026-402 is deposited. A day before 1 April 2026, or after 31 March 2027 for the biannual amounts (ss 96, 97, 103) or 30 September 2027 for the annual ones (ss 167, 174, 181), answers `not covered`.

The Territory encodings also meet each other, by links in their `NOTES.md`. A workers' compensation weekly payment reduces income replacement benefits week for week (MAI Act s 98). An accepted workers' compensation claim ends the MAI income replacement, treatment and care and quality of life benefits (s 50). Workers' compensation and motor accident benefits for the same act of violence are "associated payments" that reduce victims of crime assistance (s 47(1)(a)) or must be reported, and may have to be repaid, if they come later (ss 64-67).

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Termination/severance**: Commonwealth matter. Unfair dismissal, notice and redundancy pay are governed by the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). The ACT does not legislate general termination rights. Payment of long service leave on termination sits under the Long Service Leave Act 1976, listed under Leave/holiday pay.
- **Pensions/social security**: Commonwealth matter: Social Security Act 1991 (Cth) and the superannuation guarantee legislation.
- **Government benefits**: Commonwealth matter: social security and family assistance legislation. ACT concessions are mostly administered under subordinate instruments or programs rather than a principal Act.
- **Personal tax/withholding**: Commonwealth matter: Income Tax Assessment Acts and Taxation Administration Act 1953 (Cth), which include PAYG withholding. The ACT has no personal income tax.
- **Passenger compensation**: Commonwealth matter: aviation passenger rights, the Civil Aviation (Carriers' Liability) Act 1959 (Cth), and the Australian Consumer Law.
- **Work permits/immigration**: Commonwealth matter: Migration Act 1958 (Cth).
