Australian Capital Territory

Rates (Age 65 or Over —Rates Deferral) Determination 2026

Disallowable instrument DI2026–144
made under the
Rates Act 2004, s 46 (2) (f) (Determination for deferral of rates on application)

Part 1 Preliminary

1 Name of instrument
This instrument is the Rates (Age 65 or Over–Rates Deferral) Determination 2026.
2 Commencement
This instrument commences on 1 July 2026.
3 Definitions
In this instrument:
Act means the Rates Act 2004.
4 Determination—deferral of rates
For the purposes of section 46 (2) (f) of the Act, I determine that the determined percentage is 75%.
12 Revocation
This instrument revokes section 6 of Taxation Administration (Amounts Payable—Rates) Determination 2024 (DI2024-172).
