Australian Capital Territory

Motor Accident Injuries (Indexation of Benefits) Declaration 2026 (No 2)

Notifiable instrument NI 2026–402
made under the
Motor Accident Injuries Act 2019, section 19 (Indexation of defined benefits and quality of life damages)
1 Name of instrument
The instrument is the Motor Accident Injuries (Indexation of Benefits) Declaration 2026 (No 2).
2 Commencement
This instrument commences the day after notification.
3 AWE indexation factors and amounts
I declare that the AWE indexation factors and amounts as indexed in this instrument apply on and from the 1 October 2026 indexation day.

AWE indexation factors

1. For an amount that is to be AWE indexed biannually on 1 April and 1 October each year the factor is: 1.025.

2. For an amount that is to be AWE indexed annually on 1 October each year the factor is: 1.031.
