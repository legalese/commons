# Encoding brief: the First Home Super Saver Tax Act 2017 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**First Home Super Saver Tax Act 2017** (No. 133, 2017), Commonwealth of Australia, as made, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2017A00133.txt` (CC BY 4.0). It commenced on 1 July 2018, with Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Reducing Pressure on Housing Affordability Measures No. 1) Act 2017 (s 2, column 3).

## Seat

**The person who had amounts released under the first home super saver scheme** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"How much first home super saver tax must I pay?"* Section 3 imposes the tax payable under s 313-60 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997; s 4: "the amount of the first home super saver tax is 20% of the person's assessable FHSS released amount".

## Inputs

- The assessable FHSS released amount (a term of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997: an input, not worked out).
- Whether tax is payable under s 313-60 of that Act (an input).
- The date of the release; the Act imposes nothing before it commenced on 1 July 2018.
