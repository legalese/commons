	Schedule 2 — Transitional matters
1 Decisions not to be made before a particular day
(1)	The NHFIC must not, before:
(a)	the day determined under subitem (2); or
(b)	if no such determination is in force—the later of:
(i)	1 July 2018; or
(ii)	the day occurring 3 months after the day this Act commences;
make a decision to:
(c)	make a loan, investment or grant; or
(d)	grant financial assistance to a State or Territory; or
(e)	provide, to registered community housing providers (within the meaning of the National Housing Finance and Investment Corporation Act 2018), business advisory services and other assistance in capacity building.
(2)	The Minister may, by legislative instrument, determine a day for the purposes of this item. The day must not be a day occurring earlier than 1 July 2018.
