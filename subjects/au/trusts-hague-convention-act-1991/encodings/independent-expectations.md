# Independent expectations: Trusts (Hague Convention) Act 1991

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

"Baseline" = all four Article 2 features present (assets under trustee's control for a beneficiary/purpose; separate fund; title in trustee or nominee; accountable power and duty to manage), created voluntarily and evidenced in writing, conflict not solely between Australian State/Territory laws, settlor chose a law and that law provides for trusts of this category, trust created in accordance with it, significant elements not more closely connected with non-trust States, not manifestly contrary to public policy.

Outputs considered: (i) does the Convention apply; (ii) which law governs (chosen / most closely connected / none); (iii) must the trust be recognised (TRUE / FALSE / NOTHING = left to the forum).
