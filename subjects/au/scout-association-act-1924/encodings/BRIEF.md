# Encoding brief: the Scout Association Act 1924 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Scout Association Act 1924**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1924A00031.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The person who uses the Scout name or a Scout uniform or emblem** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Do I commit an offence by using this name or uniform; what is the penalty; and can I be prosecuted without the Minister's consent?"* Sections 2, 4, 5 and 6.

- s 2: using, without authority, the name "Scout Association", the name of a Local Branch, or a name implying another body is the Association or a Branch: an offence, 1 penalty unit.
- s 4: using a registered uniform, emblem, badge, decoration, mark or title so as to suggest authorisation by or connection with the Association or Local Branch, without the authority of the Association or the Local Branch for the area (the defendant bears a legal burden to prove authority): an offence, 1 penalty unit. Only for items registered under s 3.
- s 5: no prosecution without the Minister's consent.
- s 6: the Act does not apply to the Lifesaving Scouts of the Salvation Army.
