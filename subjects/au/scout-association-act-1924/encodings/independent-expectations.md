# Independent expectations: Scout Association Act 1924

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before opening any `.l4` file.

| # | Facts | Expected | The Act's words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A shop trades under the name "Scout Association" without authority | s 2 offence; penalty 1 penalty unit | s 2 "Any person who without authority used the name the 'Scout Association' ... commits an offence. Penalty: 1 penalty unit." |

| 3 | A club uses a name implying it is a Branch of the Association, without authority | s 2 offence | s 2 "any name implying that any other Society or body is the Association or a Branch" |

## Where I read the Act differently from the brief, or the brief is silent

- s 2 is drafted in the past tense ("used"); I read it as the conduct of using.
- s 2 "without authority" does not specify whose authority; I treat authority of the Association or a Branch as enough. s 4 is narrower: only the Association or the Local Branch **for the area**.
- s 2 has no express reversal of the burden; s 4 does. Unproven authority therefore defeats s 4 defence; for s 2 the prosecution must disprove authority (not modelled as a scenario beyond "no authority").
- The penalty unit dollar value is not in this Act (Crimes Act 1914 s 4AA); I assert only the unit count.
