# Independent expectations: Weapons of Mass Destruction (Prevention of Proliferation) Act 1995

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Defaults: supplying goods to another person; acting in Australia; I believe/suspect on reasonable grounds it may help a WMD program; no permit; no s 12 all-clear; no s 14 notice; dealing on 1 Jun 2025.

Notice period rule (s 14(4)): "a period of 12 months beginning on the day when the notice is given" ends at the end of the day before the corresponding day 12 months later (Acts Interpretation Act s 2G style); with no corresponding day, at the end of that month.
