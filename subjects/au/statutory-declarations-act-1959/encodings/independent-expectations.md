# Independent expectations: Statutory Declarations Act 1959

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before any L4 was read.

Base case (V1): approved form; signed by hand on a physical form; method identifies me and shows my intention, and is appropriately reliable; s 9 route, observed in person, prescribed person signed the declaration; use: for a Commonwealth law, no contrary intention; no false statement.
