Written from BRIEF.md and the source text (C2004A01087.txt) only, before reading any `.l4` file.

## Points where my reading may differ from the brief

1. **"Who bears the liability" when s 7(2) applies.** The Act protects the individual under s 6 and relieves the authority of s 7(1); nothing then imposes the liability on anyone. A "who bears" answer should be "neither", not "the individual".
2. **s 7(1) without s 6.** s 7(1) itself only says "while doing work for", not voluntary/organised. But it transfers only the liability the individual "would, except for section 6, incur", i.e. liability that s 6 actually removed. So s 7 follows s 6, including its s 5 and good-faith conditions.
3. **"Prescribed amount, if any".** If no amount is prescribed, the "less than" limbs (s 4(2)(a)(ii), 4(3)(a)(ii), (b)(ii)) cannot be satisfied; the regulations are not in the bundle, so the encoding must take the amount (or the comparison) as an input. I test only with the comparison given.
4. **s 4(3)(a) "continues to receive remuneration from the usual employer"** — I read the employer's pay as not counting as remuneration "for doing the work" in s 4(2)(a); s 4(3) is the route for it.
5. **s 8 "may incur"** — the duty arises even before the liability is established, where the claim relates to a liability the Commonwealth may incur under s 7. Where s 6 obviously does not apply (e.g. defamation), I expect no s 8 duty.
6. **s 9** extends to "work done by the individual" — not limited to voluntary work in its words, but the liability must be one the Commonwealth "incurs under this Act", which only s 7 creates.
