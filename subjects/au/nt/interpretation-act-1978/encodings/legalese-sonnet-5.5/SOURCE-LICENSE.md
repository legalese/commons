# Source licence

The terms that the quoted source text carries are recorded in the subject-level note, `SOURCE-LICENSE.md` in the subject folder, two folders above this one.
They are not repeated here.

What this encoding quotes: `-- src:N | ...` comment lines in the `.l4` files, and the lines quoted in `NOTES.md`.
Each is copied mechanically from `11865.txt` by a script that reads the text file, and the quote lines are checked against the text file by `srcrefs.py`.

`11865.txt` is an unofficial plain-text rendering of the reprint of the Act in force at 21 April 2023.
It is not the official text.
This encoding is not an official version of the Act, does not use the Northern Territory arms, and has not been checked against the official text.
