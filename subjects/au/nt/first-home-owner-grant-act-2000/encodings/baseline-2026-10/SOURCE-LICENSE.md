## Consequence for this subject

Reproduction is permitted on the conditions above, but the terms do not expressly grant a right to ADAPT. An L4 encoding that quotes the statute is arguably an adaptation. Verbatim deposit is within the permission. **Owner's ruling, 24 Oct 2026 (Michael):** the NT terms permit reproduction but are silent on adaptation; as for Canada, the texts may be quoted in L4 encodings in Legalese Commons, keeping the NT attribution and the unofficial-copy notice. This replaces the earlier "confirm before quoting" hold.
