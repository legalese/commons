Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Regulations 1999
1.	Citation
		These regulations may be cited as the Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Regulations 1999.
2.	Commencement
		These regulations come into operation on the day on which the Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 1998 comes into operation.
3.	Term used in these regulations
		In these regulations —
	10 year search means a search about a particular registrable event that is conducted in the Register, an index to the Register or both in respect of a period of 10 consecutive years or less.
	[Regulation 3 inserted: Gazette 15 Jun 2007 p. 2786.]

6.	Persons and bodies to whom Registrar may allow access to particulars or issue birth certificate (s. 36Q(4)(b))
	(1)	In this regulation —
	guardian, except in subregulation (2)(a), has the meaning given in the Guardianship and Administration Act 1990 section 3(1);
	represented person has the meaning given in the Guardianship and Administration Act 1990 section 3(1).
	(2)	The Registrar may allow access to the particulars referred to in section 36Q(3)(a) of the Act, or issue a birth certificate for a person showing both the person’s sex or gender before the person’s registration was changed and the person’s sex or gender after the person’s registration was changed, if an application requesting the access or issue of a certificate is made by any of the following persons or bodies —
	(a)	if the person is under the age of 18 years — a parent or guardian of the person;
	(b)	a guardian of the person, if the person has reached the age of 18 years and is a represented person who has a guardian appointed under the Guardianship and Administration Act 1990;
	(c)	if the person is deceased — the executor or administrator of the estate of the person;
	(d)	the chief executive officer of the department of the Public Service principally assisting in the administration of the Children and Community Services Act 2004.
	[Regulation 6 inserted: SL 2025/82 r. 5.]
7.	Fee to accompany application for registration of certificate
		For the purposes of section 70A(b) of the Act, an application referred to in the Gender Reassignment Act 2000 section 17(3) must be accompanied by a fee of $55.
	[Regulation 7 inserted: SL 2025/82 r. 5.]
8.	Prescribed public authorities (Act s. 35B(2))
		For the purposes of section 35B(2) of the Act, the Department of Internal Affairs of New Zealand is a public authority within the meaning given in section 35B(1)(c) of the Act.
	Note for this regulation:
		The Department of Internal Affairs of New Zealand is a department listed in the Public Service Act 2020 (NZ) Schedule 2 Part 1.
	[Regulation 8 inserted: SL 2025/220 r. 4.]
