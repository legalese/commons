## What the quoted text is

The text quoted in the `-- src:N | ...` comment lines of the modules, and in `NOTES.md`, is taken from a converter script's plain-text rendering of the compilation (`../../registers/source-bundle/mrdoc_49111.txt`).
That rendering is **not the official text**: it is the output of a converter script, its body and index disagree on some hyphens (the body reads "public halfholiday" and "ValuerGeneral", the index "public half-holiday" and "Valuer-General"), and it is not a copy supplied by the Parliamentary Counsel's Office.
Anyone relying on a provision should read it on the WA Legislation website.
The quotations are verbatim slices of that rendering, checked by `srcrefs.py`, with whitespace squeezed to single spaces; they are not edited or adapted.
