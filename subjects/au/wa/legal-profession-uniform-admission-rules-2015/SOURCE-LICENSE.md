## Who made these rules

These are not WA-drafted rules. They are made by the Legal Services Council, the
intergovernmental body established under the Legal Profession Uniform Law (the Schedule
to the Legal Profession Uniform Law Application Act 2014 (NSW)), under Part 9.2 of the
Uniform Law. They apply in Western Australia as subsidiary legislation of the State under
the Legal Profession Uniform Law Application Act 2022 (WA) s 14, and may be cited as part
of the Legal Profession Uniform Rules (WA). The deposited text is the WA consolidation
published on the WA Legislation website under s 15 of that Act; the consolidation itself
carries the notice "© State of Western Australia 2025 ... licensed under CC BY 4.0", which
is the licence relied on here. The same rules are also published for NSW and Victoria
(legislation.nsw.gov.au; Legal Services Council); those copies were not used.
