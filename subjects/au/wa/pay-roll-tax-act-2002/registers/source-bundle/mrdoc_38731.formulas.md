# Pay-roll Tax Act 2002 (WA) — formulas lost from the text deposit

The text deposit `mrdoc_38731.txt` (the consolidation served as mrdoc 38731) was converted from Word and dropped
every formula set as an equation image. This file transcribes them from the authorised rendering of the same
document: the Word file served by legislation.wa.gov.au,
https://www.legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/statutes.nsf/RedirectURL?OpenAgent&query=mrdoc_38731.docx,
fetched 2026-10-21 (sha256 9b34cb54869b5f4f66c24786c6a78967206fd121f1a40753ae804cd8eb9d1c17).

Transcribed by hand from the authorised rendering on 2026-10-21. Notation: × multiply, / divide,
− minus; brackets as printed. Letters are the section's own defined terms. Each formula is printed as a
fraction with the bracketed sum over W, then "× 100"; the screen rendering of these WMF images overprints, so
each was read from the image's text records with their positions (numerator line, "× 100" beside the fraction
bar, W below), and checked against the Table it completes (item 2 gives 5.5 at its lower bound; item 2 at its
upper bound equals item 3's starting $89 500 000).

## s 6(2) — local non-group employer, whole or part of an assessment year, Table item 2

    rate (%) = [(5 500 000 × Y) + 0.06 (W − (100 000 000 × Y))] / W × 100

- Image: `word/media/image2.wmf` in the download above
- Context: "For the purposes of item 2 in the Table to subsection (1), the rate is to be calculated as follows —"; W is the WA taxable wages for the year or part-year, Y the days in the year with WA taxable wages / the days in the year; (5) rates "are to be calculated to 4 decimal places".
