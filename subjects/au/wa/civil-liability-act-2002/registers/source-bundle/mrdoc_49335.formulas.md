## s 9(3) — non-pecuniary loss assessed above Amount C but below Amount A + Amount C

    Amount A − (Amount assessed − Amount C)

- Image: `word/media/image2.wmf` in the Word file above
- Context: "damages for nonpecuniary loss are not to be awarded in an amount that is more than the excess of the amount
  assessed over the amount calculated as follows —"
- Note: so the most that can be awarded is Amount assessed − (Amount A − (Amount assessed − Amount C)), which meets
  s 9(2) at Amount C (assessed − A) and the full assessment at A + C.
