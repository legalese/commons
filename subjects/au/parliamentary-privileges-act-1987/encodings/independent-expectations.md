# Independent expectations: Parliamentary Privileges Act 1987 (Cth)

Written from BRIEF.md and `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A03430.txt` only, before any L4 was read.

## Can a House punish me, and the most it can impose (ss 4, 6, 7)

| # | Scenario | Expected | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Natural person; conduct is an improper interference; not merely defamatory | House can punish; max 6 months imprisonment, or a fine up to $5,000 (not both) | s 4, s 7(1), (5)(a), (7) |
| 2 | Corporation; improper interference | House can punish; fine up to $25,000 | s 4, s 7(5)(b) |
| 3 | Conduct not, and not intended or likely to be, an improper interference | cannot punish | s 4 |
| 4 | Conduct is improper interference only by being defamatory/critical, outside the presence of a House or committee | cannot punish | s 6(1) |
| 5 | Same defamatory conduct done in the presence of a House or committee | can punish | s 6(2) |
| 6 | Defamatory words but intended to interfere is not "only" defamatory | can punish (if s 4 met) | s 6(1) "by reason only" |

## Offences against the Act (ss 12, 13)

| # | Scenario | Expected | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Natural person intimidates a witness about evidence | offence; max 6 months imprisonment or 50 penalty units | s 12(1) |
| 8 | Corporation does the same | offence; 250 penalty units | s 12(1) |
| 9 | Natural person punishes/injures a person on account of evidence given | offence; 6 months or 50 pu | s 12(2) |
| 10 | Natural person discloses in camera evidence without authority, not published by a House/committee | offence; 6 months or 50 pu | s 13 |
| 11 | Discloses in camera evidence that a House has since published or authorised | no offence | s 13 "unless" |
| 12 | Discloses with the authority of a House or committee | no offence | s 13 |
| 13 | Does nothing under ss 12-13 | no offence | ss 12-13 |
