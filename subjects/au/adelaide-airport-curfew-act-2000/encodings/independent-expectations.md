# Independent expectations: Adelaide Airport Curfew Act 2000

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

## Reading

- Curfew period (s 3): from 11 pm to 6 am, so 23:00-05:59 is in it. 22:59 and 06:00 are not (the period "ends at 6 am").
- Curfew shoulder period (s 3): 23:00-23:59 and 05:00-05:59.
- s 6: a take-off or landing in the curfew that is not permitted under Part 3 is an offence, with a maximum of 200 penalty units. Outside the curfew there is no offence.
- What is permitted:
  - an international aircraft with the Minister's shoulder permission, only in a shoulder period (s 9);
  - a low noise heavy freight aircraft with the Minister's permission, at any time in the curfew (s 11);
  - a propeller aircraft of 34,000 kg or less meeting the standards (s 12);
  - a jet of a type the regulations specify (s 13);
  - a take-off (not a landing) with taxi clearance before 11 pm (s 14);
  - an emergency (s 16(a), 17);
  - a take-off (only) to resume a flight interrupted by an emergency (s 16(b));
  - a dispensation kept to its conditions (s 16(c));
  - a national emergency declaration (s 16(d)).
- s 15(3): a return is due within 7 days after a non-05 weather landing by a permitted international or LNHF aircraft. A landing on day D gives a deadline of D + 7 (the day of landing excluded).
