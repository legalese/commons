# Independent expectations: Transferred Officers' Allowances Act 1948

Written from BRIEF.md and `../registers/source-bundle/C1948A00020.txt` only, before reading any `.l4` file.

## Working conventions

P = annual pension or retiring allowance (£). s 4 allowance a4; S = P + a4; s 5 allowance a5; T = P + a4 + a5; s 6 allowance a6. Total = a4 + a5 + a6.

- s 4: P < 360 only; P ≤ 288 → P/4; 288 < P < 360 → 360 − P.
- s 5: only if P ≤ 702 ("not ... exceeding Seven hundred and two"); S ≤ 260 → S/5; 260 < S ≤ 650 → 52; 650 < P < 702 → 702 − P. At P = 702 none of (3)-(5) gives a rate, so a5 = 0.
- s 6 (12 Nov 1955 to 31 Dec 1956 inclusive): T ≤ 156 → 52; ≤ 312 → T/6 + 26; ≤ 448 → 78; ≤ 500 → max(52, 526 − T); > 500 → 52.
- Before 31 Oct 1953: NOTHING (brief). Date 1 Jan 1956 unless stated (s 6 in force).

## Where I read the Act differently from the brief, or the brief is silent

- **P = 702 exactly.** s 5(1) applies, but s 5(5) needs P < 702, so the rate is nil; the brief's description agrees in effect.
- **s 6 end date.** I read "until the thirty-first day of December, 1956, and no longer" as including 31 December 1956.
- **s 6 applies at any pension size.** Unlike ss 4 and 5, s 6 has no pension ceiling, so P = 800 still gets £52. The brief agrees ("T > £500 → £52").
- **s 7 widow.** The Act speaks of the allowances payable to the husband "if he were alive and in receipt of a pension" without saying which pension; the brief takes the husband's pension as the input. The widow's own pension does not enter.
