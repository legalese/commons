# Independent expectations: Immigration (Education) Act 1971

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Conventions I adopt:
- "the period of N months starting on" day D includes D and ends at the end of the day before the corresponding day N months later. For a 1 March start, a 6-month period's last day is 31 Aug, a 12-month period's last day is the last day of February, and a 5-year period's last day is the last day of February.
- s 4C(2) and s 4D(2): an extension of N days (s 4C(5), s 4D(5)) adds N days to the end of the period.
- The person registers or starts "within" the period if the day is on or before the period's last day. Eligibility stops only once the period has passed without registration or commencement.
- Age at the visa commencement day: a person turns 18 on their 18th birthday.
- Defaults: holds a permanent visa; no vocational English; born 1 Jan 1990; visa commencement day 1 Mar 2023; paid the charge; no earlier permanent visa; not excluded; registered 1 Apr 2023; started a course 1 May 2023; no extensions; asked 1 Jan 2024.
