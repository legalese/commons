## Where I read the Act differently from the brief, or the brief is silent

- Brief s 7 list says "preventing or relieving sickness or suffering"; the Act says "preventing and relieving sickness, disease or human suffering". Immaterial for testing.
- s 8 disregards only the 6(1)(b) "available to the general public / sufficient section" limb; 6(1)(a) ("achievement would be of public benefit") must still be met (scenario 19). The brief says the same.
- s 9 applies only where the purpose fails public benefit *only because of* relationships between the Indigenous beneficiaries, and the entity receives/holds/manages native-title or traditional-rights benefits.
- s 10 removes only the public-benefit requirement in s 5(b)(i); the purposes must still be charitable purposes, and s 5(a), (c), (d) still apply (scenario 25).
- s 5(b)(ii) incidental purposes must be in furtherance of purposes "covered by subparagraph (i)", so at least one qualifying charitable purpose is needed (scenario 13).
