# Encoding brief: the Charities Act 2013 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Charities Act 2013** (No. 100, 2013), Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2013A00100.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The organisation (entity) asking whether it is a charity** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Are we a charity under the Charities Act?"* Sections 5-12. Sections 13-17 (government-entity contribution funds, and what the named purposes include) and the rest are out of scope: whether a purpose is of a given kind is an input.

- s 5: a charity is an entity that (a) is not-for-profit; (b) all of whose purposes are charitable purposes for the public benefit, or incidental or ancillary to and in furtherance of them; (c) none of whose purposes is disqualifying; and (d) is not an individual, a political party or a government entity.
- s 12(1): the charitable purposes (a) health; (b) education; (c) social or public welfare; (d) religion; (e) culture; (f) reconciliation, mutual respect and tolerance; (g) human rights; (h) security or safety of Australia; (i) preventing or relieving animal suffering; (j) the natural environment; (k) analogous purposes; (l) promoting or opposing a change in law, policy or practice in furtherance of (a)-(k) (or opposing a change that hinders them).
- s 6: public benefit (a finding, an input), presumed under s 7 for preventing or relieving sickness or suffering, advancing education, relieving poverty, distress or disadvantage, caring for the aged or people with disabilities, and advancing religion, absent evidence to the contrary; the "general public or sufficient section" limb is disregarded for relieving necessitous circumstances of individuals in Australia (s 8); native title benefits (s 9); and the whole public benefit requirement is disregarded for open, non-discriminatory self-help groups and closed or contemplative religious orders (s 10).
- s 11: disqualifying purposes are engaging in or promoting unlawful activities or activities contrary to public policy, and promoting or opposing a political party or candidate.

## Inputs

The kind of entity, whether not-for-profit, whether it is a self-help group or contemplative order under s 10; for each purpose, its kind under s 12 (or incidental, disqualifying, or none), and the findings on public benefit and evidence to the contrary.

## Rules that matter
