# Independent expectations: Civil Dispute Resolution Act 2011

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Commencement: Parts 2-5 (ss 6-19) commenced 1 August 2011 (s 2(1) item 2; endnote 3).
"Before the hearing date" (s 7(1)): the last day on which filing is in time is the day before the hearing date.
