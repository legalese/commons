# Encoding brief: the Superannuation (Pension Increases) Act 1967 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Superannuation (Pension Increases) Act 1967** (Act No. 90, 1967, as amended), Commonwealth of Australia, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1967A00090.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The eligible pensioner, or the widow** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"What increase in my annual pension am I entitled to?"* Section 4 and the definitions in s 3 it needs; s 7(1) (from the first fortnightly payment after commencement).

- s 4(1): the relevant amount × the additional unit entitlement (adjusted less actual unit entitlement).
- s 3 "the relevant amount": (a) $65; (b) where actual unit contribution is less than actual unit entitlement (and s 52 did not apply): $65 × contribution ÷ entitlement; (c)-(e): a proportion, taken as an input fraction of $65.
- s 4(2): reduced by the sum of increases under s 11 of the Superannuation Act 1957, s 4 of the 1961 Pension Increases Act and s 26 of the Superannuation Act 1963, not below nil.
- s 4(3): a widow gets one-half of her husband's increase if he elected that s 26 of the 1922-1959 Act should not apply to him (and did not revoke it); otherwise five-eighths.
- s 4(4): no increase for a pension under ss 53, 57 (unless State Fund contributions) or 58 of the 1922 Act.

## Inputs

The unit figures, the relevant amount case (and fraction for (c)-(e)), the earlier increases, whether a widow and which widow's case, and whether the pension is excluded.
