# Independent expectations: Immigration (Guardianship of Children) Act 1946

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Conventions I adopt:
- A person "turns 18" / "reaches the age of 18 years" on the 18th anniversary of birth; from that day they are no longer under 18.
- "within the period of 28 days after" a day, and "within 45 days after receipt", exclude the day of the event (Acts Interpretation Act 1901 s 36(1) item 4): the last day is the event day + 28 (or + 45).
- Default facts unless varied: child born 1 June 2015; entered Australia as a non-citizen; intends to become a permanent resident; no direction under s 4AA; has not left permanently; not exempted; day asked 1 January 2025.
