# Independent expectations: Asbestos-related Claims (Management of Commonwealth Liabilities) Act 2005

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before any L4 was read.

## Derived date

- Assent 19 October 2005 (s 2 item 1).
- Part 2 commences on the later of (a) the day after the end of the period of 7 days beginning on assent (19, 20, ..., 25 October = 7 days; the day after is **26 October 2005**) and (b) 1 July 2005. So **Part 2 commencement = 26 October 2005** (s 2(1) item 2). The Act's column 3 confirms this.
