# Independent expectations: Customs Undertakings (Penalties) Act 1981

Written from BRIEF.md and the Act only, before reading any .l4 file.

## Scenarios

| # | Facts | Expected | Working / words of the Act |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Undertaking breached; 10%; goods value $100,000; no partial compliance | $10,000 | s 4(1) prescribed percentage x value |
| 2 | Undertaking not breached | $0 | s 4(1) "where a person breaches" |
| 3 | As 1; quantity-based undertaking: 1,000 units required, 400 entered for home consumption under the relevant tariff item within the period | $6,000 | s 4(2)(a): reduction = 10,000 x 400/1000 = 4,000; 10,000 - 4,000 |
| 4 | As 1; value-based: required value $200,000, entered $50,000 | $7,500 | s 4(2)(b): reduction 10,000 x 50/200 = 2,500 |
| 5 | Unit value $50 x 2,000 units; 5%; no partial compliance | $5,000 | s 3(2): value 100,000; 5% |
| 6 | As 3 but goods entered outside the specified period(s) | $10,000 (no reduction) | s 4(2) "during the period, or each of the periods, specified in the undertaking" |
| 7 | As 3 but goods entered under a tariff item to which the undertaking does not relate | $10,000 | s 4(2) "under an item, or proposed item, of a Customs Tariff to which the undertaking relates" |
| 8 | As 3 but nothing entered (0 units) | $10,000 | reduction 0 |
| 9 | Quantity-based: 1000 required, 999 entered, 10% of 100,000 | $10 | 10,000 x 1/1000 |
| 10 | Undertaking given under s 268(2) (or as varied) breached, 20% of $30,000 | $6,000 | s 3(1) "Customs undertaking means an undertaking given under subsection 267(1) or 268(2) ... and includes such an undertaking as varied" |
| 11 | Unit value $10 x 500 units, 8%, quantity required 500 entered 125 | $300 | value 5,000; penalty 400; reduction 400 x 125/500 = 100 |

## Where I read the Act differently from the brief / gaps

- "Each of the periods": where the undertaking specifies several periods, the Act's words suggest the partial compliance has to be within the specified periods; how to aggregate entries across periods is not stated. Not tested beyond scenario 6.
- If the entered quantity exceeds the required quantity the person would not have breached (or the reduction would exceed the penalty); the Act is silent. Not asserted, noted as a gap.
- No rounding rule; exact values expected.
