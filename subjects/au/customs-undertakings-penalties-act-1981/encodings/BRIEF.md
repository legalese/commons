# Encoding brief: the Customs Undertakings (Penalties) Act 1981 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Customs Undertakings (Penalties) Act 1981**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A02425.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The person who gave a Customs undertaking** (under s 267(1) or 268(2) of the Customs Act 1901) (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"What penalty do I owe for breaching my Customs undertaking?"* Sections 3 and 4.

- s 4(1): the prescribed percentage (set out in the undertaking) of the value of the goods.
- s 4(2): where the person partly complied by entering goods for home consumption under the relevant tariff item in the specified period(s), the penalty is reduced in proportion: by the quantity entered ÷ the quantity required, or by the value entered ÷ the value required.
- s 3(2): where the undertaking sets a value per unit, the value is that value × the units.
