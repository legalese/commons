# Encoding brief: the Removal of Prisoners (Territories) Act 1923 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Removal of Prisoners (Territories) Act 1923**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1923A00014.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The prisoner sentenced to imprisonment in a Territory** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Can I be removed from the Territory to serve my sentence elsewhere; if removed, where can my conviction and sentence still be questioned; can I be sent home free after discharge; and if I escape, does my sentence keep running?"* Sections 2(1)-(2), 3, 5(1)-(2), 7A, 8(3) and 8AA(1). Licences (ss 8A, 10A), warrants' formalities and the regulations are out of scope.

- s 2(1)-(2): a prisoner is a person sentenced to imprisonment in a Territory (with the Act's exclusion); a commuted death sentence counts as the term of imprisonment.
- s 3(1): the Administrator of a Territory other than the Northern Territory may recommend removal where it appears to them that (a) there is no proper prison and removal is expedient; (b) further imprisonment there would likely endanger life or permanently impair health; (c) the offence was committed wholly or partly outside the Territory; or (d) the prisoner is of a class subject to removal under the Territory's law. s 3(2): the Governor-General may then order removal with the concurrence of the receiving State or Territory.
- s 8AA(1): no removal under a s 4(1) warrant if the Attorney-General has directed that this Act does not apply (the Transfer of Prisoners Act 1983 then governs).
- s 5(2): the conviction, judgment and sentence may be questioned in the Territory of origin as if not removed.
- s 8(3): after discharge at the end of the sentence (or release on licence or order) in the receiving State or Territory, the person may apply to be sent back free of cost if return would not breach a licence or order condition and would not be unlawful.
- s 7A: an escaped prisoner, when returned, must undergo the punishment they would have undergone had they not escaped.

## Inputs

The findings and decisions above (the Administrator's view, the Governor-General's order, concurrence, the Attorney-General's direction), and the facts of discharge and return.
