# Encoding brief: the Defence Forces Retirement Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1971 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Defence Forces Retirement Benefits (Pension Increases) Act 1971** (No. 74 of 1971), Commonwealth of Australia, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1971A00074.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The eligible DFRB pensioner, or the widow** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"By how much does my pension increase under the 1971 Act?"* Sections 5, 8, 10, 11 and 12, with the s 3 definitions they use (as inputs where they turn on the DFRB Act's scales).

- s 5: where the pension at current rate exceeds the actual pension entitlement, the increase is five-sevenths of (the pension at current rate less the basic pension entitlement).
- s 8: a pensioner who commuted part of the pension (or elected under ss 61A, 61B) gets the increase × (the rate payable before commencement ÷ the rate that would have been payable without commuting).
- s 10: the increase is reduced by any increase under the 1961 Act, Part III of the 1963 Act or the 1967 Act (not below nil).
- s 11: a pensioner on a reduced pension (the listed cases) gets a rate the Minister for Finance determines: the Act gives no figure, so NOTHING.
- s 12: a widow gets five-eighths of her husband's increase (one-half where he made the s 47(6) or s 48(4) election), computed without s 8 for his commutation; s 13 widows get a determined rate (NOTHING).
- Sections 4, 6 and 7 (s 73 pensions, s 81(2) pensions, deferred pensions) are out of scope: their figures come from other Acts or a formula missing from the text.

## Inputs

The class of pensioner; the pension at current rate, the actual and basic pension entitlements; for commutation, the two rates; the earlier increases; for a widow, her husband's figures and whether the one-half election applies.
