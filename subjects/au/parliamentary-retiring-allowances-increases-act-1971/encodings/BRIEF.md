# Encoding brief: the Parliamentary Retiring Allowances (Increases) Act 1971 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Parliamentary Retiring Allowances (Increases) Act 1971** (No. 75 of 1971), Commonwealth of Australia, as made (assented to and commenced 1 October 1971), deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1971A00075.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The former member or office-holder, or the widow, entitled to a parliamentary pension** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"What increase in my pension am I entitled to; and as a widow, what is my pension or its increase?"* Sections 3-8 and 12. Sections 9-11 (the Funds) are out of scope.

- s 3(1) weekly table: $48.16 → $13.98; $56.89 → $16.78; $57.79 → $16.78; $63.79 → $16.78. (2): widow $62.14 a week.
- s 4(1) annual table: $2,729.17 → $729.16; $3,275.00 → $875.00; $3,587.86 → $875.00. (2): widow $3,458.33 a year.
- s 5(1) annual table: $2,916.67 → $729.16; $3,500.00 → $875.00; $3,812.86 → $875.00. (2): widow $3,645.83 a year.
- s 6(1) weekly table (office-holders under ss 22H, 22K): $15.00 → $7.12; $18.00 → $8.54; $20.00 → $10.33; $23.33 → $11.36; $28.00 → $13.63. (2): the widow's pension increases by five-sixths of her husband's increase under (1).
- s 7 annual table (office-holders who ceased before 1 December 1968, or their widows): $1,050 → $183.75; $1,260 → $220.50; $2,500 → $437.50; $3,000 → $525.00.
- s 8: a s 19A(2) widow's pension (former Prime Minister) payable before 1 March 1959 increases by one-half.
- A pension amount not in a table gets no increase: NOTHING.
- s 12: from the first fortnightly payment after commencement (an input date).

## Inputs

The class (which section); the pension amount (weekly or annual as the table is); for an s 6 widow, her husband's pension amount; the first fortnightly payment date.
