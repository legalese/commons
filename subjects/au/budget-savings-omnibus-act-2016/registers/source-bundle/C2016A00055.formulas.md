Method. Recovered on 2026-10-24 by the converter `harvest/formulas/docx-formulas.py` (in the private
Legalese workbench), which walks the Word rendering paragraph by paragraph and finds every equation image, Word
symbol character (w:sym), Office Math object and picture:

- an equation image carries alt text in the Register's spoken form ("Start formula start fraction A over B end
  fraction times C end formula"); the converter parses it into the linear notation below, then reads the text
  the image itself draws (its EMF or WMF text records) and checks that the letters and digits are the same as
  the alt text's ("verified"), and that the words the alt text puts above a fraction bar are drawn above the
  words it puts below it ("fraction layout agrees");
- where the alt text and the image differ, or the image has no usable alt text, the formula was transcribed by
  reading the image, and the entry says so: the image, which is what the Register prints, governs;
- a Word symbol the text conversion dropped (×, ÷, ≤ ...) is given with its whole paragraph, restored;
- a picture that is not a formula (a flowchart, a diagram) is listed with its alt text.

Every entry was then checked against the text around the gap in the deposit: the words before it (which the
deposit keeps), the line of the deposit after which it was lost, and the terms its "where:" list defines, each
of which is looked for in the formula (an entry says which, if any, are not there).

Notation: × multiply, / divide (a fraction; a numerator or denominator of more than one term is bracketed),
− minus, ÷ divided by, ^ a superscript; other brackets as printed. Words are the provision's own.
