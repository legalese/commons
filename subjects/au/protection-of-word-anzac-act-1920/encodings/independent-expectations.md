# Independent expectations: Protection of Word "Anzac" Act 1920

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Question: if my use of "Anzac" (or a word resembling it) breaches the Regulations, what is the most I can get?

Reading of s 22:
- s 22(2): a breach of reg 2(1) involving a use in connection with a trade, business, calling or profession; an entertainment, lottery or art union; or as the name of a private residence, boat, vehicle, charitable or other institution, or a building connected with it, carries up to 12 months' imprisonment.
- s 22(3): a breach of reg 3(1) involving a street, road or park name carries up to 12 months' imprisonment.
- s 22(1)(g): any other breach of regulations made under s 22 carries only what the regulations prescribe, a fine capped at $1,000. There is no imprisonment.
- No breach: no penalty.
