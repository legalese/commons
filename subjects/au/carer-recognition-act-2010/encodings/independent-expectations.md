## s 10: can I enforce it?

| # | Scenario | Expected | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | Carer seeks to enforce a s 8(2) consultation duty in court | no legally enforceable right | s 10(1) |
| 25 | Agency failed to comply; carer seeks review of a decision on that ground | decision remains valid; not a ground for review or challenge | s 10(2) |

## Where I read the Act differently from (or more precisely than) the brief

- **"frailty and age"**: the brief says "frailty and age"; s 5(1)(d) says "is frail and aged" — both conditions must hold. Scenario 14.
- **Associated provider**: the brief says "under a contract or funding arrangement with such an agency". s 4(2)(b) also catches a second-tier entity contracted by the first entity (scenario 20), and the contract must be *for care supports* (scenario 23). s 4(3) excludes a contract a public service care agency *or another person or body* enters into with a State or Territory, so a subcontract with a State is also excluded.
- **s 5(2) is per care**: "not a carer in respect of care ... he or she provides" under a contract etc. A paid worker who also cares unpaid for a relative is a carer in respect of that unpaid care. An encoding asking a single "how do I do it" question cannot represent mixed arrangements; flagged as a gap, not tested.
- **s 8(2) consultation** attaches only to developing or evaluating care supports, not implementing or providing; s 8(1) attaches to all four.
- **s 9 has no consultation duty** and associated providers have no s 7(2) HR duty (unless independently a public service agency).
- **s 10** is unconditional: whatever the facts, the carer cannot enforce and cannot challenge a decision for non-compliance.
