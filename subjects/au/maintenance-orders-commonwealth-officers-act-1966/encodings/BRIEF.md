# Encoding brief: the Maintenance Orders (Commonwealth Officers) Act 1966 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Maintenance Orders (Commonwealth Officers) Act 1966**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1966A00059.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The person whose pay from the Commonwealth (or a Commonwealth authority) is subject to a maintenance order** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Can my Commonwealth pay be attached to enforce a maintenance order; and if there are several orders, how is my pay split between them?"* Sections 5, 6(1), (3) and 7(1), (3).

- s 5: Commonwealth money is not subject to attachment under an attachment of earnings order, except as this Act provides.
- s 6(1): where a State or Territory law provides for enforcing maintenance orders by attachment of earnings orders, it applies to the Commonwealth and Commonwealth authorities as employers; s 6(3): despite any other law preventing attachment.
- s 7(1): with two or more orders against the one person, the Commonwealth complies with them in the order in which they came into force, each from the earnings left after the earlier ones; s 7(3): a varied order is taken to have come into force, as varied, when it first did.

## Inputs

Whether the State or Territory law provides for attachment of earnings orders for maintenance; the earnings payable on the occasion; each order's date of coming into force and the amount it requires on that occasion.
