# Independent expectations: Commonwealth Motor Vehicles (Liability) Act 1959

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before opening any `.l4` file.
Commencement: Royal Assent, 4 December 1959 (s 2; endnote 3).

Two questions per scenario: **P** = does the s 5(1) conclusive presumption of agency apply;
**J** = does s 6 apply (jury question decided as for a like claim against the owner of an insured vehicle).

## Where I read the Act differently from the brief, or the brief is silent

- The brief's s 6 summary ("for a claim about an uninsured vehicle") omits that s 6 requires proceedings in a Court of a State or Territory and a damages claim against the Commonwealth or an authority (not a contribution claim). It also has no ownership/lease condition and is not cut down by s 5(1A) — so a registered-but-uninsured Commonwealth vehicle still gets s 6 (scenario 2), and a non-Commonwealth uninsured vehicle does too (scenario 7).
- s 5(3) extends only "this section" (s 5) to pre-commencement occurrences. Whether s 6 applies to proceedings about a pre-commencement occurrence is not stated; I do not assert it.
- s 5(1A) requires registration or licence "at all relevant times"; a partial registration does not displace the presumption (scenario 4).
- An occurrence on or before 8 September 1958 is outside s 5 altogether.
