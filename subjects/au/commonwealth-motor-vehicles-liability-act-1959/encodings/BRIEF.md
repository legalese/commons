# Encoding brief: the Commonwealth Motor Vehicles (Liability) Act 1959 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Commonwealth Motor Vehicles (Liability) Act 1959**, Commonwealth of Australia, as amended, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1959A00094.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The person (or the estate or dependants of the person) killed or injured by a Commonwealth vehicle** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"In my claim against the Commonwealth (or a Commonwealth authority) for death or injury caused by its vehicle, is the driver conclusively presumed to be its agent; and is a jury question decided as if the vehicle had been insured?"* Sections 3(2), 5 and 6.

- s 5(1): in a claim against the Commonwealth or a Commonwealth authority for death or personal injury caused by, or arising out of the use of, an uninsured motor vehicle owned by or leased to it (or a claim for contribution about that liability), the driver is conclusively presumed to have been its agent acting within authority.
- s 5(1A): not if, at all relevant times, the vehicle was registered or a motor vehicle licence was in force for it under a State or Territory law.
- s 5(3): for occurrences before commencement but after 8 September 1958.
- s 6: for a claim about an uninsured vehicle, jury or not is decided as for a like claim against the owner of an insured vehicle.
- s 3(2): not for occurrences outside Australia and the Territories.

## Inputs

The claim against the Commonwealth or an authority; owned or leased; uninsured (no third-party policy in force); registered or licensed; the occurrence date and place.
