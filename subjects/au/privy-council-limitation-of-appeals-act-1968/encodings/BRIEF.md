# Encoding brief: the Privy Council (Limitation of Appeals) Act 1968 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Privy Council (Limitation of Appeals) Act 1968** (Act No. 36 of 1968), Commonwealth of Australia, compilation prepared on 7 November 2000 (amendments up to Act No. 216 of 1973; register id C2004C00594), deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C1968A00036.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The litigant** who wants to ask Her Majesty in Council for leave to appeal (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"May I ask the Privy Council for leave to appeal from this decision?"* The whole operative Act: s 3 (decisions of the High Court) and s 4 (decisions of other federal courts and the Supreme Courts of the Territories). Sections 1-2 are machinery; the date the Act commenced is fixed by Proclamation and is not in the text, so whether a proceeding began before it is an input.

## Inputs

- The court whose decision it is: the High Court, another federal court, the Supreme Court of a Territory, or another court.
- For a High Court decision: whether it was on appeal from a State Supreme Court exercising non-federal jurisdiction; whether it involved applying or interpreting the Constitution, a Commonwealth law or an instrument made under one; whether the proceeding began in a court before this Act commenced.

## Rules that matter

- The Act limits only; it does not create a right to appeal. Where it does not bar asking, the answer is that this Act does not bar it.
- Where the source does not answer, give NOTHING; never invent.
- A failing assertion is a finding. Never edit an expected value to match the code.
