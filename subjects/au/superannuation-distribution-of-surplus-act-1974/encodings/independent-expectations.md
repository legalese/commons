# Independent expectations: Superannuation (Distribution of Surplus) Act 1974

Written from BRIEF.md and the deposited source only, before reading any `.l4` file.

## Readings of the Act

- **Relevant period** (s 3(1)): "the period of 10 years that ended on 30 June 1972" — so 1 July 1962 to 30 June 1972 inclusive.
- **Eligible contributor** (s 3(1)): obligation to contribute under Part III "came into existence before the end of the relevant period". The period ends at the end of 30 June 1972, so an obligation arising on 30 June 1972 counts; one arising on 1 July 1972 does not.
- Limb (a): "is a contributor on the date on which this Act receives the Royal Assent" — 11 April 1974.
- Limbs (b) and (c): "after the commencement of the relevant period but before" 11 April 1974. **Divergence from the brief's wording**: the brief says "after 1 July 1962". The Act says after the *commencement* of the period; the period commences at the start of 1 July 1962, so I read an event *on* 1 July 1962 as within the window. An event on 30 June 1962 is outside. An event on 11 April 1974 is outside (strictly "before").
- Limb (c) needs death while a contributor AND a widow or eligible children (at death or since).
- Exclusion: deferred benefits under s 119W became applicable **on or before** 30 June 1972. Applicable on 1 July 1972 does not exclude.
- **Eligible pensioner** (s 3(1)): a pension, other than non-contributory, payable (or would have been but for suspension) at any time during the relevant period under one of the listed provisions (a)-(f). Non-contributory pension alone does not qualify.
- s 3(2) deeming: deferred benefits applicable under s 119W on 30 June 1972 but not payable on or before that date → eligible pensioner.
- s 4 amounts: contributors $52,525,000; pensioners $17,490,000; total $70,015,000.
- s 6(2): entitled person died before payment → (a) widow alive at time of payment is paid; (b) otherwise persons the Board determines. If alive, the person themselves is paid (s 6(1)).
