# Encoding brief: the Superannuation (Distribution of Surplus) Act 1974 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Superannuation (Distribution of Surplus) Act 1974**, Commonwealth of Australia, as made (assented to 11 April 1974, commenced that day), deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A00074.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The contributor or pensioner of the Superannuation Fund** (or their widow) (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Am I an eligible contributor or eligible pensioner, entitled to a share of the 1972 surplus; and if the person entitled has died, who is paid?"* Sections 3, 4 and 6. The individual shares are allocated by the Treasurer on the actuary's advice (s 5) and are not computable from the Act; the amounts available are $52,525,000 for contributors and $17,490,000 for pensioners (s 4).

- Relevant period: the 10 years ending on 30 June 1972.
- Eligible contributor (s 3(1)): obliged to contribute under Part III before the end of the relevant period, and (a) a contributor on 11 April 1974, (b) became a pensioner after the commencement of the relevant period (1 July 1962) and before 11 April 1974, or (c) died in that window while a contributor, leaving a widow or eligible children; excluding a person for whom deferred benefits under s 119W became applicable on or before 30 June 1972.
- Eligible pensioner (s 3(1)-(2)): a pension (not non-contributory) payable, or suspended, at any time in the relevant period under the listed provisions; or deferred benefits applicable on 30 June 1972 but not yet payable.
- s 6(2): if the person entitled has died before payment, the widow (if alive) is paid; otherwise the Board determines.
