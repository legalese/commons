# Independent expectations: Protection of Cultural Objects on Loan Act 2013

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Conventions I adopt:
- s 8(2)(a)(i): "the end of 24 months starting on the day the object is imported". If an object is imported on 15 March 2024, the 24 months run 15 Mar 2024 to 14 Mar 2026 inclusive, and the Part ceases at the end of 14 March 2026. Where there is no corresponding day (import on 29 Feb 2024), the period ends at the end of 28 Feb 2026 (Acts Interpretation Act s 2G). So the **last protected day** = import + 24 months − 1 day.
- Export (s 8(2)(a)(ii)): the Part ceases on export. I test only the day before the export and the day after it.
- Minister's declaration (s 8(2)(b)): it has effect only if its day is at least 24 months after the import day. I treat the declared day as the day protection ceases, so the day before is protected and the day after is not. A purported declaration earlier than 24 months has no effect.
- Defaults: a foreign individual lender; a temporary loan arrangement; the object is imported mainly for exhibition; the institution is approved; an s 8(1)(d) connection exists; the import contravenes no prescribed law; not Class A; not excluded; imported 15 March 2024; asked 1 January 2025; no export or declaration.
