# Independent expectations: Control of Naval Waters Act 1918

Written from BRIEF.md and the source text only, before reading any L4.

Date reading for s 8(3) ("after the expiration of 3 months after the making of a demand"): the 3 months run from the day after the demand and end on the corresponding day 3 months later (end of the last day of the month if there is no corresponding day; Acts Interpretation Act 1901 s 36 convention). Sale is possible from the following day. E.g. demand 15 Jan 2024 -> period ends 15 Apr 2024 -> sale from 16 Apr 2024.

The maximum penalty is 10 penalty units (ss 6(2), 6A(2)); the dollar value of a penalty unit is not in this Act.
