## Points where my reading may differ from the brief

- The Act cites "Privy Council (Limitations of Appeals) Act 1968-1973" (plural "Limitations"): a drafting slip for the Privy Council (Limitation of Appeals) Act 1968, as amended to 1973. No effect on the logic.
- "Could, but for this Act, have been asked" includes the 1968 s 3(2) pre-1968 saving: a pre-1968 proceeding with federal content could be asked under 1968 s 3, so it is caught by the 1975 bar — but such a proceeding necessarily also began before 1975 and so is saved. Consistent.
