# Encoding brief: the Privy Council (Appeals from the High Court) Act 1975 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Privy Council (Appeals from the High Court) Act 1975** (No. 33 of 1975), Commonwealth of Australia, as made, deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A00255.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The litigant** who wants to ask Her Majesty in Council for special leave to appeal from the High Court (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"May I still ask for special leave to appeal from this High Court decision?"* The operative s 3. It closes the matters that remained open under s 3 of the Privy Council (Limitation of Appeals) Act 1968-1973, except for proceedings begun in a court before this Act commenced (a date fixed by Proclamation, not in the text: an input).

## Inputs

- Whether special leave could, but for this Act, have been asked under s 3 of the 1968 Act.
- Whether the proceeding was commenced in a court before this Act commenced.

## Rules that matter

- The Act only limits. Where special leave could not have been asked under the 1968 Act anyway, this Act does not decide it: the answer is NOTHING.
- A failing assertion is a finding. Never edit an expected value to match the code.
