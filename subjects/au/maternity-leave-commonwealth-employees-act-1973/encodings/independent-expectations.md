# Independent expectations: Maternity Leave (Commonwealth Employees) Act 1973

Written from BRIEF.md and `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A00024.txt` only, before reading any L4.

Conventions:
- A period of N weeks "commencing on" day D runs from D to D + 7N - 1 inclusive. So 6 weeks from D ends on D + 41, 12 weeks on D + 83, and 52 weeks on D + 363.
- "6 weeks before the expected date of birth" (EDB) is EDB - 42 days. A confinement must be on or after EDB - 140 days (20 weeks; s 3 "not earlier than twenty weeks before").
- Compulsory absence (s 6(1)(b)):
  - (i) still pregnant at EDB - 42: from EDB - 42 to confinement + 41.
  - (ii) confined before EDB - 42: from confinement to confinement + 41.
  - Confinement exactly on EDB - 42 gives the same dates either way.
- Longest leave (s 6(1)(a), (c)): 52 weeks from the start of the compulsory period, as moved by s 7(2).
- s 7(2):
  - (a) confined more than 6 weeks before EDB: the permission to continue duty has no effect.
  - (b) confined before the permitted date, but not more than 6 weeks before EDB: the period starts on the confinement.
  - (c) otherwise: it starts the day after the permitted date.
- s 7(3): a resumption date before the end of the compulsory period ends that period the day before. A later date has no effect.
- Paid days (s 6(3), (4)):
  - She is taken to be absent from the start of the compulsory period through the longest leave, unless she resumes under s 7(3), in which case her absence ends the day before resuming.
  - If the absence exceeds 12 weeks, the first 12 weeks are paid (start to start + 83). Otherwise the whole absence is paid.
  - Days before the day her continuous service first exceeds 12 months (Q) are not paid. The paid window is clipped, not moved (s 6(4) "not entitled ... in respect of any absence that occurs before the day"). Day Q itself is paid.
- Defaults: the Act applies; EDB 1 Jul 2024; confined 1 Jul 2024; no permission to continue or resume; Q = 1 Jan 2020; no s 6(4C) or (4F) circumstance.
