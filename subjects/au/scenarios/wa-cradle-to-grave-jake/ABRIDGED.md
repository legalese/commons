# Cradle to grave, abridged — Jake in twenty beats

The same life as [`SCENARIO.md`](SCENARIO.md), two beats a chapter, with his age and what the law says back. Every Act he meets is deposited and, on the day of writing, unencoded; the beats are flagged `"abridged": true` in [`facts.json`](facts.json).

Where Jun Hao's stops were for a human to decide, Jake's are for a different reason: the law often answers "nothing happens". No charge, no conviction, no record, no distinction for a child born outside marriage, no entry levy, no family exclusion order, no duty on shares. A voice that says "nothing" accurately is as grounded as one that says "this".

## Chapter 0 — Born

**Beat 1 · 30 August 2006 · KEMH.** *Citizen?* — Australian Citizenship Act s 12.

**Beat 2 · October 2006 · a kitchen table.** *What does a second child bring?* — Family Assistance Act: the payments of the day; the 2006 rates are in no row, which the voice says.

## Chapter 1 — Primary school and the letter

**Beat 3 · 20 May 2016 · the letter.** *Whose duty is attendance?* — School Education Act s 23: the parents'; s 38: their offence; ss 39-40: a panel and an agreement first.

**Beat 4 · October 2018 · Year 7.** Nothing legal happens. The voice is quiet.

## Chapter 2 — The shopping centre

**Beat 5 · 20 November 2021 · a store entrance.** *What is this?* — Criminal Code s 378: stealing. *Will I go to court?* — Young Offenders Act ss 22-30: not if the police caution or refer, and they refer.

**Beat 6 · 14 February 2022 · the juvenile justice team.** *What happens when I finish the plan?* — s 30: no charge, no conviction, nothing recorded.

## Chapter 3 — TAFE and no national service

**Beat 7 · 2 June 2023 · outside the campus.** *Is this illegal?* — Tobacco and Other Products Control Act s 106: for the shop that sold it to a sixteen-year-old; not for him.

**Beat 8 · 20 November 2026 · a bunk at a mate's.** *Is it spent yet?* — Spent Convictions Act s 3: there is nothing to spend. The voice says "nothing" and means it.

## Chapter 4 — The platform years

**Beat 9 · 6 March 2027 · an e-bike.** *What am I?* — Fair Work Act s 15AA and Part 3A-2: a contractor, and an employee-like worker the Commission may set standards for; ABN Act s 8: and a business.

**Beat 10 · 12 April 2029 · a car door.** *Is it workers' comp?* — Workers' Compensation and Injury Management Act s 5: no, a contractor; Motor Vehicle (Third Party Insurance) Act s 4: the driver's insurer; Civil Liability Act Part 2: the thresholds.

## Chapter 5 — Tahlia and Ruby

**Beat 11 · 22 January 2033 · Armadale Hospital.** *We're not married. Does it matter?* — to the citizenship, the registration, the parental leave and the family payments: no. The law has no word for it.

**Beat 12 · 16 September 2033 · a registry office.** *What does this change for Ruby?* — Marriage Act s 45 makes the marriage; nothing in it reaches the child.

## Chapter 6 — The security job and the debts

**Beat 13 · 10 March 2036 · the Magistrates Court.** *Where does an underpayment go?* — Fair Work Act s 548: a small claim; s 544: six years back. $4,100.

**Beat 14 · 20 April 2037 · a debt agreement administrator's office.** *Is there another way?* — Bankruptcy Act Part IX: a proposal, the Official Receiver's gate, the creditors' vote by value. They vote yes.

## Chapter 7 — Separation and an apartment

**Beat 15 · 15 June 2038 · a Services Australia assessment.** *How much do I pay?* — Child Support (Assessment) Act Part 5: the formula on both incomes and the care.

**Beat 16 · 20 August 2039 · the Family Court.** *How long apart?* — Family Law Act s 48: twelve months. *What do we divide?* — s 79: almost nothing.

## Chapter 8 — BetStop and the stairwell

**Beat 17 · 8 May 2046 · his sister's phone.** *Can she have me banned?* — Casino Control Act s 25: the casino and the Commission may exclude; a family may not apply. *Can I?* — Interactive Gambling Act Part 7A: BetStop, for every licensed app at once.

**Beat 18 · 5 October 2053 · a wet stairwell.** *What do I get?* — Workers' Compensation and Injury Management Act Parts 2-3: income, medical, a lump sum for fourteen per cent. *And fifteen?* — Part 7: the door to a common-law claim, one point away, which a specialist decides.

## Chapter 9 — The stroke and the pension

**Beat 19 · 28 April 2063 · a rehabilitation ward.** *Do I qualify?* — Social Security Act s 94: twenty points under the Impairment Tables. He is rated at twenty, exactly; the rule is exact and the rating is a judgement.

**Beat 20 · 2 May 2079 · a small apartment.** *No will.* — Administration Act s 14: Ruby takes all; Public Trustee Act s 10: a small estate, no grant; the super to her, taxed; no duty.

## Notes for the screenplay

- The "nothing happens" beats (6, 8, 11, 12) are the point of this life, and they should be played as relief, not anticlimax.
- Two stops for a human: the creditors' vote (14) and the specialist's percentage (18, 19). One for an absence of law: the family exclusion order (17).
- Figures on screen: $89, $20, $4,100, $690, fourteen and fifteen, twenty points.
