# Cradle to grave, abridged — Daniel in twenty beats

The same life as [`SCENARIO.md`](SCENARIO.md), two beats a chapter, with his age and what the law says back. Every Act he meets is deposited and, on the day of writing, unencoded; the beats are flagged `"abridged": true` in [`facts.json`](facts.json).

Haziq asked what he owed. Daniel asks the same, and three times the answer comes from somewhere the library cannot reach: a Constitution that has no subject directory, a foreign civil code, and the common law on what counts as income.

## Chapter 0 — Born

**Beat 1 · 9 April 1996 · KEMH.** *Australian?* — s 12, yes. *Italian?* — by descent, under a law that is not in the library. Two citizenships on day one; one of them will cost him something in 2040.

**Beat 2 · May 1996 · a Medicare card.** *Anything else?* — Family Tax Benefit for his parents, replayed under an Act that did not then exist.

## Chapter 1 — School

**Beat 3 · November 2008 · a letter.** *Who decides the program?* — a policy, not a law.

**Beat 4 · 28 November 2013 · an ATAR.** Nothing legal happens.

## Chapter 2 — Commerce and the JD

**Beat 5 · 24 February 2014 · a lecture theatre.** *Is there help?* — HECS-HELP, yes; Youth Allowance, no, on his parents' income.

**Beat 6 · 27 February 2017 · the JD.** *Is a postgraduate law degree supported?* — Higher Education Support Act Part 3-3: FEE-HELP.

## Chapter 3 — The Bar, a celebrant, London

**Beat 7 · 24 August 2020 · the Supreme Court.** *What can I do now?* — the Uniform Law s 10: engage in legal practice; ss 43-49: with a certificate renewed each year.

**Beat 8 · 14 June 2027 · Australia House.** *Is she Australian?* — Australian Citizenship Act s 16: by descent, on application, because her father was a citizen when she was born.

## Chapter 4 — Founding Jarrah

**Beat 9 · 15 March 2031 · ASIC's portal.** *What does the company need?* — Corporations Act s 117; ss 180-184 for what he owes it; s 708 for the investor without a prospectus; a director ID.

**Beat 10 · 1 June 2031 · three desks.** *What do I owe them, and what doesn't apply yet?* — Fair Work, super at twelve, workers' comp, the WHS duty; not payroll tax under the threshold; not the Privacy Act under three million.

## Chapter 5 — A round, a breach, a house

**Beat 11 · 3 April 2036 · 2 a.m.** *Do I tell anyone?* — Privacy Act Part IIIC: now that the company is big enough, yes; thirty days to assess, then the Commissioner and the users.

**Beat 12 · 20 May 2038 · Nedlands.** *What does it cost?* — Duties Act: about $207,378; no surcharge, both citizens; no tax on the apartment they sold, their home.

## Chapter 6 — The candidate and the acquirer

**Beat 13 · 15 March 2040 · the Italian consulate.** *Can I stand?* — Constitution s 44(i): not while Italian. The voice has no subject directory for the Constitution and says so; it quotes Re Canavan and stops. He renounces.

**Beat 14 · 15 June 2043 · a signing room.** *Duty on the shares?* — Duties Act: none, since 2013, unless the company is land-rich. *The ACCC?* — Part IVA: below the thresholds.

## Chapter 7 — Exit, foundation, his father

**Beat 15 · 30 June 2050 · a completion dinner.** *Is my gain taxed?* — Division 115: half of it. *The small business concessions?* — Division 152: out of reach above the net asset test.

**Beat 16 · 3 November 2052 · two houses.** *Who gets the house in Calabria?* — not the will. Italian forced heirship: a quarter to his mother, half to the children, a quarter free. The voice reads the Australian Act and names the foreign code it does not hold.

## Chapter 8 — Investor, elder

**Beat 17 · 5 October 2053 · a stairwell he has never used.** *Is it ours?* — the contractor's, as employer; and Work Health and Safety Act s 20 for the tenant with management or control.

**Beat 18 · 9 April 2065 · a study.** *Can I leave a third to the Foundation?* — yes; the Family Provision Act is the only limit, and only if someone invokes it.

## Chapter 9 — The end

**Beat 19 · 21 October 2078 · a hospital room.** *Does the Register settle it?* — Human Tissue and Transplant Act s 22: his consent, and the family confirms.

**Beat 20 · 15 November 2078 · a kitchen table.** *In what order?* — the house to Priya by survivorship; the super to her tax-free by nomination; the residue into the trust; the gift to the Foundation; no duty of any kind.
