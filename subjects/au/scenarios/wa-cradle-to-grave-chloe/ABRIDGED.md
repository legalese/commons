# Cradle to grave, abridged — Chloe in twenty beats

The same life as [`SCENARIO.md`](SCENARIO.md), cut to two beats a chapter. Each is a scene, with Chloe's age and what the law says back. On the day of writing every Commonwealth and Western Australian Act she meets is deposited and none is encoded, so every answer below is a statement of what the row will say when it exists; the beats are flagged `"abridged": true` in [`facts.json`](facts.json).

## Chapter 0 — Born

**Beat 1 · 14 March 2003 · KEMH.** *Is she Australian?* — Australian Citizenship Act s 12: yes, a parent is a citizen.

**Beat 2 · 2 April 2003 · the Registry.** *By when must we register her?* — Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act s 13: within sixty days.

## Chapter 1 — Primary school

**Beat 3 · 1 February 2010 · a school gate.** *Does she have to start this year?* — School Education Act s 9: yes, she is six and a half by the June before.

**Beat 4 · June 2011 · a passport office.** *Can an eight-year-old have a passport?* — Australian Passports Act s 11: yes, with the consent of each person with parental responsibility.

## Chapter 2 — Growing up

**Beat 5 · 14 March 2018 · a supermarket.** *Do they have to pay me super?* — Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act s 28: not under eighteen unless you work more than thirty hours a week.

**Beat 6 · 14 March 2021 · a kitchen.** *What switches on today?* — enrolment and compulsory voting; marriage; a will at eighteen under the Wills Act s 7; jury service; an enduring power of attorney; the organ donor register, which is a choice, not a presumption.

## Chapter 3 — University

**Beat 7 · 22 February 2021 · UWA.** *What am I actually paying?* — Higher Education Support Act: nothing now; a HECS-HELP debt repaid through tax once income passes the threshold.

**Beat 8 · 20 November 2023 · an internship desk.** *Am I an employee?* — Fair Work Act ss 12-13: not if it is a vocational placement; if it is work, yes, and super follows.

## Chapter 4 — First job

**Beat 9 · 29 February 2024 · an MRT-less commute, a bus.** *Is 11% right?* — s 19: yes, and it becomes 11.5% on 1 July and 12% the July after.

**Beat 10 · 20 October 2024 · a laptop.** *Do I owe tax?* — Income Tax Assessment Act and the Rates Act: nothing on $27,500; the return is still due.

## Chapter 5 — A house and a marriage

**Beat 11 · 1 September 2027 · a rented unit.** *Where does the bond go?* — Residential Tenancies Act s 29: to the Bond Administrator.

**Beat 12 · 20 February 2028 · a display village.** *Do we get the grant?* — First Home Owner Grant Act: $10,000 for a new home; Duties Act: no duty at the first home owner rate under the threshold.

## Chapter 6 — First child

**Beat 13 · 22 August 2030 · a kitchen calendar.** *How many weeks, and who pays?* — Paid Parental Leave Act: twenty-four weeks from the Commonwealth, through the employer; Fair Work Act s 70: twelve months unpaid with a job to come back to.

**Beat 14 · 1 October 2030 · a letter from Services Australia.** *What do we get for him?* — Family Assistance Act: the Newborn Upfront Payment, the Newborn Supplement, Family Tax Benefit on the family income.

## Chapter 7 — The long middle

**Beat 15 · 8 March 2035 · a shelter.** *Do we have to register the dog?* — Dog Act ss 15-16: with the shire, and a lower fee because she is sterilised.

**Beat 16 · 14 July 2036 · a summons in the mail.** *Can my boss say no?* — Juries Act ss 58B-58C: no; Fair Work Act s 111: and he pays make-up for ten days.

## Chapter 8 — Mid-life

**Beat 17 · 28 April 2040 · a townhouse in Baldivis.** *What is the duty?* — Duties Act: about $55,185 at the general rate; no surcharge for citizens; Community Titles Act: the corporation's contributions.

**Beat 18 · 27 January 2060 · the Armadale house.** *Mum had no will.* — Administration Act s 14: the children equally.

## Chapter 9 — Retirement and old age

**Beat 19 · 14 March 2070 · a kitchen in Baldivis.** *Do I get the pension?* — Social Security Act ss 43, 1064: no, on assets; the Seniors Health Card, yes.

**Beat 20 · 2 November 2088 · Mandurah.** *What do we do this week?* — the funeral director registers the death; the will; probate under the Administration Act; the super to the children taxed under Div 302; no duty on anything.

## Notes for the screenplay

- Two legislatures on screen: a lower third names the state and the Commonwealth, and the audience will notice that the same morning engages both.
- The stops are not for missing law in this life, except that on the day of writing every row is missing. When the rows land, the honest stops are the regulations: the by-laws for the duty-free concession, the award rates, the Impairment Tables.
- Figures on screen: $641.67, $10,000, 24 weeks, $55,185, and nothing at the end.
