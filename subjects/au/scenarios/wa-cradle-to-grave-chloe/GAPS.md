# Gap register for Chloe's scenario

On 13 October 2026 the Commonwealth and Western Australian statute books are deposited in Commons and encoded nowhere. This register is therefore not a list of holes in a library but the order in which to build one, taken from how often and how early a sunny-day life asks each Act. The entries a simulation would emit are in the two ledgers, [`../../requirements.jsonl`](../../requirements.jsonl) for Commonwealth law and [`../../wa/requirements.jsonl`](../../wa/requirements.jsonl) for the state's, one `no-row` entry per Act with a count for every event that touches it.

- Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Regulations: conditions of release; reg 6.17A (binding nominations).

## E. Seams

1. **Two layers.** Twenty-two events engage a Commonwealth Act and a state Act together. The simulation's routing has to carry the layer, and the ledgers are split by it.
2. **With Jake's file.** The parents' deaths are hers: the will to Sue in 2052, the intestacy in 2060 with equal shares. Her 2046 search for a family exclusion order is in his file only; a joint replay should put it in both.
3. **With Daniel's file.** Her job change in 2034 is to his company; her share scheme, her parental leave paid through the company, and the 2050 sale are the same events seen from the employer's side.
4. **Replay under current law.** The 2003 birth is registered under an Act that was then in force in substance but not in detail; the 2006 family payments are the current Act's. The Singapore scenarios have the same anachronism and say so.
5. **Nothing is encoded.** Every status in this life is `NONE`, `REGS`, `ADMIN-POLICY` or `CASE`. The first row that lands changes the column; run `check-status.py` before any demo.
