# Social Security Act 1991 — formulas lost from the text deposit

The text deposit `C2004A04121.txt` (register id C2026C00336, six volumes in one file) was converted from Word and
dropped every formula set as an equation image. This file transcribes the formulas the baseline encoding needs
from the authorised Word rendering served by the Federal Register of Legislation API, fetched 2026-10-21:

- VOL04: volumenumber=4 (C2026C00449VOL04.docx, sha256 61d75005d7539f9066a97338a5d9ede31265c5bea8380941ff155f1ab6430b76)

**Compilation.** The Register no longer serves C2026C00336 as "Latest"; the downloads are the later compilation
C2026C00449. For each provision below, the 8 paragraphs before and after the formula, converted from the
download with the same converter as the deposit, were compared with the deposit and found identical word for
word (for 1064-G4 this covers the whole of Table G-2). No difference was found.

Transcribed by hand from the authorised rendering on 2026-10-21. Notation: × multiply, / divide,
− minus; brackets as printed. Words are the provision's own defined terms.
