# Encoding brief: the Human Rights (Sexual Conduct) Act 1994 (Cth), in L4

## The subject

**Human Rights (Sexual Conduct) Act 1994** (Act No. 179 of 1994), Commonwealth of Australia, compilation prepared on 5 August 2009 (C2009C00395), deposited at `../registers/source-bundle/C2004A04852.txt` (CC BY 4.0).

## Seat

**The adult whose private sexual conduct is in question** (the repo's `CLAUDE.md` rule of 2026-10-18).

## Scope

Top level: *"Is my conduct protected by s 4, and may a law interfere with it?"* The operative s 4 (with s 3, external Territories). Section 4(1): sexual conduct involving only consenting adults acting in private is not to be subject, by or under any law of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory, to any arbitrary interference with privacy within the meaning of Article 17 of the ICCPR. Section 4(2): an adult is 18 or more.

## Inputs

- The dates of birth of everyone involved, and the date of the conduct (adulthood is worked out: 18 on the eighteenth birthday; a 29 February birthday falls on 28 February in a common year).
- Whether everyone involved consented; whether they were acting in private.
- Whether the interference is under a law of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory, and whether it is arbitrary within Article 17 (a judgment for others: the answer is conditional on it).
