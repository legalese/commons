## Decisions taken (and the text that licenses each)

- A period is counted from its first day: item 2 includes the specified day, items 3 and 6 start the day after (s 44(1)).
- A period of N months is counted by s 4 "month" and s 44(1) item 8 from its first day counted; a period of N years by repeating s 4 "year"; a period of weeks is NOTHING.
- Section 44(2) applies to the last day of any period (days, months, years).
- The moment of midnight that ends a specified day is outside the operation of an expiring provision (s 30 reads "until the end of the specified day").
- Public holidays are always an input list; a part-day public holiday does not make a day a non-business day (s 4 "business day", s 46).
- An unmet condition introduced with "if", "unless" or "provided that" gives JUST FALSE; a case outside a grant limited in its own wording gives NOTHING (the rulings on `JUST FALSE` and `NOTHING`).
- The seat asks public holidays as one run of consecutive days, and a time within a day as hours since the day began.
