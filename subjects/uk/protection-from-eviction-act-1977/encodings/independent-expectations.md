# Independent expectations — Protection from Eviction Act 1977

Written from BRIEF.md and the revised text (point in time 1 May 2026) only, before reading any `.l4` module.
"Court required" means: under this Act it would not be lawful to recover possession / enforce the right otherwise than by proceedings in the court.

HA 1988 came into force on 15 January 1989 (the date used for s 3(2C), s 5(1B) and s 8(5)).

## Readings taken where the Act is open

- R1. "Not less than 4 weeks before the date on which it is to take effect": earliest effect day = day of giving + 28 days (brief).
- R2. "Not less than two months before": earliest effect day = corresponding day two months after giving, or the last day of that month if it has none (brief). Same method for any shorter agreed period expressed in months under (1ZA)(a)(i).
- R3. "Period of 6 months beginning with X": last day = the day before the corresponding day six months later; if that month has no corresponding day, the last day of that month (brief). So 31 Dec 2026 -> 30 Jun 2027, but 30 Dec 2026 -> 29 Jun 2027.
- R4. "Period of 28 days beginning with the day after X": last day = X + 28 (brief).
- R5. An agreement under (1ZA)(a)(i) for a period longer than two months is not "agreement under sub-paragraph (i)"; so (ii) (two months) applies. Same for a joint assured tenancy where not all tenants agreed (1ZB).
- R6. s 3(2C) disapplies only "excluded tenancy"; a pre-1989 excluded *licence* stays excluded.
- R7. s 3A(8) (hostels) excludes licences only; a tenancy of a hostel room is not excluded by (8).
- R8. Sch A1 para 1(2): where the person is still engaging in the conduct on the first-evidence day and it continues past that day, the last day for the notice of intent is the end of the 6 months beginning with the last day of the conduct (this is always no earlier than the para 1(1) date). If the conduct stops on the first-evidence day, para 1(1) alone applies.
- R9. Para 5: the final notice can only be given after the end of the period for representations, so the earliest day is representations-end + 1.
- R10. A statutorily protected tenancy is outside s 3(1); the Act (s 3) does not itself require proceedings for it (its own statute does). Expect "s 3 does not apply / not required under s 3", or a REFUSE if the encoding says the Act is silent — not "court required".
- R11. s 1A(1) "premises in England": premises in Wales -> no penalty.
