2026 No. 170
SOCIAL SECURITY
The Social Security (Up-rating) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2026
Made 24th March 2026
Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1
The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) of the Social Security Act 1988, section 70(8) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, sections 28(2), 30(2), 31(2), 32(2), 34(2), 79(1), and 95 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 and all other powers enabling them to do so.
In accordance with section 13(2) of the Social Security Act 1988, they have consulted with the Welsh Ministers.
In accordance with section 97(2) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018, the Scottish Ministers have informed the Scottish Commission on Social Security of their proposals, notified the Scottish Parliament that they have done so and made their proposals publicly available by such means as they consider appropriate.
In accordance with section 96(2) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.
1	Citation, commencement and interpretation
(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Up-rating) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2026.
(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4) these Regulations come into force on 1 April 2026.
(3) Regulation 12(2) and (3) come into force on 5 April 2026.
(4) Regulations 2, 9, 10, 13, 14 and 16(2) come into force on 6 April 2026.
(5) In these Regulations—
“the Adult Disability Payment Regulations 2022” means the Disability Assistance for Working Age People (Scotland) Regulations 2022,
“the Best Start Foods Regulations 2019” means the Welfare Foods (Best Start Foods) (Scotland) Regulations 2019,
“the Best Start Grants Regulations 2018” means the Early Years Assistance (Best Start Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2018,
“the Carer Support Payment Regulations 2023” means the Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2023,
“the Carer’s Allowance Regulations 1976” means the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976,
“the Child Disability Payment Regulations 2021” means the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021,
“the Child Winter Heating Payment Regulations 2020” means the Winter Heating Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2020,
“the Funeral Support Payment Regulations 2019” means the Funeral Expense Assistance (Scotland) Regulations 2019,
“the Pension Age Disability Payment Regulations 2024” means the Disability Assistance for Older People (Scotland) Regulations 2024,
“the Pension Age Winter Heating Payment Regulations 2024” means the Winter Heating Assistance (Pension Age) (Scotland) Regulations 2024,
“the Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance Consequential Amendment Regulations 2024” means the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) (Consequential Amendment, Revocation and Saving Provision) Regulations 2024,
“the Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance Regulations 2025” means the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025,
“the Winter Heating Payment Regulations 2023” means the Winter Heating Assistance (Low Income) (Scotland) Regulations 2023,
“the Young Carer Grants Regulations 2019” means the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

18	Saving and transitional provisions
The saving and transitional provisions in the schedule have effect.

(1) This paragraph applies where an individual’s application for assistance was made on or after 1 April 2025 and before 1 April 2026.
