## What this deposit is

`registers/source-bundle/aep-Edw1cc1929-25-9-extract.md` is an extract written by another Claude Code session which read the XML in Meng's browser on 2026-10-29. By its own provenance paragraph it is a transcription of the rendered XML and not a byte-exact download; it leaves out the XML's metadata link block and three image references. It is deposited byte for byte, with that session's first-person notes in it. The text under its heading "Text with no repeal annotation (verbatim from `<Body>`)" is the source text. Its status table and its variant-readings table are that session's extraction from the XML's commentaries and are marked as such in the file.

## Four things this deposit did not check

1. **A notice specific to this document.** Only the site-wide footer was read. The `<Metadata>` block of the data.xml has no licence field (the publisher is given as "Statute Law Database"). Nobody looked for a notice on this document that would say otherwise.
2. **The site's re-use page.** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/re-use returned HTTP 504 when the extract's author tried it on 2026-10-29, so it has not been read.
3. **The text of the Open Government Licence v3.0 itself.** Neither session read it for this deposit. For the attribution wording only: the sibling deposit `uk/interpretation-act-1978` records, in its `SOURCE-LICENSE.md`, that the licence page was read on 2026-10-21 and that, where the information provider gives no specific attribution statement, the statement to use is "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0." That is a borrowed claim, not re-read here.
4. **Whether the site's editorial layer is "otherwise stated".** The page presents a "traditional translation" with obsolete characters modernised, an image of the original, and commentaries (the repeal annotations and the variant readings from *Statutes of the Realm*). That layer is the site's own work. The footer says the Open Government Licence applies to all content unless otherwise stated, and whether anything on this page is "otherwise stated" was not checked beyond the footer.
