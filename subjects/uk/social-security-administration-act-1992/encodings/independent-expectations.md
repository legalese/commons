# Independent expectations: SSAA 1992 ss 71, 111A, 112, 115A

Written from BRIEF.md and the revised text (point in time 8 July 2026) only, before reading any `.l4` file.

## Readings adopted

- R1. s 71(1) "whether fraudulently or otherwise": fraud is irrelevant to recoverability.
- R2. s 71(1) "material fact": a misrepresentation of an immaterial fact does not ground recovery (if the encoding takes materiality as an input).
- R3. s 71(5A): recovery requires the determination to have been reversed or varied on appeal, revised (s 9 SSA 1998) or superseded (s 10). "Except where regulations otherwise provide" is outside scope; with none of the three, the amount is not recoverable.
- R4. s 71(1) amount: "the amount of any payment which he would not have made ... but for the misrepresentation or failure" = the overpaid amount given as input, in full, when recoverable; 0 / not recoverable otherwise.
- R5. s 71(3): recoverable from the person who misrepresented or failed to disclose (who need not be the payee).
- R6. s 111A(4): in Scotland, "knowingly" replaces "dishonestly" in (1)-(1E). So in Scotland a knowing but not dishonest false statement or failure is a s 111A offence; a dishonest-but-not-knowing one is not.
- R7. s 112 needs knowledge of falsity (or of the change affecting entitlement) but no dishonesty. A dishonest and knowing false statement is within both sections (they overlap; prosecutor elects).
- R8. A notification "prompt" (as soon as reasonably practicable, s 111A(1G)/112(1F)) means no failure-to-notify offence. An excluded change, or ignorance that the change affects entitlement, means no offence.
- R9. Maximum imprisonment: s 111A(3) six months summary, seven years (84 months) on indictment; s 112(2) three months, summary only (no indictment route).
- R10. s 115A(1): notice may be given where an overpayment is recoverable under s 71 (etc.), attributable to the person's act or omission, and there are grounds for proceedings. s 115A(1A): no overpayment made, grounds for proceedings, and an overpayment attributable to the act/omission would have been recoverable.
- R11. s 115A(3): penalty = max(350, min(5000, floor(50% of overpayment to the penny))). (3A): £350 flat.
- R12. s 115A(5): last day to withdraw = day of agreement + 13 days.

Note: the task prompt mentions "£10,000"; the Act's maximum is £5,000 (s 115A(3)(b)), reached by an overpayment of £10,000. £700 is likewise the overpayment at which 50% meets the £350 minimum.

## Ambiguities

- A1. s 111A(1) for a statement: in E&W, does "dishonestly makes a false statement" need knowledge of falsity? Not expressly; dishonesty is the element. I test only dishonest+knowing (guilty) and knowing-only (not 111A).
- A2. Scotland, "knowingly makes a false statement": dishonesty without knowledge is not enough (scenario 13).
- A3. s 115A(1) refers to the overpayment being "recoverable"; the encoding may compute that from s 71 or take it as input.
- A4. s 115A(1A) applies where no overpayment was made; if an overpayment was made and also (1) applies, (3) governs.
- A5. The £5,000 maximum is under (3)(b); the brief agrees.
