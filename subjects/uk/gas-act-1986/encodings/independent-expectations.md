Date conventions follow the brief where it states them. Where I read the Act differently I say so, and give the answer I think the Act gives.

Base facts unless varied: supplier is the consumer's relevant supplier; written demand for gas charges; amount not genuinely in dispute; notice of intention given after the power arose.

Convention (brief): payment by demand + 28 is in time; power from demand + 29. Deposit: by request + 7; power from request + 8. Earliest act = notice day + 7.
