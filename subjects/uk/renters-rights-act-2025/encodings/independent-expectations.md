## Ambiguities and readings

1. **s 57(2) and 29 February.** Brief: the window runs from the day after "the corresponding date five years earlier". 29 Feb 2032 has no corresponding date in 2027. Reading taken: the corresponding date is the last day of that month, 28 Feb 2027 (the usual common-law "corresponding date" approach, and what calendar add-years libraries do), so the window starts 1 Mar 2027. An alternative (1 Mar 2027 as the corresponding date, window from 2 Mar) would flip G5.
2. **Same-day previous penalty (G3).** "previous" means imposed earlier; a penalty on the same date is inside the window because the window ends with that date. If the encoding treats only strictly-earlier dates as previous, G3 flips. Weak expectation.
3. **s 4A and pre-commencement tenancies.** The inserted section has no commencement carve-out of its own (transitional provisions are elsewhere and out of scope). No test asserts on pre-commencement for s 4A.
4. **"Initial rent" and the first rent period.** The first rent period is always initial rent whatever its length; a later period counts only if it *ends* on or before day 28.
5. **B25 / substitute rent day for an effective term.** The Act gives a substitute rent day for any rent period by definition, but it only matters when the term is of no effect; tests ask for the substitute rent day directly.
6. **s 56(2) and a non-specific rent stated** (D3): the requirement is two-limbed; a range or "offers over" fails (a).
