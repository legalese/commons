- D2 (a duty): a provision that says a party must do something gets two things when it has a trigger or a period: a DECISION function "is the party obliged" (JUST FALSE when the text's own "where/if/on receipt" trigger is unmet: 2026-11-05 case-by-case site "a duty whose if or where is unmet", one fork line) and, where a period is stated, a pure DATE function "done in time" (JUST TRUE/JUST FALSE by the period: ruling 2026-11-05 (a)). A deontic rule (`PARTY ... MUST ...`) sits beside it for the traces; it is thin and carries no WITHIN where the text states no period ("immediately", "forthwith", "without delay": phrasebook 5.9).
- D3 (lists): a list the text introduces with "means" or "are" is read as closed (JUST FALSE for a case outside it), following the keel's F1; a list under "applies to", "may", "includes" or "in particular" is open (NOTHING). Marginal reading (a) in NOTES.
- D4 (parties): core `Party` lacks the appropriate audit authority, the Lord Advocate, the Attorney General, the senior statutory auditor, the person proposed as auditor, the Financial Reporting Council, a supervisory body and the Independent Supervisor. Deontic rules use the nearest core constructor with a comment (`Competent authority` for the appropriate audit authority and the Independent Supervisor; `Auditor` for the outgoing auditor and for the person proposed; `Director` for each director). Wanted constructors are in NEEDS-CORE.md.
- D5 (three-valued helpers): `coa12-logic.l4` defines `coa12 ...` helpers (not a provision, so the section-label rule cannot apply; the part id is the prefix). Wanted in the core: NEEDS-CORE.md.
- D6 (penalties): a penalty is a core `Penalty` value; a pound figure needs the statutory maximum and level 3 as INPUTS (the Act does not state them).
