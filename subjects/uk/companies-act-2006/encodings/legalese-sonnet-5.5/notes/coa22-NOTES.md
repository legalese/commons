## Where PLAN.md and the brief differ

The brief governs. In the matters this part touches I found no instruction in PLAN.md that contradicts the brief. Three points are worth recording (numbered 0 to 2):

0. The brief says seams are named "as PLAN.md section 8 names it". The copy of PLAN.md I was given has sections 1 to 6 (4.1 and 4.2 under 4) and no section 8. The naming rule for a seam is in its section 4 (`s <answering section> <the question that section answers>`, type `MAYBE BOOLEAN`), and I followed that.
1. PLAN.md section 6 lists the seams of this range from a script's reading of cross-references (s 1002 to s 1148; s 1005 and s 1009 to Part 26; ss 1025, 1028 and 1032 to s 453; ss 1028A and 1032A to ss 549 and 555; s 1030 to s 14; s 1033 to ss 80 and 81; Part 32 to ss 431 to 442 of the 1985 Act). I took as an input only the seams that a sentence of my range needs the answer of: `s 1(1)`, `s 112`, `s 1121`, `s 29`, `sch 4` and `s 66` (section "Seams" below). The rest are plain facts the person states, named in the words of the section that cites them.
2. PLAN.md section 5 says both supplied builds accept `REFUSE`, `#ASSERT REFUSED` and `YMD`. I used none of them (checked: `grep -c 'REFUSE\|YMD' coa22-*.l4` is 0 for every file); `BECAUSE` appears only in the eight `LEST BREACH BY ... BECAUSE` clauses of the regulative rules, which the brief allows.

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (coa22)

A function answers `JUST FALSE` only where the text itself says no to the case in its own words: a "means" definition, a condition the text introduces with "only if", "unless", "until", "where", "when" or "on", or with "if" where it gates a duty or a definition (a grant named for a power and gated by a plain "if" answers NOTHING outside its gate: forks F16 to F26), a flat negative ("does not", "may not", "is not"), or a period the text fixes. It answers `NOTHING` where the text lists without closing the list, says "includes", leaves a term undefined, is silent, points outside the sources, or limits a grant or prohibition in its own wording and the case falls outside the limit. `NOTHING` means not covered by this encoding, never "no": a caller must not turn it into FALSE. Each FALSE branch in the modules carries a `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "..."` comment, and each NOTHING branch a `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)` comment; `work/checkquotes.py` checked that every quoted fragment in them is a verbatim slice of the text (checkquotes: 483 quoted parts (72 verbatim but from lines outside the nearest @ref), 0 problems; the ones counted as verbatim but from lines outside the nearest @ref are cross-subsection quotations, which the script reports and allows).
