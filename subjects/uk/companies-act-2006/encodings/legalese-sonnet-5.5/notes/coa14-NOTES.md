## Seat

The individual who is, or is about to become, a director, a company secretary or a member (shareholder) of a company registered under the Act, or who deals with one, asking on facts they can state what the Act requires or lets them do, what rights they have against the company or its directors, what they must disclose, file, approve or vote on and by when, and what follows (liability, offences, penalties) if it is not done.
(Assumed, not ruled: the Act is addressed to companies, so the individual is whoever stands in a company's management or ownership.)
There is no seat for an official.
Where a section addresses only the registrar or the court, the seat layer asks what it means for the person: whether the Act still stops a reduction or a resolution taking effect (ss 627(5), 644(4)), whether the Act bars the court from confirming a reduction (s 648(2)), whether the registrar is barred from registering an order (s 650(2)), whether a creditor is entitled to object (s 646(1)), whether the court may dispense with a creditor's consent (s 646(4)).

## Marginal readings for a lawyer (coa14)

The brief leaves two readings to a lawyer and asks that every branch resting on one be listed with the other reading stated.
**Reading (a):** a failed "if" counts as the text's no, clause by clause, within the provision that holds the "if" (an inference: "if A then B" does not itself say "if not A then not B"), and a flat negative ("does not", "may not", "is not liable") counts as the text saying no.
Other reading: a failed "if" or a flat negative answers `NOTHING`, and only a "means" definition, a closed list or a stated period answers `JUST FALSE`.
**Reading (b):** a prohibition or grant limited by its own wording does not say no outside its limit, so the case outside the limit answers `NOTHING` (for example a grant to "holders of not less than 15%").
Other reading: the case outside the limit answers `JUST FALSE`.
Both are the status quo of this encoding.

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (coa14)

This is the rule of 2026-10-29 (assumed, not ruled by a lawyer) in the terms of this range.
Every answer is a `MAYBE BOOLEAN`, a `MAYBE NUMBER` or a `MAYBE` of a core type, or (where the question asks for a day or an amount the text always fixes) a plain `DATE` or `NUMBER`.
**`JUST TRUE` is a yes the text gives in its own words**: the company must transfer the sum, the officer commits the offence, the holders may apply to the court, the notice was given in time.
**`JUST FALSE` is a no the text gives in its own words**: a definition that says "means" is not met; a condition the text introduces with "if", "where", "only if" or "unless" fails; a period the text fixes ("within one month after", "within 15 days after") has ended without the act; an "until" has been reached; a flat negative applies ("is not liable", "may not", "does not apply").
In a question titled as a bar ("does the Act bar ...", "does the Act still stop ..."), `JUST TRUE` is the text saying no to the act and `JUST FALSE` is only "the bar does not apply": it is never a grant.
**`NOTHING` is "not covered": the Act, as encoded, does not say on these facts, and it is never a no.**
In this range it means one of: the text is silent (a company that has not issued shares at a premium is not told whether it must transfer a sum); a list is not closed ("in particular", "includes": the kinds of reduction in s 641(4), the transfer of shares in s 616(3)); a term is undefined ("default", "an appropriate spot rate", "prescribed particulars"); the text points outside the sources (Part 26, Part 26A, s 988, s 763, the Insolvency Act 1986); or a grant or prohibition is limited by its own wording and the case falls outside the limit (holders of less than 15% under s 633(2), a company that is not limited by shares under s 641(1)(a), articles that say nothing under s 618(5)).
A director, secretary or member who gets `NOTHING` has not been told "no", and has not been told "yes" either: it means ask someone the text does not name, or read the provision the answer points to.
A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`.
For each seam it takes, the seat passes the person's own yes or no to layer 1 as `JUST`, so a `NOTHING` in a seat answer comes from the text and never from a missing input; the seat takes no input for s 988 or s 1062 (it asks "a person is to acquire all the shares" as a plain yes/no, and has no question for s 649(6)(a)).
Each branch of layer 1 that answers `JUST FALSE` carries `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "<the quoted words>"` and each that answers `NOTHING` carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)`, where `<why>` is one of silent, list not closed, includes, undefined term, points outside the text, limited by its own wording; the counts are in the next section.

## Findings: what the text turns out to say

Each finding was read in the text at the lines cited; none is a lawyer's view.

1. **A conditional or late redenomination lapses after 28 days (s 622(4), (5)(b), (6); lines 8365-8369).** A resolution may specify conditions (4) or a later effective day (5)(b), but (6) makes it lapse if the redenomination "has not taken effect at the end of the period of 28 days beginning on the date on which it is passed". With a resolution of 1 March 2023 the period ends on 28 March 2023 (fork F-622-6); a condition that is not met by then, or an effective day after it, leaves the resolution lapsed.
2. **The 10% cap is measured after the reduction (s 626(4); line 8406).** The reduction "must not exceed 10% of the nominal value of the company's allotted share capital immediately after the reduction". A reduction of 10 leaving 100 is within the cap and a reduction of 11 leaving 100 is not (both tested); the cap is not 10% of the capital before.
3. **Five offence sections of the range carry no daily default fine, and the parallel ones do (lines 8332, 8355, 8401, 8430-8432, 8495, 8502-8526, 8570-8574, 8601-8603, 8633-8635, 8727-8729).** The offences of ss 619(5), 621(5), 625(5), 635(3) and 636(3) to 640(3) are punished on summary conviction only, by a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale and, for continued contravention, a daily default fine of one-tenth of level 3. The offences of ss 627(8), 644(9), 647(2) and 656(5) are punished by a fine on indictment and a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum on summary conviction, with no daily default fine; s 643(5) adds imprisonment (two years on indictment; twelve months in England and Wales and six months in Scotland or Northern Ireland on summary conviction) and also has no daily default fine. So two scales are used in the range, and the daily fine appears only on the level-3 one.
4. **A late delivery under s 644 leaves the resolution valid but not yet effective, and arguably not "supported" (ss 642(1)(b), 644(4), 644(6)(a); lines 8555, 8590, 8595).** s 644(6)(a) says the validity of a resolution is not affected by a failure to deliver the documents within the 15 days; s 644(4) says the resolution does not take effect until they are registered; s 642(1)(b) makes the resolution "supported by a solvency statement" only if it and the statement "are registered in accordance with section 644". The text does not say whether a registration of documents delivered late is "in accordance with section 644"; the encoding takes that as the person's yes or no (the input `the resolution and the solvency statement are registered in accordance with section 644`).
5. **s 652(1)(a) does not list s 627, and the text explains why (lines 8408, 8694).** The list of statements of capital is ss 644, 649, 1028A and 1032A and paragraph 7 of Schedule 4 to the 2015 Act; a statement of capital delivered under s 627 is not named; s 626(6) says "Nothing in Chapter 10 applies to a reduction of capital under this section", and s 652 is in Chapter 10.
6. **When a court-confirmed reduction takes effect depends on the kind of scheme (s 649(3); lines 8656-8664).** On delivery of the order and statement for a Part 26 scheme (or on registration if the court so orders); on delivery for a Part 26A scheme of any company other than an overseas company not required to register under s 1046, on publication in the Gazette for such an overseas company, and in either case on registration if the court so orders; on registration in every other case. (aa)(iii) says "in either case", which the encoding reads as (aa)(i) and (aa)(ii) (fork F-649-3).
7. **Subsection (2) of s 641 bars only the solvency-statement route; subsection (2A) bars both routes (lines 8533-8534).** A reduction that would leave no member holding other than redeemable shares is barred under (1)(a) only; a reduction as part of a takeover scheme is barred under (1)(a) or (b), unless (2B) applies.
