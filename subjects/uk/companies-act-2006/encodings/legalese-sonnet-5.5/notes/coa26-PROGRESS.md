## How the files are made
- Source templates live in `DEPOSIT/../work/tpl/*.l4t` (not shipped); `python3 -I DEPOSIT/../work/build.py [name...]` expands

  Edit the .l4t, never the generated .l4. `work/units.py` lists the 504 coverage units (work/units.json); `work/cov.py` builds the NOTES coverage table.
- Run: `L4=<L4_BIN> L4_MAIN=<L4_MAIN> ./check.sh`; srcrefs: `python3 -I srcrefs.py check --strict <raw> coa26-*.l4`.

## Decisions (license in brackets)
- Decision functions answer MAYBE BOOLEAN; "if"/"only if"/"means"/"where" failing = JUST FALSE with a FALSE-kind comment; scope limits, "may ... with" and silence = NOTHING with a NOTHING-kind comment [brief rule of 2026-10-29 and rulings of 2026-11-04/05].
- s 1209 inert (1); s 1210(1)(d),(g) blank (3); s 1210(4) inert (2).
- s 1213(7)/s 1215(6): the text shows BOTH "one-tenth of the statutory maximum" and "one-tenth of the greater of £5,000 or the amount corresponding to level 4 on the standard scale for summary offences": rendering of an amendment; chose the second (later) words, per-day fine; fork.
- Regulative rules use the core `Action of a party` record and PARTY/MUST/WITHIN/HENCE/LEST (works on main 1.6.110, probed). DEONTIC values cannot be asserted with EQUALS: each duty is also a decision function that is asserted; #TRACE outputs are read by eye and recorded.
- Parser trap measured: `ELSE IF ... THEN (...) ELSE` over several lines fails with "incorrect indentation"; use BRANCH. A multi-line LIST after a function name fails; put the LIST in a WHERE local.
- `(f) WITH` call in #ASSERT must be bracketed: `(f WITH a IS 1) EQUALS x`.
