## Seat

The individual who is, or is about to become, a director, a company secretary or a member (shareholder) of a company registered under the Act, or who deals with one, asking on facts they can state what the Act requires or lets them do, what rights they have against the company or its directors, what they must disclose, file, approve or vote on and by when, and what follows (liability, offences, penalties) if it is not done. (Assumed, not ruled: the Act is addressed to companies, so the individual is whoever stands in a company's management or ownership.) There is no seat for an official: what the registrar and the court may do is encoded in layer 1 and answered in layer 2 only as what it means to this person.

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (coa13)

The rule of 2026-10-29 (assumed, not ruled by a lawyer) as it reads in this part. Every decision function answers `MAYBE BOOLEAN` (a few answer a NUMBER or a DATE, or a constructor of its own type).

- `JUST TRUE`: the Act says yes: the condition is met, the permission is given, the liability, offence or power arises.
- `JUST FALSE` ("a no"): the Act says no in its own words: a "means" or "are to" definition ("“ordinary shares” means shares other than ..."), a condition introduced with "if", "where", "so long as", "unless" or "except" that is not met ("Where a private company has only one class of shares ...", "unless— (a) ... and (b) ..."), a flat negative ("may no longer be converted into stock", "are not in the nature of real estate", "is immaterial", "do not apply", "without prejudice"), a period the text fixes in its own words ("within one month", "not exceeding five years", "within the six months immediately preceding"), a limit of a defence or an exemption ("only if and to the extent that ..."), and a bare "must" not done (the 2026-11-05 ruling; one fork line each). Each such branch carries `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "<the quoted words>"`.
- `NOTHING` ("not covered"): the Act does not decide the case: it limits a grant, an offence or a prohibition in its own wording and the case falls outside the limit (`limited by its own wording`: "a director who knowingly contravenes", "every officer of the company who is in default", "may be excluded by provision contained in the articles of a private company"), says "includes" (`includes`: "include the sale of ordinary shares ... held as treasury shares"), lists without closing (`list not closed`: "This section is subject to"), is silent (`silent`), leaves a term undefined (`undefined term`: "cash" other than foreign currency), or points outside the sources (`points outside the text`: s 1121 "officer in default", s 1072, s 283, Part 26A, the Stock Transfer Act 1963). Each such branch carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)`, <why> being one of silent, list not closed, includes, undefined term, points outside the text, limited by its own wording.
- A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`. The seat questions return the same three answers: "yes", "no", "not covered". `REFUSE` is not used.
- A seam input of type `MAYBE BOOLEAN` is another part's answer: its `JUST FALSE` is that part's no and its `NOTHING` is "not covered" there; `NOTHING` passes through (points outside the text).
- A function that answers a sum answers a NUMBER (the called-up share capital, the equity share capital, the amount of a discount, the amount a liable person pays, the proportion of securities to offer) and one that answers a day answers a DATE.
- A prohibition with an "unless" or "except" is asked as "is prohibited": JUST FALSE once the "unless" is met.
- A function named for a grant or power ("may", "the court may") answers NOTHING, not JUST FALSE, when its "if" gate is not met: JUST FALSE would read as "may not" (fork F-grant-gate). JUST FALSE stays where the text closes the gate itself ("only if", a flat negative, a bar), for a bar, a duty or a function that asks the provision's own question, and, pending a ruling, for a "may" function whose gate is "where", "when" or "upon" and for an "entitled" function (listed in the fork register).
