## Policy (decided once, applied everywhere)
- means/is definitions fail -> JUST FALSE; if/where/when/unless/until/in the event of fail -> JUST FALSE (marginal a);
  grant or prohibition limited by its own wording (not more than N words, named complement) outside -> NOTHING (marginal b);
  silent / undefined / points outside / list not closed / includes -> NOTHING;
  bare "must" not done -> JUST FALSE (fork F5); "subject to the articles" -> input, NOTHING or JUST FALSE as noted (fork F4);
  an "unless"/"until" bar is encoded as the bar (TRUE = bar holds, FALSE = bar lifted); a safe harbour's failed "if" -> NOTHING (fork F10).
- Counting: "within/not more than N days after D" is `date <= D + N` (the Act gives no counting rule outside s 360; fork F7); clear days only where s 360 lists the provision.
- Hours (48h, 24h, working-day rule): inputs are hours required and hours of the period that are not part of a working day.
