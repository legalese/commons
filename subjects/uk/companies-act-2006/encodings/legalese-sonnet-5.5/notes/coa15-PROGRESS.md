## How the files are made (READ THIS FIRST after a compaction)
- Sources of the .l4 files are TEMPLATES in `DEPOSIT/../work/src/*.l4.tpl` (not shipped). `python3 -I DEPOSIT/../work/build.py [name]` expands them into `DEPOSIT/encodings/legalese-sonnet-5.5/*.l4`.
  Macros: `@@QR a b | label` (quote lines of raw lines a..b by srcrefs.py + `@ref label (src:A-B)`), `@@Q a b`, `@@REF a b | label`,
  `@@OFFENCE|@@OFFENCE_OFFICERS|@@OFFENCE_DIRECTORS sec | a b | condition | if-words`, `@@PENALTY3 sec | a b`, `@@PENALTYA sec | a b`.
  A later declaration that cites an earlier quote run uses `@@REF`, but under `--strict` its locator must lie inside the nearest run above it: otherwise use `@@QR` again.
- `DEPOSIT/../work/run.sh file.l4` runs the stable binary (L4_BIN) on one file and prints errors, warnings, assertion counts.
- `DEPOSIT/../work/units.py` parses the text into coverage units (headings, sections, subsections, paragraphs, defined terms).
- NOTES.md is generated by `DEPOSIT/../work/build_notes.py` from `work/units.py` (the text's units), `work/refmap.py` (the declarations whose `@ref` locators reach each unit and their `-- fork F-...` tags), `work/jr-spec.txt` (the jural relations), `work/notes/*.md` (the prose) and `work/check-output.txt` (what `check.sh` printed). Do not hand-edit NOTES.md; the fork register's text is in `build_notes.py`. Other helpers: `work/trace-expect.py` (traces against `-- expect:`), `work/why.py` (expected against actual for failed assertions).
- Check the quotes: `python3 -I srcrefs.py check --strict INPUTS/ukpga-2006-46.txt <files>` (also `--refs`).
