## Seat

An individual who is, or is about to become, a director, a company secretary or a member (shareholder) of a company registered under the Act, or who deals with one, asking, on facts they can state, what the Act requires or lets them do, what rights they have against the company or its directors, what they must disclose, file, approve or vote on and by when, and what follows (liability, offences, penalties) if it is not done. (Assumed, not ruled: the Act is addressed to companies, so the individual is whoever stands in a company's management or ownership.) There is no seat for an official: the registrar and the court appear only as parties the text names and as what their acts mean to the individual. `coa1-seat.l4` asks 103 questions, each named "Under section N, ...", with `@export` and `@desc`, grouped under `§` headings in the text's order (Part 2, Part 3, Part 4; the charity questions and the formation questions are marked in their headings as arising only in that situation). `coa1-cases-seat.l4` has at least one case per question (227 assertions).

### Name-test sweep of 2026-11-12

### Reading a result: FALSE and NOTHING (coa1)

The rule of 2026-10-29 (assumed, not ruled by a lawyer) as it reads in Parts 2 to 4. Almost every function answers `MAYBE BOOLEAN`; a few answer a list, a string, a number or a regulative rule.

- `JUST TRUE`: the Act says yes on these facts: the application states what it must, the registrar shall register, the person dealing with the company is protected.
- `JUST FALSE` ("a no"): the Act says no in its own words. In this subject that means: a requirement the text states with a bare "must" that is not met ("The memorandum must be in the prescribed form"), a condition the text states with "if", "where", "on", "when", "unless" or "until" that fails or is met (F2, F14), a period the text fixes that has run (15 days, 28 days, one year), a flat negative ("shall not be called into question", "does not affect", "is not liable", "need not", "is void" where the text says so), a "means" or "is a ..." definition whose limbs fail (F5). Each such branch carries `-- FALSE-kind (the text says no in its own words): "<the quoted words>"`.
- `NOTHING` ("not covered"): the Act does not decide the case: it lists without closing the list ("include", "The following are ..."), grants or prohibits something limited by its own wording and the case falls outside the limit ("may amend its articles by special resolution", "In favour of a person dealing ... in good faith"), is silent, leaves a term to another part of the Act (s 112 "member", s 1121 "in default", s 283 "special resolution", s 790C "registrable person") or to another Act, or says "includes". Each such branch carries `-- NOTHING-kind (<why>)`. **A caller must never turn `NOTHING` into `FALSE`.**
- A function returning a list returns the empty list where the text's own condition fails (F19, F27); a string or number function is total over the cases the text names and has no `NOTHING` unless it is a `MAYBE`.
- A regulative rule is a `DEONTIC`; a `#TRACE` shows `FULFILLED`, a `BREACH` with its `BECAUSE`, or the obligation still standing (a duty with no time limit is never discharged by waiting). The same duty is also a decision function where the text gives facts to test.

## What the text turns out to say (findings)

1. **Two subsections are numbered (4) in s 12A** (lines 179 and 180-184): the first gives three defined terms "in Part 21A (see sections 790C and 790K)", the second four (adding "permission of a court to act"). Both are rows; see F11.
2. **Omitted words show as " ..." or ". . . ."** at lines 74 (s 9(2)(c)), 94 (s 9(5)(a)), 127 (s 10(2)(c)(iii)) and 370 (s 32(2)(c)(ii)); the blank paragraphs at lines 128 (s 10(2)(d)) and 372 (s 32(2)(d)) are `repealed or blank in this text`. Nothing was supplied for them.

5. **s 27(4) is the only fixed money figure in the range** (GBP 200); all other amounts are "level 3 on the standard scale" (ss 26(4), 30(3), 32(4), 34(6), 35(4), 36(4), 45(5)) and "one-tenth of level 3" for the daily default fine (ss 26(4), 30(3), 34(6), 35(4)).

8. **s 40(2)(b) has three paragraphs that differ in kind**: (i) a flat negative, (ii) a presumption "unless the contrary is proved", (iii) a flat negative with "by reason only of": three functions, three FALSE-kind quotations.

## What the stable binary does not accept, and what it does

Measured on 2026-11-13 with `L4_BIN` (1.6.110) and `L4_MAIN` (1.6.109); the binary governs, so no module uses these.

- **Loud:** `#ASSERT` on a `DEONTIC` value (`x EQUALS FULFILLED`) is an error, `eval/unsupported-equality` ("Trying to check equality on types that do not support it"). So every regulative rule is run with `#TRACE`, its expected outcome is written in a comment next to it, and the outcome was read in the run output (identical on both builds); the same duty is also a decision function with assertions. This is the construct the brief says to report: "a regulative rule cannot be asserted".
- **Loud:** a multi-line prefix application whose second argument starts a new line at the function's own column, `append x` newline `(append ...)`, is a layout error (`unexpected (`); `concat (LIST a, b, c)` with the parts as `WHERE` locals works.
- **Loud:** a positional record built with `OF` needs commas between the arguments (`T OF (Date 1 10 2009), "A"`); `LIST T OF ...` is not a way to write a list of records.
- **Silent:** `NOT a OR b` on one line reads as `NOT (a OR b)` (this cost four wrong answers before the tests caught them); every `NOT` is bracketed. With the `NOT` on its own line and the connective on the next at the same column it reads as `(NOT a) OR b`, but the layout is not relied on.
- **Silent:** a `GIVEN` input named like a top-level function shadows it without a diagnostic (`s 13(2)` once took a parameter named `s 13(1) the document is a statement of compliance`); the inputs that carry an answer of this part's own earlier section are named `the answer under s N that ...`.
- **Accepted and measured:** two modules in one folder that each declare a record field with the same name (`states the prescribed particulars of the rights attached to the shares` in `s 10(2)(c) statement for a class of shares` and in `s 32(2)(c) statement for a class of shares`) compile and test clean when imported together (ss 10 and 32 are in different modules, and the seat imports both); `WAITUNTIL` as a clock advance in `#TRACE` works on both builds; `add years` of the library clamps a 29 February to 28 February; parentheses, commas and apostrophes inside a backtick name are fine; `concat` of locally named lists works.
- **Accepted though the brief says the stable line does not have them** (the keel measured the same): `REFUSE`, `#ASSERT REFUSED` and `YMD`. None of the eight files uses them (`grep -n "REFUSE\|YMD\|TYPICALLY\|BECAUSE \"" coa1-*.l4` finds only `LEST BREACH ... BECAUSE`).
