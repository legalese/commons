GIVETH A MAYBE Period
`tst imprisonment of` MEANS
    CONSIDER p
    WHEN NOTHING THEN NOTHING
    WHEN JUST pen THEN pen's `imprisonment not exceeding`

GIVEN p IS A MAYBE Penalty
GIVETH A MAYBE `Fine limit`
`tst fine of` MEANS
    CONSIDER p
    WHEN NOTHING THEN NOTHING
    WHEN JUST pen THEN pen's `fine not exceeding`

GIVEN p IS A MAYBE Penalty
GIVETH A BOOLEAN
`tst a penalty is stated` MEANS
    CONSIDER p
    WHEN NOTHING THEN FALSE
    WHEN JUST pen THEN TRUE

`tst 2 years` MEANS Period WITH `number of units` IS 2, unit IS Years
`tst 7 years` MEANS Period WITH `number of units` IS 7, unit IS Years
`tst 12 months` MEANS Period WITH `number of units` IS 12, unit IS Months
`tst 6 months` MEANS Period WITH `number of units` IS 6, unit IS Months
`tst fine up to the statutory maximum` MEANS `a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum` WITH `fraction of the statutory maximum` IS 1

GIVEN e IS A STRING
      w IS A STRING
GIVETH A BOOLEAN

    CONSIDER `sch 8 the provisions that define or explain the expression` e
    WHEN NOTHING THEN FALSE
    WHEN JUST xs THEN any (GIVEN s YIELD s EQUALS w) xs
