# Source license

The quoted legal text in this encoding is the English translation of Magna Carta (1215) printed in W. S. McKechnie, *Magna Carta: A Commentary on the Great Charter of King John* (Glasgow: James MacLehose and Sons, 1905).
The terms that text carries, where it came from, and what the deposit does not establish about it are recorded once, in `../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md` of this run, and are not repeated here.
That file's conclusion is the one that applies: no attribution is required by the terms it read, and it says what it does not establish (who made the English, whether the translation is faithful to the Latin, whether the electronic text matches the print).
This encoding quotes the translation verbatim in its `-- src:` lines, so the question the stub raises is a real one here; this file does not settle it and does not add a grant.
The licence of the encoding itself is Apache-2.0, like the rest of canon.
