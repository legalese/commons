## Ambiguities and readings

1. s 21(1) "knowing that the employee is disqualified": knowledge implies the fact, so no offence under (1) if the employee is not in fact disqualified (O4).
2. s 17(4A): a cancellation (16(4)(a)) is not listed, so no appeal lies (A2). No objection -> no appeal (A1).
3. s 15(2) does not depend on whether s 15(3) is established (s 15(5)); the excuse is a separate answer.
4. The general limit is an input; I assume it is given in months.
5. s 15(6)(c) "at least 28 days after": the earliest date is given + 28 (per brief).
6. 29 Feb birthday: adult on 28 Feb in a non-leap year (per brief), tested via a century year 2100 since 16th birthdays of leap-day births otherwise always fall in leap years.
