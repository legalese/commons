## Decisions that are not forks

- Regulation 25(5)'s payment, liability and occupation conditions are plain BOOLEAN inputs to Sch 4 para 6 and Sch 5 para 8 (out of range: regulation 25, uc1). Schedules 1, 2 and 3 are encoded as components; regulation 25's combination is not restated.
- Nothing is rounded: regulation 6 is outside this range and the text does not say at which step it bites (an earlier note in this file said the amounts were rounded once at the end of each element; that was a plan, and no module does it).
- Para 14(3): the element is floored at nil.
- Figures the text states are encoded: 96.55 pounds (Sch 4 para 14(1)); 14% and 25% (para 36(3), (4)); 4 bedrooms (para 26); 9 assessment periods (Sch 5 para 5(2)); 10% (Sch 2 para 6(4)); the ages and periods in the text.
- The local housing allowance is a rent officer's figure under an Order outside this text: a NUMBER input.
- The Secretary of State's judgements (Sch 2 para 10; Sch 4 paras 24(5), 29(4), 31, 32, 35(5); Sch 5 para 6) are BOOLEAN inputs worded as the condition.
- Children for Sch 4 para 10 are counts by kind (under 10 or not, by sex), plus the same four counts for children who satisfy the disabled child condition; the minimum number of bedrooms is found by exhaustive recursion over the counts.
- The per-child rule `Sch 4 para 12(6)(a) and (b)` is not chained into the count: the seat asks for the number of children who satisfy it.
- Paragraphs 15(2)(e) and 16(2)(f) list the same six payments (child disability payment is missing from both).
