# Source license

The regulations and laws quoted by this row are Israeli primary legislation and subsidiary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset's files at fs.knesset.gov.il) and Kovetz HaTakanot (the issues of Reshumot). Israel publishes no free official consolidated text of these regulations: the official version of a regulation is its publication in Reshumot with every amendment.
- **Working texts**: the Hebrew Wikisource consolidations of seven sets of regulations under the National Insurance Law, deposited in `registers/source-bundle/aids/` with revision ids and sha256 values (NOTES.md section 2). They are consolidated working texts and are **not official versions**. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Kovetz HaTakanot files**: fetched from olaw.org.il, an archive of Reshumot issues that is not the Knesset's own site; the record is in `registers/source-bundle/official/SOURCES.json`.
