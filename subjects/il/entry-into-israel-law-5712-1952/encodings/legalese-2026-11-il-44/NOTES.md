## 2. Scope

The row records the seven cross-references named in section 5, as typed pairs, and keeps one defect as two expected-red assertions.
The seat is the individual to whom section 12A1 applies: a foreign resident (the Law's "תושב זר") charged with an offence under section 12(א)(1) whose vehicle was seized.
This row asks no question from that seat, because it records references; the person it would serve is that individual, who would want to know which seizure power the section means and whether that power exists.
The row draws no conclusion about what a court would do with the defect.
The row also records two citations by address whose number now names something else or nothing (S2, S3), one sound citation of the same kind as a control (C8), and one waiting period that two texts give differently (P1 against P3).
A further citation, the 1985 enacting formula that numbers the power as "13(d)", is recorded in fork F9 as a citation that is not a defect; it is not typed and has no assertion.
It keeps those three that do not hold (S2, S3, and P1 against P3) as three more expected-red assertions, five in all.
For the added items the individual is a person on whom an order of removal has been given; the row asks no question from that seat.
Section 13(e) is added by a temporary provision, Amendment 44 (SH 3506 p. 338), and applies to the class of persons in section 13(e) as read by that provision.
This row does not describe that class, and its quoted line marks the omitted words with [...].
What the row records about section 13(e) is its number, its text apart from the class, and its period.
The row states only what each text says and takes no view on any person or on any purpose of any provision.

## 6. Fork register
