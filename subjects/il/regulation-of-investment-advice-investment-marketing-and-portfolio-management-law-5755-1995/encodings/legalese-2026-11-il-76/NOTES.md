## 1. What this row is

The row records one item, I1: an instruction of 2024 and the clause it names, as typed values, and tests them.
The instruction is printed in SH 3232 (issue date 3.7.2024), printed page 995, as item (6) of section 18.
It is one line: `(6) בסעיף 39(ב)(2א), אחרי ״סעיף״ יבוא ״3(א4)(3), (ד) או״;`.
The page prints the path 39(ב)(2א), the anchor word סעיף and the words `3(א4)(3), (ד) או`, with the words אחרי and יבוא around them.
The clause is paragraph (2א) of subsection (ב) of section 39, as SH 2274 (issue date 27.1.2011) printed it on printed page 245, in item (11)(ב) of section 2.
It reads `(2א) מסר מידע כוזב או מטעה בבקשה לקבלת רישיון לפי סעיף 5, או בדיווח לרשות לפי סעיף 27.` and ends with a period inside the closing mark.
The page prints the word סעיף twice in the clause: once before the numeral 5 and once before the numeral 27.
The row types the instruction, the clause as one string and as a list of 18 words (the word סעיף is word 11 and word 17), and the count of the anchor word in the clause, which is 2.
The red assertion says that the anchor word occurs exactly once in the clause as printed; it fails, because the count is two.
Between the two instruments, SH 2420 (issue date 11.12.2013), printed page 133, item (3) of section 38, prints a line that names paragraph (2א) of subsection (ב) of section 39 as a place after which something comes.
The row types that line and no word of the paragraph it prints under it.
The row records what the pages print and counts a word; it records no reading of the instruction.
The item has two limbs.
Limb (a): the target is repealed, missing or says something else, read from an official instrument.
Limb (b): the citing words still read so in the latest official instrument read.
Subsection letters are written in Latin letters in identifiers: (א) is Alef and (ב) is Bet.
Issue dates are the dates the footers print, typed as "issue date", and not as commencement dates.
English titles of instruments are working translations.

## 2. Seat

The seat is the individual who reads the printed instruments to see which words the pages print.
The row asks that individual no question, because it records references: there is no input to ask for and no `@export`.

Quotation lines in the modules are of two kinds.
A `src:N` line is expanded by `tools/srcquote.py` from the excerpt; `python3 -I tools/srcquote.py --check <excerpt> *.l4` exits 0.
An `ext:[shNNN-pPPP]` line quotes the Hebrew read from the deposited image of issue NNN, printed page PPP, and `ext:[shNNN-cover]` quotes its cover; a line that says "joined" gives the printed lines of the line above it joined with one space.
`python3 -I tools/hebcheck.py <excerpt> *.l4 NOTES.md` exits 0: every run of Hebrew outside a quotation line is in the excerpt or on an `ext:` line of the row.
The tool checks that a typed string equals a stretch of a quotation line; it does not check the quotation line against the page.
The quotation lines were compared with the deposited crops by eye.
The Hebrew words typed from each band were also compared with the text layer of the PDF page, as sets of words: order and digits are not compared, and a layer that repeats or reverses words was not used to decide anything.
The result, band by band: every typed word is in the layer for SH 3232 p.996 (section 20(א) and its margin note), SH 2274 p.245 (the line that opens item (11) and item (11)(ב)), the SH 2274 cover band, the SH 2274 footer, SH 2420 p.133 (item (3)) and SH 2420 p.130 (the heading).
For SH 3232 p.995 every typed word is in the layer except the two words that carry a printed quotation mark.
For the heading bands of SH 3232 p.993 and SH 2274 p.239, and the cover band of SH 3232, every typed word is in the layer except the word מס׳, whose geresh the layer stores as another character.
The SH 2420 footer line is in the layer except the day letter ח׳ with its geresh.
The footer line of SH 3232 is stored in part reversed in the layer, so it was compared with the image by eye only.
Where the image and a text layer disagree, the image decides; the one such place found is the dash of section 20(א), which the layer stores as a hyphen and the quotation line types as the image shows it, an en dash.
Where a quotation mark is printed around words, the `ext:` line types the Hebrew gershayim; a footnote reference number printed beside a heading is left out of the quotation line.
The numerals and letters of a label or path are typed in reading order, numeral first (fork F4).
The footers' Hebrew and Gregorian dates were run through the Hebrew calendar converter after its control (30 Kislev 5785 is 2024-12-31): SH 3232, 27 Sivan 5784, is 3.7.2024; SH 2274, 22 Shevat 5771, is 27.1.2011; SH 2420, 8 Tevet 5774, is 11.12.2013; 3 pairs, 0 mismatches.
