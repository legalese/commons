# Source license

The text of the **Families of Soldiers Killed in Action (Benefits and Rehabilitation) Law, 5710-1950** (חוק משפחות חיילים שנספו במערכה (תגמולים ושיקום), תש״י–1950) and of the instruments that amend it is Israeli primary legislation or regulation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: not claimed by this subject. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text: the official version of a law is its original publication in Reshumot together with every amending Act. This subject reads amending instruments only (Sefer HaChukim 106, 1559, 2216, 3072 and 3493, and Kovetz HaTakanot 10331); it does not read the original publication of the Law.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A4%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%94_(%D7%AA%D7%92%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9D), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text from "The Open Book of Laws" (ספר החוקים הפתוח) on Hebrew Wikisource. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date: a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English title of the Law in this subject is a working translation of the Hebrew title, not an enacted title.
