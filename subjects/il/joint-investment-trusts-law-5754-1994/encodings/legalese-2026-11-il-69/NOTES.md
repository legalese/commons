## 1. What this row is

The row records two items of the Law as typed values and tests them.
Item I1 is paragraph (4) of subsection (a) of section 124, which as first enacted (SH 1480, 23.8.1994, printed page 342) ends with a citation of subsection (b) of section 23.
An instruction of 2011 (SH 2274, 27.1.2011, printed page 261, item (20)(a)(2)) replaces the quoted figure of that citation with other words, and item (8) of the same law (printed page 256) repeals subsection (b) of section 23.
The word that precedes the figure in the paragraph is outside the instruction's quotation marks, so the instruction as written leaves it standing in front of the words it puts in place.
Item I2 is section 113d(a)(1) as SH 2463 (30.7.2014, printed page 652) prints it: it applies section 41 to a foreign fund.
Section 9 of SH 2243 (15.6.2010, printed page 544) repeals section 41, and section 14 of the same law starts that law on the later of two alternatives: three months from its publication, or the entry into force of regulations made under the sections it names.
Beside each item the row keeps one sound reference: for I1, the citation of subsection (a) of section 23 that the replacement words carry, and for I2, the citation of section 42 in the next paragraph of the same subsection.
Each item has two limbs: limb (a), the target is repealed, missing or says something else, read from an official instrument; limb (b), the citing words still read so in the latest official instrument read.
Limb (b) is read from the items printed after the citing words in three later issues (SH 2654, SH 3232 and SH 3579), all deposited; for I2 the row also records one item printed in the issue that prints the citing words (SH 2463, item 12).
The row records what the pages print and what the consolidation prints.
It does not say which reading of paragraph (4) is the right one, what a court would do, how any provision should read, or anything about anyone's liability.
Section letters are written in Latin letters for the Hebrew letters the Law uses (113d is 113 dalet; subsection (a) is alef, (b) bet).

## 2. Seat

The seat is the individual who serves as a director or holds a post in the manager of a joint investment trust (to whom the paragraph of I1 is addressed), and the individual who invests in a foreign fund (to whom section 113d is applied).
The row asks that individual no question, because it records references: there is no input to ask for and no `@export`.
