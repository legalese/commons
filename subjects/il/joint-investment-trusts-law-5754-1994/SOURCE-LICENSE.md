# Source license

The text of **Joint Investment Trusts Law, 5754-1994** (חוק השקעות משותפות בנאמנות, התשנ״ד–1994) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Sefer HaChukim issues named in `registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` (1480, 2243, 2274 and 2463), from https://fs.knesset.gov.il/, and one issue of Kovetz HaTakanot (7445), taken from an unofficial archive of the printed issue (olaw.org.il, not a government site). Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text: the
  official version of a law is its original publication in Reshumot together with every amending Act.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA
  (Hebrew Wikisource, The Open Book of Laws), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where
  any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English title above is a working translation, not an enacted title.
