# Source license

The text of the **Economic Competition Law, 5748-1988** (חוק התחרות הכלכלית, התשמ"ח-1988) is Israeli primary legislation, as are the instruments under it that this subject quotes.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, orders or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), and of each regulation, order and notice in Kovetz HaTakanot, together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
  The Knesset address that the consolidation's header gives for the Law's original is https://fs.knesset.gov.il/11/law/11_lsr_210423.pdf; it is not deposited in this subject.
- **Working texts**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), consolidated Hebrew texts. **Not official versions.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted. A reading that rests only on a Wikisource consolidation says CONSOLIDATION-ONLY in `NOTES.md`; a reading from a cropped image of an official instrument says which page.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English titles in this subject are renderings made for the row, not enacted titles.
