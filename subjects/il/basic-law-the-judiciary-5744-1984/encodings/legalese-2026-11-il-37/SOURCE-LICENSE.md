# Source license

The text of **Basic Law: The Judiciary, 5744-1984** (חוק־יסוד: השפיטה, התשמ״ד–1984) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the publication in Reshumot cited as ס״ח תשמ״ד, 78; as amended by Amendment No. 4, ס״ח תשפ״ה, 434; Knesset legislation database lawitemid 2000042; Wikisource page חוק-יסוד: השפיטה. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text: the official version of a law is its original publication in Reshumot together with every amending Act.
- **Official amending instrument deposited**: `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/25_lsr_6165425.pdf`, the Knesset's PDF of the Sefer HaChukim issue.
  Basic Law: The Judiciary (Amendment No. 4), Sefer HaChukim 3388, 1 April 2025, pp. 434-436: s 1 (s 4(b) replaced, s 4(c) and new s 4(d) to (f)), s 2 (new ss 4A, 4B), s 3 (commencement and application).
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%93%3A_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%98%D7%94&action=raw (Hebrew Wikisource, "The Open Book of Laws"), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding.
Record which text was used and its date: a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.
Where the consolidation and the official instrument differ, the instrument is right; the encoding's NOTES.md lists each difference found.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial.
The English titles are descriptive glosses, not enacted titles.
