# Source license

The texts quoted by this row are Israeli primary legislation: the **Minimum Wage Law, 5747-1987** (חוק שכר מינימום, התשמ"ז-1987), the **Minimum Wage (Working Youth and Apprentices) Regulations, 5748-1987**, the National Insurance Law [Consolidated Version], 5755-1995 and 5728-1968, and the Interpretation Law, 5741-1981.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), and of each regulation in Kovetz HaTakanot, together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working texts**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), consolidated Hebrew texts. **Not official versions.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this row

Statute text may be deposited and quoted. A reading that rests only on a Wikisource consolidation says CONSOLIDATION-ONLY in `NOTES.md`; a reading from a page image of an official instrument says which page.

## Status of the deposits

The Law's consolidation was deposited on 2026-10-08 (page revision 3079167, last edited 2026-08-26T07:30:08Z) and is not altered by this row.
The consolidation of the Youth and Apprentices Regulations was deposited on 2026-11-10 (page revision 2928721, last edited 2025-05-13T16:46:01Z), as the raw MediaWiki source of the Wikisource page.
The National Insurance Law and Interpretation Law consolidations are read in place from their own subjects and are not copied.
The official pages are in `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/` (Sefer HaChukim 1211 and 1621, from the Knesset's server) and `registers/source-bundle/official/` (Kovetz HaTakanot 5074 and 5942, from `olaw.org.il`, a website that hosts scans of Reshumot issues and is not a government site), each with 100 dpi page images of the pages read, rendered for this row. Only the pages read are deposited as PDFs (page extracts of the issues), as `NOTES.md` section 2 lists.
`registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` gives the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of each file.
The order on the shortening of the work week, signed by the Minister of Labour, Welfare and Social Services on 15 March 2018, is quoted from the Ministry of Labour's hosted copy on gov.il (`registers/source-bundle/official/extension-order-short-week-2018-ministry-copy.pdf`, with page images beside it), not from the Yalkut HaPirsumim; it is an official order, treated here like the other official instruments above, and a hosted copy is not the gazette publication.
