# Source license

The text of **Planning and Building Law, 5725-1965** (חוק התכנון והבניה, התשכ"ה-1965) is Israeli primary legislation, and the regulations this subject cites are Israeli secondary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. The sibling subjects of this repository state that under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions; I copied that statement from them and did not re-check it here.
- **Official version**: the original publication in Reshumot (Sefer HaChukim for the Law and its amending Acts, Kovetz HaTakanot for regulations), together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text. The official Sefer HaChukim pages this subject deposits come from the Knesset's file server, `https://fs.knesset.gov.il/`; the Kovetz HaTakanot pages are copies held by `https://olaw.org.il/`, an archive that is not the Knesset's.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה
  ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text, and three Wikisource pages of regulations named in `registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json`. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date: a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English title above is the form used for the Law in English-language material; it is not an enacted title.

## Status of this deposit

This subject deposits EXCERPTS of the Hebrew working text of the Law and of two regulations, and one short regulation page whole: `registers/source-bundle/planning-and-building-law-5725-1965.he.wiki.excerpt.txt` holds thirteen blocks of lines copied unaltered from the raw MediaWiki source of the Wikisource page, and its first lines give the whole page's size and sha256 and the line numbers copied; the other two excerpts say the same of their pages.
It is an unofficial consolidation as maintained at retrieval (the Law's page revision 3099048, last edited 2026-10-29T14:00:44Z), not a historical vintage.
The official pages are in `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/` and `registers/source-bundle/official/`: whole issues of Sefer HaChukim (2450 and 2635) and of Kovetz HaTakanot (7682, 6729 and 6922), and a page extract of Sefer HaChukim 2165; `registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` gives the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of every file.
No part of the subject other than what a row cites is deposited.
