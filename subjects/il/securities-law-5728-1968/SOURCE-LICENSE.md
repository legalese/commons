# Source license

The text of **Securities Law, 5728-1968** (חוק ניירות ערך, התשכ"ח-1968) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. The sibling subjects of this repository state that under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions; I copied that statement from them and did not re-check it here.
- **Official version**: the original publication in Reshumot (Sefer HaChukim), together with every amending Act. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text. The official pages this subject deposits come from the Knesset's file server, `https://fs.knesset.gov.il/`.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_ניירות_ערך
  ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date: a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English title above is the form used for the Law in English-language material; it is not an enacted title.

## Status of this deposit

This subject deposits an EXCERPT of the Hebrew working text, not the whole page: `registers/source-bundle/securities-law-5728-1968.he.wiki.excerpt.txt` holds ten blocks of lines copied unaltered from the raw MediaWiki source of the Wikisource page, and its first lines give the whole page's size and sha256 and the line numbers copied.
It is an unofficial consolidation as maintained at retrieval (page revision 3088555, last edited 2026-10-13T11:30:12Z), not a historical vintage.
The official pages are in `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/`: one whole issue of Sefer HaChukim (2380) and page extracts from two others (2274 and 2253); `registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` gives the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of every file.
No part of the subject other than what a row cites is deposited.
