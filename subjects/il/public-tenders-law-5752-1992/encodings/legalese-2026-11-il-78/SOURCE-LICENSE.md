# Source license

The text of **Public Tenders Law, 5752-1992** (חוק חובת המכרזים, התשנ״ב–1992) and of the regulations and order made under it is Israeli primary legislation and delegated legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Sefer HaChukim issues named in `SOURCES.json` (2495, 2827 and 3443), from https://fs.knesset.gov.il/. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text: the
  official version of a law is its original publication in Reshumot together with every amending Act.
- **Regulations and orders**: the Kovetz HaTakanot issues named in `SOURCES.json` (5653, 6586, 7027, 7543, 10718 and 10771) are read from an archive of the printed issues at https://olaw.org.il/, which is **not an official site**; the pages read from it are page images of the printed issues.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חובת_המכרזים
  ("Reshumot / Sefer HaChukim (רשומות, ספר החוקים), published by the Knesset; consolidated working text from "The Open Book of Laws" (ספר החוקים הפתוח) on Hebrew Wikisource"), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
  A second Hebrew Wikisource page, of the preference regulations of 1995, is used the same way; its page name and revision id were not recorded.
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where
  any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English titles are working translations; none of them is an enacted title.
