# Row il-78: Public Tenders Law, 5752-1992, and the preference regulations, a targeted record of two references

Status on 2026-11-10: draft, version 0.1.0, target compiler main.
No domain expert has read this against the source.
Every claim below was checked against a deposited page image or the deposited excerpt named beside it; where it was not, it says so.
This row does not encode the Law.

## 1. What this row is

The row records two items of the Public Tenders Law, 5752-1992 ("the Law"), as typed values and tests them.
The items are labelled I1 and I2.
Each item has two limbs: limb (a), the target is repealed, missing or says something else, read from an official instrument; limb (b), the citing words still read so in the latest instrument read.
English titles of instruments are working translations, not enacted titles.
"The preference regulations" are the regulations whose title KT 5653 prints on page 562.

Item I1 is regulation 18 of the preference regulations, as Kovetz HaTakanot 5653 (KT 5653, issue date 15.1.1995) printed it on page 567.
Regulation 18 prints the provisions 4(א), 5(ב), 7(ד) ו־9(א) in its list.
Item 3 of the amending regulations of 2007 (KT 6586, issue date 10.5.2007, printed page 830) prints that chapter ג׳ of the principal regulations is repealed.
Item 5 of the same amending regulations deletes the words 7(ד) ו־9(א) from regulation 18, and names neither 5(ב) nor 4(א).
Regulation 5(ב) is printed on page 564 of KT 5653, under the heading "פרק ג׳: חובת שיתוף פעולה עסקי" that is printed on that page.
None of the pages read prints regulation 18 as a whole after item 5 of KT 6586.
The consolidation of the regulations (UNOFFICIAL, so CONSOLIDATION-ONLY) prints 4(א) and 5(ב) in regulation 18, "(בוטל)" at the heading of the chapter and "(בוטלה)" at regulation 5.
The red assertion of I1 says that the provision regulation 18 names as 5(ב) lies outside the chapter that item 3 repeals.
It fails, because the 1995 print puts regulation 5(ב) under the heading of that chapter.

Item I2 is the order of 2023 (KT 10718, issue date 13.7.2023, printed page 2104).
Its opening words print "בתוקף סמכותי לפי סעיף 3 לחוק חובת המכרזים (תיקון מס׳ 24 – הוראת שעה) (תיקון), התש״ף–2020 (להלן – החוק)".
Its section 1 prints "התקופה הקבועה בסעיף 1 לחוק" and the date "י״ט בתמוז התשפ״ה (15 ביולי 2025)".
The 2020 law (Sefer HaChukim 2827, SH 2827, issue date 15.7.2020, printed page 228) prints two numbered sections, 1 and 2.
Its section 2 has the margin note "הוספת סעיף 3" and prints a quoted paragraph headed by the numeral 3 inside the quotation marks.
The red assertion of I2 says that the instrument the order's opening words define as "החוק" has a numbered section 3.
It fails, because the print of SH 2827 has numbered sections 1 and 2.
The 2015 law (SH 2495, issue date 15.7.2015, printed page 197) and the 2025 law (SH 3443, issue date 15.7.2025, printed page 748) are typed by their own two numbered sections each.

Beside each item the row keeps controls: tests that find the string, the number or the heading where it is, so that a failing test is a result about the pages and not a test that cannot succeed.
The row asks no question of anyone, because it records references.

## 2. Seat

The seat is the body that publishes a tender under the Law and the preference regulations.
The row asks that body no question: it records references, there is no input to ask for, and no `@export`.
