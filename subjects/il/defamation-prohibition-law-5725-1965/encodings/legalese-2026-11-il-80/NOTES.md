# Row il-80: Defamation (Prohibition) Law, 5725-1965, a targeted record of two references

Status on 2026-11-10: draft, version 0.1.0, target compiler main.
No domain expert has read this against the source.
Every claim below was checked against a deposited page image or the deposited excerpt named beside it; where it was not, it says so.
This row does not encode the Law.

## 1. What this row is

The row records two items of the Defamation (Prohibition) Law, 5725-1965 (the Law) as typed values and tests them.
Item I1 is paragraph (6) of section 13, as enacted (SH 464, issue date 30.7.1965, printed page 242).
Its words name "פקודת ועדות חקירה" and, in the same clause, a section of Basic Law: The Knesset.
Item 7 of SH 508 (issue date 14.8.1967) deletes a string in paragraphs (5) and (6) of section 13; the name is still in the words that remain.
Section 30 of the Commissions of Inquiry Law (SH 548, issue date 8.1.1969, printed page 32) prints "פקודת ועדות חקירה – בטלה."
No page the row reads prints a commencement for that Law, so the row types its commencement as NOTHING and 8.1.1969 as the issue date.
Paragraph (5)(ב) of section 15 reaches paragraph (6) by the words "כאמור בסעיף 13(6)" and does not name the Ordinance.
Item I2 is section 7 (SH 464, printed page 240), whose words name the numbers "2(2) עד 15, 58 עד 61 ו־63 עד 68א" of "פקודת הנזיקים האזרחיים, 1944"; item 4 of SH 508 inserts "55ב" after "15".
The Ordinance was printed again as a New Version (Dinei Medinat Israel, New Version 10); its section 93 prints a commencement of 1 October 1968, and its comparison table (printed page 280) lists old section numbers beside new ones.
In the table's left-hand double column the row for old 68 is followed directly by the row for old 69, and the rows for old 55ב print the new-section cells 64, 65, 66 and 67.
Beside each item the row keeps controls: a test that finds the name, the lettered numbers or the cell where it is, and the places where the same texts agree.
Each item has two limbs: limb (a), the target is repealed, missing or says something else, read from an official instrument; limb (b), the citing words still read so in the latest official instrument read.
The row says what the pages print and nothing more.
It does not say what the Ordinance provided, how paragraph (6) or section 7 should read, whether a reference held when it was made, or what a court would do.
The row does not read the sections of the New Version that the table names, only the table.
The English titles of instruments are working translations.
Numbers and letters of sections are typed in Latin letters in identifiers (alef, bet, gimel); the Hebrew is on the quotation lines.

## 2. Seat

The seat is the person who sues, or is sued, over a publication to which section 7, section 13 or section 15 of the Law is applied.
The row asks that individual no question, because it records references: there is no input to ask for and no `@export`.

Quotation lines in the modules are of two kinds.
A `src:N` line is expanded by `tools/srcquote.py` from the excerpt; `python3 -I tools/srcquote.py --check <excerpt> *.l4` exits 0.
An `ext:[tag]` line quotes the Hebrew read from the deposited image named by the tag (`sh464-p242` is SH 464, printed page 242; `nv10-p280` is the New Version, printed page 280).
`python3 -I tools/hebcheck.py <excerpt> *.l4 NOTES.md` exits 0: every run of Hebrew outside a quotation line is in the excerpt or on an `ext:` line of the row.
The tool checks that a typed string equals a stretch of a quotation line; it does not check the quotation line against the page.
The quotation lines were compared with the deposited crops by eye, one crop at a time.
They were also compared by script with the text layer of the page of the whole file, word by word: 298 of the 368 Hebrew words on the `ext:` lines that belong to a page with a text layer were found there (SH 464 page 240: 29 of 41; page 241: 9 of 9; page 242: 99 of 108; SH 508 page 133: 43 of 49; SH 548 page 28: 4 of 9; page 32: 4 of 17; SH 1030 page 302: 58 of 64; page 305: 24 of 29; the New Version page 266: 20 of 30; page 278: 8 of 12).
The words not found are mostly the footer lines, the footnotes and the margin notes, which the text layers of these pages do not hold in a form the comparison matches, and words that a layer splits or joins at a hyphen or a maqaf; those words were compared by eye only, and where a layer disagreed with the image the image decided.
The comparison table of page 280 has no usable text layer, so every cell of it was read from the image alone, at 300 dpi, and one cell (fork F6 in section 8) was read again at three times the size.
Where a term is printed between quotation marks, the `ext:` line types the Hebrew gershayim.
