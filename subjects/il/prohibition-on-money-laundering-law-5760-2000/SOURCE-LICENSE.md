# Source license

The text of the **Prohibition on Money Laundering Law, 5760-2000** (חוק איסור הלבנת הון, התש"ס-2000) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Law's original publication in Sefer HaChukim 5760, p. 293, together with every amending Act. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
  The Knesset address that the consolidation's header gives for the original is https://fs.knesset.gov.il/15/law/15_lsr_300267.pdf; it is not deposited in this subject.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הלבנת_הון ("Reshumot / Sefer HaChukim (רשומות, ספר החוקים), published by the Knesset; consolidated working text from "The Open Book of Laws" (ספר החוקים הפתוח) on Hebrew Wikisource"), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English titles in this subject are renderings made for the row, not enacted titles.
