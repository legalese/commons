# Source license

The texts quoted by this row are Israeli primary legislation: the **Prohibition on Money Laundering Law, 5760-2000** (חוק איסור הלבנת הון, התש"ס-2000), the **Prevention of Infiltration (Offences and Jurisdiction) Law, 5714-1954**, and the instruments that amend the Law's First Schedule or the Infiltration Law's temporary provisions.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, orders or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), and of each order in Kovetz HaTakanot, together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working texts**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), consolidated Hebrew texts. **Not official versions.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this row

Statute text may be deposited and quoted. A reading that rests only on a Wikisource consolidation says CONSOLIDATION-ONLY in `NOTES.md`; a reading from a page image of an official instrument says which page.
