# Source license

The texts quoted by this row are Israeli primary legislation: the **Companies Law, 5759-1999** (חוק החברות, התשנ"ט-1999), the **Companies (Fees) Regulations, 5761-2001**, the **Companies (Motion for Compromise or Arrangement) Regulations, 5762-2002**, the **Companies Ordinance [New Version], 5743-1983**, and the **Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law, 5778-2018**.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), and of each regulation in Kovetz HaTakanot, together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working texts**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), consolidated Hebrew texts. **Not official versions.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this row

Statute text may be deposited and quoted. A reading that rests only on a Wikisource consolidation says CONSOLIDATION-ONLY in `NOTES.md`; a reading from a page image of an official instrument says which page.

## Status of the deposits

The four Wikisource texts are deposited as excerpts, each behind a header that gives the page revision, the size and sha256 of the whole page as retrieved, and the lines copied; no other line of a page is part of an excerpt.
The excerpts were made on 2026-11-10 from pages retrieved on 2026-10-21 (the Companies Law, revision 3088229) and 2026-11-10 (the other three), and they alter no earlier deposit in this subject.
The official pages are in `registers/source-bundle/official/` (Kovetz HaTakanot 6074, 6178 and 7639, from `olaw.org.il`, a website that hosts scans of Reshumot issues and is not a government site) and `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/` (Sefer HaChukim 2708, from the Knesset's server), each deposited as an extract of just the pages this row cites, cut out with qpdf from the file as downloaded, with page images of those pages.
`registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` gives the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of each file.
