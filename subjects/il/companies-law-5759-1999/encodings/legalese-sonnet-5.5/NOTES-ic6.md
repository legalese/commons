**The source is an unofficial consolidation.**

## Seat

The seat is **a person who founds, directs or holds shares in a company under the Companies Law**: a founder, a director or officer, or a shareholder including a minority shareholder.
They ask what the Law lets them do, what it requires of them, what they may require of the company, and what can be done to them.

Why this person.
This range is where the Law bites on the company and on the people who run it and own it: a foreign company's duty to register and the fine that follows (ss 346-349), the court's power to bind shareholders and creditors to a compromise (s 350), striking a company off the register and what happens to its shareholders and officers afterwards (ss 351ב-351ח), the shareholder's remedies (ss 352-353א), the Registrar's financial sanction and its collection from a director (ss 354-362), the defaulting company and its controller (s 362א), the Securities Authority's sanction on a reporting corporation (ss 363א-363ב), the transitional provisions that decide which old rules still govern a company's articles and voting rights (ss 367-372), and the governance and fund-investment schedules that a director or shareholder reads when they decide how the company is run.
An official gets no seat.
The Registrar, the court, the Minister, the Securities Authority and the Registrar of Endowments appear only as what may be done to the person, what the person is owed, or a fact the person states about them ("did the Registrar find ...").

Layer 2 has 180 questions in 14 groups (`§§` headings, in the text's order) and 708 worked cases.
Every question has `@export` and `@desc` (Hebrew, then ` / ` and an English gloss), takes plain inputs one per fact the person can state (yes or no, a number, a date, a text, or a choice from the module's own list), and calls the layer-1 rule.
Every top-level name the seat defines is specific to its provision, because the names of all pieces will sit in one folder: each question's name ends with the locator of its provision (`... (סעיף 346(א))`, `... (תוספת 3 פרט 2(ב))`), the two local choice types carry the section in their names, and their constructors end `לעניין סעיף 350(א)` and `לעניין סעיף 363א(א)`; the helpers start `ic6-seat - `.
Each question's records are built inside it from those answers; a record field the rule called never reads is a fixed placeholder with a comment.
Of the 201 layer-1 rules, 194 are reached by a seat question (directly or through another rule); the 7 that are not are listed below with the reason.

**Assumed, not ruled** (design choices of this layer; each can be reversed without touching layer 1):

1. A judgment the Law gives to the Registrar, the court, the Minister or an authority ("a reasonable basis to think", "just and right", "the Minister determined") is asked as a yes or no about what that official found or determined; it is never guessed. This follows the CLAUDE.md default of 2026-10-18.
2. The Law's own figures are used as it states them (6,000 shekels in s 354(א), 250,000 in s 356(ב1), the 14, 30, 45, 60 and 90 days, the two and three years). The Registrar's index update of s 354(א) has its own question (s 355(ב)), which asks for the index values. Amounts that another Law fixes (the Penal Law's fines, the Securities Law's Sixth Schedule) are inputs.
3. Where a question about "my company" does not ask whether it was incorporated abroad, the company was incorporated under this Law (or the Ordinance). The foreign-company questions and the sanction questions ask.
4. Kinds of company (private, public, bond company, reporting corporation) are not asked as conclusions. The questions ask the facts the core's definitions read (listed, offered to the public under a prospectus, offered abroad, held by the public), and only the facts the rule called reads: the others are placeholders with a comment.
5. Section 350(ט) speaks of every class meeting the court convened; the seat asks about one (the usual case). With several, the layer-1 rule takes the list of their results.
6. An input that layer 1 takes as `MAYBE` (a date that may not exist) is asked as a yes or no plus a date, so that every input is plain.
7. A share of capital or of votes is entered as a fraction (0.5 for half, 0.25 for a quarter), because the layer-1 rules compare against `50%` and `25%`.
8. Compliance questions about the company (the First Schedule's recommended provisions, the compensation policy of Schedule 1A, the fund's investment rules of Schedule 3) are asked as a director or shareholder would ask them about their own company; nothing in them is a duty of the person unless the text makes it so.
9. The Hebrew wording of the questions, and the English glosses, are the author's own and have not been read by a Hebrew-speaking lawyer.

## Fork register

**F-ic6-1. "חברה פרטית או חברת חוץ שאינן תאגיד מדווח" (src:3176, src:3247; ic6-common.l4).**
Readings: (a) the plural "שאינן" (are not) covers both limbs, so a private company must not be a reporting corporation either, and a foreign company must not be a reporting corporation; (b) it covers the foreign company only.
Taken: (a). The verb is in the plural and follows both nouns; and section 354(ב) treats a "reporting corporation" separately, which would make no sense if a reporting private company were already inside subsection (א).
Looked at: the wording of sections 354(א), 354(ב), 362א(א) and the core's `תאגיד מדווח` (src:254). Effect: `חברה פרטית או חברת חוץ שאינן תאגיד מדווח לפי חלק 9` is false for a private company that is a reporting corporation (tested).

**F-ic6-2. How a period of days, a month or a year is counted (every time limit in the range).**
The text writes "בתוך חודש לאחר", "בתוך ארבעה עשר ימים ממועד", "בתוך 90 ימים ממועד פרסומה", "בתוך שנה ממועד המחיקה" and the like. The Interpretation Law, which fixes how such a period is counted, is not among the inputs.
Readings: (a) the day of the event is day 0 and the last day of the period is inside it (a period of 14 days from 1 March ends on 15 March); (b) the day of the event counts as day 1.
Taken: (a), the usual reading of "ממועד" ("from the date of"). A period of months ends on the same day of the later month, or on the last day of that month when it has no such day (`add months`, `add years` of the standard library `daydate`).
Effect: every time-limit rule is tested on both sides of the last day. A reader who prefers (b) moves each boundary by one day.

**F-ic6-3. "אחת לשנה" in section 348 (src:2974).**
Readings: (a) one report within twelve months of the previous report; (b) one report in each calendar year; (c) one report in each year of the company's registration.
Taken: (a), as the only reading that needs no date the text does not give. With no earlier report the text gives no due date, so the rule answers NOTHING (not covered). The form is the Minister's (`הדין וחשבון הוגש בנוסח שקבע השר`, a plain input).
Effect: a company that reports on 1 March 2025 and 2 March 2026 is late under (a) and in time under (b).

**F-ic6-4. The daily fine of section 349 (src:2977).**
"קנס נוסף ... לכל יום שבו נמשכת ההפרה, מהיום שהחברה קיבלה הודעה מאת רשם החברות".

**F-ic6-5. The vote in a class meeting, section 350(ט) (src:2997).**
The text: "הסכימו לפשרה או להסדר רוב מספרם של המשתתפים בהצבעה למעט הנמנעים שבידם יחד שלושה רבעים של הערך המיוצג בהצבעה".
Readings: (a) one test with two limbs: those who agreed are more than half in number of the participants, abstainers left out, and hold at least three quarters of the value represented in the vote; (b) the relative clause "שבידם יחד" binds the three quarters to a majority-in-number group that is not necessarily all those who agreed; (c) the value represented includes the value of the abstainers.
Taken: (a). If a majority in number holds three quarters of the value and has agreed, the whole body of those who agreed is at least that majority and holds at least that value, so (a) and (b) give the same answer; and "הערך המיוצג בהצבעה" is read as the value of those who voted for or against, abstainers being "left out" of the count in the same breath ("למעט הנמנעים"). "Three quarters" is read as at least three quarters: exactly 75 percent agrees (tested at 75 of 100 and 74 of 100).
Also taken: "בכל אסיפת סוג שכונסה" means that every class meeting that was convened must agree; with no class meeting convened the clause has nothing to say, so the rule is false (tested with an empty list). The court's approval is a second, separate condition.
Effect: a reader who counts the abstainers' value in the base moves the three-quarters line.

**F-ic6-6. The day count of section 351(ג) (src:3093).**
"תעביר ... העתק מאושר שלו לרשם בתוך שבעה ימים מיום שניתן; ... קנס ... לכל יום שבו נמשכת ההפרה."

Taken: (a), as in F-ic6-2. Counting the days of the continuing breach: the breach begins after day 7, and every day up to and including the day of delivery (or, if not yet delivered, the day of the check) counts (delivery on day 8 is one day; on day 17, ten days). The text does not say whether the day of the late delivery itself counts; counting it is the cautious reading for the person, because the fine is "for every day in which the breach continues".
Effect: the number of days is a count, not an amount; the amount per day is section 61(ג) of the Penal Law's (not among the inputs).

(Entry F-ic6-8 also governs every other "חלפו" in the range: the six months of section 354(ב1)(2)(ב) and (4), the 30 days of section 362א(ב). By contrast "within N days" and "בתוך" always include the last day, and "registered for a year" (351ג(א)(3)) is complete on the anniversary.)

**F-ic6-16. Continuing and repeat violations, section 363ב (src:3288-3289).**
"בהפרה נמשכת ייווסף על סכום העיצום הכספי החלק החמישים שלו לכל יום שבו נמשכת ההפרה. בהפרה חוזרת ייווסף ... סכום השווה למחצית העיצום הכספי".
Questions the text leaves open: (a) from which day the days of a continuing violation are counted (from the start of the violation, or from the day the sanction could first be imposed, or from the day it was demanded); (b) whether the fiftieth is of the basic amount or of the amount so far (compounding); (c) whether the half for a repeat is of the basic amount or of the sanction with the continuing addition.
Taken: (a) not settled: the number of days is a plain input of the rules (`מספר הימים שבהם נמשכת ההפרה`), so a caller counts as the Authority does; (b) and (c): each addition is computed on the basic amount, not on the sum so far ("החלק החמישים שלו", "מחצית העיצום הכספי כאמור": of the sanction in the section). A repeat violation is a violation "בתוך שנתיים" (two years to the same date, that day inside) of an earlier violation of the same provision for which the Authority imposed a sanction under this sub-chapter.
Effect: 1,000 basic, 10 days, a repeat: 1,000 + 200 + 500 = 1,700 (tested).

**F-ic6-17. Section 377(2): the proviso and the end of three years (src:3361).**
"סעיפים 143 ו־145 יכנסו לתוקף בתום שלוש שנים מיום פרסומו של חוק זה או במועד מוקדם מזה שיקבעו השר ושר האוצר, ובלבד שנקבעו הוראות בדבר דיווח אלקטרוני לפי חוק ניירות ערך".
Readings of the proviso: (a) it conditions only the earlier day the two Ministers may set; (b) it conditions the whole entry into force, so the sections come in at the end of three years only if the electronic-reporting provisions have been set.
Taken: (b), because "ובלבד" follows both alternatives and a reading that lets the sections come in with no electronic-reporting provisions would leave the proviso with nothing to do for the default day. Where the proviso is not met the rule answers NOTHING (the text does not say when the sections come in).
"בתום שלוש שנים מיום פרסומו": taken as the anniversary of the day of publication (publication 27 May 1999: 27 May 2002). The publication day is an input; the Knesset record in SOURCES.md gives 27 May 1999, which the text itself does not state. "מועד מוקדם מזה": the earlier day is used only if it really is earlier than the end of three years (tested with a later day, which is ignored).

**F-ic6-18. The Third Schedule: "exceeds", "above", and "a majority of the votes of the members present" (src:3509-3511, 3523, 3535).**
Item 2(ב): "לא תשקיע בניירות ערך של מנפיק אחד בשיעור של יותר מ־20% ... עלה השווי הכספי על הסכומים המפורטים להלן, תקבע הקרן כי ההשקעה בתאגיד אחד ... לא תעלה על השיעורים הנקובים לצדם: (1) מיליון שקלים חדשים – 50% ... (2) עשרה מיליון שקלים חדשים – 25%".
Readings: (a) the 20 percent ceiling (securities of one issuer) and the 50/25 percent ceilings (everything invested in one corporation) are separate and both apply; (b) the 50/25 percent ceilings replace the 20 percent one once the value passes one million.
Taken: (a). The 20 percent clause is unconditional and speaks of securities; the bands speak of the total investment in one corporation (deposits, savings plans and securities it issued) and apply "when the financial value exceeds". The two rules are separate rules in the encoding. Above ten million both bands are crossed and the lower ceiling (25 percent) is taken. "Exceeds" and "above" (item 3, 30 million) are strictly more (tested at 1,000,000, 1,000,001, 10,000,000, 10,000,001, 30,000,000 and 30,000,001). Where the value does not exceed one million, this part of (ב) sets no ceiling (NOTHING).
Item 6: "החלטות ועדת ההשקעות יתקבלו ברוב קולות החברים הנוכחים". Readings: (a) more than half of the members present must vote for (an abstention counts against); (b) more votes for than against among those who vote. Taken: (a), as the words "החברים הנוכחים" fix the base as the members present, not those voting.
Item 6, frequency: "אחת לרבעון לפחות" is read as at least once in each of the four quarters of the year; the other frequency the board may approve is not less than twice a year, tested as at least two meetings.
The text fixes no unit for the "financial value" of the surplus funds beyond new shekels; whole shekels, as everywhere in this encoding.

**F-ic6-19. The First Schedule A, part B item 1(א): what "פרט משנה זה לא יחול על נושא משרה הכפוף למנהל הכללי" lifts (src:3428).**
"ביסוס הרכיבים על ביצועים בראייה ארוכת טווח, ועל פי קריטריונים הניתנים למדידה; ואולם חברה רשאית לקבוע כי חלק לא מהותי מהרכיבים ... יוענק על פי קריטריונים שאינם ניתנים למדידה ...; פרט משנה זה לא יחול על נושא משרה הכפוף למנהל הכללי".

"שלוש משכורות חודשיות השנה": the sum of the components for the year at most three monthly salaries (tested at 30,000 and 30,001 against a monthly salary of 10,000).

**F-ic6-20. The First Schedule, item 3, last clause (src:3380).**
"דירקטור בתאגיד בשליטת חברה ציבורית או חברה פרטית שהיא חברת איגרות חוב רשאי לכהן כדירקטור באותה חברה."
Readings: (a) a separate permission: a director of a corporation the company controls may sit on the company's board even if he is subordinate to the general manager (tested); (b) a clarification that sitting on a subsidiary's board does not by itself make a person "subordinate".
Taken: (a), the words "רשאי לכהן" being a permission in its own sentence after a semicolon. Under (b) the clause adds nothing the rule would not already say for a person who is not subordinate.

**F-ic6-21. The First Schedule, item 7: "ברוב קולות" and the two percent (src:3393, 3395).**
"דירקטורים חיצוניים בחברה ציבורית ימונו באסיפה הכללית ברוב קולות, ובלבד שהתקיימו כל אלה: (1) במניין כלל הקולות ... לא יובאו בחשבון קולות ... וכן קולות הנמנעים; (2) סך קולות התומכים מקרב בעלי המניות שאינם בעלי השליטה ... עלה על שני אחוזים מכלל זכויות ההצבעה בחברה".

## What the text turns out to say

These are things the text turns out to say (or not to say) that a reader would not guess from the headings.
"Asserted." means a case in `ic6-cases-seat.l4` or in a layer-1 tests file asserts it; the file is named.

1. Section 354(א) states the basic sanction as 6,000 shekels. The Wikisource editors' note beside it reads 9,380 for 2026, which is the editors' figure, not the Act's. The encoding takes 6,000 and the update of s 355(ב) (the sanction times the latest index over the index before the Law, rounded to ten if the Registrar rounds); it does not check the 9,380. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`: the sanction amount of 6,000 and of 12,000, and the update 6,000 to 6,087 and, rounded, 6,090).
2. The label `351ו` opens two sections with the same heading, the same amending year and the same three subsections (src:3135 and src:3140); they differ only in a link template in subsection (ב). One set of rules serves both, and the coverage table has a row for each copy. Not asserted: no case can tell the two copies apart, since the words are the same.
3. An objection to striking a company off may be filed up to and including day 90 from the notice (s 351ה(ב)), and the Registrar may strike off only once 90 days have passed (s 351ה(ה)), so the first day he may act is day 91. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 351ה(ב) and (ה): day 90 and day 91).
4. A class agrees to a compromise only if the participants voting for outnumber those against (a tie fails) and hold at least three quarters of the value voting (exactly three quarters passes). Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 350(ט): a tie on heads, 75 of 100 and 74 of 100 of the value).
5. Section 354(ג) allows "double the sanction" on a repeat, and paragraphs (2) to (4) of (ב1) already impose a double; the encoding reads the repeat as four times the base after such a sanction (fork F-ic6-11). Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 354(ג): 12,000 after a base sanction, 24,000 after a double one).
6. The 250,000-shekel cap in s 356(ב1) is a cap on the additional daily sanction, which is one sixtieth of the sanction per further day, and not on the total; and the additional sanction is not available for unpaid fees. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 356(ב1)).
7. Section 359(א) gives an appeal to the Magistrates' Court, within 30 days of receiving the demand, only for a decision concerning a public-benefit company; for any other company the section is silent and the encoding answers NOTHING. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 359(א)).
8. Section 348 requires an annual report "once a year" but fixes no first due date; with no earlier report the encoding answers NOTHING. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 348).
9. Section 377 gives no publication date for the Law, yet sections 143 and 145 come into force three years from it; the date (27 May 1999 in the Knesset record in `SOURCES.md`) is an input. The proviso (electronic-reporting provisions made under the Securities Law) conditions the whole entry into force, so without it the encoding answers NOTHING (fork F-ic6-17). Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 377: 27 May 2002, an earlier day, and NOTHING).
10. The defence of s 354(ב1)(5)(ד), that the company complied with the other public-benefit rules although unrecorded, is open for only three of the five violations that paragraph lists. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 354(ב1)(5): the same facts with an open and a closed violation).
11. A company whose securities are traded only abroad reports to the Registrar as a company that is not a reporting corporation, unless the Minister determined that ss 142 to 145 apply instead (s 365); a company that is also traded in Israel is outside s 365. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 365).
12. The chapter on compromise and arrangement reaches a foreign company only if it does business or has assets in Israel (s 350(יא)), or the Minister has applied the chapter to it by order (s 351א). Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, ss 350(יא) and 351א).
13. The Securities Authority may impose its sanction only on a reporting corporation and only after the violation has lasted more than the minimum (60 days for a missing chair, 90 for the others listed): exactly 60 days is not enough, and the days the supervisor needs to approve appointments are deducted. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 363א(ב) and (ד)).
14. A public-benefit company needs no year as a defaulting company before it can be struck off, but it does need the Registrar of Endowments' consent (s 351ג(ב)). Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 351ג).
15. The Registrar may not demand the sanction from a director if the company named another person as responsible in its annual report and that person has not first been asked and failed to pay (s 360(ה)); a director who is himself the person named is not protected. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 360(ה) and (א)).
16. In the Third Schedule, item 2(ב), the 50 and 25 percent ceilings on a single corporation apply only when the surplus funds exceed one million and ten million shekels respectively, and exactly one million or ten million is not above; at or under one million that clause sets no ceiling (only the 20 percent per issuer applies). Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, Third Schedule item 2(ב)).
17. Section 367(א) keeps three runs of sections of the Companies Ordinance (164-201, 244-367, 370-382) and a few sections on conditions; section 368 of the Ordinance lies between the second and third runs and is not kept, while section 369 is kept only for a company struck off under 368 before commencement. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 367(א): sections 367, 368, 369 and 370).
18. In Schedule 1A part B item 1(א), "this sub-item shall not apply to an office-holder subordinate to the general manager" lifts the whole sub-item (fork F-ic6-19); the total of the non-measurable part may then be at most three monthly salaries, and 30,001 against a monthly 10,000 fails. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, Schedule 1A part B 1(א)).
19. The thirteenth purpose of the Second Schedule (setting up funds or organisations that support a body acting for the listed purposes) counts only if the supported body does act for one of the others; a subject not listed is not a public purpose. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, Second Schedule).
20. First Schedule item 7(2) requires the supporting votes of non-controlling shareholders to exceed two percent of all the voting rights, and "exceed" is strict: exactly two percent fails. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, First Schedule item 7(2)).
21. The Fourth Schedule's second item is met by a mobile number or by a second e-mail address that differs from the first; a second address equal to the first does not count. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, Fourth Schedule).
22. For the daily fine of s 349 the day the company receives the Registrar's notice counts as the first day (fork F-ic6-4); a breach that ended the day before counts none. Asserted (`ic6-cases-seat.l4`, s 349).

## What the stable binary does not accept

What I met on the stable build `l4` 1.6.98 (all with `L4_TARGET=unstable`), and what I did about it:

- There is no record-update operator: `existingRecord WITH field IS x` is not accepted. Records are built whole, with `Type WITH field IS ...` for every field (in the tests, with the positional `OF`).
- `x elem list` does not parse as an infix test; `elem x list` does.
- A percentage literal after a call or a bare argument needs brackets: `f (25%)`, `JUST (25%)`, `(50%) (10%)`; `JUST 25%` and `50% 10%` were mis-parsed.
- `NOT` is parenthesised everywhere (`NOT (a AND b)` versus `(NOT a) AND b`), as the Guide says.
- `AND` and `OR` at the same indentation raise a lint warning; the rule for s 367(א) was restructured with local definitions in a `WHERE` so each level of the condition is one operator.
- A stub rule built from one string (`GIVETH A BOOLEAN` over a single string) is typed STRING, not BOOLEAN; the repealed-section stubs in `ic6-repealed.l4` are `GIVETH A STRING`.
- `REFUSE`, `#ASSERT REFUSED`, `BECAUSE` (outside `LEST BREACH`), `TYPICALLY` on a record field, `@lang` and `@nlg` are not used, as the Guide and the brief say; I did not probe them.
- A rule that returns a count of days (`סעיף 349 - מספר הימים שבגינם נוסף קנס יומי` and others) is used instead of a regulative `PARTY ... MUST` rule: the brief asks that every time limit be testable, and a compliance predicate over dates can be asserted in `check.sh`.
- Hebrew: Hebrew backticked identifiers, Hebrew `@export` and `@desc` text with ` / ` and an English gloss, Hebrew `§` headings, and `-- src:N |` quote lines that carry `{{ח:...}}` templates were all accepted by `l4 run`, `l4 check` and `srcrefs.py check --refs`. Typographic quotation marks (” “) inside names are accepted; ASCII double quotes were kept out of annotation text.
- `srcrefs.py` rule R2 (a locator must lie inside the quoted lines of the same file) rejected locators for ss 351(א), 354 and the aggregator of Schedule 1A part A until the quote ranges were widened; this is a fix to my quoting, not a limitation.
- A record field label and an input of the same name coexist: the seat's two helper rules (`ic6-seat - עובדות החברה שלי`, `ic6-seat - התאגיד שלי`) write `field IS input` with the same name on both sides, and their cases pass. The question rules still give record-building inputs first-person names (`צירפתי רשימת הדירקטורים`) because they read better to the person asked, not because the build needs it.
- A seat module that imports seventeen modules is slow when other pieces are running `l4` on the same machine: one foreground run of `ic6-seat` and its cases did not finish in 120 seconds. Nothing failed there; `check.sh` should be given a long timeout.

## Before this is worth relying on

1. **Compare the text with the official one.** The source is an unofficial Wikisource consolidation. A reviewer with the official Sefer HaChukim text and the amending Acts should check every figure and every cross-reference, in particular the 6,000 shekels of s 354(א) (the editors' note beside it reads 9,380 for 2026), the 250,000 of s 356(ב1), the percentages of the Third Schedule, and the label `351ו`, which opens two sections in this file (src:3135 and src:3140; fork F-ic6-9).
2. **Commencement of the amending Acts.** The text carries `תיקון:` years beside each section; the encoding treats the whole consolidation as in force and does not model which amendment began when. Sections 346 and 351ב-351ג carry the amending year תשפ״ד־2 (chapter 3A, striking off, is its work); sections 354, 362א and 363א-363ב carry years between תשס״ד and תשע״ו. The dates those Acts came into force are not among the inputs.
3. **The twenty-one forks** (fork register above) are readings I chose where the text does not settle the question. Each is tested on both sides, but none has been reviewed by a lawyer qualified in Israel. The ones that move an answer most: F-ic6-5 (the class vote), F-ic6-8 (when "have passed" is satisfied), F-ic6-11 (the repeat sanction is four times the base), F-ic6-15 (the appeal is given only for public-benefit companies), F-ic6-17 (the proviso of s 377(2)) and F-ic6-19 (the exemption in Schedule 1A part B item 1(א)).
4. **Other Laws and regulations are inputs.** The Penal Law (the fines of s 349), the Securities Law (the Sixth Schedule amounts of s 363א(א)), the Insolvency Law, the Companies Regulations (fees) and the Minister's orders are not among the inputs. The cross-reference table lists each; a user must supply the outcome.
5. **No independent test pass.** The rules, the tests and the seat were written by one agent. Every expected value was written from the text. In the layer-1 tests none was changed to make a case pass. In the seat pass one case group failed (Third Schedule item 4(a)(2)): I had glossed the input backwards (as "members who are not qualified" instead of "members for whom paragraph (1) holds"). I re-read the text (src:3528), corrected the gloss and the expected values, and the group then passed; I did not copy the rule's output. A second reader of the text has not repeated the tests.
6. **The Hebrew wording of the seat questions and the English glosses** were written by the model and have not been read by a Hebrew speaker.
7. **The seat is one person's questions.** It does not ask about every combination (for example, several class meetings in s 350(ט)); layer 1 does cover them.
