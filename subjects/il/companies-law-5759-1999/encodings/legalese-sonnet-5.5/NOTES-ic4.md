# NOTES: piece ic4 of the Israeli Companies Law, 5759-1999 (חוק החברות): Part 8, Part 8A and the opening of Part 9

**What this is.**
Part 8 (acquisition of companies: merger, special tender offer, forced sale of shares), Part 8A (dissolution), and sections 343 to 345 (the opening of Part 9: changing the kinds of corporation).
Lines 2081 to 2515 of the source, 87 sections in all.

**The source is an unofficial consolidation.**
The text is the raw MediaWiki source of the Hebrew Wikisource page of the Law, which is a volunteer consolidation.
Israel publishes no free official consolidated text.
Nothing in this folder has been verified against the official text, and no sentence here claims it has.
Which amendments the consolidation shows as in force is the consolidation's word, not checked here.

**Layout.**
Layer 1 is the Law as seven modules `ic4-part*.l4` (and `ic4-nouns.l4`, helpers only), every rule cited with `@ref` and with mechanically copied `-- src:N |` quote lines, and tested in the seven `ic4-tests-*.l4`.
Layer 2 is `ic4-seat.l4` (the questions) and `ic4-cases-seat.l4` (the cases).
`ic4-seat.l4` and `ic4-cases-seat.l4` are generated from staged parts and a hand-written case list kept outside this folder; the `.l4` files in this folder are the deliverable and are ordinary L4.

## Seat

The seat is a person who founds, directs or holds shares in a company under the Companies Law: a founder, a director or officer, a shareholder including a minority shareholder.
The questions are asked as that person asks them ("May I ...?", "Must we ...?", "Has the trustee met his duty to ...?"), and the facts are ones that person could state.
The seat is ruled by the brief; the reason is that the Legalese website turns a seat layer into a question form for the person the law is applied to.

**What the person asks about, by Part.**
- Merger (ss 314 to 327): whether the general meeting must approve, whether the board may approve, how the special vote ends, whether a time limit was met, whether the court may delay the merger on a creditor's request, whether the merger is carried out.
- Special tender offer (ss 328 to 334): whether a purchase needs a special tender offer, the board's duties and the office-holder's liability, whose vote is counted, whether the offer is accepted, what shares bought in breach carry.
- Forced sale (ss 336 to 342): whether a purchase above 90 percent is barred, whether a full tender offer is accepted, the appraisal remedy, when a dissenting shareholder of a private company can be bought out.
- Dissolution (ss 342ב to 342נג): the special resolution, the grounds for dissolution by a court, who may apply, the trustee's duties and the shareholder's share of the balance, voluntary dissolution (the affidavit, the meetings, the notices), the fast track for an inactive company, the offences, cancelling a liquidation.
- Part 9 (ss 343 to 345): telling the Registrar of a change of kind, limiting or unlimiting the shareholders' liability, a cooperative society becoming a company.

**How Hohfeld's pairs are met.**
Where the text gives the person a claim, privilege, power or immunity, the facts about the other party on which the text makes it depend are plain inputs: whether the trustee has filed, whether the Registrar has registered, whether the court was persuaded.
So "What am I owed?" is answered together with the other party's side of the same relation.
Where the text also puts a duty or liability on the person, the paired question is asked (for example "Must we attach the affidavit to the invitation?").
Provisions that address only an official are asked for what they mean to the person: what may be done to the company, what the person must be given, and by when.
There is no seat for an official.

**Conventions of the question form.**
- Every question has `@export` and, on every input, `@desc`, each in Hebrew, then ` / `, then an English gloss.
- Inputs are plain: yes or no, a number, a fraction of one (25 percent is written 0.25), a number of days or months, or a choice from a short list (the office held, the kinship, who the applicant is, which act was done).
- A date is three inputs (year, month, day); an optional figure is a yes-or-no input and a number.
- A time limit is answered with one of five states (not arisen, done in time, done late, not yet done and still open, not yet done and out of time); a duty with no stated period is answered with one of three (not arisen, done, not yet done).
- Where a layer-1 rule needs a record of the core (for example the facts about a company) the question builds it inside its `WHERE` from the plain inputs.
- No question restates a rule: each calls a layer-1 rule by name.
- A question quotes no source text; the source lines and the `@ref` are on the rule the question calls.

**Input names.**
Every input is named for the fact, one input per fact, never equal to a constructor of `צד` or to a rule, type or field name of the core or of this piece (the keel's rule), and none begins with the article ה and a party noun: a yes-or-no input that would begin so is written with `אם` ("whether") in front.
Two input names of the keel's own modules (`ic-core-deals.l4`, `ic-core-people.l4`, `ic-core-securities.l4`) are the keel's and were not touched.

- **Text:** (א1): `על אף האמור בסעיף קטן (א), מיזוג לא טעון אישורה של האסיפה הכללית ...`; (ב): `היו מניות חברת היעד מחולקות לסוגים, טעון המיזוג גם אישור אסיפות סוג בחברת היעד.`
- **Readings seen:** (a) (א1) lifts only the general meeting's approval, so class meetings are still needed; (b) (א1) lifts all approvals of shareholders.
- **Taken:** (a).
- **Licensed by:** (א1) is "notwithstanding subsection (א)" and speaks of "the general meeting"; (ב) is a separate subsection saying "also" class meetings.

### F6: s 323: "have passed"

- **Where:** s 323.
- **Text:** `וחלפו שלושים ימים ממועד קבלת החלטת האסיפה הכללית בכל אחת מן החברות המתמזגות וחמישים ימים מהמועד שבו הומצאו הצעות המיזוג לרשם`
- **Readings seen:** (a) at least 30 (and 50) days have elapsed; (b) more than 30 (and 50).
- **Taken:** (a): `AT LEAST 30` and `AT LEAST 50`.
- **Licensed by:** "Thirty days have passed" is satisfied on the thirtieth day; the same counting as F1.

### F7: "a majority of three quarters"

- **Where:** ss 327(א), 342ב.
- **Text:** `רוב באסיפה הכללית של שלושה רבעים מקרב בעלי המניות המשתתפים בהצבעה, למעט הנמנעים` (s 327(א)); `ברוב של שלושה רבעים מקולות בעלי המניות המשתתפים בהצבעה` (s 342ב).
- **Readings seen:** (a) at least three quarters; (b) more than three quarters.
- **Taken:** (a): for times 4 is at least 3 times (for plus against).
- **Licensed by:** A "majority of three quarters" is a qualified majority measured by a fraction; "at least" is the usual meaning of a qualified majority stated as a fraction.

### F8: s 328(א): "becomes" and "rises above"

- **Where:** s 328(א).
- **Text:** `לא תבוצע רכישה שכתוצאה ממנה ייהפך אדם להיות בעל דבוקת שליטה ... וכן לא תבוצע רכישה שכתוצאה ממנה יעלה שיעור החזקותיו של הרוכש מעל ארבעים וחמישה אחוזים`
- **Readings seen:** (a) the buyer crosses the line in this purchase (before below, after at or above); (b) the buyer ends above the line, whatever he held before.
- **Taken:** (a).
- **Licensed by:** "Becomes" and "rises" are changes of state. Under (b) a buyer who already holds a control block would need a special tender offer for every purchase, which the text does not say.

### F9: s 333(ב): which cap when both apply

- **Where:** s 333(ב).
- **Text:** `... לא תוקנינה זכויות הצבעה למניות שבהחזקתו בשיעור העולה על עשרים וחמישה אחוזים או ארבעים וחמישה אחוזים, לפי הענין`
- **Readings seen:** (a) 25 percent; (b) 45 percent; (c) the higher; (d) both caps apply in turn.
- **Taken:** (a): the lower cap, 25 percent, when a rise passes both lines.
- **Licensed by:** Where there is no control-block holder the rise crosses the control-block line first; the buyer has by then crossed 25 percent in the same rise. The text says "as the case may be" and does not rank the cases.

### F10: s 334: "for a year"

- **Where:** s 334.
- **Text:** `במשך שנה ממועד הצעת הרכש`
- **Readings seen:** (a) twelve months, the bar applies while fewer than twelve have passed; (b) twelve months, the bar includes the twelfth month.
- **Taken:** (a): the bar ends at twelve months.
- **Licensed by:** A year counted from a date ends on its anniversary, twelve months later.

### F11: s 338: "as sections 336(ג) and 337(א) or (א1) provide"

- **Where:** s 338(א), (ג), (ד).
- **Text:** `ניצע בהצעת רכש מלאה שהתקבלה כאמור בסעיפים 336(ג) ו־337(א) או (א1)`; (ג): `הצעת הרכש המלאה שהתקבלה כאמור בסעיף 337(א) או (א1)`.
- **Readings seen:** (a) accepted under either 336(ג) or 337; (b) accepted under both; and for the waiver in (ג), (c) only offers accepted under 337, or (d) both routes.
- **Taken:** (a) for the right to ask; (c) for the waiver: the waiver is for 337 acceptance only; the six months are inclusive of the sixth.
- **Licensed by:** The two routes are alternatives (an offer cannot be accepted both ways), and (ג) names 337 alone.

### F12: s 341(א): the base of the eighty percent

- **Where:** s 341(א).
- **Text:** `בעלי מניות שבבעלותם שמונים אחוזים מן המניות העומדות להעברה הסכימו ... במנין בעלי המניות האמורים לא יובאו בחשבון בעל שליטה במציע ...`
- **Readings seen:** (a) the base is all the shares up for transfer and the excluded persons' shares leave the numerator; (b) the base excludes the excluded persons' shares too.
- **Taken:** (a): the caller states the fraction; the rule compares it with the rate.
- **Licensed by:** "מן המניות העומדות להעברה" is the base; the exclusion is stated of the counting of those who agreed.

### F13: s 342ב: abstentions

- **Where:** s 342ב.
- **Text:** `ברוב של שלושה רבעים מקולות בעלי המניות המשתתפים בהצבעה`
- **Readings seen:** (a) abstentions are not counted (the majority is of for plus against); (b) abstainers are "taking part" and count against.
- **Taken:** (a).
- **Licensed by:** Section 327(א) says `למעט הנמנעים` expressly and s 342ב does not; but s 342ב speaks of the votes (`קולות`) of the shareholders taking part, and an abstention casts no vote. Reading (b) would make an abstention count as a vote against.

### F14: s 342ו: "within a year" and "for a year"

- **Where:** s 342ו(2).
- **Text:** `החברה לא התחילה בעסקיה בתוך שנה לאחר רישומה, או הפסיקה את עסקיה למשך שנה`
- **Readings seen:** (a) twelve months, the twelfth inside; (b) more than twelve.
- **Taken:** (a): at least twelve months.
- **Licensed by:** As F10.

### F15: s 342לו(ב): "each calendar year after it"

- **Where:** s 342לו(ב).
- **Text:** `יכנס הנאמן אסיפה כללית של החברה בתום השנה הראשונה ובתום כל שנה קלנדרית שלאחריה`
- **Readings seen:** (a) the calendar year in which the first year ends counts: its 31 December is a meeting date; (b) only calendar years that begin after the first year ends.
- **Taken:** (a).
- **Licensed by:** "After it" can be read as after the first year's end (the same calendar year counts if its end is later) or after the calendar year of the end (it does not). (a) gives the shareholders more meetings, not fewer.

### F16: s 342מז(א): when the Registrar's duty arises

- **Where:** s 342מז(א).
- **Text:** `ירשום בתום 10 ימי עבודה מהמועד האחרון להגשת התנגדויות`
- **Readings seen:** (a) the duty arises when 10 working days have passed and has no later limit; (b) the Registrar must register on the tenth working day exactly.
- **Taken:** (a).
- **Licensed by:** The text says "at the end of 10 working days" and states no outer limit; (b) would make registration on the eleventh day a breach, which the text does not say.

### F17: s 345(ג): "three quarters of the members entitled to vote who voted"

- **Where:** s 345(ג).
- **Text:** `ברוב של שלושה רבעים לפחות מן החברים הזכאים להצביע ושהצביעו בעצמם או על ידי שלוח`
- **Readings seen:** (a) three quarters of the members who voted, counted by members; (b) three quarters of those entitled to vote; (c) counted by their votes, not heads.
- **Taken:** (a).
- **Licensed by:** "ושהצביעו" restricts the base to those who voted; the text speaks of members (`חברים`), and a cooperative society's votes are by member.

## What the stable binary does not accept

Each item below was met in this work and either fixed or avoided; none leaves a rule unencoded.
- **No record update on an existing value.** `base WITH field IS v` on a value already built is a parse error on 1.6.98 (probed 2026-10-25); a record is built whole, and tests use small constructor functions (`רכישה לדוגמה`, `ניצע לדוגמה`) instead.
- **`length` is not defined.** `count` is the prelude's name for the length of a list.
- **A section-level `GIVEN` is not used**, as the brief says; every rule declares its own inputs.
- **A percent sign after a call needs brackets.** `half 10 50%` is a type error, `half 10 TIMES (50%)` is accepted (probed); fractions are written `(25%)`.
- **`NOT a OR b` reads as `NOT (a OR b)`**, so every `NOT` is parenthesised, as the brief says.
- **`@ref` must sit after the quote run and above the declaration**, and a `DECLARE` also gets its `@ref`; `l4 check` warns when a line follows code on its line or a quote run ends the file, and none is left.
- **`REFUSE` and `BECAUSE` are not used**; a case outside the encoding returns a constructor or NOTHING.
- **Hebrew is accepted throughout**: backticked names, `@export` and `@desc` with ` / ` and English, `@ref סעיף 342כד (src:N)` over a quote run carrying the `{{ח:ת}}` template, and `srcrefs.py check` reports 0 problems on all the modules. Nothing Hebrew was rejected.
- **A question with no input is accepted** (`@export` over a `DECIDE` that has no `GIVEN`; used for the number of years in s 342לט(ב)).
- **Dates**: `DATE` as the result of an `@export`ed question passes `l4 check`; its output through a form has not been exercised here.
- **Lists in a question**: a list is built from two yes-or-no or numeric inputs with `LIST a, b`; there is no list input.

## Before this is worth relying on

- **Not verified against the official text.** The source is the Hebrew Wikisource consolidation; its currency (which amendments are in force) is its maintainers' word.
  A reader should compare the quoted lines with the Reshumot text before relying on any figure.
- **Seventeen forks are readings, not findings** (the register above). The ones most likely to change an answer are F1 (the counting of days, which moves every time limit by a day), F3, F5, F8, F9 and F17.
  A lawyer should confirm or overrule each, and an overruling is a change to one rule and its tests.
- **Out-of-range inputs.** Twenty-one cross-references are plain inputs (table above). Their outcomes decide, among other things, whether the special vote applies at all (ss 268, 275) and which deemed provisions bind an old company (s 377).
  Until the other pieces are merged, a user must supply them.
- **The Insolvency Law is applied, not encoded.** Part 8A says "the provisions of the Insolvency Law apply with the necessary changes"; the rules record that they apply and which, and nothing more.
- **The Minister's regulations** are inputs. A rule that turns on "as the Minister prescribed" is only as good as the user's answer.
- **Judgments handed to others.** The court's findings (a reasonable fear, fair and reasonable, just and equitable, justified), the Registrar's satisfaction and the board's opinion are inputs named for the finding. The encoding says what follows from the finding; it never says whether the finding would be made.
- **Working days** (s 342מז(א)) are counted by the caller.
- **The seat's glosses** are the encoder's, not the Law's; the English glosses are plain-language guides, and the Hebrew is the text.
- **Cases are written by the encoder who wrote the rules.** An independent test pass (a reader who writes expected values from the text without seeing the rules) has not been done; it is the single check most worth adding.
- **Dates.** The only date in the range is the one s 336(ג) states; the Law's own enactment and commencement are in other pieces.
- **Not a legal opinion.** Nothing here advises anyone on a real situation.
