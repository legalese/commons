## Decisions taken (with the section that licenses each)

- A rule returns BOOLEAN (the relation holds) unless the Law leaves a case open, when it returns MAYBE with NOTHING (ss 3, 11, 13, 21, 40, 63(c), 74(c), 78(b)).
- A judgment handed to a court or the Registrar is a BOOLEAN input named for the finding (ss 6, 7, 55, 62, 65, 72, 91).
- A rule the articles may vary has inputs for whether and how they vary it (ss 20(a), 22, 24, 67, 78, 85, 98(d), 100, 104, 107).
- "Within N days or months" counts the last day inside the period (F-ic1-4, section 13(a)); a year is twelve months elapsed.
- An ordinary majority is more votes for than against; a percentage majority is the votes for as a share of the votes for and against, AT LEAST the share (F-ic1-6).
- Seat questions are phrased from the person's side; two were reworded so that TRUE means what the question asks (s 20(c) and s 20(d)).
- Section 48(d) takes "all the organs" to include every organ of section 46 (F-ic1-20).
