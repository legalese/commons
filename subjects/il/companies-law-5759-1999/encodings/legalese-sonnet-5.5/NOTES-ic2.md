Naming: rules are named `` `סעיף N - …` `` (N the section label in ASCII digits, then the Law's own words); records of facts `` `עובדות …` ``; inputs are descriptive phrases and never a party noun with the article ה; questions begin with the question word.
These conventions are the keel's (assumed there, not ruled).

## Seat

The seat is **a person who founds, directs or holds shares in a company under the Companies Law**: a founder, a director or other office-holder, or a shareholder, including a minority shareholder.
They ask what the Law lets them do, what it requires of them, what they may require of the company, and what can be done to them.

Why this seat: the sentences of the Law in lines 948-1684 are addressed to the company ("the company shall keep a register", "the company shall report to the Registrar"), to a general manager, an auditor, a director or a shareholder.
The person who needs an answer is the individual behind them: the founder who must see that the registered office and the registers exist, the director who must disclose a conviction or give notice and whose term can end, the shareholder who wants to inspect a document, ask for a copy, vote or bring a derivative claim.
So the questions are asked as that person asks them ("Must my company appoint a general manager?", "May I inspect this register?", "Does my term as director end if I am convicted?") and take the facts that person can state.

There is no seat for an official.
The Registrar, the court, the Securities Authority, the Minister, the company's auditor and its internal auditor appear only as what the person may expect of them or what they may do to the person: the Registrar must record a registered office, the court may order a director's term to expire, the internal auditor must carry out an urgent audit that the chairperson of the board, or of the audit committee, directs.
Where a provision addresses only such a body, layer 1 encodes what it decides and the seat asks only what it means to the person (what may be done to them, what they must be given, by when).

Two layers:
- **Layer 1** is the sixteen modules `ic2-p*.l4`, one per Part, chapter or sub-chapter of the range, written from the text alone, each rule citing its source lines; each relation is stated once, neutrally (see the Jural relations table).
- **Layer 2** is `ic2-seat.l4` (363 questions, each with `@export` and `@desc` in Hebrew then ` / ` and English, under `§§` headings in the Law's own order) and `ic2-cases-seat.l4` (1246 assertions, at least one per question).
  The seat never restates a rule: each question builds the records its rule expects from plain answers and calls the layer-1 rule.
  Where the text gives the person a claim, privilege, power or immunity, the question takes as inputs the facts about the other party on which it depends; where it also puts a duty or a liability on the person, the paired question is asked beside it.

How the seat layer was made is recorded under "Before this is worth relying on".

## Jural relations

One row per section or group of sections that creates a relation between two parties, in the order of the text; Hohfeld's correlatives pair the columns: a claim with a duty, a privilege with a no-right, a power with a liability, an immunity with a disability.
Layer 1 states each relation once, as a rule named for the relation and not for either party; the seat questions look at the same rule from the person's side (what I am owed, what I owe, what I may do, what may be done to me), taking the other party's facts as inputs.
A provision that creates no relation (a deleted paragraph, a Minister's regulation-making power left inert, a pure title) is listed with `none`.
The company is a party in most rows: it stands for the person who runs it, and the seat asks the question from that person's side.

## Fork register

**F1. "Within N days / months / years": is the last day inside the period?**
Occurs in 121(ג) and (ד) (three years), 123(ב), 123א(ב), 141, 158(א1), 159, 173(ה), 223 (fourteen days), 159 (ninety days), 196 (forty-five days), 197(3) (seventy-five days), 182(ב) (twenty-one and four days before the meeting), 172(ג) (six and nine months), 242 and 249א (three months), 245 (three years, six years), 249 (two years, one year), 161 and 226 (five years).
Readings: (a) the period "within N days of the event" includes the Nth day, so an act on day N is in time; (b) it ends the day before.
Taken: (a), in every rule: elapsed time AT MOST N (or, for a period that must not be shorter, AT LEAST N). The text never says "beginning with the day" or "not including the day", and (a) is the ordinary reading of "תוך N ימים" / "בתוך N ימים".
Effect: a notice given on the fourteenth day is in time; on the fifteenth it is late. Every threshold has a boundary test on both sides.

**F2. "One of the following" (אחד מאלה) in 121(ג), 239(ב) and 245(א1): cumulative or alternative?**
Readings: (a) paragraphs (1) and (2) are alternatives (either suffices); (b) both are needed.
Taken: (a). The text says "ובלבד שיתקיים אחד מאלה" (provided that one of these holds), then lists two paragraphs; paragraph (2) is a fallback for when the majority of paragraph (1) is missed but opposition is small.
Effect: tested (a resolution that fails paragraph (1) and meets paragraph (2) passes).
In 239(ב) the rule asks whether the directors were appointed validly by the general meeting, so in either alternative the meeting must have passed its resolution at all (`ההחלטה נתקבלה ברוב קולות באסיפה הכללית`); the opening words "ימונו על ידי האסיפה הכללית" say that the meeting appoints, and a resolution that failed appoints no one. Paragraph (2) does not repeat the point, and reading (a) is not taken to excuse it (tested both ways).
In 121(ג) the rule asks only whether the meeting may resolve, so paragraph (2) alone is enough there and the flag is read in paragraph (1) alone.

**F3. "The majority" in the general meeting (121(ג)(1), 239(ב)(1), 245(א1)(1)): what is it?**
Section 1 does not define an ordinary majority (KEEL-NOTES, item 13), and the Law uses the phrase in sections 20, 85 and 107, all in piece ic1.
Taken: the fact that the resolution passed by the majority the Law requires of the general meeting is a plain BOOLEAN input (`ההחלטה נתקבלה ברוב קולות באסיפה הכללית`), and the special rule is stated on top of it: the votes of the disinterested non-controlling shareholders must contain a majority, counted by the core's `מנין קולות` (for plus against, abstentions set aside, as the text itself says "לא יובאו בחשבון קולות הנמנעים").

**F4. "If no sum has yet been paid on account of the consideration fixed for the share – the amount not yet paid" (130(א)(1)(ב)).**
The words "טרם שולם … סכום כלשהו" can be read (a) as: some part of the consideration remains unpaid, so the unpaid amount is entered; or (b) as: nothing at all has been paid yet, and the (whole) amount is entered.
Taken: (a). The register shows the sum still owed; on reading (b) a partly paid share would show no balance, which defeats the point of recording the unpaid amount.
Effect: the detail `הסכום שטרם שולם על חשבון התמורה` is due whenever any part of the consideration is unpaid (tested). Reading (b) is a change of the one input `טרם שולם חלק מהתמורה שנקבעה למניה`.

**F5. "Without his consent" in 153(א): does it qualify the suspension as well as the ending of the term?**
The sentence reads: "The internal auditor's term shall not be ended without his consent and he shall not be suspended from his function, unless the board decided so after getting the audit committee's position and after he was given a reasonable opportunity to be heard".
Readings: (a) the consent limb qualifies only the first prohibition, so consent permits an ending of the term but a suspension needs the board's route in every case; (b) the board's route is the only way for both, consent being only a fact that the board will consider.
Taken: (a), the words "שלא בהסכמתו" sit inside the first clause only.
Effect: `סעיף 153(א) - מותר להפסיק את כהונת המבקר הפנימי` is true on consent alone; `סעיף 153(א) - מותר להשעות את המבקר הפנימי מתפקידו` is not (tested).

**F6. The figure in 158(א): 500,000 shekels, the Wikisource note, and the index.**
The Act's words are "500,000 שקלים חדשים", followed in the text file by an editorial note "{{ח:הערה|(מתואם לשנת 2005; בשנת 2026, 731,660 ש״ח)}}"; the same sentence says the sum is index-linked, rounded to the nearest ten shekels, and that the Minister publishes it each February in Reshumot.
Readings: (a) the constant is the Act's 500,000, with the sum as last published by the Minister an input; (b) the constant is the note's 731,660.
Taken: (a), as the keel's instruction 7 requires: the note is Wikisource's, dated to 2026, and not the Act.
Effect: with no published sum the limit is 500,000; with a published sum (e.g. 731,660) that sum is the limit (both tested at the boundary). The Minister's power to publish the sum is carried only as that input. The sum for a public-benefit company is the one in section 19(ג) of the Associations Law, a plain input.

**F7. An article that sets the preparation period above nine months (172(ג)).**
The text: statements are prepared within six months of the determining or special date, "or within another period fixed in the articles, provided that no period exceeding nine months be fixed".
Readings: (a) an over-limit article is void and the six-month default applies; (b) an over-limit article is void and the text says nothing about what then applies; (c) nine months applies.
Taken: (b), because the text states no consequence; the rule `סעיף 172(ג) - התקופה לעריכת הדוחות, בחודשים` returns NOTHING for an over-limit period and the "in time" rule then returns NOTHING (both tested).

**F8. The five routes of 197 and the three paragraphs of 194(ד), 195: alternatives.**
Section 197 says "if one of the following holds" and lists five paragraphs; 195 says "in one of the following ways" and lists three; 194(ד) says "if one of the following holds" and lists two.
Taken: alternatives in all three (the same reading as F2).
Effect: each route of 197 alone lets the plaintiff file (tested one by one); a company may take any one of the ways of 195 (tested).

**F9. Who must disclose under 225(א): a candidate for any company, or only for a public or bond company?**
The sentence is "A candidate for director shall disclose to the appointer": it names no kind of company.
The bar it refers to (226) is limited to public companies and private bond companies; the disclosure counts the period "in which he is prohibited from serving as director under section 226", which is a stretch of time that runs from the judgment whatever company is asked about.
Readings: (a) the duty reaches a candidate for every company, while the period runs; (b) it reaches only a candidate for a public or bond company, the only ones where the bar bites.
Taken: (a). The rules of 225(א) take no company.
Effect: a candidate for a private company that is not a bond company, convicted of a listed offence two years ago, must disclose it (tested), though the bar of 226 does not stop his appointment there (tested). Reading (b) adds the company to the three rules of 225(א) and requires a public or bond company.

**F10. The stay of 226(ד): "the expiry of the term under this section".**
Section 226 contains a bar on appointment and no expiry of a term; the expiry on conviction is in 232.
Readings: (a) the stay reaches the bar on appointment (226) and the expiry on conviction (232), the only expiry that rests on 226; (b) literally, it reaches the bar on appointment only, and an expiry "under this section" has no object.
Taken: (a). The record of a conviction has one field, that a stay is in force, read by `סעיף 226 - ההרשעה מונעת מינוי` and `סעיף 232 - הדירקטור חייב להודיע לחברה על ההרשעה`.
Effect: a stayed conviction neither bars the appointment nor obliges the director to notify the company (both tested). Reading (b) removes the stay from the 232 duty. The date and terms of the stay are the court's: the input says only whether it is in force.

**F11. How long the bar of 226א lasts.**
The text says that a person on whom the enforcement committee imposed a measure barring him is "not appointed to a company in which he may not serve under that decision"; it names no period.
Readings: (a) the bar lasts as long as the decision's own period (the prohibition "under that decision" ends when the decision's period ends); (b) it is permanent.
Taken: (a); the record of an enforcement measure has the field `טרם חלפה התקופה שקבעה ועדת האכיפה המינהלית בהחלטתה`, which also serves 225(א)(3) and 232א, the two places where the text names the period.
Effect: a measure whose period has passed does not bar (tested). Reading (b) drops the field from `סעיף 226א - המועמד מנוע מלהתמנות כדירקטור בחברה מחמת אמצעי אכיפה`.

**F12. "Despite section 240" in 245(א).**
The company "may, despite the provisions of section 240, appoint him for two further periods of three years".
Readings: (a) the whole of section 240 is set aside for the further appointment: no residence, expertise, tie, conflict or cross-appointment rule is asked again; (b) only the bar on ties in 240(ב), which an external director's own service would otherwise raise, is set aside.
Taken: (a), the literal words; the conditions that may stop him during service remain through 240(ו), 245א, 246 and 247.
Effect: `סעיף 245(א) - סעיף 240 אינו מונע מינוי לתקופה נוספת` is TRUE and the rule `סעיף 245(א) - אפשר למנות את הדירקטור החיצוני לתקופה נוספת` reads only the number of further periods already served (tested). Reading (b) adds the rules of 240(א) and 240(ג) to (ה) to that rule.

**F13. The one-year period of 249(א) for "a relative who is neither his spouse nor his child".**
The first sentence bars benefits to the former external director, his spouse and his child; the last words give a shorter period, one year, "for a relative who is neither his spouse nor his child", a person the list before it does not name.
Readings: (a) the bar covers such a relative for one year; (b) the list is the whole of the bar and the one-year words have no object.
Taken: (a), the reading in which every word of the sentence does some work.
Effect: `סעיף 249(א) - מספר השנים מתום הכהונה שבהן אסור להעניק טובת הנאה` is 1 for `קרוב אחר של הדירקטור החיצוני לשעבר` and 2 for the other three recipients (tested). On reading (b) the fourth recipient is never barred.

**F14. "As long as no auditor has been appointed to it under sub-section (ב)" in 159(א): any appointment, or the Registrar's only?**
The sentence says the notice to the Registrar does not detract from the company's duty to appoint an auditor "as long as no auditor has been appointed to it under sub-section (ב)", and the next clause contemplates that the company itself appoints one after giving notice.
Readings: (a) the duty ends when any auditor is appointed, whether by the company or by the Registrar under (ב); (b) it ends only on the Registrar's appointment under (ב), so a company that appointed its own auditor would still owe the duty.
Taken: (a), since (b) would leave the company under a duty it has already met, and the sentence that follows asks the company to report its own appointment.
Effect: `סעיף 159(א) - חובת המינוי נמשכת` is false once an auditor is appointed by anyone (tested).
Reading (b) adds a fact to that rule's inputs: that the appointment was the Registrar's.

**F15. "Additional classes of companies" the Minister may set in 240(א).**
The sentence lets a public company or a private bond company whose shares or bonds were offered or listed abroad appoint external directors who are not resident in Israel, and adds that the Minister may set "סוגים נוספים של חברות" in which this is possible.
Readings: (a) the Minister's classes are further companies, in addition to those two, so a company of such a class may appoint a non-resident whatever its kind; (b) they are further classes within the public and bond companies, which are the only companies that have external directors (239(א)).
Taken: (a), the literal words: "additional" to the case just described, with no limit to it.
Effect: `סעיף 240(א) - היחיד כשיר להתמנות כדירקטור חיצוני מבחינת התושבות` reads the Minister's-class fact as a limb of its own, beside residence and beside the offering-abroad limb of the public and bond companies (tested: a private company that is not a bond company with the class fact true admits a non-resident, and with only the offering abroad true does not). Reading (b) puts that fact inside the public-and-bond limb.

- The core's independent-director record `עובדות לדירקטור בלתי תלוי` takes the input `מתקיימים תנאי הכשירות לפי סעיף 240(ב) עד (ו)`; the rule that supplies it is `סעיף 240(ב)-(ו) - מתקיימים תנאי הכשירות` in the module of sections 239 to 244.
The core's `YMD` shim for dates was used; no other shim was needed.

## What the text turns out to say

Numbered findings that are not forks (the forks are in the Fork register).
Each ends "Asserted." where a test or a seat case asserts it.

1. **A conviction abroad ends a term only through the court.** Section 232 makes a director's term expire on his notice of a conviction under 226(א)(1) or (א1); it does not name the foreign conviction of 226(א)(2).
   That conviction bars an appointment (226) and is a ground for the court under 233(2), not a ground that ends the term by itself. Asserted.
2. **An order opening proceedings ends the term even where it no longer bars the appointment.** Section 227(א) bars an individual "as long as he is not discharged", but 228(א)(4) ends a term when the order is given, with no condition of discharge. Asserted.
3. **Resignation and expiry by notice sit beside 245(ב).** Section 245(ב) says an external director is dismissed or his term ended only as 233, 246 and 247 provide, yet any director may resign (229) and an external director's term expires on his own notice (245א, listed in 228(א)(5)). The Law does not reconcile them; each rule is encoded as written. Asserted.
4. **249ג names 240(ו) and no other part of 240.** An independent director's qualifications come through the definition in section 1 (conditions of 240(ב) to (ו) and the audit committee's approval) and 249ב, not through 249ג; 249ג adds only 240(ו), 244, 245א, 246, 247, 249 and 249א(א). Asserted.
5. **The two thresholds of two percent point in opposite directions.** Under 239(ב)(2) the votes against among the disinterested non-controlling shareholders may be at most two percent; under 245(א1)(1)(ב) the votes in support must exceed two percent. At exactly two percent the first is met and the second is not. Asserted.
6. **237(ג) lifts one bar only.** In a private company that is not a bond company, one who serves as director or alternate may be an alternate if the articles so provide, but the first bar of 237(ב) (not qualified to be a director) stays. Asserted.
7. **226(ב) reaches the offences of 226(א) only.** The court's power to shorten the bar speaks of "the offences of sub-section (א)"; for an offence it found under (א1) the period it set governs. Asserted.
8. **Where the text leaves a question open the question returns NOTHING, and a case asserts it.**
   The Law does not say: who manages a company with no general manager, for any company other than a private company that is not a bond company (119(ב)); which document is the better evidence when the register and the certificate do not contradict (133(ב)); who is the internal auditor's organisational supervisor, and which body approves his work plan, when neither the articles nor the board named the chairperson or the general manager (148) or a permitted body (149);
   when the financial statements are due where the articles set a period above nine months (172(ג), fork F7); the record date of a dividend earlier than the day of the decision (182(א)); the part of the court fee a plaintiff pays on filing a derivative claim, where the Minister's rate is not supplied (199(א));
   when the term of a first director, of a director appointed by the general meeting, or a later start of a term runs, where the articles provide otherwise or do not permit it (220, 221, 222); whether a corporation may serve as director of a public company (235); who may dismiss a director, and how, where the articles provide otherwise (230); how many external directors a private company that is not a bond company needs (239(א)); and whether a further term may be approved under 245(א1) in a company that is not public. Asserted.
9. **The Minister's regulations are outside the inputs.** Wikisource notes in the range record published regulations at sections 121, 126, 141, 171, 182, 240 (three notes), 241 and 244; their figures and outcomes are inputs, or are taken as the Act states them, and are never read from the regulations.

## What the stable binary does not accept

Hebrew compiled without any change: backticked Hebrew names, `@export` and `@desc` in Hebrew followed by ` / ` and English, `§` headings in Hebrew, and `@ref` over `-- src:N |` quote lines carrying the `{{ח:ת}}` template were all accepted by the stable 1.6.98, and `srcrefs.py check --refs` reported 0 problems on them.
Nothing in the range needed a construct the stable build rejects.
Workarounds that were needed, each loud (an error at `l4 check`):
- `fromMaybe X r's f` does not parse; the genitive binds weaker than application, so the argument is written `(r's f)`.
- An input named like a constructor of the core (for example `החברה` or `הדירקטוריון`, constructors of `צד`) clashes with it; inputs are named by phrases, never by a party noun.
- A comparison in a rule whose input was named differently in the signature and the body gave an ambiguity error; the names were made the same.
Silent traps that return a wrong answer with no diagnostic, and how the modules avoid them:
- `NOT a AND b` parses as `NOT (a AND b)`: every `NOT` is parenthesised.
- Positional arguments of the same type can be swapped without any error: records are used for rules with more than three facts, and every test states the order of its arguments in a comment.
- `l4 run` prints each satisfied assertion twice; counts of satisfied assertions are half the number of `assertion satisfied` lines.
Time: `l4 run ic2-seat.l4` takes about fifty seconds and `l4 run ic2-cases-seat.l4` several minutes, because both load all sixteen modules; `check.sh` takes correspondingly long.

## Before this is worth relying on

1. **How the seat layer was made: four forks wrote most of it, against the brief's "no subagents".**
   Layer 1 (the sixteen `ic2-p*.l4` modules), all layer-1 tests, the coverage table, the jural and cross-reference tables and the fork register are the piece agent's own work, written in one context.
   Layer 2 is two files, `ic2-seat.l4` (the questions) and `ic2-cases-seat.l4` (the cases).
   The brief says "no subagents", and the piece agent nevertheless spawned four forks of itself for layer 2; this was a deviation, reported to the lead on 2026-10-25 before the final report.
   Fork A wrote the questions and cases for sections 119-145, fork B for 146-175, fork C for 176-224 and fork D for 239-249ג; the piece agent wrote the slice for 224א-238 and assembled the two files from the five slices.
   The lead ruled that the fork-written slices stay, on condition that the piece agent read every question and every case against the text itself and reported the deviation here and in the final report.
   The lead measured the forks from their transcripts at 37.5M cache-read tokens in all, with no reads outside the sandbox.
   The review was done: the piece agent read every question and every case of the four fork slices against the section it answers, checking each expected value against the words of the section and not against the rule, and ran these mechanical checks on the whole (every call in a question matches the rule's argument order and types, every question has `@export`, a gloss and an `@desc` for each input, every question has a case, no name is declared twice, and both files run clean).
   The review did not re-derive the expected values by a second independent route, and it cannot show how a fork reached an expected value, only that the value agrees with the text as the piece agent reads it.
   It found one defect in the forks' own work: in fork D's question on 240(ו) two arguments were passed in the wrong order (both BOOLEAN, so it ran and gave the right answers on the cases); fixed.
