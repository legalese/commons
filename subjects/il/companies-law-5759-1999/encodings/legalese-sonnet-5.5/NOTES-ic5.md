## Seat

The seat is a person who founds, directs or holds shares in a company under the Companies Law (a founder, a director or officer, a shareholder including a minority shareholder), asking what the Law lets them do, what it requires of them, what they may require of the company, and what can be done to them.
It is the seat the brief fixed for the whole Law, and Commons requires every encoding to be written from the seat of the individual to whom the law is applied, never an official's (Commons ruling of 2026-10-18).
For this chapter the person is a founder or director of a public-benefit company or fund, a shareholder in one, or a donor to a fund; a few questions are put to a person who wants to be, or is, an external examiner (345כד3, 345כד4), because a founder, director or shareholder may be one and the Law burdens them in that role.
Provisions that address only an official are encoded in layer 1 for what they decide, and appear in layer 2 only for what they mean to the person: what may be done to them, what they must be given, and by when.
There is no seat for the Registrar of Endowments, the Registrar of Companies, the Attorney General, the Minister or the court.
Layer 2 is two files.
`ic5-seat.l4` has the questions: 210 questions under 52 `§§` section headings in the text's order, each with `@export` (Hebrew, ` / `, an English gloss) and, on every input, `@desc` worded as the person is asked.
`ic5-cases-seat.l4` has the worked cases: 700 assertions, every question at least once, with the facts a real person in the seat would state; where the Law is silent the expected value is the explicit not-covered value.
The conventions are these.
- A question takes plain inputs, one per fact the person can state, and calls the layer-1 rules; it never restates a rule. Where a layer-1 rule needs a fact that another rule computes (that 345יב(ב) lifts paragraphs (1) and (3), that the Registrar of Endowments answered in time, that the reports behind an affidavit are as required), the question calls that rule on the plain inputs instead of asking again.
- A figure or condition the Law leaves to the Minister is asked as "did the Minister set ...", with a second input for the figure; where a layer-1 rule takes a `MAYBE`, the question takes a yes/no input and the figure as two inputs and builds the `MAYBE` in its body.
- A judgment the Law hands to an official or the court is an input worded as that official's act ("did the Registrar of Endowments confirm ...", "was the court persuaded ..."), and is never guessed.
- Hohfeld pairs: where the text gives the person a claim, privilege, power or immunity, the question takes the facts about the other party; where it also puts a duty or liability on the person, the paired question is there. The pairs are in the `## Jural relations` table; the main ones are 345ב(ב) (the Registrar of Endowments's 45 days; the Registrar of Companies's disability), 345ג (inspection; registration on the company's application), 345יד (who may file and defend a derivative claim), 345יח (a request for an investigation; the Registrar's power; the costs and security that may be laid on the person), 345כח (the court may order the office-holder to return assets; the shareholder may ask; the Registrar of Endowments's notice) and 345כד1 (what a supervisor may demand or enter; what the person may insist on: the badge and the certificate).
- A question marked "Situational" in its comment is put only to a person in that situation: a company that resolved to change its articles (345ב(ג)), or an applicant to be an external examiner (345כד3(א)).
- Every question name ends with the section its rule comes from, `(סעיף N)`, so that it is specific to what it decides; the `@export` text is the name followed by a question mark, with an English gloss after ` / `.
Provisions with no question, and why (the same list heads `ic5-seat.l4`): the 19 regulation-making and similar powers marked `inert` in the coverage table; the twelve layer-1 rules whose subject is an official's duty or choice towards another official, the court or the Knesset (345ב(ה), 345ג1, 345טז(ב) and (ג), which are asked together, 345יח(ב) last limb, 345כב(ב), 345כד1(א), 345כד2(ז), 345כד3(ב), 345כד3(ו), 345כד5, 345לו(א)); and 345כה, repealed.

## Jural relations

One row per provision that creates a Hohfeldian relation (a claim and the duty correlative to it, a privilege and the no-right, a power and the liability to it, an immunity and the disability), naming the party who holds the right, privilege, power or immunity, the party who bears the correlative, and the layer-1 rule that encodes the relation once, neutrally.
Where the text puts a power in an official's hands, the person's side is the liability to it; the official never has a seat.
A provision that creates no such relation (a definition, a counting rule, a title) is listed once with `none`.
The officials here are the Registrar of Endowments, the Registrar of Companies, the court, the Attorney General and the Minister; the keel's `צד` has the Registrar of Companies (`הרשם`), the court (`בית המשפט`) and the Minister (`השר`), and `ic5-parties.l4` adds the Registrar of Endowments and the Attorney General, which the keel lacks.

## Fork register

Each entry gives the readings seen, the one taken, and the text that licenses each.
No authority (a court decision, a commentary, the official text, the Interpretation Law) was consulted: every entry rests on the wording of the Hebrew text deposited here and on the sections named, and each is open to a domain expert.
Where I looked and found no ambiguity, the last entries say where.

**F1. How a period "within N days from the day ..." is counted (src:2530, 2543, 2665, 2667, 2688, 2710, 2724 and every other time limit of the chapter).**
Readings: (a) the day of the event is not counted, and the last day of the period is the N-th day after it, which is inside the period; (b) the day of the event is day 1, so the last day is one day earlier; (c) the N-th day is outside the period.
Taken: (a). The text says only "בתוך 45 ימים מיום ש…" (within 45 days from the day that …) and states no counting rule; the usual counting rule is in the Interpretation Law, which is not among the inputs, so this is the encoder's assumption, stated rather than found.
Effect: a deadline is the event date plus N days (`PLUS N`); a person who acts on the N-th day is in time; a person who must wait for a period to run out (src:2543) may be acted against from day N+1. Tested at N and N+1.

**F2. "A majority of the directors and the general manager" in the affidavit of section 345ב(ג) (src:2531).**
Readings: (a) a majority of the directors, and also the general manager; (b) a majority of the set made up of the directors and the general manager together.
Taken: (a). "מרבית" (a majority of) attaches to "הדירקטורים" and the general manager follows as a separate signatory; reading (b) would let the affidavit pass with every director signing and the general manager not, or the reverse.
Effect: `סעיף 345ב(ג) - התצהיר חתום כנדרש` is true only with more than half of the directors and the general manager. A "majority" is more than half (exactly half of four is not a majority; tested).

**F3. Whether the duty to apply to the Registrar of Endowments survives registration (section 345ב(ז), src:2535).**
Readings: (a) the duty is to obtain registration in the register, so it ends once the company is registered by any route (including the Registrar of Endowments's own initiative under 345ג(ד)); (b) the duty to apply stands for every public-benefit company that did not declare, registered or not.
Taken: (a). The sentence ties the duty to the purpose of registration "בפנקס", and the Registrar's later duty to correct the certificate presupposes the registration has happened.
Effect: the input `החברה כבר נרשמה בפנקס` switches the duty off.

**F4. A company that responds in time to the Registrar of Endowments's notice of intention to register it (section 345ג(ד)(2), src:2543).**
The text says what happens if the company does not respond within 45 days (it is registered) and says nothing about a company that does respond.
Readings: (a) the Registrar of Endowments may not register it by this route (a response blocks); (b) he may register it anyway; (c) the text does not say.
Taken: (c). The result type has a constructor `הדין אינו קובע מה דינה של חברה שהגיבה להודעה במועד`, returned and asserted, and the encoding does not guess.

**F5. Whether approval under section 345ה can validate a change of the ban on distributing profits or a provision contrary to section 345ז (src:2554-2555).**
Readings: (a) approval "in accordance with this section" exists only for changing purposes, by the route of (b) to (e), so those two kinds of change never have effect; (b) any change, including those two, has effect once it is "approved in accordance with this section".
Taken: (a). The sentence bars the two kinds of change in terms ("אינה רשאית לשנות … ולא תכלול …"), and subsection (b) opens only a route for replacing, adding and removing purposes.
Effect: `סעיף 345ה(א) - אין תוקף לשינוי התקנון` is true for those two kinds whatever the approval input says (tested).

**F6. "A small-value and reasonable gift given as customary in the circumstances" (section 345ז(ב), src:2570).**
The text fixes no amount and no test of reasonableness. Taken: each limb (small-value, reasonable, customary) is a plain Boolean input; the rule is their conjunction. Reading the three as one concept ("small and reasonable according to custom") gives the same conjunction.

**F7. Who decides that a purpose is a "close purpose" (section 345ה(ג)(1), src:2558).**
Readings: (a) the company asserts closeness when it applies, and the Registrar of Endowments may disagree and tell the company, whereupon the court route of (2) applies; (b) the Registrar of Endowments alone decides.
Taken: (a). The text says "if the purpose … is close … registration by the Registrar of Endowments is needed; if he thought it was not close, he informs the company, and it acts as in (2)", which presupposes a first view by the company.
Effect: two inputs, `המטרה … קרובה …` and `רשם ההקדשות סבר שהמטרה אינה מטרה קרובה והודיע על כך לחברה`; the court route applies unless the first is true and the second false (tested on all four combinations). For a purpose to be removed the first input stands for "the articles have a purpose close to the one removed" ((2) puts the removal case under the court when there is none).

**F8. "At least once a year" (section 345ח(ה)(6א), src:2584).**
Readings: (a) a review is overdue once more than 12 months have passed since the last one; (b) a review is due in every calendar year; (c) due every 12 months, on the 12-month day itself.
Taken: (a), the plainest reading of "from time to time and at the least once a year" for a duty that runs from a last review. The text says nothing about a first review, so the duty is stated only for a committee that has set criteria and has a last review to count from.
Effect: at 12 months the review is not yet overdue; at 13 it is (tested at 0, 12 and 13).

**F9. "Turnover" when no financial year has passed (section 345ח(ט), src:2595).**
The definition averages the receipts of the last three financial years, or of the years that have passed since the company was set up if fewer than three.
When no financial year has passed there is nothing to average and the text says nothing. Taken: the explicit not-covered value, `NOTHING`, asserted. A company with 1 or 2 years averages over those years (tested).

**F10. "A transaction in a minimal sum" (section 345יב(ב), src:2619).**
The text fixes no amount, and no regulation fixing one is among the inputs. Taken: the two limbs of the exemption are plain Boolean inputs (`העסקה היא בסכום מזערי`; `תנאי העסקה זהים לעסקאות אחרות של החברה עם הציבור הרחב`), ORed. A reading that "minimal sum" is a threshold the Minister may fix has no support in this section.

**F11. What section 345יב(ב) lifts (src:2619, with src:2614-2616).**
Subsection (b) says "paragraphs (a)(1) and (3) do not apply" to a minimal transaction or one on ordinary terms.
Readings: (a) paragraph (2) (an extraordinary transaction with a relative, shareholder or promoter needs the court) stays in force even for such a transaction; (b) subsection (b) was meant to lift all three.
Taken: (a), on the words: (b) names (1) and (3) and omits (2).
Effect: an extraordinary transaction with a relative, shareholder or promoter needs the court even if it is in a minimal sum (tested); a transaction with a director or committee member in a minimal sum needs nothing further under (a), because paragraph (2) does not cover them.

**F12. The four defences of section 345יג(ב) (src:2625-2629).**
Readings: (a) the office-holder escapes if he proves any one of the four ("אחד מאלה"), and the burden of proving it is his; (b) all four are required.
Taken: (a), on the words "אלא אם כן הוכיח נושא המשרה אחד מאלה" (unless the office-holder proved one of these). The inputs are named for what he proved, not for the facts, so the burden is visible: a defence not proved is a defence not available.
Effect: for an unregistered company the presumption of breach holds unless at least one defence is proved (tested one at a time).

**F13. A transfer of a share without consideration (section 345טו(a), src:2637).**
The subsection bars transferring a share "בתמורה" (for consideration) without the court's advance approval. It says nothing of a transfer without consideration (a gift).
Readings: (a) such a transfer is not barred by this subsection; (b) it is barred as well, as the purpose of the section would suggest.
Taken: (a), on the words. The rule is named `סעיף 345טו(א) אינו אוסר את העברת המניה` (subsection (a) does not bar the transfer) so that it answers only what the subsection decides; whether a gift is otherwise limited (the articles, subsection (b), other law) it does not say.
Effect: a transfer without consideration comes out true whatever the other inputs say (tested).

**F14. Who may ask for an investigation (section 345יח(a), src:2651).**
Readings: (a) the Registrar of Endowments's power turns on the reasonable basis alone, since he may also act on his own initiative; the list of requesters (a shareholder with at least 25% of the capital or of the votes, the audit committee, the Attorney General) says whose request the section names; (b) the power exists only on a named requester's request or his own motion with the same conditions.
Taken: (a). `סעיף 345יח(א) - רשם ההקדשות רשאי לחקור בעניני החברה` takes only the reasonable basis; `סעיף 345יח(א) - הבקשה לחקירה היא בקשה שהסעיף מונה` says whether a request is one the section names. "At least 25 percent" includes exactly 25% (tested).

**F15. "Two months" after the determining date of 31 December (section 345כ(a)(1), src:2665).**
The affidavit must also rest on a reviewed financial statement "if more than two months have passed since the determining date".
Readings: (a) two calendar months from 31 December end on the last day of February (28 or 29), so a statement is needed from 1 March; (b) 31 December plus two months is 2 March (the days run over); (c) 60 days, so from 1 March in a common year and 29 February in a leap year.
Taken: (a), with `add months` clamping the day to the end of the month, as the library does; the text says "months" not days.
Effect: filing on 28 February 2026 (and on 29 February 2024) needs no reviewed statement; filing on 1 March needs one (tested for 2026 and for the leap year 2024). Reading (b) would move the first day to 3 March.

**F16. Leftover assets when the articles' provisions exist but the persuasion fails (section 345כא(b), src:2673).**
The text applies the articles to the assets "provided that" the court (or, in voluntary winding up, the Registrar of Endowments) is persuaded; and sends the assets to a purpose the court fixes "in the absence of such provisions or if it is impossible to act on them".
Readings: (a) provisions that do not win the persuasion are not "such provisions", so the fallback applies; (b) the text leaves that case open.
Taken: (a), so the result type needs no extra constructor; the first branch needs the provisions, that they can be followed, and the persuasion of whoever the winding-up route names. An asset fixed in advance to return to the transferor (subsection (c)) comes first.
Effect: `סעיף 345כא(ב) - מה נעשה בנכסים שנותרו לאחר פירעון החובות` returns the court-fixed purpose when the persuasion fails (tested).

**F17. The Wikisource "או" inside paragraph (4) of section 345כד3(a) (src:2721).**
The deposited text reads "הוא לא הוכרז פסול דין {{ח:הערה|או}} לא ניתן לגביו צו לפתיחת הליכים ומתקיימים לגביו הליכי חדלות פירעון כל עוד לא הופטר, ואם הוא תאגיד – גם לא ניתן לגביו צו פירוק". The word "או" sits inside a Wikisource `{{ח:הערה|…}}` template: an editorial insertion, not the Act's text.
Readings: (a) the paragraph is a list of two disqualifications (declared legally incompetent; insolvency proceedings against him not yet ended), each of which bars the approval, and for a corporation also a winding-up order; (b) the editorial "or" makes the paragraph satisfied if either negative holds, so that a person declared incompetent but not insolvent would qualify.
Taken: (a). The paragraph is one of five conditions that must all be met ("כל אלה"), and the plain sense of two "he was not …" clauses under "all of these" is that he is neither. Reading (b) would make the paragraph nearly vacuous.
Effect: `סעיף 345כד3(א) - רשם ההקדשות רשאי לתת אישור לשמש בודק חיצוני` is false if the applicant was declared incompetent, or has insolvency proceedings pending, or (for a corporation) a winding-up order; each alone is tested.

**F18. What "if he thinks it involves a public interest" qualifies (section 345כו, last limb, src:2753).**
The Attorney General "may open any proceeding under this Law concerning a public-benefit company and appear and argue in such a proceeding if he thinks that it involves a public interest".
Readings: (a) the condition qualifies only appearing and arguing (a proceeding he did not open); (b) it qualifies opening a proceeding as well.
Taken: (a). "In such a proceeding" (בהליך כאמור) points back to the proceeding already described, and "it involves" refers to that proceeding; the power to open comes first with no condition.
Effect: two rules, one needing only that the proceeding concerns a public-benefit company, the other also his view of the public interest (tested).

**F19. Rounding to the nearest NIS 1,000 (section 345לו(b), src:2828).**
The text says the updated amounts "are rounded to the nearest amount that is a multiple of 1,000" and does not say which way an exact half (a remainder of NIS 500) goes.
Readings: (a) half up; (b) half down; (c) half to even.
Taken: (a), the ordinary reading of "nearest" for amounts of money, written out as `FLOOR (x / 1000 + 0.5) * 1000`.
Effect: 123,500 becomes 124,000 (tested); under (b) or (c) it would be 123,000 or 124,000. No update is applied in this encoding: the amounts stay the Act's own.

**F20. "Donations whose value is lower than NIS 20,000" from a single donor (section 345לז(b), src:2832).**
Readings: (a) the value is the total the fund received from that donor in the financial year, and a donor giving exactly NIS 20,000 in total is allowed; (b) each donation must be at least NIS 20,000.
Taken: (a). The text says "from a single donor donations" in the plural, and the donor count in the same subsection runs by financial year.
Effect: the rule takes one number, the value received from the donor in the year; 20,000 passes and 19,999 fails (tested).

**F21. How section 345לח(a)(1) and (2)(ב) fit together (src:2837, 2839).**
Paragraph (1) says that until the asset is sold it is not counted in the value of the fund's assets for the minimum distribution; paragraph (2)(ב) says that if the board extended the period, then in the additional period the asset's value is treated as double the appraisal amount.
Readings: (a) in the first period the asset counts as nothing; in the additional period it counts at double its appraisal (paragraph (2)(ב) is the exception to (1) and a deterrent against delay); (b) the double value is only a ceiling on what is not counted.
Taken: (a). Reading (b) leaves paragraph (2)(ב) with no effect on any calculation.
Effect: `סעיף 345לח - הערך שנחשב לנכס שהתקבל כתרומה לענין שווי נכסי הקרן` returns 0 in the first period and twice the appraisal in the additional period; an unsold asset after the additional period ended is outside the section, and the answer is `NOTHING` (tested).

**F22. The audit committee's confirmation and the conditions it confirms (section 345מב(a), "independent director" in a fund, src:2883-2888).**
The definition says an independent director is an individual "for whom the audit committee confirmed that the following eligibility conditions hold", and then lists the conditions (not a relative, no affiliation, no conflict, expertise).
Readings: (a) the person is independent only if the committee confirmed and the conditions in fact hold; (b) the confirmation alone makes the person independent, the conditions being what the committee must be satisfied of.
Taken: (a), so a confirmation that the facts contradict does not make a director independent, and a person who meets every condition but was never confirmed is not independent yet. Reading (b) would turn every condition into an input nobody checks.

6. Section 345ה(ג): a purpose that is "close" goes to the Registrar of Endowments for registration, unless he tells the company he does not think it close, in which case, like a purpose that is not close, it needs the court's approval. Asserted.
7. Section 345ו: an after-the-fact approval of an act beyond the purposes needs both the general meeting and the court; either alone is not enough. Asserted.
8. Section 345ז(b): the small-gift exception fixes no amount ("small-value", "reasonable", "customary" are all left open), so each is a plain input (fork F6). Asserted.
9. Section 345ח(ה)(6א): criteria the committee set in advance must be reviewed "at least once a year"; the review is overdue once more than 12 months have passed since the last one, so exactly 12 months is still in time (fork F8). Asserted.
10. Section 345ח(ט): "turnover" is an average over up to three financial years; a company with no financial year behind it has no turnover figure, and the rule returns `NOTHING` rather than zero (fork F9). Asserted.
11. Section 345ט(א): the duty to appoint an internal auditor arises only above NIS 10 million (or the Minister's higher sum); a turnover of exactly NIS 10,000,000 is not above it, and NIS 10,000,001 is. Asserted.
12. Section 345י(א): an employee or paid service provider may sit on the board only within the Minister's provisions for the class of company, never as chair, and up to exactly a quarter of the board (two of eight is allowed, three of eight is not). Asserted.
13. Section 345י(א): "control" is presumed at 25% or more of the issued capital or of the voting rights, and a person and his relative are one person, so a 20% holder and a 5% holder who are relatives together hold 25% and control, while 20% and 4% do not. Asserted.
14. Section 345יב(ב) lifts paragraphs (1) and (3) of 345יב(א) for a transaction in a minimal sum or on the terms of the company's dealings with the public, but not paragraph (2): an extraordinary transaction with a director's relative still needs the court's approval however small it is (fork F11). Asserted.
15. Section 345יב(א)(1): a director's paid services barred by 345י(א), and an engagement on the terms of his office, are not "transactions" for paragraph (1), so they need no further approval under it. Asserted.

19. Section 345יח(א): a shareholder's request for an investigation counts if he holds at least 25% of the issued capital or at least 25% of the voting rights, and exactly 25% is enough (fork F14); the Registrar of Endowments's own power to investigate turns on the reasonable basis alone. Asserted.

## What the stable binary does not accept

Build 1.6.98 of `l4`, run as the stable binary (target dialect `main`), over 33 modules (the keel's six, this piece's 27).
The items below were each seen on this run, either in the 33 modules or in a small probe file I wrote and then deleted; where I am only repeating the brief I say so.
Accepted, in every module: Hebrew in backticked names (rules, types, constructors, inputs, questions), in `@export` and `@desc` lines followed by ` / ` and an English gloss, in `@ref` lines with a Hebrew label and a `src:N` locator, and in `§` and `§§` headings; `IF c THEN JUST x ELSE NOTHING` as a parenthesised argument; `EQUALS (JUST (YMD 2029 12 31))` and `EQUALS NOTHING` in an `#ASSERT`; rule names containing parentheses, digits and a hyphen.
Not accepted, or accepted with a misleading message:
- A multi-word type name written without backticks after `IS A` or `GIVETH A` (the probe `GIVEN k IS A a kind`). The binary reports "the arities of the types do not match" and "could not find a definition for the identifier a", which does not say that a backtick is missing. Loud: a compile error.
- `length` on a list: "I could not find a definition for the identifier length". `count` is the name. Loud.
- The same rule name defined in two modules imported together: "There are multiple definitions for the identifier ... and I do not have sufficient information to make a choice between them." The probe was two one-line modules each defining `the limit`. Loud, but only at the use. Every rule here is named `סעיף N(...) - ...` for that reason.
- Not an error, though I had expected one: the same constructor name in two different enumerations is accepted, both in one file and across two imported modules, and `CONSIDER ... WHEN` picks the one the type decides. I still gave every enumeration constructor a distinctive suffix, because the brief asks for names that stay unique after the merge, and the keel's `צד` already holds `אדם אחר`, `בית המשפט` and others.
- Likewise an input with the same name as a constructor (the 345לט(ד) case, where I renamed the input) was accepted in a probe, with `WHEN` choosing the constructor and `THEN` the input. I did not keep the clash, but I do not claim the binary rejects it.
Not tried again, because the brief records them as rejected and nothing here needed them: `REFUSE`, `#ASSERT REFUSED`, `BECAUSE`, `TYPICALLY`, `SET OF`, `EVERY`, `@lang`, `@nlg`, and a section-level `GIVEN`.
`YMD` is not in the stable prelude; the keel defines it, and this piece does not redefine it.
A failed assertion shows as a `DiagnosticSeverity_Error` ("assertion failed", seen once, on a seat case I had written wrongly and corrected to the text); a refusal is a warning, per the brief, and none occurred. `check.sh` counts both.
`srcrefs.py` in strict mode needs each block's `@ref` locators to cover the quote run of its own block: a rule that combined two subsections (345טז(ב) and (ג)) needed its own quote run to pass. Loud.
Nothing Hebrew was rejected by the binary or by `srcrefs.py`.

## Before this is worth relying on

- A reader who knows Israeli company and charity law should read this against the official text (Sefer HaChukim 5759, p 189, and the later amending Acts as published in Reshumot). The source here is the Hebrew Wikisource raw page, an unofficial consolidation; nothing in these notes is verified against the official text, and the two editorial slips found in it (forks F17 and F23) suggest there may be others.
- The 23 forks are readings I took and wrote down with the words that license them; none rests on a court decision, a commentary or the Interpretation Law, none of which was consulted. The ones that change answers most are F1 (the counting of days: every deadline of the chapter), F2 (the affidavit's majority), F5 (what an approval can validate), F11 (what 345יב(ב) lifts), F12 (the burden of the defences), F16 (leftover assets), F20 and F22 (the donor figures).
- Every judgment the text hands to the Registrar of Endowments, the court, the Attorney General or the Minister is an input; the encoding says what follows if the judgment is made, never whether it will be.
- The Minister's regulations and orders (the pay limits of 345י(ב), the expense cap of 345יא, the fees of 345כז, the exemptions of 345לא1 and 345לב, the donor-count rules of 345לז(ג), the further rules of 345לט(ו), 345מד and 345מה(ג)) are not in the inputs, and the regulations the Wikisource notes name are not encoded; an answer that depends on one needs it added as an input.
- The amounts and percentages are the Act's own as the text states them; an update under 345לו(א) would change them, and no update is among the inputs.
- The Second and Third Schedules, the Companies Ordinance, the Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law and the other laws named in the cross-reference table are outside the inputs; what they provide is a plain input in each case.
- The range holds no date of enactment or commencement, so the encoding cannot say what was in force on a given date; the `תיקון` years on the headings are the years of the amending Acts, not dates.
- The 210 seat questions and their 52 section headings have not been put in front of a person in the seat, the English glosses on `@export` and `@desc` are assumed (not ruled), and the Legalese Interact form was not run over them.
- The final check ran with `L4_TARGET=unstable`, so it did not compare the stable binary with a second one; all 1,550 assertions ran on 1.6.98 only.
- The jural-relations table is my reading of who holds which right, privilege, power or immunity; it should be read beside the sections.
