# Source license

The text of **Basic Law: Israel as the Nation-State of the Jewish People** (חוק־יסוד: ישראל – מדינת הלאום של העם היהודי) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the publication in Reshumot, Sefer HaChukim 5778 no. 2743 (14 Av 5778, 26 July 2018), p. 898. The Knesset's PDF of that issue is deposited at `registers/source-bundle/official/sefer-hachukim-5778-no-2743-p898.pdf`, with the page image that was read.
- **Working text**: the raw MediaWiki text of the Hebrew Wikisource page "חוק-יסוד: ישראל – מדינת הלאום של העם היהודי" ("The Open Book of Laws"), a volunteer-maintained consolidation. **Not an official version.** Its editorial notes ("ראו ...") are not part of the Law.
  Wikisource's own editorial additions are CC BY-SA 4.0; the statute text needs no attribution.
- **Other instruments** quoted or cited (the Palestine Order in Council 1922, the Law and Administration Ordinance 5708-1948, the Declaration on the State Flag, the Interpretation Law and the Laws named in the consolidation's notes) are likewise without copyright; each deposited file's authority (official or aid) is in `registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json`.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding.
Where an encoding relies on an unofficial consolidation it says so; a finding that rests only on one is labelled CONSOLIDATION-ONLY.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English titles are unofficial.
