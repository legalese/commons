# Row il-73: Sick Pay Law, 5736-1976, a targeted record of two cross-references

Status on 2026-11-11: draft, version 0.1.0, target compiler main.
No domain expert has read this against the source.
Each claim below rests on a deposited page image or on the deposited excerpt named beside it; the claims that rest on anything else say so.
This row does not encode the Law.

## 1. What this row is

The row records two items of the Law as typed values and tests them.
The English title is a working translation; the enacted title is the Hebrew one.

Item I1 is section 4A(b)(2), which SH 2203 section 132 (issue date 23.7.2009) inserts after section 4.
Its words name two enactments: the Bankruptcy Ordinance [New Version], 5740-1980, in the words "הוכרז פושט רגל לפי פקודת פשיטת הרגל", and the Companies Ordinance [New Version], 5743-1983, in the words "ניתן צו פירוק לפי פקודת החברות".
SH 2708, the Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law (issue date 15.3.2018), prints in section 358 that the Bankruptcy Ordinance is repealed ("בטלה") but for section 97 of the Ordinance.
It prints in section 359 items (4) and (5) that sections 195 to 201 and the chapters yod-alef to yod-zayin (11 to 17) of the Companies Ordinance are repealed ("בטלים").
The words of (b)(2) as SH 2203 prints them were reached by one later instruction only, a pair of section 1 of SH 2459 (issue date 15.7.2014) that replaces one word and touches neither name.

Item I2 is section 11, in which SH 856 section 10 (issue date 25.3.1977) puts the words "גימלה לפי פרק ג׳2 לחוק הביטוח הלאומי", a reference to chapter ג׳2 of the National Insurance Law [Consolidated Version], 5728-1968.
The 1995 print of that Law (SH 1522, issue date 15.5.1995) prints the chapter whose title is "ביטוח נפגעי תאונות" as chapter ו׳, with ג׳2 in brackets in the margin beside its heading, and its table of chapters gives ו׳ in the 1995 column of the row whose 1968 entry is ג׳2.

Each item has two limbs: limb (a), the target is repealed, missing or says something else, read from an official instrument; limb (b), the citing words still read so in the latest official instrument read.
Section letters are written in Latin letters for the Hebrew letters the Law uses (4A is 4 alef; 342C is 342 gimel; 342F is 342 vav; 342G is 342 zayin).

The row types the words of sections 1 and 25 of the Interpretation Law as the pages print them, and the words of sections 373(a), 373(b), 375(a) and 376(1) of SH 2708 as the page prints them.
The row does not say whether any of these provisions bears on the citing words.

## 2. Seat

For I1 the seat is the employer to whom the rule of section 4A on dismissal during the entitlement to sick pay applies.
For I2 the seat is the employee whose sick pay section 11 excludes for a period covered by a payment under another enactment.
The row records references and asks that individual no question, so it has no input to ask for and no `@export`.

Quotation lines in the modules are of two kinds.

`tools/hebcheck.py` is the one of row il-67 with one change, documented in its header: a Hebrew run may also occur on an `ext:` line of the `.l4` files given, so that a token typed as a string in a module is bound to a quoted line (before, only the excerpt counted).

Every `ext:` line was also compared, word by word, with the text layer of its page in the whole file, by a script that is not in this folder (the whole files are not deposited).
Every word was found in the layer except the footer lines (no layer carries a footer) and these: "שנעדר" (SH 2203 page 243, whose layer is doubled and garbled), "ועדת" (SH 2708 page 417), the two words joined by a maqaf in SH 814 section 11 ("על־פי", "אי־כושר", which the comparison splits at the maqaf), and the words "פרק" and "סימן" in the headings of DMI 37 pages 813 and 815.

## 7. Limb (b): the other instruments

The consolidation's header (CO; it is on the pinned whole page and not in the excerpt) names seven instruments: the Law as enacted (SH 814, p. 206), SH 856 (p. 152), SH 2024 (p. 914), SH 2203 (p. 243), SH 2285 (p. 670), SH 2459 (p. 604) and SH 2970 (p. 840).
The heading line of section 4A carries two year tags and the heading line of section 11 carries one (CO; in the excerpt): the years of SH 2203 and SH 2459 on 4A, and the year of SH 856 on 11.

The instruments that carry an instruction on section 4A are SH 2203 (it inserts the section) and SH 2459 (one pair reaches the words of (b)(2)).
The instrument that carries an instruction on section 11 is SH 856 (section 10).
None of the others does: SH 814 prints the Law as enacted, with no section 4A and with section 11 as enacted; and the pages of SH 2024 (p. 914), SH 2285 (p. 670) and SH 2970 (p. 840) are not deposited, and the text layer of each was searched by script for the number of section 4A and for the words "section 11" and gave no hit.
That search is the only reading of those three pages made for this row, and it does not exclude an instruction that the layer prints in another form.
The typed `the tag-table rows` carries these answers, with a flag for the pages that are deposited.

SH 2708 is itself an instrument that could have touched the words of (b)(2): the list of indirect amendments printed on its cover has fifteen entries, and the Sick Pay Law is not among them (read from the deposited cover crop; the text layer of the cover has no occurrence of the word "מחלה", while the same search finds "פשיטת" once).
SH 2459 pair (11) is the one pair that reaches (b)(2), and its Schedule item 21 names the Law with no exception.
So, as far as the pages read show, the words of (b)(2) after 2014 are the 2009 words with "שהמעביד" replaced by "שהמעסיק", which is the word the consolidation prints.
The quoted (b)(2) of the 2009 page and the consolidation's line differ in that word and in the glyphs of the quotation marks around "פסק מלפעול"; the consolidation prints "לעניין" and so does the page.

Not established either way: the completeness of the consolidation's tag lists; any instrument published after the consolidation's revision date (2023-08-27) that touched section 4A(b)(2) or section 11; an indirect amendment made inside another law, which the Law's own tag list does not show.
So limb (b) of each item is "holds, on the instruments read", and the typed `how limb (b) was read` says so.
