# Source license

The texts quoted by this row are Israeli primary legislation: the **Criminal Procedure Law [Consolidated Version], 5742-1982** (חוק סדר הדין הפלילי [נוסח משולב], התשמ״ב–1982) and the instrument printed in Sefer HaChukim 2999 that inserted the sections the row reads.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, orders or Knesset records.
- **Official version**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working text**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), a consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.
