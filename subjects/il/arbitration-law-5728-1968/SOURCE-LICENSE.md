# Source license

The text of the **Arbitration Law, 5728-1968** (חוק הבוררות, תשכ״ח–1968) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Sefer HaChukim issues and the Dinei Medinat Yisrael New Version named in `SOURCES.json` (Sefer HaChukim 535, 233, 400, 1030, 1110, 1123, 2169, 2253 and 2760 deposited as crops; 737, 2186 and 2707 read and not deposited; New Version 19 deposited as crops), from https://fs.knesset.gov.il/. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text: the
  official version of a law is its original publication in Reshumot together with every amending Act.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7%20%D7%94%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA

- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where
  any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English title of the Law is a working translation of the Hebrew title the first page of Sefer HaChukim 535 prints, and it is not an enacted title; the later amending instruments spell the year with the letter ה.
