# Source license

The text of the **Property Tax and Compensation Fund Law, 5721-1961** (חוק מס רכוש וקרן פיצויים, התשכ"א-1961) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Law's original publication in Sefer HaChukim 5721, p. 100 (the citation as the footnote of Kovetz HaTakanot 11193, printed page 1886, gives it), together with every amending Act. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
  The Knesset address that the consolidation's header gives for the original is https://fs.knesset.gov.il/4/law/4_lsr_208817.pdf; it was not fetched and is not deposited in this subject.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מס_רכוש_וקרן_פיצויים ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח, on Hebrew Wikisource), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English names in this subject are renderings made for the row, not enacted titles.
