# Source license

The text of **Courts Law [Consolidated Version], 5744-1984** (חוק בתי המשפט [נוסח משולב], התשמ״ד–1984) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the publication in Reshumot cited as ס״ח תשמ״ד, 198; as amended by Amendment No. 105, ס״ח תשפ״ה, 438; Knesset legislation database lawitemid 2000140; Wikisource page חוק בתי המשפט. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text: the official version of a law is its original publication in Reshumot together with every amending Act.
- **Official amending instrument deposited**: `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/25_lsr_6165552.pdf`, the Knesset's PDF of the Sefer HaChukim issue.
  Courts Law [Consolidated Version] (Amendment No. 105), Sefer HaChukim 3389, 1 April 2025, pp. 438-440: s 1 (s 2A), s 2 (s 6), s 3 (new ss 6B, 6C), s 4 (s 7), s 5 (s 8(a)), s 6 (s 25), s 7 (indirect amendment of the Knesset Law), s 8 (commencement and application).
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A4%D7%98&action=raw (Hebrew Wikisource, "The Open Book of Laws"), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding.
Record which text was used and its date: a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.
Where the consolidation and the official instrument differ, the instrument is right; the encoding's NOTES.md lists each difference found.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial.
The English titles are descriptive glosses, not enacted titles.
