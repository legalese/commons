# Source license

The text of **Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981** (חוק הגנת הפרטיות, התשמ"א-1981) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the original publication in Reshumot (Sefer HaChukim 5741, p 128) together with every amending Act. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הפרטיות ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח, on Hebrew Wikisource), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English title above is the form the Knesset and the Ministry of Justice use in their own English-language material; it is not an enacted title.
