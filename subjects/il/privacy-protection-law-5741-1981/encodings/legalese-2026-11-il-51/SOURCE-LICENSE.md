# Source license

The texts quoted by this row are Israeli primary legislation and subsidiary legislation: the **Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981** (חוק הגנת הפרטיות, התשמ"א-1981) and its Amendment 13 (Sefer HaChukim 3287), five sets of regulations made under it or under the Administrative Offences Law, and the Interpretation Law, 5741-1981.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), of each regulation in Kovetz HaTakanot, and of each notice in Yalkut HaPirsumim, together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working texts**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), consolidated Hebrew texts. **Not official versions.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this row

Statute text may be deposited and quoted. A reading that rests only on a Wikisource consolidation says CONSOLIDATION-ONLY in `NOTES.md`; a reading from a page image of an official instrument says which page.

## Status of the deposits

Three consolidations are deposited as excerpts (the Law and two sets of regulations), each under a header that gives the Wikisource revision, the whole-page sha256 and byte count, and the original line numbers copied; the other three regulation pages are deposited whole.
All were fetched on 2026-11-10 as the raw MediaWiki source of the Wikisource page; the revisions are in `NOTES.md` section 2 and in `SOURCES.json`.
The Interpretation Law consolidation is read in place from its own subject and is not copied.
Only the pages the row reads are deposited, in `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/` and `registers/source-bundle/official/`: page extracts of three PDFs (Sefer HaChukim 3287 pp. 1430, 1433, 1461, 1472 and 1495, from the Knesset's server; Kovetz HaTakanot 6299 pp. 323 and 324 and 6913 p. 1430, from `olaw.org.il`, a non-official archive: a website that hosts scans of Reshumot issues and is not a government site), and cropped page images with no PDF (Sefer HaChukim 3287 pp. 1438 and 1474, Kovetz HaTakanot 10637 p. 1722, Yalkut HaPirsumim 15000 p. 654).
The whole files are not in the bundle; their URLs, sha256 and sizes as downloaded are in `SOURCES.json`.
`registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` gives the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of each file.
