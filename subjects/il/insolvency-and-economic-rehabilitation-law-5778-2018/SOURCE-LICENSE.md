# Source license

The texts quoted by this subject are Israeli primary legislation: the **Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law, 5778-2018** (חוק חדלות פירעון ושיקום כלכלי, התשע"ח-2018) as enacted in Sefer HaChukim 2708, and the **Execution Law, 5727-1967** (חוק ההוצאה לפועל, התשכ"ז-1967) in a Hebrew Wikisource consolidation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working texts**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), consolidated Hebrew texts. **Not official versions.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted. A reading that rests only on a Wikisource consolidation says CONSOLIDATION-ONLY in the row's `NOTES.md`; a reading from a page image of an official instrument says which page.

## Status of the deposits

`registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/` holds a three-page extract of Sefer HaChukim 2708 (printed pages 310, 366 and 407), cut out of the file the Knesset's server served on 2026-11-10, and 100 dpi page images of printed pages 366 and 407, the only two pages of it that were read from images; only the pages read are deposited, and `SOURCES.json` gives the sha256 and size of the whole file as downloaded.
`registers/source-bundle/execution-law-5727-1967-excerpt-rev3099076.he.wiki.txt` is an excerpt, not the whole page, of the Hebrew Wikisource page of the Execution Law, revision 3099076 (last edited 2026-10-29T14:03:51Z); its first lines give the sha256 and size of the whole page as retrieved and the line numbers copied.
The Wikisource consolidation of the Insolvency and Economic Rehabilitation Law itself is not deposited.
`registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` gives the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of each file.
