- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the publication in Reshumot cited as ס״ח תשס״ה, 889; Knesset legislation database lawitemid 2000505; Wikisource page חוק הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (קופות גמל). Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text: the
  official version of a law is its original publication in Reshumot together with every amending Act. The instruments read for row il-75 (Sefer HaChukim issues 2507 and 2932, Kovetz HaTakanot issue 10016, and fourteen further issues of Sefer HaChukim) are listed in `../../registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json`.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English titles below are the forms the Knesset, the Ministry of Justice and the regulators use in their own English-language material, but none of them is an enacted title.
