# Row il-75: Supervision of Financial Services (Provident Funds) Law, 5765-2005, a targeted record of two references

Status on 2026-11-11: draft, version 0.1.0, target compiler main.
No domain expert has read this against the source.
Every claim below was checked against a deposited page image or the deposited excerpt named beside it, or against a page that was viewed and is not deposited; where it was not, it says so.
This row does not encode the Law.

## 1. What this row is

The row records two items of the Law as typed values and tests them, I1 first and then I2.
Item I1 is item 13א of Part B of the First Schedule, inserted by section 23 of the instrument printed in Sefer HaChukim (SH) 2507 (issue date 5.11.2015).
Its words cite "24א(ב)(8)" and "24ב(א)(11)".
Section 24א, inserted by section 11 of the same instrument, prints a subsection (ה) that reads "הוראות סעיף זה יעמדו בתוקפן עד ט״ו בניסן התשע״ח (31 במרס 2018)."
Section 27(ב) of the same instrument prints a commencement date, 1 April 2016, for section 24א and for the Schedule item as worded in sections 11 and 23.
Item I2 is the definition of "המועד הקובע", inserted by section 4 of the instrument printed in SH 2932 (issue date 15.11.2021).
Its words end with a pointer to "34ו(ד)".
Section 34ו, printed in the same issue on pages 56 and 57, prints the subsection labels (א), (ב) and (ג), and the section printed after it on page 57 is numbered 34ז.
Section 31 of SH 2932 prints "תחילתו של חוק זה ביום כ״ח בטבת התשפ״ב (1 בינואר 2022), אלא אם כן נקבע בו אחרת."
Beside each item the row keeps controls: tests that find what they look for where it is, so that the two failing assertions record what the pages print and are not tests that cannot succeed.
Each item is read in two limbs.
Limb (a) is what an official instrument prints at the place the citing words name: a text, a different text, or no such place.
Limb (b) is whether the citing words still read so in the latest official instrument read.
The row says what the pages print.
It states nothing about what an end date, a missing subsection letter, a commencement or an order does to the Law or to anyone, and nothing about how a court would read any of it.
Subsection letters and the letters after a section number are written in Latin letters in identifiers: Alef is the first letter, Bet the second, Gimel the third, Dalet the fourth, He the fifth, Vav the sixth, Zayin the seventh.
English titles of instruments are working translations; the Hebrew titles are on the `ext:` lines.

## 2. Seat

The seat is a reader of the Law's printed text: the individual who reads item 13א, section 24א, the definition and section 34ו as the instruments print them.
This row asks that individual no question, because it records references: there is no input to ask for and no `@export`.
