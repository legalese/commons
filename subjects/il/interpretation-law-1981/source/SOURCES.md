## The official texts could NOT be obtained

The Wikisource headers link official Knesset PDFs.
`fs.knesset.gov.il` answered every request from this network with an HTML page titled "Unavailable" (131,618 bytes, HTTP 200), not a PDF, so none of them was read.
The links, for anyone who can fetch them from Israel:

- the Interpretation Law as enacted (Sefer HaChukim 5741, p. 302): https://fs.knesset.gov.il/9/law/9_lsr_209361.pdf
- its amendments (5750, 5751, 5754): https://fs.knesset.gov.il/12/law/12_lsr_210794.pdf , https://fs.knesset.gov.il/12/law/12_lsr_211706.pdf , https://fs.knesset.gov.il/13/law/13_lsr_203772.pdf
- the Ordinance [New Version] (Dinei Medinat Israel 5714, p. 2): https://fs.knesset.gov.il/2/law/2_lsr_vn_525666.pdf

The Legal Data Hunter index does not carry the Interpretation Law (a title search and a reference resolution both found nothing).
Every claim about this law therefore rests on one unofficial text each, and the encoding's NOTES.md must say so.

## Where the other rows' dates depend on this text

Our HVAC rows disagree by a day on how to end "three years from" a day and "five years" from a grant.
Section 10 of the Interpretation Law is the provision that decides it.
