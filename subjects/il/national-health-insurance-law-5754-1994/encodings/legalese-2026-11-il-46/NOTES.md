# Row il-46: three groups of citations around the National Health Insurance Law, 5754-1994

Status: draft.
Target compiler: main.
No domain expert has read this against the source.
This row is not an encoding of the Law.
It records eight citations as typed values, each as two descriptions: what the citing words say they point at, and what the target is.
Four of the eight are defects, kept in an expected-red file; four are sound controls.
It also records one change of version (section 6), and a fifth defect that is not a citation, P4, with its control: a day given twice in one amending instruction (section 6).

## 1. Scope

The row covers three groups, P1 to P3, and one control group.
P1: the definition of the term for the reduced amount in reg 1(א) of the Reduced Rates Regulations, 5755-1995 (as amended in 2001) cites s 1 of the National Insurance (Reduced Rates of Insurance Contributions) Order, 5759-1999.
Only the clause that defines the term is quoted; no other part of reg 1(א) is.
P2: s 14(ו1) of the Law refers to the reduced amount determined under s 341 of the National Insurance Law.
Only those words of s 14(ו1) are quoted; no figure of the subsection is.
P3: reg 1 of the Special Provisions Regulations and reg 1(4)(ב) of the Exemption Regulations, both 5755-1995, cite chapters of the National Insurance Law by letter.
P4: Amendment 15 to the Law (SH 1798 p. 433), s 1(1), replaces a Hebrew and Gregorian date pair in s 58(א), paragraph (1) of the definition of an absence year; the Hebrew date and the Gregorian date of the new pair name different days.
Only s 1(1) of that page is quoted.
The controls are citations made by s 14 of the Law and by s 1 of the Order, whose targets are as the citing words say, and the pair that the same instruction replaces, which names one day.
The row quotes nothing else of the three regulations, nothing else of s 14 and nothing else of s 58.
A statement that a regulation was or was not amended after the instrument read is, for the three regulations, not established here: their own consolidations are not deposited.

## 2. Seat

The persons to whom the Law and the regulations are applied are an insured person and the person who pays the contributions for them.
The row asks no question from either seat, because it records references and does not state a rule.

## 3. Sources and their standing

The official instruments are Sefer HaChukim (SH, the Knesset's PDFs) and Kovetz HaTakanot (KT, from olaw.org.il, a private archive of the Reshumot issues).

The SH pages have a text layer, and `tools/pdfcheck.py` checks every Hebrew word of an `ext:[SH ...]` line against it.
The KT pages are images only, so the Hebrew typed from a KT page is not machine-checked.
Three Hebrew Wikisource consolidations are deposited. None is official, and a reading that rests only on one is labelled CONSOLIDATION-ONLY (on its `ext:` line, in the value of the limb, and in section 7).

## 6. What the row records for each citation

Limb (a) asks whether the target is as the citing words say.
Limb (b) asks whether the citing words still read so in the latest official text of the citing provision.
A limb says whether it was read from the official instrument, from the consolidation only, from a mixture, or from the latest official instrument read with a later change not ruled out.
There is no REFUSE; a question the sources do not answer is NOTHING from a MAYBE.

P1.
The clause says that the term means what it means in s 1 of the Order.
The Order's s 1 does not contain the term, on the page images and in the consolidation; none of the three deposited consolidations contains the term at all (search result: 0 occurrences in each).

```
-- ext:[KT 6143 p.276, read from the page image] | "סכום מופחת" – כמשמעותו בסעיף 1 לצו הביטוח הלאומי (שיעורים מופחתים של דמי ביטוח), התשנ"ט–1999
```

P2.
The words are הסכום המופחת שנקבע לפי סעיף 341 לחוק הביטוח הלאומי.
SH 3384 p. 396, s 19(2) adds to s 334(א) the definition of "מדרגת גבייה מופחתת", and s 19(3) puts that term into s 341 in place of words not quoted here.
What s 341 prints today is the consolidation only.

P3.
The two regulations say chapter ג׳ and chapter ו2.
The consolidation prints chapter ג׳ as the maternity chapter and has no chapter ו2.
The chapter letters match the 1968 numbering: the concordance table in the consolidation gives old ג׳ as new ה׳ and old ו׳2 as new ט׳.
SH 1607 p. 22, s 12(1), item (א), changed the Law's definition of the National Insurance Law from the 1968 version to the 1995 version; it was published on 7 January 1997, after the regulations (12 January 1995).
That the letters went stale for this reason is an inference, not something the texts say.

P4.
One instruction gives a day twice.
SH 1798 p. 433, s 1(1), replaces in s 58 of the Law, in subsection (א), paragraph (1) of the definition of an absence year (שנת היעדרות), a Hebrew and Gregorian date pair by another.

```
-- ext:[SH 1798 p.433, read from the page image] | (1) בסעיף קטן (א)(1), במקום "י"ג באדר התשנ"ט (1 במרס 1999)" יבוא "י"ד באדר התשס"א (1 במרס 2001)";
```

The old pair names one day: 13 Adar 5759 is 1 March 1999.
The new pair does not: 14 Adar 5761 is 9 March 2001, and 1 March 2001 is 6 Adar 5761, eight days earlier.
Both conversions were made with the calendar routine, which reproduced 30 Kislev 5785 = 31 December 2024 first.
The definition excludes, from the count of days, any period before that date (paragraph (1)), and an absence year is 12 consecutive months.
Those are the definition's own words, read from the consolidation (revision 3081417): תקופה של 12 חודשים רצופים, and תקופה שקדמה ליום י״ד באדר התשס״א.
They are CONSOLIDATION-ONLY for the present wording; the page image shows the instruction replacing the date only.
So on the definition's own words, a twelve-month period that starts on or after 9 March 2001 does not touch the 8 days in doubt (1 to 8 March 2001), and the last period that could include any of them ended on 7 March 2002.
This is arithmetic from the definition, not a reading of any later provision; no later instrument was read for s 58(א).
The header of s 58 in the consolidation lists three entries later than 5761, תשס״ג־2, תשס״ה־5 and תשס״ח־6; none of them was read.
The row does not say what the rule did to anyone; it records the pair.
It is not a live defect.

## 7. Fork register

Each item gives both readings, and the Hebrew that licenses each.
The row does not choose between them.
Where a reading rests only on a consolidation, it is CONSOLIDATION-ONLY.

F1 (P1).
Reading A: the citation is read as written, so the term means what s 1 of the Order says, and s 1 does not say it.
Licensed by the clause (quoted in section 6) and by the absence of the term from the Order.
Reading B: a court reads around it, and takes the term to be the sum that s 1 fixes.
Licensed by the Order's own words, CONSOLIDATION-ONLY (the Order's consolidation, line 15): סכום השכר או ההכנסה שאינם עולים על מחצית השכר הממוצע, and by the heading of s 1, שיעור מופחת של דמי ביטוח.

F2 (P2).
Reading A: the words are read as written, so the amount is one determined under s 341, and s 341 as printed is a power to fix a sum by order; no order under s 341 was read for this row.
Reading B: a court reads s 14(ו1) together with s 341's last clause and s 334(א), and takes the amount to be the threshold.
Licensed by the consolidation of the National Insurance Law, CONSOLIDATION-ONLY: מדרגת הגבייה המופחתת in s 341, and מדרגת גבייה מופחתת in s 334(א).

F3 (P3).
Reading A: the letters are read in the 1995 consolidation, so chapter ג׳ is the maternity chapter and there is no chapter ו2, and neither citation points at what its words describe.
Licensed by the chapter headings, CONSOLIDATION-ONLY: פרק ג׳ and פרק ט׳.
Reading B: a court reads the letters in the 1968 numbering, as the concordance table shows, and each citation points at what its words describe.
Licensed by the concordance rows, CONSOLIDATION-ONLY: פרק ג׳ against פרק ה׳, and פרק ו׳2 against פרק ט׳.
Whether a rule of interpretation about replaced provisions would also give Reading B is not examined here.

F4 (P4).
Reading A: the Hebrew date is right, so the day is 9 March 2001.
Licensed by the Hebrew date י״ד באדר התשס״א, which the instruction gives and the consolidation prints.
Reading B: the Gregorian date is right, so the day is 1 March 2001.
Licensed by the Gregorian date in the same words, 1 במרס 2001, which the consolidation also prints.
The row does not choose between them.
