# Source license

The texts quoted by this row are Israeli primary legislation: the **Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981** (חוק הגנת הצרכן, התשמ"א-1981), its amending instruments named in `NOTES.md`, and the **Communications (Broadcasts) Law, 5786-2026**.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations or Knesset records.
- **Official versions**: the original publication of each law in Sefer HaChukim (the Knesset), together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working text**: Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח), a consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this row

Statute text may be deposited and quoted. A reading that rests only on the Wikisource consolidation says CONSOLIDATION-ONLY in `NOTES.md`; a reading from a page image of an official instrument says which page.

## Status of the deposits

The consolidation is deposited as an EXCERPT: the 19 lines of the raw wikitext of the page (revision 3099046, last edited 2026-10-29, retrieved on 2026-11-10) that this row quotes. The rest of the page is not deposited.
The official instruments are in `registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/` (Sefer HaChukim 2159, 2310, 3569 and 3572, from the Knesset's server), with 100 dpi page images of the pages read, rendered for this row. Only the pages read are deposited: Sefer HaChukim 2159 and 2310 whole (two pages each), Sefer HaChukim 3572 as a three-page extract, and Sefer HaChukim 3569 as cropped page images only.
`registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json` gives the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of each file, and for the excerpt the line numbers it was taken from.
`SOURCES.json` keeps the URL, retrieval date, sha256 and size of the whole file as downloaded for the two instruments not deposited whole.
