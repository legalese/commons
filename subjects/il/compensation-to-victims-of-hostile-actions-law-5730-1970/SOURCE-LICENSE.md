# Source license

The text of the **Compensation to Victims of Hostile Actions Law, 5730-1970** (חוק התגמולים לנפגעי פעולות איבה, תש״ל–1970) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Law's publication in Sefer HaChukim 5730, together with every amending Act. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התגמולים_לנפגעי_פעולות_איבה ("Reshumot / Sefer HaChukim (רשומות, ספר החוקים), published by the Knesset; consolidated working text from "The Open Book of Laws" (ספר החוקים הפתוח) on Hebrew Wikisource"), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English titles in this subject are renderings made for the row, not enacted titles.
