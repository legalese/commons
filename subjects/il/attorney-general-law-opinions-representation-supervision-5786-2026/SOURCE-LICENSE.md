# Source license

The text of the **Attorney General Law (Opinions, Representation and Supervision), 5786-2026** (חוק היועץ המשפטי לממשלה (חוות דעת, ייצוג ופיקוח), התשפ״ו–2026) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none.
  Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the publication in Reshumot, Sefer HaChukim 5786 no. 3550 (5 Av 5786, 19 July 2026), pp. 802-804.
  The Knesset's PDF of that issue is deposited at `registers/source-bundle/official/sefer-hachukim-5786-no-3550-p802.pdf`, with the page images that were read.
- **Aids**: the Knesset's unofficial text of the Law, the bill as put to the second and third readings (pages 2 to 6 only), page 217 of the issue of Hatza'ot Chok HaKnesset in which the first-reading bill appeared, and raw MediaWiki text of Hebrew Wikisource pages ("The Open Book of Laws", a volunteer-maintained consolidation, **not an official version**).
  Wikisource's own editorial additions are CC BY-SA 4.0; the statute text needs no attribution.
- **Other instruments** read or cited (the Civil Procedure Amendment (State as Litigant) Law 5718-1958 with its two amendments, the Civil Service (Appointments) Law 5719-1959, the Ordinance (Appearance of the Attorney General) [New Version], the Complaints Commissioner Law 5776-2016) are likewise without copyright; each deposited file's authority (official or aid) is in `registers/source-bundle/SOURCES.json`.
- **Knesset database records** (`registers/source-bundle/knesset-odata/`) are responses of the Knesset's public OData service; they record facts about the bill (the readings, the votes, the committee, the merged bills), not text of the Law.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding.
Where an encoding relies on an unofficial consolidation it says so, and a statement that rests only on one is labelled CONSOLIDATION-ONLY.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English titles are unofficial.
