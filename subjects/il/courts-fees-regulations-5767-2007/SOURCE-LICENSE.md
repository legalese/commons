# Source license

The text of the **Courts (Fees) Regulations, 5767-2007** (תקנות בתי המשפט (אגרות), התשס"ז-2007) is Israeli secondary legislation, and the instruments that amend, suspend or are cited by it are Israeli legislation too.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Regulations as made in Kovetz HaTakanot 6579 (18 April 2007), printed page 720, together with every amending instrument. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
  The issues the row reads are deposited only as cropped images of the lines read; the whole issues were supplied as local copies, from olaw.org.il, a private archive of the printed issues (not a government site), and are not deposited.
- **Working texts**: three pages of Hebrew Wikisource ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח): the Fees Regulations (https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_המשפט_(אגרות)), the Civil Procedure Regulations, 5779-2018 (https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי) and the Courts Law (https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט), maintained consolidated Hebrew texts. **Not official versions.**
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English names in this subject are renderings made for the row, not enacted titles.
