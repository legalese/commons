# Source license

The text of the **Sale (Apartments) (Assurance of Investments of Persons Acquiring Apartments) Law, 5735-1974** (חוק המכר (דירות) (הבטחת השקעות של רוכשי דירות), התשל"ה-1974) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the Law's original publication in Sefer HaChukim 5735, p. 14 (the citation as footnote 33 of Sefer HaChukim 3510, printed page 365, gives it; the footnote was read from the page image and is not in the deposited crops), together with every amending Act. Israel publishes no free official *consolidated* text.
  The original publication was not fetched and is not deposited in this subject.
- **Working text**: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)_(הבטחת_השקעות_של_רוכשי_דירות) ("The Open Book of Laws", ספר החוקים הפתוח, on Hebrew Wikisource), a maintained consolidated Hebrew text. **Not an official version.**
  The Banking (Licensing) Law, 5741-1981 is cited by one item and is deposited only as two lines of its Hebrew Wikisource page, also unofficial.
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text. Wikisource's own editorial additions, where any survive, are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date:
a consolidated working text has no official status, so an encoding that relies on it must say so.

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English names in this subject are renderings made for the row, not enacted titles.
