## 1. What is encoded, and what is not

Every article of Título II is encoded:

- arts. 155 (by g0) and 156 (furto);
- 157 (roubo);
- 158–160 (extorsão);
- 161–162 (usurpação);
- 163–167 (dano);
- 168–170 (apropriação indébita);
- 171 and 171-A (estelionato);
- 172–179 (outras fraudes);
- 180 and 180-A (receptação);
- 181–183-A (disposições gerais).

Each punishing provision gets:

- the case record;
- the predicate that the crime is made out, consummated and, where modelled, attempted;
- the pena cominada, as a `Pena cominada`;
- the causes of increase and decrease, as constants, `incide …` predicates and an exported list;
- the heaviest-form selection, where several preceitos compete;
- the limits after the causes and the attempt;
- the ação penal, where the article states it.

Art. 183-A's increase is listed among the causes of every offence of the Título.
Arts. 181–183 reach every crime of the Título, so they take a record of their own (`Fato contra o patrimônio`).

Deliberately not encoded:

- **The attempt** of arts. 164, 166, 168-A, 169, 171-A, 172–179, 180 and 180-A. Those modules decide the consummated crime only (fork F-g3-28). The attempt is modelled for arts. 155–163, 165, 168 and 171.
- **Wording that a later law replaced, where the earlier words are not in the source.** Such a rule refuses by a named REFUSE (PLAN § 10):
  - every rule of arts. 157, 171, 180 and 180-A that yields a penalty, an option or the ação penal, for a fact before 4.5.2026 (Lei nº 15.397/2026);
  - art. 158's penalty for the § 2º form before 4.5.2026;
  - art. 159's penalties before 10.8.2009 (Lei nº 8.072/1990 art. 9º and the revoked art. 224; F-sweep-b-02);

  - art. 172 before 28.12.1990;
  - art. 180's crime predicates before 26.12.1996.
- **Paragraphs a later law added** do not refuse. They simply do not apply before that law's vigência:

## 7. Findings about the Code

No assertion fails, so none of these is a failing test. They are observations for a lawyer.

1. **Art. 157 § 5º has a lower minimum than § 3º II.** Lei nº 15.397/2026 raised § 3º II's minimum to 24 years. That left § 5º (Lei nº 15.358/2026: 20 to 40) with a lower minimum than the ordinary latrocínio. The encoding applies § 5º, by its maximum (F-g3-09).
2. **Art. 175 § 1º prints "metal de ou outra qualidade"** in the compiled text (F-g3-29).
3. **Art. 158 § 4º's wording** omits "ultraviolenta" in Lei nº 15.358's own text (sweep-b's finding; F-sweep-b-22).
4. **Arts. 165–166** may be displaced by Lei nº 9.605/1998 arts. 62–63. Neither the P2 sweep nor g3 fetched that law (F-g3-18).
5. **Art. 180 § 5º was revoked by Lei nº 15.358/2026, and nothing replaced it.** It gave the judge options: not applying the penalty to the culpable receiver, and art. 155 § 2º for the wilful one. No provision of art. 180 gives one now, so the encoding returns `nenhuma opção desde a revogação do § 5º`.

## 8. Traps met

- **An unannotated `NOTHING` fixture takes the type of its first use.**
  - The fixture was `uma empresa como vítima MEANS NOTHING`, with no `GIVETH`.
  - The estelionato tests failed with a message naming an unrelated type: "expected MAYBE OF Vítima, but here MAYBE OF `Circunstância do fato`".
  - Earlier, in the gerais tests, it produced "An ASSERT directive is expected to be of type BOOLEAN but is here of type Vítima" and "multiple definitions for __EQUALS__", at about the thirteenth assertion, whatever that assertion said.
  - Naming every case as a constant made it pass; annotating the fixture removed the cause.
  - Give every fixture a `GIVETH`.
- **A result-enum constructor with the same name as a predicate** (`isento de pena (art. 181)` as both) errors at every use. The constructors now read `o agente é isento de pena (art. 181)` and `procede-se somente mediante representação (art. 182)`.
- **The AND/OR same-column lint treats `...` as an AND.** A quoted string joined by `...` at one column, with an `OR` group below it at the same column, draws the warning. Art. 175 caput was restructured so the quote is a comment.
- **`BRANCH` after `ELSE`** needs its arms right of the `BRANCH` column; a nested `IF` is simpler there.
