**Art. 123 is in the homicide module, out of the Code's order.** The exported `o crime de matar que os fatos constituem (arts. 121 a 121-B e 123)` must be able to choose it: otherwise a mother's puerperal killing of her newborn would be priced as homicide qualified by art. 121 § 2º IX (victim under fourteen), 12–30 years instead of 2–6 (fork F-g2a-18). Art. 122 cannot join it: its §§ 6º–7º send the agent to arts. 129 and 121, so it imports both modules.

**Not encoded, on purpose:**

- The ATTEMPT of art. 122 (complete with the conduct since Lei 13.968/2019) and of the crimes of danger of Capítulo III (art. 135 is omissive and has none). Attempts are encoded for arts. 121, 121-A, 121-B, 123, 124–126 and 129 (intentional forms).
- The attempt of a qualified grade of injury ("tentativa de lesão grave"): an attempted injury gets the forms that do not depend on the result (caput, § 9º, § 13).
- Concurso de pessoas in infanticide (does the puerperal state communicate under art. 30?).

## 9. Changes after the independent test pass (2026-09-30)

The conductor passed on three items from ta-g2's test pass (369/380 agree over arts. 121–154-B).

1. **Art. 129 "de outrem" was not encoded** (an encoding error). Self-injury made out the crime. New element `a vítima é outrem (art. 129, caput)` (the agent's and the victim's `identificação` differ) in the consummated and the attempted injury, and so in the culpable one too.
   **The same defect was in every crime I own whose object is "alguém" / "outrem" / "pessoa"**, and is fixed the same way: art. 121 "Matar alguem" (`a vítima é alguém que não o agente (art. 121, caput)`, which carries arts. 121-A, 121-B, 123 and the homicide art. 122 § 7º builds), art. 122 "Induzir ou instigar alguém" (`a vítima é alguém que não o agente (art. 122)`), and every crime of Capítulo III (`a vítima é outra pessoa que o agente`, conjoined with each of the eight crime predicates, § 1º of art. 130 included). Art. 129 § 6º inherits it from the consummated injury. Arts. 124–128 already compared agent and pregnant woman; art. 137 has no victim.
2. **Art. 123 did not require the mother** (a latent error). A father with the puerperal findings entered was an infanticide. New predicate `a agente é a mãe da vítima (art. 123)`: the agent is a woman, the victim's ascendant, and the victim is her own child (the certified filiation, which "ascendente" alone does not give — a grandmother is one too). A father is now art. 121's, qualified by § 2º IX for a newborn.
3. **"por qualquer outro título tiver autoridade sobre ela" read only one relation** (a missing fork). Decided and recorded as **F-g2a-34**: it reaches `guarda, cuidado ou vigilância da vítima` as well as `outra relação de autoridade sobre a vítima`. The helper is shared by arts. 121 § 2º-B II, § 2º-C II and 129 § 12 II b, so all three change.
