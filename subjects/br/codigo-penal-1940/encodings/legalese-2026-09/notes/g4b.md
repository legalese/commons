Every offence has a crime predicate (consummated, attempted where the crime admits it, culpable where the Code provides it), the preceito, and the limits once the causes are applied.
All offences share one header, `Dados comuns do fato`: the agent, the fact's date, dolo or culpa, how the result is imputed, and the attempt facts.

**The result-qualified forms.**
Art. 258 is one enum and one rule, `a forma qualificada do art. 258 que incide`, whose death arm is tried first, and which needs a result the agent caused at least through fault and did not want (F-g4b-02).
Arts. 263 and 285 reach art. 258 through it; art. 264, parágrafo único has its own two arms; art. 267 §§ 1º–2º carry their own death forms, which is why art. 285 returns nothing for art. 267.
Art. 285 also returns nothing for art. 282, by this encoding's reading (F-g4b-09).

**What refuses, and why.** Each refusal is named and carries its reason.

- Text before the law that wrote it: art. 288 before 19.9.2013; arts. 272–277 before 3.7.1998; the penalties of arts. 267 and 270 before 26.7.1990; art. 282 before 8.6.2026; art. 266's penalty before 4.5.2026.
- Art. 266, § 2º in Lei 15.181/2025's wording, for facts from 29.7.2025 to 3.5.2026: refused rather than reconstructed, as instructed.
- A crime now defined by a special law this encoding does not carry: possessing or manufacturing an explosive device from 23.12.2003 (Lei 10.826/2003, art. 16, § 1º, III); the intentional form of art. 259 (Lei 9.605/1998, art. 61); an association for drug trafficking or terrorism (Lei 11.343/2006, art. 35; Lei 13.260/2016).
- Art. 288, § 2º where the solicited crime is one that Lei 8.072/1990, art. 8º names: whether art. 8º reaches § 2º is not decided here.
- Art. 273, § 1º-B, V alone: its penalty was declared unconstitutional by the STJ with no substitute (F-sweep-c-16).

**Provisions added later are FALSE before their date, not refused**: art. 265, parágrafo único (7.12.1967), art. 266, § 1º (2.4.2013), art. 286, parágrafo único (1.12.2021), art. 288-A (28.9.2012) and art. 288, § 2º (30.10.2025).

**Inert**: arts. 279 and 281, revoked, carried as labelled stubs that nothing reads.

**Not encoded**: the fine's day-fine arithmetic (cp-pena's), the first two phases of dosimetry, and every special law named above.
The penalty of homicídio culposo that art. 258 and art. 264, parágrafo único borrow (art. 121, § 3º, detenção 1 to 3 years) is restated in `cp-g4b-perigo-comum.l4`, not imported, because another group owns art. 121.

## 2. The forks, in prose

Thirty-two entries in `registers/parts/g4b.forks.json`; the register holds the readings, quotes and citations.

**The Título's structure.**

- F-g4b-01: the danger in arts. 250–256 must be a common one, to an indeterminate number of people or goods; the Capítulo's heading says so, and the fact supplier certifies it.
- F-g4b-02: the qualifying death or injury is one the agent caused at least through fault and did not want; a wanted death is a homicide.
- F-g4b-11: the attempt is modelled where execution can begin without being completed; not for the pure omission of art. 269, the habitual crimes of arts. 282 and 284, or the associative crimes of arts. 288 and 288-A.

**Arts. 250–259.**

- F-g4b-03: "as penas", plural, reaches the fine too.
- F-g4b-04: the increases of arts. 250, § 1º and 251, § 2º reach only the intentional forms.
- F-g4b-05: art. 251's substance clause governs all three modes; the substance then chooses between the caput and § 1º.
- F-g4b-25: the culpable form of art. 251 is an explosion caused through fault; a careless throw that does not explode is no crime.
- F-g4b-06: "lesão corporal de natureza grave" in art. 258 includes the gravíssima of art. 129, § 2º.
- F-g4b-07: when both a death and an injury result, only the death's arm of art. 258 applies.
- F-g4b-26: three arms of art. 258 are causes of increase on the crime's own preceito; the fourth replaces the preceito with homicídio culposo's and adds a third.
- F-g4b-27: art. 257's "por ocasião de ... desastre ou calamidade" governs both of its modes.
- F-sweep-c-17: art. 259's intentional form is revoked by Lei 9.605/1998, art. 61; the culpable form survives.
- F-sweep-c-18: possessing or manufacturing an explosive device passed to Lei 10.826/2003 on 23.12.2003; art. 253 remains for the other verbs, for explosive substances, gases, and material for manufacture.

**Arts. 260–266.**

- F-g4b-08: art. 260's elements are the text's; no danger element beyond inciso IV's.

**Arts. 267–285.**

- F-sweep-c-04: art. 268's "determinação do poder público" may come from any of the four levels of government, each within its sphere (settled by Tema 1246).
- F-g4b-09: art. 282 as re-written in 2026 sends a death or serious injury to arts. 121 and 129; art. 258 no longer reaches it through art. 285.
- F-g4b-10: "exercer" is habitual, in art. 282 and in all three incisos of art. 284.
- F-g4b-12: Tema 1003's 1-to-3-year penalty covers every verb of § 1º applied to an unregistered product. **This reach rests on a search summary** of embargos de declaração in June 2023; no STF text was read, because the STF's pages answer HTTP 403. Settled by Tema 1003 for the thesis, not for the reach.
- F-g4b-13: when a fact under § 1º-B, I also fits a hypothesis whose 10 to 15 years stand, those apply.
- F-sweep-c-16: § 1º-B, V alone has no computable penalty.
- F-g4b-14: art. 272, § 1º-A's product is the caput's, falsified.
- F-g4b-15: art. 274's "não expressamente permitida" qualifies every item.
- F-g4b-16: art. 276's "e" joins two classes of product into one.

**Arts. 286–288-A.**

- F-g4b-17: art. 286 needs a crime, not a contravenção, that the incitement points to.
- F-g4b-18: art. 287 needs a determinate criminal fact, or its author, praised.
- F-g4b-19: art. 288 needs a stable bond aimed at crimes not yet determined.
- F-g4b-20: "armada": a weapon of any kind at the group's service.
- F-g4b-21: every associate counts toward the three, even one who cannot be punished.
- F-g4b-22: art. 288-A's categories are a finding.
- F-g4b-23: associations for trafficking or terrorism fall under their special laws; art. 8º's 3 to 6 years remain for heinous crimes and torture.
- F-g4b-24: art. 288, § 1º is the 2013 parágrafo único, renumbered, and applies from 19.9.2013.
- F-g4b-28: art. 8º of Lei 8.072/1990 reaches art. 288 as re-written in 2013, including its decrease for the associate who denounces the group; it does not reach § 2º.

## 6. Open questions for a Brazilian criminal lawyer

1. Does Tema 1003's repristinated penalty reach every verb of art. 273, § 1º applied to an unregistered product (F-g4b-12)? This encoding says yes, on a search summary rather than the STF's text.
2. When a medicine is both unregistered (§ 1º-B, I) and of unknown origin (§ 1º-B, V), does inciso I's penalty apply (F-g4b-13)?
3. Does Lei 8.072/1990, art. 8º reach art. 288 as re-written in 2013, and does it reach § 2º (F-g4b-28)?
4. Is art. 288, § 1º the 2013 parágrafo único renumbered, with no older lineage to gate (F-g4b-24)?
5. Art. 282, § 1º is also a renumbered parágrafo único, whose text dates from 1940. The encoding gates the whole article at 8.6.2026 and refuses earlier facts; should § 1º reach back?
6. Does an explosive **substance**, as distinct from a device, count as an "artefato explosivo" under Lei 10.826/2003, art. 16, § 1º, III? The encoding says no, so art. 253 keeps it.
7. The Senado XML shows "Alteração" rows for Lei 8.072/1990 against art. 285 and art. 267, § 1º, but in this range that law's text changes only the penalties of arts. 267 and 270. Are these indexing artefacts?
8. When did Lei 9.605/1998 enter into force? The refusal of art. 259's intentional form does not depend on it, since the milder later law reaches earlier facts; but a date-gated arm would.
9. Art. 285 no longer bringing art. 258 to art. 282 (F-g4b-09): is that the courts' reading of the 2026 re-write?
