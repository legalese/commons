**Arts. 359-L, 359-M, 359-M-A and 359-M-B (F-sweep-c-05)** is the one fork where the law is in motion.
The encoding applies the text in force: in one context, two or more Capítulo II crimes take the gravest penalty increased by a sixth to a half, capped at the art. 69 sum; in a crowd, one who neither financed nor led has the penalty reduced by a third to two thirds.
