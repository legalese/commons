**On the independent test authors' doubling conventions** (`notes/conductor.md`): g0's convention is ta-g2's and ta-g5's, the fraction added. Applied to a pena cominada by `a pena cominada com a causa`, it gives the resulting limits that ta-g3 records, so the two convert without loss.
