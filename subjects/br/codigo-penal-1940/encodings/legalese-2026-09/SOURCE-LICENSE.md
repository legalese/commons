**Not protected by copyright.**
Lei nº 9.610, de 19 de fevereiro de 1998 (the Copyright Act), art. 8º:

> Não são objeto de proteção como direitos autorais de que trata esta Lei: […]
> IV - os textos de tratados ou convenções, leis, decretos, regulamentos, decisões judiciais e demais atos oficiais;

That was checked on 2026-09-29 against Planalto's own text of Lei nº 9.610 (https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9610.htm), not quoted from memory.
A Decreto-Lei is a *lei* for this purpose, so the statute's text may be reproduced, including verbatim quotation inside an encoding.

One point is not settled here.
Planalto's compilation adds editorial matter to the statute: the "(Redação dada pela Lei …)", "(Incluído pela Lei …)" and "(Vide …)" notes, and the page layout.
It is published by the Casa Civil da Presidência da República, which makes it arguably one of the *demais atos oficiais* in art. 8º IV.
No ruling on that point was found, so treat the notes as **probably unprotected, not established**.
The encodings quote the statute's text, not the notes.
