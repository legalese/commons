Piece `pb2` of the encoding of the Social Security Benefits Plans Law (Lei nº 8.213, de 24 de julho de 1991, Planos de Benefícios da Previdência Social).
Written by one agent (model: Sonnet 5.5) in one context, from `lei-8213-1991.pt.txt`, with the keel's `pb-core.l4` as the shared vocabulary.

**What is encoded.**
Lines 455-780 of the text: Título III, Capítulo II, Seção III (Subseções I and II, arts. 28 to 40, with arts. 29-A, 29-B, 29-C, 38-A and 38-B), Seção IV (arts. 41 and 41-A) and Seção V, Subseções I to IV (arts. 42 to 58).

**Target and method.**
The target is `main`, and the binary is the stable `l4` 1.6.98 (`1.6.98 (main, 7689b0d, 2026-10-19)`), itself a main build.
Method: read the live lines of the range (`live.py`, which tracks the strikethrough across lines), write the module for one Subseção or Seção, quote the source mechanically with `srcrefs.py quote`, check the module, write its tests from the text before running them, and write the notes rows for it, in that order, seven times; then the seat layer over all seven; then the seat cases.

## Seat

The seat is the one the brief fixes: **a person insured under Brazil's Regime Geral de Previdência Social, or a dependant of one, asking whether they qualify for a benefit (a retirement, disability, sickness, maternity or death benefit), how much it is, from when and for how long, and what they must do or declare.**
It is the individual to whom the Law is applied, which Commons requires first (`CLAUDE.md`, "Whose seat").
No seat is written for an official (the Previdência Social, the INSS, the Ministry, the Executive) and none for the company: the company's duties appear as what the person is owed or exposed to (art. 43 § 2, art. 51, art. 58 §§ 1 to 4), and the company's own liabilities (art. 57 §§ 6 and 7) are in layer 1 only.

Layer 1 is the seven `pb2-*.l4` modules, written from the text alone.
Layer 2 is `pb2-seat.l4` and `pb2-cases-seat.l4`: 98 questions, an `@export` on all but the 29th (art. 38, which returns a DEONTIC: its `@export` is commented out ahead of legalese/l4-ide#779, a draft, not landed, which refuses it) and an `@desc` on each input (three questions take no input: 29, 54 and 62 in the order of the file), grouped under `§` headings in the order of the text (salário-de-benefício; the CNIS; the correction of the salários; the option about the fator previdenciário; the auxílio-acidente and concomitant activities; the floor and the ceiling of the monthly income; if I cannot prove my salários; the register of the segurado especial; the guaranteed grant to the segurado especial; the abono anual; the readjustment; and the five benefits).
A group that arises only for a person in a particular situation says so in a comment under its heading (the segurado especial, the person who cannot prove the salários, the one who is or may be incapacitated, and so on), so that the form can ask "Isso aconteceu com você?" before showing it.
The questions are in Brazilian Portuguese in the first person ("Tenho direito à aposentadoria por invalidez?"), take plain inputs (yes/no, numbers, dates, or a choice from the modules' own lists), and build the records the rules expect (`Segurado`, `Valores de referência`) inside the question, with a comment on every placeholder field.

Where the defaults ruled on 2026-10-18 apply, they are followed: the Act alone; the actors the text names; a judgment handed to someone else is a conditional input named for whose conclusion it is (the perícia médica of the Previdência Social, the Poder Executivo's list of harmful agents, the Regulamento's criteria, the INSS's criteria); the Law's own definitions are worked out from facts; one case is one insured person.

What the seat does **not** ask, and why (the same list is in the header of `pb2-seat.l4`):

- **Lists.** Four layer-1 rules take a list: the mean of the highest 80% of the salários (art. 29), the INPC variations (art. 29-B), the last 12 salários (art. 29 § 10) and the salários of concomitant activities (art. 32). A form takes no list, so the seat asks the mean as the figure the INSS's statement shows, the INPC as one accumulated variation, the last 12 salários as 12 numbers and the activities as two numbers. The salário-de-benefício of the rural worker with periods under other categories (art. 48 § 4) needs a list of salários and has no whole question; the monthly figure it fixes for the months as segurado especial is asked.
- **The factor and the figures the Law leaves out.** The fator previdenciário (art. 29 § 7; its formula is in an Anexo that is not in the text), the INPC variation, the conversion factor of special time (art. 57 § 5) and the Gratificação de Natal fraction (art. 40) are asked as numbers the person can read from the INSS's statement. A different figure changes the answer.
- **The salário mínimo and the teto.** The Law fixes neither; the person states them for the date of the benefit.
- **No question** for art. 57 §§ 6 and 7 (the company's contribution: nothing the insured states), art. 38-A caput and § 1 and art. 38-B § 5 (inert), art. 41-A § 4 (what a working day is, folded into the dates the payment questions ask), and art. 29 §§ 7 and 8 as such (the fator is asked as a number; the record of its elements is explanatory).

## Needs from the core

Nothing was missing for the rules to be written; these are what a merge would be better for:

1. **Seat helpers.** `pb2-seat.l4` defines two helpers that any piece's seat will need: `eu como segurado` (a `Segurado` from a category, a sex and an age) and `os valores de referência que informei` (a `Valores de referência` from the salário mínimo and the teto, with the limite mínimo as a placeholder). If the lead imports more than one piece's seat into one module, the second definition makes every use an error. They should be promoted to the core, or each piece's helpers renamed.
2. **The carência in months.** Arts. 39, I and 48, § 2 each need "o número de meses de contribuição correspondente à carência do benefício pretendido" (art. 25, pb1). The keel's table has the BOOLEAN `o segurado cumpriu a carência exigida` but not the number. This piece names the input with the words of art. 48 § 2 and art. 39, I; a row in the keel table would keep pb1, pb2 and pb3 spelling it alike.
3. **A rural worker.** Keel instruction 8 says there is no constructor; this piece works out `art. 48, § 1º — o segurado é trabalhador rural` from the category and two facts (finding 12). If pb1 or pb4 need the same notion (art. 11 is pb1's), the rule could move to the core.
4. **The working days of a payment.** The record `Dias úteis do pagamento (art. 41-A, §§ 2º a 4º)` (three dates) is local to `pb2-reajustamento.l4`. Art. 22 (pb1, "primeiro dia útil seguinte") needs a working-day date too (keel fork F-8); if both want a shared record, the lead can promote this one.
5. **"An aposentadoria".** Arts. 31 and 34, II name "qualquer aposentadoria" and this piece lists the four constructors of `Prestação` each time. A core helper `a prestação é aposentadoria` would shorten both and would serve pb3 and pb4.

## What the text turns out to say

1. **Dates.** The range contains no date of enactment or making of the Law, no commencement provision and no rule that fixes a commencement by a period; so there is no date to carry as a defined value with an `@ref` from this piece (the title date and the date of entry into force are the core's). The dates it does contain are the Law's own and are carried in the rules: 31 December 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024 and 2026 (art. 29-C § 2), 30 June of the following year and five years after (art. 38-A §§ 4 and 5), 1 January 2023 and 1 January 2025 (art. 38-B). Art. 55 § 2 ("anterior à data de início de vigência desta Lei") uses the core's `a data de entrada em vigor da Lei nº 8.213`. Asserted.
2. **The 80% of art. 29.** The salário-de-benefício is the mean of the highest salários that make up "oitenta por cento de todo o período contributivo", and the text says nothing about a period whose months are not a multiple of five. With 7 months the rule uses 5 (5.6 rounded down); with a single month it uses none and gives `NOTHING`. Fork F-PB2-02. Asserted.
3. **The fator previdenciário is not in the text.** Art. 29 § 7 sends the formula to "o Anexo desta Lei", and a search of the text for "Anexo" finds only that line. The factor is an input of the inciso I rules; the text lists what it is calculated from (age, the IBGE life expectancy of § 8, and contribution time with the years of § 9), and a record holds those. Fork F-PB2-16. No case asserts the absence of the Anexo; it is a reading of the text.
4. **The points of art. 29-C.** The sums rise by one point "em" each of five dates, read as on that date: on 31 December 2018 the man's required sum is 96 and the woman's 86; on 31 December 2026 they are 100 and 90. A person who reached the requirement and did not ask keeps the points of the day they reached it (§ 4); a teacher needs 30 or 25 years and has five points added to the sum (§ 3). Fork F-PB2-06. Asserted.
5. **A woman who teaches (art. 29 § 9).** Ten years are added to her contribution time for the fator, not fifteen. Fork F-PB2-05. Asserted.
6. **A reference to a revoked paragraph.** Art. 31 says the auxílio-acidente counts "observado, no que couber, o disposto no art. 29 e no art. 86, § 5º", and in this text art. 86 § 5º is struck and marked "(Revogado pela Lei nº 9.032, de 1995)" (line 1157). Art. 86 § 1 also cites that § 5º. There is nothing live to observe, and the rule does not pretend there is. No case asserts it: it is a fact about two lines of the text.
7. **Art. 36 outlives the articles that used to cite it.** Art. 36 (the empregado doméstico who cannot prove the payment of contributions gets the benefit of minimum value) is live, but art. 35 now covers the doméstico, art. 34, I counts the contributions due though the household employer did not pay them, and arts. 37 and 38 no longer cite art. 36. The rule is encoded as printed: a doméstico who can prove the value of his salários but not the payment still gets the minimum benefit through art. 36 alone. Fork F-PB2-09. Asserted.
8. **Two windows for the register and for the segurado especial.** The yearly update for year Y is due by 30 June of Y+1, and is forbidden after 30 June of Y+6; after that, the period counts only if the sale of the production and the payment of the contribution were in due time (art. 38-A §§ 4 to 6). From 1 January 2023 the rural activity is proved by the register alone, and before it by self-declaration (art. 38-B §§ 1 and 2; fork F-PB2-10); the register could still be made, updated and corrected up to and including 1 January 2025 (§ 3). Asserted.
9. **The 30-day line of art. 43 § 1.** The empregado's aposentadoria por invalidez starts on the 16th day of the withdrawal (the withdrawal date plus 15), or on the request if "mais de trinta dias" passed between them: 30 days is still the 16th day, 31 is the request. For the other categories the same line runs from the start of the incapacity. Asserted.
10. **The ages are the compilation's.** Arts. 48 and 51 and arts. 52 and 56 print 65 and 60, 60 and 55, 70 and 65, and 30, 25 and 35 years, as amended up to Lei nº 9.876/1999 and Lei nº 9.032/1995. No provision of arts. 48 to 56 carries wording later than those two laws, and this piece encodes the live text as printed and does not correct it from outside knowledge (keel instruction 14). Asserted (the ages are the ones the text prints).
11. **The 25% is outside the cap and not in the pension (art. 45).** The increase "será devido ainda que o valor da aposentadoria atinja o limite máximo legal", is recalculated with each readjustment, ends with the death and is "não ... incorporável ao valor da pensão". Art. 33's "ressalvado o disposto no art. 45" is carried by adding the 25% to the capped value. Asserted.
12. **A rural worker is derived, not declared.** Art. 48 § 1 points to art. 11, I, a; V, g; VI and VII. There is no such category in the core, so the rule `art. 48, § 1º — o segurado é trabalhador rural` works it out: a segurado especial always is; an empregado or an avulso only if the service is rural; a contribuinte individual only if he is the one of art. 11, V, g (an input from pb1's text). Fork F-PB2-19. Asserted.
13. **Art. 47 and the five years.** The fifth anniversary of the start is inside the period of inciso I ("dentro de 5 anos"); the day after is inciso II. In inciso II the three steps of six months are read as running from the date of the verification to the day before the same date six, twelve and eighteen months later; for a verification on 31 August the library moves the date to the last day of the shorter month (28 February). Asserted.
14. **The percentages (arts. 50 and 53).** The aposentadoria por idade reaches 100% of the salário-de-benefício only at 360 contributions (70% plus 1% for each complete group of 12); the aposentadoria por tempo de serviço is 70% at 25 (woman) or 30 (man) years and 100% at 30 or 35, 6% for each new complete year; below the years of art. 52 art. 53 gives nothing (`NOTHING`). Asserted.
15. **Two live rules multiply for one benefit.** For the aposentadoria por tempo de serviço, art. 29, I gives the mean times the fator previdenciário and art. 53 then gives 70% to 100% of that salário-de-benefício; both are live in this text. The seat composes them by taking the salário-de-benefício as an input of art. 53 (keel name `o salário-de-benefício em reais (art. 29)`). No case asserts the two together; each rule is asserted alone.
16. **Art. 56 is silent on the carência.** The professor's and the professora's aposentadoria needs only the 30 or 25 years of "efetivo exercício em funções de magistério" on the article's own words; art. 52 asks the carência and art. 56 does not, and art. 56 names no level of teaching. The rule reads the article as written; the seat warns in the description of the question that the carência of art. 25 may also be asked. Fork F-PB2-23. Asserted.
17. **The years of special work are not in the text.** Art. 57 says "15, 20 ou 25 anos, conforme dispuser a lei"; the rule takes the years as an input and gives `NOTHING` for any other number. The list of harmful agents is "definida pelo Poder Executivo" (art. 58 caput), another input. Asserted.
18. **Art. 55 counts service time "mesmo que anterior à perda da qualidade de segurado".** The counting rule takes no input about the quality of insured, so it holds whether or not art. 15 is met. No case asserts it: it is the absence of an input.
19. **Notes that are not provisions.** The "(Vide Decreto ...)" notes on art. 40 (seven decrees), the "(Vide Medida Provisória nº 316)" notes on art. 41-A and the "(Vide Lei nº 9.732)" note on art. 57 § 6 are part of the lines quoted and are not provisions; the decrees on the payment of the abono anual are not among the inputs. No case asserts it.
20. **Which payment window.** A benefit of up to one salário mínimo is paid in the window of art. 41-A § 3 (from the fifth working day before the end of the month of competence to the fifth working day of the next month); a centavo above it, in the window of § 2 (the first to the fifth working day of the next month). Asserted.

## Before this is worth relying on

1. **A Brazilian social-security lawyer should read each module against the official text**, rule by rule. Nothing here is verified against the official text, and the text is a plain-text rendering of a compilation, not the law as it stands (finding 10).
2. **The 25 forks are my choices**, nobody else's; the ones most likely to change an answer are F-PB2-02 (rounding the 80%), F-PB2-06 (the points dates), F-PB2-19 and F-PB2-20 (who is a rural worker and the three routes of art. 48), F-PB2-23 (the carência of the professor's aposentadoria) and F-PB2-08 (the benefit of "valor mínimo" as the salário mínimo).
