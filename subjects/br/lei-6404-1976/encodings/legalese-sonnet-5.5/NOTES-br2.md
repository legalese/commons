## Seat

**The seat** is um acionista, inclusive o acionista minoritário, de uma companhia (sociedade anônima), perguntando o que a Lei lhe garante, o que lhe exige, e o que a companhia, os administradores e o acionista controlador podem fazer em relação a ele (the brief's wording). In English: a shareholder of a company, minority shareholders included, asking what the Law gives them, what it requires of them, and what the company, its managers and its controlling shareholder may do in relation to them.

**Why this seat for these chapters.** Capítulos V a IX are mostly about the company's instruments (debêntures, bônus de subscrição), its birth (subscription, deposit, the constituent meeting, filing and publication) and its books. A shareholder meets them in six ways, and the questions follow those:

1. as the holder of a preference right (Arts. 57, § 1º and 77, parágrafo único) and as a voter on a change of the estatuto that debenture holders may veto (Art. 57, § 2º);
2. as a creditor that ranks behind or ahead of debenture holders in a liquidation (Art. 58, § 4º);
3. as someone who needs to know which organ may decide an issue (Arts. 59 and 76) and who answers if it is made irregularly (Art. 62, § 1º);
4. as a debenture holder as well (Arts. 71 and 66 to 70: convening, voting, approving a change);
5. as a subscriber while the company is being formed (Arts. 80 to 93: the entrada, the deposit, the paper of the subscription, the meeting, the vote, the founder's status, who answers), as a founder where the shareholder is one (Arts. 82 to 84, 86, 88, § 1º and 93: what the founders owe the subscribers) and at the company's filing (Arts. 94 to 99);
6. as a person who may obtain, challenge or compel the exhibition of the books (Arts. 100 to 105).

**The layer.** `br2-seat.l4` holds 47 questions under six `§§` headings in the text's order. Each has `@export` (the question in the first person, with the citation) and `@desc` on every input (worded to the shareholder). Each calls one rule of layer 1, building the core's records (`Perfil da companhia`) and the piece's own records (`Emissão de debêntures (art. 59)`, `Situação da constituição (art. 80)`) in a `WHERE` from the plain answers, with a stated placeholder for every field the rule called does not read. `br2-cases-seat.l4` has 136 assertions; the count written equals the count satisfied (the last block of `## What check.sh prints`).

**What gets no question, and why.** A provision that decides nothing the shareholder would ask about is encoded in layer 1 and has no question. The brief asks a question for every provision that puts a duty or a liability on the person, so these are the omissions, each with its reason:

- *Addressed to the company, to the agente fiduciário, to the CVM, to the registry, to a court or to the first administrators, with no fact the shareholder can state or claim from it:* the contents of the escritura and of the certificates (Arts. 61, 64, 65, 72, 79), the agente fiduciário's qualification and duties (Arts. 66 to 70), the CVM's powers over the agente and over a registration (Art. 67, parágrafo único; Art. 82, §§ 1º and 2º), the cédula and the issue abroad (Arts. 72 and 73), the extinction records (Art. 74), the company's filings, publication and the creation of branches (Arts. 95 to 98), the books' contents and electronic keeping by the issuing agent (Art. 101) and the depositary institution's statements (Art. 102).
- *A duty of the meeting or of the notary, not of the shareholder as such:* the meeting's own conduct and minutes (Art. 87, §§ 1º and 4º), the contents of the constitutive deed (Art. 88, § 2º) and the announcement of the meeting (Art. 86, parágrafo único).
- *The terms of the debenture contract and the machinery of the debenture holders' meeting,* which concern the company and its debenture holders: Arts. 52 to 56, 58, §§ 1º to 3º, 5º and 6º, 63, 65, 69, and Art. 71, §§ 2º to 4º and 8º to 10. A shareholder who is also a debenture holder reads them in the layer-1 modules; the questions asked of that shareholder are the ones that reach a shareholder as such (the preference, the approval of a change to the estatuto, the order of payment in a liquidation) and the three a debenture holder uses (convening, voting, approving a change).
- *How an organ decides, with no consequence for the shareholder beyond the question already asked about who decides:* the meeting's power to leave the value and number of series of an issue undetermined and to delegate its conditions to the board (Art. 59, §§ 3º and 4º).
- *Ruled out by the "none" rows in `## Jural relations`:* Arts. 60 (repealed), 62 II, §§ 3º, 4º (repealed), 62, §§ 5º and 6º (inert), 63, 78 and 80, parágrafo único (no relation).

The founder questions (Arts. 82, 83, 84, 86, 88, § 1º and 93) are asked in the first person of a founder ("Sendo fundador, ..."); a shareholder who is not a founder skips them, and the heading comment of the group says so.

**Questions that are conditional on a situation.** The group "Debêntures", "Bônus de subscrição", "Constituição da companhia" and "Formalidades complementares" arise only when the company has issued such securities or is being formed; the `§§` heading comments say so, so the form can ask first.

**Hohfeld, from this seat.** The rows of `## Jural relations` give both sides of every relation. Seen from the shareholder: the claims are Arts. 57, § 1º (preference to subscribe convertible debentures), 62, § 1º (the administrators answer for an irregular issue), 77, parágrafo único (preference to subscribe bônus), 92 (the founders and the intermediary institution answer), 100, § 1º (certificates), 103 and 104 (the books); the privileges are Art. 87, § 2º (one vote per share), Art. 88 (the status of founder) and Art. 90 (representation by an attorney); the power is the request of shareholders with 5% of the capital under Art. 105 (and, as holder of 10% of the debentures, the call under Art. 71, § 1º); the immunity is Art. 87, § 2º (the majority has no power to change the draft estatuto). The liabilities the shareholder bears are the subscriber's duties (Arts. 81 and 85) and having the company declared constituted over their opposition unless the opponents hold more than half of the capital (Art. 87, § 3º).

**What a substitution would change.** The only figures in the range that something outside the text can substitute are the CVM's (the time limit of Art. 104, parágrafo único, the standard multiples of Art. 65, § 1º, the quorum reduction of Art. 71, § 8º) and a larger initial realisation of capital that another law may demand (Art. 80, parágrafo único). All four are plain inputs; the Law's own figures (10% in Arts. 71, § 1º and 80, II; 50% in Arts. 71, §§ 3º and 5º, 87 and 97; 5 days, 6 months, 30 days, 60 days, 4 months, 5 years; 5% in Art. 105) are fixed in the rules.

## Needs from the core

Nothing here blocks the piece; each item is a local stand-in that the lead may promote.

1. **`Parte` constructors the core lacks.** The core's 21 constructors have no entry for the issuing agent of certificates (Art. 27, piece br1), the registry of commerce and its officer (Arts. 62, § 2º, 95 to 98), the judge (Art. 103, parágrafo único), the Banco Central do Brasil (Arts. 72, 73), the bank that holds the deposit (Art. 81) or the holder of a bônus de subscrição (Art. 75, parágrafo único). Where a rule needed a `Parte` it used the nearest core constructor (`a instituição financeira` for the issuing agent in Arts. 101 and 103; `o debenturista` for a holder), and where the party is only a fact (the bank, the registry, the judge) the rule takes it as a plain input or names it in the rule's wording. If the lead adds `o agente emissor de certificados`, `o juiz` and `o titular de bônus de subscrição`, Arts. 101 and 103 can name them.
2. **A shared enumeration of calls of a meeting.** `Convocação da assembleia de debenturistas (art. 71, § 3º)` (first, second, third call) and `Convocação da assembleia de constituição (art. 87)` (first, second) are defined here, each with its citation, because the core has none. Pieces br3 (Arts. 125 and 135, installation of the general meeting) and br4 will need the same three words. A core `Convocação` (primeira, segunda, terceira) would let the quorum rules share a type.
3. **A shared enumeration of the social books.** `Livro social (art. 100)` is defined here. Pieces br3 (Art. 130, the minutes), br4 (Art. 142, minutes of the board) and br5 cite the books of Art. 100 by name; if the lead wants one type, it is this one.
4. **A fraction test.** Several rules here test "no mínimo X% de Y" by multiplication (`AT LEAST 10% TIMES ...`); the core already shows the pattern in its tests, so no new core rule is needed. Listed so that a reader does not look for one.
5. **The core's `YMD` shim collides with a newer prelude.** Measured on a newer build (see `## What the stable binary does not accept`): any file that imports `br-core` and writes `YMD 2026 3 10` reports "multiple definitions for the identifier `YMD`", because the newer prelude carries its own `YMD` (`daydate.l4:135`). The stable 1.6.98 has none, which is why the shim is right for the target. When the lead moves the merged folder to a build with the prelude `YMD`, the core's shim needs to go (or be renamed); the pieces cannot do it, since the core is read-only to them.

## What the text turns out to say

Each finding is something the rule-by-rule encoding showed, not something it assumed. "Asserted." means a case in the named file asserts it; where a finding is about the file and not about a rule, it says what checks it instead.

1. **The compiled text keeps the superseded wording next to the live one.** 73 of the 436 lines of this range carry the struck marker, among them every line of Art. 60 and of its §§ 1º to 4º, the first versions of Art. 59, §§ 1º to 4º, and the old Capítulo IX list of books (lines 764 and 775 to 779). None of them is quoted or encoded. A line that holds both live and struck words (for example line 461, the "Revogado" note of Art. 60, and line 547, limb `a` of Art. 66, § 3º) is never quoted. Checked by `srcrefs.py check` (0 problems over every module), not by a case.
2. **Art. 64, IV still speaks of the real-estate registry although Art. 62, II is repealed.** The inscription of the escritura in that registry (Art. 62, II) is gone, but the certificate of the debenture must still say the date and the office of the registry in which the issue "foi inscrita". Read (fork F10) as: where the issue was in fact entered there, the certificate says so; where it was not, there is nothing to state. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap5-emissao.l4` (the Art. 64 block, "Inciso IV").
3. **Art. 59, IX and Art. 71, § 7º leave the vote count undefined by this chapter.** For the resolution that unbundles the nominal value from interest and other rights, § 7º counts votes "pelo direito econômico proporcional possuído por titular", a basis and not a number; this chapter gives no number, so the rule returns `NOTHING` for the number of votes in that resolution, and one vote per debenture otherwise (§ 6º). Asserted in `br2-tests-cap5-assembleia-extincao.l4` and in `br2-cases-seat.l4`.
4. **Art. 71, § 5º sets a floor but not what happens below it.** The escritura's majority "não será inferior à metade das debêntures em circulação"; the text does not say what governs an escritura that fixes less. The rule returns `NOTHING` for the approval in that case, not a refusal and not a default majority. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap5-assembleia-extincao.l4` (400 of 1,000) and in `br2-cases-seat.l4`.
5. **Art. 71, § 3º names two calls; § 9º mentions a third.** Installation: first call with half the debentures in circulation (exactly half is enough), second call "com qualquer número" (zero present still installs, read literally). Nothing gives an installation quorum for a third call, and § 9º says a reduced-quorum resolution "somente poderá ser adotada em terceira convocação", so a reduced-quorum resolution on a second call is refused. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap5-assembleia-extincao.l4`.
6. **Art. 71, § 10 is strict, Art. 71, § 1º is not.** "Mais de metade" in the dispersal test: one holder with exactly half still leaves ownership dispersed; "10% (dez por cento), no mínimo" in § 1º: exactly 10% suffices to convene. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap5-assembleia-extincao.l4`.
7. **Art. 80, II: exactly 10% is enough, and the inciso gives way to a larger figure in another law.** The Law's 10% is "no mínimo"; the parágrafo único switches the inciso off for companies whose activity-law requires more, and the other law's figure is not in this text, so the rule answers that the inciso does not apply and leaves the larger test to the caller. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap7-constituicao-requisitos.l4` (100 of 1,000 satisfies, 99 does not; the larger-figure case).
8. **Art. 81 and its parágrafo único count in two ways.** The deposit is due within 5 days of receiving the sums (receipt day is day 0, the fifth day is in time); the return right arises when the six months from the deposit pass without the company having been constituted, and a company constituted on the last day (2 September for a 2 March deposit) was in time. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap7-constituicao-requisitos.l4`.
9. **Art. 87 uses three different thresholds.** Installation on first call: at least half of the capital present (exactly half installs); second call: any number; Art. 87, § 3º: the company is declared constituted unless subscribers holding more than half of the capital oppose (exactly half does not block). Asserted in `br2-tests-cap7-constituicao-requisitos.l4` and in `br2-cases-seat.l4`.
10. **The compiled text carries "(Vide Decreto-Lei nº 2.296, de 1986)" on Art. 87.** The instrument is not in the input and nothing in it is encoded; the sentence of the article is encoded as it stands. Not asserted (there is nothing to assert).
11. **"Fundador" exists only in the private-subscription article.** Art. 88 makes every subscriber a founder in a private subscription; for a public subscription the article says nothing and the rule returns `NOTHING`, although Art. 92 then speaks of founders and Art. 82 of the founders' application. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap7-constituicao-particular.l4` and in `br2-cases-seat.l4`.
12. **Art. 99, parágrafo único covers only the earlier acts.** The company answers for the acts and operations the first administrators did before the formalities only if the general meeting resolves so; for later acts the paragraph is silent and the rule returns `NOTHING`. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap8-formalidades.l4` and in `br2-cases-seat.l4`.
13. **The scope of the books changes from one provision to the next.** Art. 100, § 1º gives certificates for incisos I to III; §§ 2º and 3º allow electronic replacement for incisos I to V; Art. 104 makes the company liable for defects in incisos I to III; none of them speaks of the books of the conselho de administração, the diretoria and the conselho fiscal (VI and VII) for certificates or replacement. For books outside a provision's scope each rule returns `NOTHING`. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap9-livros.l4` and in `br2-cases-seat.l4`.
14. **Art. 100, VII has no "se houver".** The book of Atas e Pareceres do Conselho Fiscal is required as written (fork F19), although the Law lets a conselho fiscal be non-permanent (Art. 161, piece br4); inciso VI, by contrast, says "se houver" for the conselho de administração's book. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap9-livros.l4`.
15. **Art. 105: exactly 5% is enough.** The court may order the exhibition of the books "em sua totalidade" at the request of shareholders with 5% "no mínimo" of the capital; the rule tests the combined holding by multiplication. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap9-livros.l4` (50 of 1,000 satisfies, 49 does not) and in `br2-cases-seat.l4`.
16. **Art. 74 counts five years from the extinction day.** The day of the extinction is day 0 and the last day is the same calendar day five years later; keeping the documents until the day before is a breach. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap5-assembleia-extincao.l4`.
17. **Art. 89 says what a public deed is not needed for.** "A incorporação de imóveis para formação do capital social não exige escritura pública": the rule answers FALSE. Asserted in `br2-tests-cap7-constituicao-particular.l4`.

## What the stable binary does not accept

Sorted by how the failure shows. All measured on the 1.6.98 binary given to this run, with `JL4_LIBRARY_PATH` unset.

**Loud (the checker refuses, with a message).**

- **A bare `BRANCH` arm indented no further than `BRANCH`.** `DECIDE f IS BRANCH` followed by arms at the indentation of `DECIDE` is a parser error: `incorrect indentation (got 5, should be greater than 15)`. Put `BRANCH` alone on its line and indent the arms beneath it (measured with a four-line rule; the form with `BRANCH` alone on its line checked and its assertion was satisfied).
- **An input named like a core constructor.** `` GIVEN `o fundador` IS A BOOLEAN `` collides with the core's `Parte` constructor `o fundador`: "multiple definitions for the identifier". Every input here that would have been named for a party is named for the fact instead.
- **Names defined in two modules imported together.** Not met: every name carries its citation, so the nine layer-1 modules and the seat layer import together without a clash (`br2-seat.l4` imports eight modules and the core and checks clean).

**Silent (the checker accepts and the answer is not what it looks like).**

- **A bare `NOT` before `AND`.** `` NOT a AND b `` is read as `NOT (a AND b)`. Measured with a two-input rule: with `a` true and `b` false, `g` is true, which is the `NOT (a AND b)` reading; `(NOT a) AND b` would be false. Every `NOT` in this piece is parenthesised. Once an unparenthesised `NOT` inside an `AND`/`OR` list made one of this piece's own assertions fail, and the cause was the precedence, not the rule.
- **A rule call or a `WITH` record before `EQUALS`.** An `#ASSERT` that compares a call with `EQUALS` needs the call in brackets; every such assertion in the tests and cases is written `(call) EQUALS value`.

**Not accepted by the dialect (not used; taken from the Guide, section 5, and not re-measured here).** `REFUSE` and `#ASSERT REFUSED`; `BECAUSE` outside `LEST BREACH ... BECAUSE`; `@lang` and `@nlg:xx`; a `GIVEN` on a section; `TYPICALLY`; sets; an opaque `DECLARE`; `EVERY` and `WHOSE`; `AFTER` windows; `RULES EFFECTIVE DATE`; the temporal built-ins and the ledger. A provision the text covers only partly returns `NOTHING` for the rest, and the cases assert it.

**Other constraints observed.** There is no record update, so each question in the seat builds a whole record from its inputs. Each `#ASSERT` is on one line. `YMD` comes from the core. A duty is written as a compliance rule (a boolean, or a boolean over the core's `Prazo`), not as a `DEONTIC`, because an assertion cannot compare a `DEONTIC` value and `check.sh` counts assertions.

**On a newer build (measured once, not the target of this piece).** The `l4` on this machine's `PATH` prints `dev` for `l4 --version`. `check.sh` run with it over this folder, instead of the stable 1.6.98: the nine layer-1 modules, `br-core.l4` and `br2-seat.l4` report 0 errors, and six test or case files report 92 errors, none of them an assertion that failed. 88 are "There are multiple definitions for the identifier `YMD`": the core's shim (`br-core.l4:207`) against the `YMD` the newer prelude carries (`daydate.l4:135`), in every file that writes a date literal. The other 4 are in `br2-tests-cap5-caracteristicas.l4`, "multiple definitions for the identifier `__EQUALS__`": the predefined one against `Prelude.Sets` (`prelude.l4:1316`). Nothing was changed for this: the brief's target is the stable binary, and the core is read-only. ## Before this is worth relying on

1. **The text is a converter's rendering, not the official text.** `lei-6404-1976.pt.txt` is a plain-text rendering, made by a converter script, of a compiled text retrieved on 2026-10-21. Only the text published in the Diário Oficial da União is official. Every quotation in a `-- src:N |` line is checked against that file and not against the official text, so a wrong character in the rendering is a wrong character in the quotation. A lawyer reading the Brazilian law should check each rule against the Planalto page and the Diário Oficial.
2. **Nothing here has a date.** The range holds neither the enactment date (Art. 1 title block, line 5, piece br1) nor the entry into force (piece br7). No date constant and no commencement rule is built. The amendments the compiled text shows as "(Redação dada pela Lei nº ...)" or "(Incluído pela Lei nº ...)" (for example Lei nº 14.711, de 2023, on Art. 59, VIII and IX and on Art. 71, §§ 7º to 10; Lei nº 9.457, de 1997, on Art. 100) are encoded as the live text, with no version of the rule before them: the encoding answers for the text as compiled, today, and not for a past date.
3. **Nothing was built or deployed.** There is no pipeline run, no export, no wizard and no `encoding.json` here; the brief did not ask for them. No file was added to git, committed or pushed.
4. **One agent wrote the rules and the tests.** There was no independent test author and no second reader. The expected values were written from the text before the run, with the text beside them, and no assertion has ever been edited to pass, but the same reading produced both sides. A reviewer should reread the tests that pin a judgement: the forks in `## Fork register`, and the `NOTHING` cases.
5. **Judgements are inputs.** Whether a flaw "resulted" from an omission, whether a delay was culpable, whether a conflict of interest exists (Art. 66, § 3º, e), whether an immediate call was immediate, whether a deliberation was taken "na forma" required, are facts the caller states. The encoding does not decide them.
6. **Inputs from outside the range are what the caller says.** The 36 rows of cross-references listed under `## Cross-references out of my ranges` take the outcome of a rule in another piece (or a CVM or Executive instrument) as a plain input. A merge that wires them to the real rules is not done here.
7. **The seat is one reading.** The shareholder's questions in `br2-seat.l4` are written for a person who may also be a subscriber, a debenture holder or a minority holder. A question asked as a debenture holder (Arts. 71) assumes the shareholder also holds debentures; a question asked as a subscriber (Arts. 80 to 93) assumes the company is still being formed.
8. **What was read of the skills' `source-patterns/` directory.** The directory has eleven area files (`01` to `11`) and an index, `source-patterns.md`. Read: the index (twice); `05-duties-powers-consequences.md`, lines 1 to 260; `08-judgement-and-discretion.md`, lines 1 to 330; `09-text-that-is-not-a-rule.md`, lines 1 to 140; `11-when-the-encoding-cannot-answer.md`, from entry 11.3 (the read was capped at its first 230 lines, so it may not have reached 11.6); `07-presumptions-and-defaults.md`, entry 7.3; `06-parties-and-things.md`, entry 6.9; `04-dates-and-periods.md`, entries 4.3 to 4.5.
